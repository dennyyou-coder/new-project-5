## Safety and service boundary

- The articles stop ordinary troubleshooting for swelling, smoke, melting, burnt odor, unusual heat, damaged power equipment, liquid intrusion or an internal electrical fault.
- Model-specific instructions are not promoted into universal contact cleaning, temperature, reset, filter washing or router-security rules.
- Battery, BMS, charging circuit, sealed enclosure, motor and internal electrical work remain with the responsible authorized or qualified service party.
- Wi-Fi troubleshooting does not request or expose passwords, weaken network security or use a factory reset before preserving maps and evidence.
