## Current ownership correction

The April 2024 closing created Beko Europe B.V. with Beko/Arçelik at 75% and Whirlpool Corporation at 25%. That is no longer the current equity conclusion.

On 19 June 2026 Arçelik A.Ş. disclosed an agreement for wholly owned Beko B.V. to acquire Whirlpool EMEA Holdings LLC's 25% interest. A 23 June 2026 KAP/PDP entry records completion. The transaction ended the shareholder relationship while the Whirlpool trademark licence remained in force. The current research position is therefore:

- Beko B.V. owns 100% of Beko Europe B.V. after the June 2026 closing.
- Whirlpool Corporation no longer holds the 25% equity interest.
- Beko Europe remains a licensee of the Whirlpool brand in specified jurisdictions.
- Whirlpool Corporation retained its EMEA KitchenAid small- and major-domestic-appliance business in the original 2024 transaction.
