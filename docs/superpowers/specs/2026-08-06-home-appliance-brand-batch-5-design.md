# Home Appliance Brand Batch 5 Design

## Scope

Publish three standalone brand-intelligence profiles: Hisense, De’Longhi, and Groupe SEB. This batch adds no ownership articles and does not tag or alter existing articles.

## Page boundaries

- Hisense is a consumer brand spanning multiple Hisense group companies. The page must separate Hisense Group Holdings, Hisense Home Appliances Group, Hisense International, regional sellers, factories, importers, and warrantors.
- De’Longhi is the historic consumer brand of the listed De’ Longhi S.p.A. group. The page must separate the listed parent, controlling shareholder, regional commercial companies, factories, sourcing partners, retailers, and warranty providers.
- Groupe SEB is a corporate group profile for SEB S.A., not a claim that “Groupe SEB” is the product trademark on every appliance. The page must distinguish the listed company from its consumer and professional brands, subsidiaries, plants, distributors, and service entities.

## Evidence and visuals

Each page uses at least three official or regulatory sources, an official transparent logo, one authentic official hero image rendered at 1600 by 1000 pixels, and two dedicated explanatory SVG visuals. Manufacturing and channel evidence remains limited to the entity, market, model, and reporting period supported by the source.

## Categories

- Hisense: primary Home Appliances & Small Appliances; secondary Floorcare & Home Cleaning based on current official regional vacuum and wet/dry floorcare ranges.
- De’Longhi: primary Home Appliances & Small Appliances only.
- Groupe SEB: primary Home Appliances & Small Appliances; secondary Floorcare & Home Cleaning because the group’s current official portfolio and reporting include Rowenta-led floorcare.

## Release gate

The published profile count increases from 67 to 70. All three routes, category memberships, local image assets, sitemap entries, production build, desktop layout, 390-pixel layout, and browser console must pass. Existing article relationships remain unchanged.
