# Equipment Technical Profiles Batch 08 Design

## Objective

Publish three evidence-backed equipment database pages through the established World Clean Biz technical-profile system:

- `/equipment/walk-behind-floor-sweeper`
- `/equipment/ride-on-floor-sweeper`
- `/equipment/carpet-spot-extractor`

This batch creates technical equipment pages only. It does not create SEO articles, ownership records, brand records, component pages or new shared page architecture.

## Selection rationale

- Walk-behind and ride-on sweepers have different operator relationships, turning envelopes, hopper handling, dust-control duties and transport constraints, so buyers need narrower records than the existing Floor Sweeper overview.
- Carpet spot extractors address portable response to localised carpet and upholstery soil. They are not substitutes for self-contained or tank extractors used across full rooms.
- All three categories have current official model documentation, at least four already-published WCB brand profiles and clear commercial purchasing decisions.

## Identity boundaries

### Walk-Behind Floor Sweeper

Include manually propelled and powered sweepers whose operator walks behind during normal sweeping. Exclude seated ride-on, stand-on, autonomous, street and sweeper-scrubber formats. Keep brush path, hopper, filter area, propulsion, slope and theoretical productivity tied to the exact configuration.

### Ride-On Floor Sweeper

Include sweepers with an integrated seated operator position during normal sweeping. Exclude walk-behind, stand-on, autonomous, street and combination sweeper-scrubbers. Separate low-dump and high-dump configurations, and verify turning, overhead clearance, slope, braking, dust filtration and raised-hopper restrictions.

### Carpet Spot Extractor

Include portable commercial spray-extraction machines officially intended for local carpet, stair, upholstery or vehicle-interior treatment. Exclude domestic-only products, dry compound machines, steam cleaners and self-contained full-area extractors. Tank volume, spray pressure, airflow, water lift, heater, tool and textile approval remain model-specific.

## Evidence and visual design

Each record follows `content/equipment/README.md`, declares at least five primary sources, six to eight representative models across four or more published brand profiles, labelled WCB assessments and component families without links. No OEM, factory, supplier, certification, compatibility or achieved-productivity relationship is inferred.

Each profile receives one official real hero image at 1600 x 1000, two official WebP content photos at 1500 x 900 or larger, and one responsive WCB verification map. Every diagram states: `Family labels do not establish cross-model compatibility`.

## Publication

Records remain draft until schema, evidence, asset, focused test, full test, production build, desktop, 390 px, Preview and browser-console checks pass. Production releases only through GitHub `main` and the Vercel Git integration.
