# Home Appliance Brand Batch 6 Design

## Scope

Publish three standalone brand-intelligence profiles: Rowenta, Gorenje, and SUPOR. This batch adds no ownership articles, does not tag existing articles, and leaves all existing article relationships unchanged.

## Page boundaries

- Rowenta is a consumer brand in Groupe SEB's portfolio. The page must separate the brand from parent SEB S.A., Rowenta France S.A.S., SEB Développement, regional sellers, factories, importers, retailers, and warranty providers.
- Gorenje is a consumer brand that joined Hisense Group in 2018. The page must separate the brand from Gorenje gospodinjski aparati, d.o.o., Hisense Europe, Hisense Group, manufacturing sites, regional sales companies, importers, retailers, and warranty providers.
- SUPOR is a consumer brand whose principal listed operating company is Zhejiang Supor Co., Ltd. The page must separate the brand from Zhejiang Supor, direct parent SEB INTERNATIONALE S.A.S., ultimate parent SEB S.A., subsidiaries, manufacturing sites, exporters, retailers, and after-sales entities.

## Evidence and visuals

Each page uses at least three official, regulatory, or official investor-relations sources, an official logo, one authentic official product or factory hero image rendered at 1600 by 1000 pixels, and two dedicated explanatory SVG visuals. Manufacturing and channel evidence remains limited to the entity, market, product family, and reporting period supported by the cited source.

## Categories

- Rowenta: primary Floorcare & Home Cleaning; secondary Home Appliances & Small Appliances because the official portfolio covers vacuuming, floor washing, garment care, air treatment, and related home appliances.
- Gorenje: primary Home Appliances & Small Appliances; secondary Floorcare & Home Cleaning because the current official international range includes vacuum cleaners, stick vacuums, floor cleaners, and handheld vacuums.
- SUPOR: primary Home Appliances & Small Appliances; secondary Floorcare & Home Cleaning because SUPOR's official company materials identify vacuum cleaners and other home-environment appliances in the portfolio.

## Release gate

The published profile count increases from 70 to 73. All three routes, category memberships, local image assets, sitemap entries, production build, desktop layout, 390-pixel layout, and browser console must pass. Existing article relationships remain unchanged.
