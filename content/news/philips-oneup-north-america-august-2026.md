Versuni announced the Philips OneUp electric mop's introduction in the United States and Canada on August 18, 2026. The [North American launch](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/oneup-your-cleaning-routine-versuni-launches-the-philips-oneup-mop-in-north-america-302852579.html) covered the OneUp 3000 and 5000 series, extending Philips-branded floorcare into cordless electric mopping.

The system dispenses clean water and recovers dirty water into separate tanks. A reusable microfiber pad provides floor contact, while the integrated water supply removes the need for a separate bucket. The device is guided by the user and belongs to a different category from an autonomous robot vacuum.

Versuni lists maximum runtimes of 50 minutes for one model and 70 minutes for the other, with coverage and water capacity also varying by configuration. The company describes use on compatible hard floors including sealed wood, laminate, vinyl and stone. Floor-care instructions and the precise model remain relevant when assessing suitability.

The product's central proposition is control of clean and dirty water during a mopping session, together with a reusable cleaning surface. Versuni is the licensee behind Philips domestic appliances, so the brand on the machine and the business responsible for this launch are distinct names. The August announcement establishes the regional introduction of these two series rather than a universal release across all markets.
