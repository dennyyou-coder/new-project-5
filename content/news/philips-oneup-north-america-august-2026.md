Versuni announced the Philips OneUp electric mop's entry into the United States and Canada on 18 August 2026. The cordless range comprises the OneUp 3000 and 5000 series.

The system dispenses clean water and collects dirty water separately, using a reusable microfiber pad. Versuni lists maximum runtimes of 50 and 70 minutes depending on the model. The launch extends Philips-branded floorcare into electric mopping; the device should not be described as an autonomous robot vacuum.
