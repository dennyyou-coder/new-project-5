The FCC announced conditional approval on 9 September 2026 for four [Husqvarna](/brands/husqvarna) robotic mower platforms: Automower 305V iQ, 310V iQ, 420V iQ and 440V iQ. Husqvarna subsequently confirmed the decision in a company statement.

The named platforms are exempt from the Covered List restriction on foreign-produced advanced robotic devices. Husqvarna said the new platforms were expected to launch in the United States in 2027. The approval is a regulatory milestone for specified products, not confirmation of immediate retail availability.
