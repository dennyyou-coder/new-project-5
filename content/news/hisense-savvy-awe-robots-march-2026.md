[Hisense](/brands/hisense) presented the Savvy household robot at AWE 2026, alongside the Moii companion robot and Harley humanoid, according to the exhibition organizer's closing report. The Shanghai event ran from 12 to 15 March.

The display illustrates Hisense's work on broader home-service robotics. An exhibition appearance does not establish a retail launch date, final price or verified ability to perform every demonstrated household task.
