Southern Metropolis Daily reported on 11 April 2026 that [Narwal](/brands/narwal) co-founder Wu Yihao had left his management positions and would retain a strategic advisory role. His previous responsibilities included product delivery, supply-chain operations and global marketing.

The report said Narwal had not responded to questions about the detailed reasons and responsibilities by publication time. It did not establish a completed stock-market listing or a change of ownership.
