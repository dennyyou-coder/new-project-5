DJI launched its second generation of robot vacuums, the [ROMO 2](/brands/dji-romo) series, in China on May 11, 2026. The line has two models, the ROMO P2 and ROMO A2. Pre-orders opened on launch day, and sales began on June 11.

Each model comes with either a water-tank base or a slimmer base that connects to the water supply and drain. In China, launch prices were CNY 5,499 and CNY 5,999 for the A2, and CNY 5,999 and CNY 6,499 for the P2. DJI took the series global on September 3, with orders in Europe from September. European prices start at €1,199 for the A2 and €1,299 for the P2.

DJI rates suction at 36,000 Pa. A LiDAR-guided side arm swings out to 123° and, DJI says, reaches 4.5 cm further into corners than the first generation. The company says the obstacle sensing can pick out cables about 2 mm across as well as glass and mirrors. The base uses four high-pressure water jets to clean the mop, and DJI claims up to 365 days without maintenance. These figures come from DJI's own tests.

The first ROMO went on sale in China in August 2025 and reached Europe in October 2025. A second generation within a year shows DJI is staying in the category rather than treating it as a one-off. The price puts ROMO 2 in the same range as the flagships from Roborock, Ecovacs and Dreame, the brands that lead global shipments.
