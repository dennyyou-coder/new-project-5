The UPC's Düsseldorf Local Division revoked an [ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) inspection and evidence-preservation order against [Roborock](/brands/roborock) on 19 December 2025 in case UPC_CFI_834/2025. Legal reporting says confidentiality obligations were retained.

The court addressed the completeness of information submitted for an order obtained without hearing the other party. This procedural decision should not be presented as a final judgment on patent infringement or validity.
