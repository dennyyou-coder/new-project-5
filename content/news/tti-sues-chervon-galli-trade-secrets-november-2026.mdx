[TTI](/brands/tti) North America, [Milwaukee](/brands/milwaukee) Electric Tool and TTI's sales company R&B Sales & Marketing sued [Chervon](/brands/chervon) North America, Chervon Holdings and Chervon's chief executive, Joseph Galli Jr., on November 9, 2026. The case was filed in the US District Court for the Northern District of Illinois in Chicago as case 1:26-cv-13942, and the plaintiffs demand a jury trial.

Galli was TTI's chief executive from 2008 until he retired in 2024, and became Chervon's CEO on September 10, 2026. The 57-page complaint alleges that from June 2025 a serving TTI sales executive passed him confidential store, sales and customer information and Milwaukee financial slides. It also says Chervon hired at least 30 former TTI employees between September 10 and November 3, and that 18 of the 19 outside hires Chervon announced in that period came from TTI.

TTI brings eight counts, including trade-secret misappropriation under federal and Illinois law, tortious interference with contract and unfair competition. It seeks damages, disgorgement, punitive damages and injunctions limiting Chervon's use of TTI information and its hiring of TTI employees whose restrictions have not expired. The complaint does not state an amount.

Chervon's Hong Kong shares fell more than 9% intraday on November 12. As of November 19, the docket showed no injunction motion and no response from Chervon or Galli. The claims are allegations the court has not ruled on.

The full account of the complaint is in [WCB's article on the case](/blog/tti-sues-chervon-galli-trade-secrets), and [the analysis](/blog/tti-strikes-back-chervon-lawsuit-analysis) sets out where it could go.
