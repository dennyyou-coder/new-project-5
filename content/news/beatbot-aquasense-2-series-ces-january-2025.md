[Beatbot](/brands/beatbot) introduced the AquaSense 2 series at CES on January 7, 2025, with three models spanning different pool-cleaning functions and price levels. The company named AquaSense 2, AquaSense 2 Pro and AquaSense 2 Ultra as the new family.

At the top of the range, the Ultra combined camera, infrared and ultrasonic sensing for mapping and navigation. Cleaning coverage varied by model, with the lineup extending across floor, wall, waterline and surface maintenance. Those differences matter when identifying a product: functions described for the Ultra were not a promise for every machine in the series.

Beatbot also announced a three-year warranty for the family and a charging-dock design intended to make everyday handling easier. The [CES release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/beatbot-unveils-the-revolutionary-ai-powered-aquasense-2-ultra-at-ces-2025-302344052.html) scheduled purchase availability for February 10, 2025, with U.S. launch MSRPs of $1,499 for AquaSense 2, $2,499 for Pro and $3,450 for Ultra.

The prices document the original launch position and are not current promotional offers. The series became an earlier step in Beatbot's product timeline before the company [introduced AquaSense X and its filter-cleaning station at CES 2026](/news/beatbot-aquasense-x-ces-january-2026). Those are separate generations and different maintenance workflows, even though both announcements used CES to introduce the range.
