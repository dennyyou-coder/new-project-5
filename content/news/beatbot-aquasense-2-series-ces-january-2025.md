[Beatbot](/brands/beatbot) unveiled the AquaSense 2 series on January 7, 2025, at CES. The range comprised AquaSense 2, AquaSense 2 Pro and AquaSense 2 Ultra.

The Ultra combined camera, infrared and ultrasonic sensing for pool mapping, while the broader lineup differentiated floor, wall, waterline and surface-cleaning functions by model. Beatbot also announced a three-year warranty for the new family and a charging-dock design intended to simplify handling.

The release scheduled purchases for February 10, 2025. It listed U.S. launch MSRPs of $1,499, $2,499 and $3,450 respectively; these historical launch prices do not describe current promotions.
