Globe (Jiangsu), the company behind [Greenworks](/brands/greenworks), disclosed first-half 2026 revenue of CNY 2.994 billion, up 0.59%, in its half-year report. It reported a net loss attributable to shareholders of approximately CNY 165 million, compared with profit of CNY 133 million a year earlier.

Operating cash flow was also negative at approximately CNY 237 million. The figures cover the consolidated business, including a broad outdoor power equipment portfolio, and should not be read as the financial performance of robotic mowers alone.
