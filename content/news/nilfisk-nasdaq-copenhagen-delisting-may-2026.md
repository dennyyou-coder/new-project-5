[Nilfisk](/brands/nilfisk) announced its delisting from Nasdaq Copenhagen on May 12, 2026. The company identified that date as the last day of trading and official listing for its shares.

The move followed Freudenberg's completion and settlement of the takeover offer on April 15, when ownership exceeded 90% of the share capital and voting rights. Nilfisk's notice said the remaining shares were acquired in accordance with the Danish Companies Act.

Nilfisk described itself as privately held following the transaction and discontinued its investor-relations website. This is the May delisting milestone, separate from the April completion of the takeover offer.
