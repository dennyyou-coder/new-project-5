[Dyson](/brands/dyson) introduced the V16 Piston Animal to the U.S. market on 2 June 2026. The company lists suction of up to 315 air watts and a dual-conical cleaner head intended to reduce hair wrapping around the brushes.

A compaction mechanism compresses collected debris inside the bin. These are manufacturer-stated features of the V16 model. The U.S. release is a regional availability announcement and should be distinguished from the product's earlier presentations in other markets.
