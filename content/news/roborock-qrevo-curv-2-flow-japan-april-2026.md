[Roborock](/brands/roborock) launched the Qrevo Curv 2 Flow in Japan on 28 April 2026 through Amazon. The company described it as its first robot vacuum using a roller mop, with the SpiraFlow system supplying clean water and recovering dirty water during cleaning.

The Japanese announcement lists up to 20,000 Pa suction and a roller that extends toward edges. It also describes a protective arrangement for carpets. These specifications relate to the named model and market.
