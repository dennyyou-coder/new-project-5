[Tennant](/brands/tennant) reported its 2025 financial results on February 23, 2026, alongside an update on disruption following the North American launch of its enterprise resource planning system.

Full-year sales were $1,203.5 million, down 6.5% from 2024. Fourth-quarter sales fell 11.3% to $291.6 million, and the company reported a quarterly net loss of $4.4 million. Management attributed the quarter's shortfall to production disruption during the system transition.

Tennant said it was taking actions to stabilize operations. WCB's later coverage of [first-quarter 2026 results](/news/tennant-first-quarter-2026-results) follows the subsequent recovery update rather than assuming the February issues were already resolved.
