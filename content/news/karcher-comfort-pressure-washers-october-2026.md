[Kärcher](/brands/karcher) announced a new premium Comfort pressure-washer range on October 20, 2026. Its [official media notice](https://s3.kaercher-media.com/int/inside-kaercher/company/media-information.html) identifies model classes K 4 through K 7 and highlights ease of use.

The announcement concerns a family of pressure washers, rather than one machine. The reviewed notice does not specify country-by-country availability, pricing or the equipment included with each model. Those details remain to be confirmed in regional product listings.
