AVC data published on 18 November 2025 put China's online robot-vacuum retail sales for January–October at approximately CNY 16.6 billion, up 17%. Unit sales reached approximately 5.28 million, up 18.5%.

The cumulative ten-month result differs from data for individual shopping festivals. It covers monitored online retail in China, rather than global shipments or total manufacturer revenue.
