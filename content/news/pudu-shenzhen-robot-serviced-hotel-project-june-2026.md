[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) announced a cooperation agreement with Shenzhen Culture & Tourism Industry Development on June 1, 2026, for a robot-serviced hotel on the West Artificial Island of the Shenzhen–Zhongshan Link.

The plan covers reception, delivery, food service and cleaning through coordinated robot fleets. CC1 Pro and MT1 cleaning robots operated during the signing-event demonstration, alongside delivery and service machines.

The companies described a phased rollout, with selected rooms and robot-powered services scheduled for trial operations by the end of 2026. This was a project agreement and roadmap, not confirmation that the completed hotel was already open to guests.
