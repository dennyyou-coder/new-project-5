[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) announced a cooperation agreement on June 1, 2026, to develop a robot-serviced hotel on the West Artificial Island of the Shenzhen–Zhongshan Link. Its partner is Shenzhen Culture & Tourism Industry Development, with the project intended to connect several robotic services within one hospitality setting.

The [joint project announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pudu-robotics-and-shenzhen-ctid-co-ltd-launch-the-worlds-first-full-scenario-robot-serviced-hotel-project-302786945.html) covers reception, room delivery, food service and cleaning. Rather than concentrating on one machine, the plan calls for different robot types to work through a shared operating framework across public areas, guest services and back-of-house activities.

The signing event included demonstrations of that portfolio. CC1 Pro and MT1 machines performed floor-cleaning tasks, while FlashBot demonstrated ordering and delivery. T300 handled luggage-transport duties, and BellaBot Pro and KettyBot Pro served refreshments. These demonstrations provided an initial view of the intended applications, separate from a completed operating hotel.

The partners described a phased rollout with a longer-term roadmap extending toward 2030. Selected rooms and robot-supported services were scheduled to begin trial operations by the end of 2026. That timetable makes the June news a development agreement and operational plan: full hotel completion and the opening of all proposed services were still future stages when the announcement was issued.
