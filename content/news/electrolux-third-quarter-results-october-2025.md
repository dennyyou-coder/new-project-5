[Electrolux](/brands/electrolux) reported third-quarter 2025 net sales of SEK 32.318 billion on 30 October. Organic sales increased 4.6%, while operating income reached SEK 890 million.

The operating margin was 2.8%. Organic growth adjusts for factors such as currency movements and business scope, so it should not be treated as identical to the percentage change in reported sales.
