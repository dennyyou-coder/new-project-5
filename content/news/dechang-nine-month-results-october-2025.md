Ningbo Dechang Electrical Machinery reported first-nine-month 2025 revenue of approximately CNY 3.288 billion on 30 October, up 10.09%. Attributable net profit fell 46.29% to CNY 161.96 million.

The filing discussed pressure associated with tariffs, pricing and currency movements. The consolidated figures cover the manufacturer's appliance and motor businesses, with different exposures across products and customers.
