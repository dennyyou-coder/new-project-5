The FCC announced on 28 July 2026 that foreign-produced advanced robotic devices and power inverters had been added to its Covered List, following national-security determinations received the previous day.

The notice affects equipment authorization for covered devices and provides a route for conditional approvals. Its definitions and exceptions matter: the action should not be described as a blanket ban on every existing robot or every underwater cleaning device. Companies must assess the specific device and approval status against the published notice.
