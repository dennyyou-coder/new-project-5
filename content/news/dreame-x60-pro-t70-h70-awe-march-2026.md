[Dreame](/brands/dreame) presented the X60 Pro round-mop robot and T70 Ultra Station and H70 Ultra Station wet cleaners at AWE on 13 March 2026, according to TechWeb reporting published on 15 March.

The robot presentation highlighted a second-generation extending arm, while the wet cleaners targeted edge cleaning and heavier soil. The source identifies the round-mop X60 Pro; it does not support labeling that model as a steam version. Regional product names and configurations remain separate.
