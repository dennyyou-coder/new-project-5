[Dreame](/brands/dreame) presented the X60 Pro round-mop robot and two flagship wet cleaners at AWE on March 13, 2026. TechWeb's March 15 report described the launch as an update to edge cleaning, access around furniture and treatment of heavy soil.

The X60 Pro round-mop model used a second-generation extending-arm arrangement for its mop and side brush. The report also described binocular obstacle sensing and dirt recognition. This was the round-mop configuration shown at the Chinese exhibition; the report did not identify it as a steam-cleaning robot.

The T70 Ultra Station introduced an extending edge-cleaning mechanism, with a sensor helping the head follow walls and a manual extension option for narrower gaps. The H70 Ultra Station instead emphasized a combination of steam and hot water for heavier kitchen dirt. Their heating and cleaning systems should be matched to the individual product rather than combined into one specification list.

According to the report, the T70 was priced at CNY 3,899 and had entered Chinese sales channels on March 12. The H70 was listed at CNY 4,999 with sales planned for April. Both featured station-based maintenance functions, making their exhibition presentation part of a wider effort to reduce routine handling between cleaning sessions.
