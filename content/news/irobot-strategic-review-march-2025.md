[iRobot](/brands/irobot) announced a formal strategic review on March 12, 2025, covering options such as debt refinancing, a possible sale or another strategic transaction.

Its full-year 2024 revenue was $681.8 million, compared with $890.6 million in 2023. The company disclosed substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern and said there was no fixed review timetable or assurance that a transaction would result.

This was an exploratory review, not an acquisition agreement. The subsequent [December 2025 Picea agreement](/news/irobot-picea-restructuring-agreement-december-2025) and [January 2026 completion](/news/irobot-picea-acquisition-completed-january-2026) are recorded separately.
