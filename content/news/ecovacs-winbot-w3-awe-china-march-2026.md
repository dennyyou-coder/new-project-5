[ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) presented the WINBOT W3 window-cleaning system at AWE on 12 March 2026. The display highlighted a station with separate clean- and dirty-water tanks and automatic washing for the cleaning pad.

The Chinese exhibition coverage describes the WG858-01EA model. Installation, tethering and operating requirements remain relevant for window cleaners even when the station automates pad maintenance. The event presentation is distinct from the earlier U.S. market announcement.
