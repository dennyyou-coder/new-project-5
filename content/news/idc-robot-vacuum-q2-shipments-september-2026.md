Seoul Economic Daily reported on 29 September 2026 that global robot vacuum shipments reached 7.885 million units in the second quarter, citing IDC figures shared by [Roborock](/brands/roborock). Sales revenue for that category was USD 3.89 billion.

Roborock's shipment share was 23.7%, compared with 20.8% a year earlier. Roborock, [Dreame](/brands/dreame), [ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs), Xiaomi and [MOVA](/brands/mova) together accounted for 75% of shipments. The broader household cleaning robot total was 11.205 million units; it covers more products and should not be treated as the robot-vacuum-only figure.
