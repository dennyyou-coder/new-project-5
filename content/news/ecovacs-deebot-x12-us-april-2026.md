[ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) announced the DEEBOT X12 range for the United States on 14 April 2026. Its highlighted features include FocusJet stain treatment and a 27 cm roller-mop system designed to wash the floor while managing dirty water.

The announcement also describes a bagless station configuration. Features and station arrangements depend on the version selected, so specifications for the range should not automatically be assigned to every X12 package. The release marks the U.S. introduction of the named series.
