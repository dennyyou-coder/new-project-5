[ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) announced the DEEBOT X12 range for the United States on April 14, 2026, extending its roller-mop robot lineup. The [regional introduction](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ecovacs-introduces-deebot-x12-omnicyclone-the-new-flagship-robot-vacuum-designed-to-tackle-real-world-messes-with-ease-302740796.html) highlighted stain treatment, continuous roller washing and station automation as the main elements of the cleaning system.

FocusJet is designed to treat stubborn marks before the roller passes over them. The 27 cm roller then washes the floor while the machine manages clean and dirty water. This combines targeted treatment of a stain with the mechanical action of the roller, rather than relying on the same cleaning pass for every type of soil.

The range also includes a bagless station configuration, represented by the [DEEBOT X12 OmniCyclone](/products/ecovacs-deebot-x12-omnicyclone). The station is part of the ownership experience as well as the charging arrangement, handling collected material and cleaning-system maintenance between runs. Station specifications depend on the package selected.

The April release marks the U.S. introduction of the named X12 series. It follows the earlier exhibition presentation of ECOVACS' broader product portfolio and gives the range a separate regional news event. The X12 designation covers configurations that should be compared individually; a bagless station or another feature listed for one version does not automatically apply to every X12 offer.
