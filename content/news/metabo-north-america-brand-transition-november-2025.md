KOKI Group announced on 17 November 2025 that the [Metabo](/brands/metabo) brand in North America would transition to Metabo HPT. The company described the move as part of a strategy to bring its regional power-tool portfolio under a unified identity.

The announcement concerns branding in North America. It should not be read as a new acquisition of Metabo or an automatic renaming of the brand in every other market.
