[Tennant](/brands/tennant) released its 2024 results on February 17, 2025, reporting annual net sales of $1.2867 billion, up 3.5%. Adjusted EBITDA reached $208.8 million, while net income declined to $83.7 million.

Alongside the results, the company announced X6 ROVR, a larger autonomous scrubber aimed at facilities including warehouses, hospitals, schools and retail sites. It incorporated an autonomous charging station, with commercial availability planned for the second quarter of 2025.

The story's date follows the earnings release, rather than the 2024 reporting period. Later developments are covered in WCB's [2025 results report](/news/tennant-2025-results-erp-disruption-february-2026).
