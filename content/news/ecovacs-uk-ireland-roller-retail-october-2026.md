[ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) outlined a wider UK and Ireland retail rollout for its roller-mopping robots in an October 6 report carried by Retail Times. The announced expansion was scheduled from mid-October and included the [DEEBOT X12S OmniCyclone](/products/ecovacs-deebot-x12s-omnicyclone).

The report named AO, Argos and Marks Electrical among the channels involved. It positioned the expansion as a way for more shoppers to encounter the company's OZMO ROLLER 3.0 technology through retail, following earlier online availability. It did not establish that every named retailer would stock every model in every location.

ECOVACS' UK and Ireland sales manager, Graham Mitchell, described growing customer interest in how a robot cleans. The company is emphasizing the roller's continuous rinsing process as a point of differentiation from rotating mop pads. Those descriptions explain the retail message rather than constitute an independent comparison of cleaning performance.

The channel update follows the company's [IFA UK lineup announcement](/news/ecovacs-uk-cleaning-robot-lineup-ifa-2026). For the X12S, it adds distribution context to the product introduction. Model names, bundles and local availability remain relevant when comparing listings across the two markets, particularly where closely named DEEBOT variants are offered alongside one another.
