[Dreame](/brands/dreame) unveiled the [Aqua20 Pro Ultra Roller X Complete](/products/dreame-aqua20-pro-ultra-roller-x-complete) at IFA Berlin on 3 September 2026, adding steam to its roller-mop robot vacuum lineup.

The company describes a core steam source rated at 180°C, paired with hot-water mopping and a roller that extends outward by up to 8 cm. Fresh-water roller cleaning and an extending side brush are also part of the announced system.

The temperature refers to the manufacturer’s steam-source claim; it should not be read as a verified temperature across the floor. WCB has not independently tested the machine’s cleaning or bacteria-removal performance.

Dreame also showed other cleaning products at the event. Pricing, regional packages and retail availability for this exact model require separate checks rather than assumptions based on the exhibition announcement.
