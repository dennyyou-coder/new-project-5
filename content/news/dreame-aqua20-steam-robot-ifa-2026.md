[Dreame](/brands/dreame) unveiled the [Aqua20 Pro Ultra Roller X Complete](/products/dreame-aqua20-pro-ultra-roller-x-complete) at IFA Berlin on September 3, 2026. The [launch announcement](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/dreame-unveils-next-generation-innovations-at-ifa-2026-reimagining-smart-living-302868946.html) introduced a steam-assisted roller-mop robot alongside the company's wider home-appliance lineup.

The machine uses a steam source rated by Dreame at 180°C, followed by hot-water mopping to collect loosened dirt. That figure describes the core steam source, rather than a measured temperature across the floor. The roller receives fresh water during cleaning and can extend outward by up to 8 cm to reach recessed edges and spaces around furniture.

Dreame also described an extending side brush, a retractable navigation sensor and camera-based obstacle recognition. A cover closes over the roller when the robot moves onto carpet, while lifting mechanisms raise cleaning components as the floor situation changes. These features address both access around furniture and the transition between wet cleaning and dry surfaces.

The UK announcement listed a planned price of GBP 1,299 and described availability as coming soon through Dreame, Amazon and selected retailers. It did not give a firm retail date. The same presentation included the Pano 10 window cleaner and T-series wet-and-dry machines, which remain separate product lines.
