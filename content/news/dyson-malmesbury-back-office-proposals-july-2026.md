[Dyson](/brands/dyson) was proposing to remove about 170 back-office roles at its Malmesbury campus, the Gazette and Herald reported on 21 July 2026. The company said it was reviewing its organization and seeking to reduce some support roles.

The report described a proposal, rather than confirming that every affected role had already been removed. Malmesbury remained a research, design and development base and home to the Dyson Institute. The proposed changes concerned staffing at that site, not a closure of the campus.
