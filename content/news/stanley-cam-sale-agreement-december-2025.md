Howmet Aerospace announced on 22 December 2025 that it had agreed to acquire Consolidated Aerospace Manufacturing from Stanley Black & Decker for approximately USD 1.8 billion in cash. CAM supplies precision fasteners, fittings and engineered aerospace components.

The deal was expected to close in the first half of 2026, subject to regulatory approvals and customary conditions. The December announcement was a signed agreement, not confirmation that the transaction had already completed.
