Ninebot's service-robot revenue more than doubled in 2025, reaching CNY 2.002 billion, according to a March 31, 2026 report by Ebrun carried by Sina. The reported increase was 104.26%, with unit sales rising 105.63% to approximately 338,900.

The report identified robotic mowing as the main service-robot activity and put the category at 9.4% of group revenue. It also reported a category gross margin of 52.35%. Those figures show a business growing rapidly from a smaller base within a company still dominated by mobility products.

Ninebot's overall revenue was approximately CNY 21.28 billion, up 49.9%, while attributable net profit reached CNY 1.76 billion, an increase of 62.2%. Research and development expenditure rose to CNY 1.25 billion. Electric two-wheelers remained the largest revenue contributor, so the consolidated growth rate should not be read as a mower-only result.

The company described an international channel network spanning online marketplaces, branded websites, major retailers and specialist dealers. That infrastructure provides context for the overseas expansion discussed in the report. For comparison with later Navimow updates, the 2025 figures remain category-level disclosures; they should be distinguished from financial statements for a named subsidiary or sales of a particular mower family.
