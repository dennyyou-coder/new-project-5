[Milwaukee](/brands/milwaukee) previewed new cordless products at its August 2026 PIPELINE event. Coverage published on 13 August identified the M18 REDLITHIUM FORGE HD15 battery among the additions, alongside tools and accessories across several professional trades.

The published roadmap listed January 2027 availability for the HD15 battery, illustrating that the showcase included future releases as well as products closer to sale. Individual tool availability varies; the event date should not be treated as a single launch date for the entire lineup.
