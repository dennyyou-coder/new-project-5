[MOVA](/brands/mova) announced on June 15, 2026 that its LiDAX Ultra and LiDAX Ultra AWD robotic mower families had received Red Dot Design Awards.

The company distinguished the two series by yard requirements, positioning the AWD range for more demanding terrain and highlighting LiDAR and vision-based mapping. The award announcement covered product families rather than one universal capacity or regional package.

The recognition is a design milestone. It should not be read as an independent test of slope performance or mowing output, and specifications must remain tied to the exact mower ordered.
