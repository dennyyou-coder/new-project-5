[iRobot](/brands/irobot) reported a sharp decline in third-quarter revenue on November 6, 2025, with production and shipping problems adding to difficult market conditions. Its [quarterly results](https://media.irobot.com/2025-11-06-iRobot-Reports-Third-Quarter-2025-Financial-Results) covered the three months ended September 27 and recorded revenue of USD 145.8 million, against USD 193.4 million a year earlier.

Chief executive Gary Cohen said sales fell below internal expectations because of market headwinds, production delays and unexpected shipping disruption. The shortfall increased cash usage and made it harder to cover the company's fixed costs. iRobot recorded a GAAP operating loss of USD 17.7 million, compared with operating income of USD 7.3 million in the prior-year quarter.

Cash and cash equivalents stood at USD 24.8 million at quarter-end, down from USD 40.6 million in June. The company also disclosed USD 5 million in restricted cash, which was fully drawn on September 30, and said it had no further available capital sources at that time.

Revenue declined in the United States, EMEA and Japan. The release directed investors to its regulatory filing for information about the ongoing strategic review. These figures describe iRobot's position before the subsequent restructuring and ownership change, preserving a clear record of the financial pressure preceding that transaction.
