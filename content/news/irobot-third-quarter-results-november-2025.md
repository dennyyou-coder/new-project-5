[iRobot](/brands/irobot) released its third-quarter 2025 results on 6 November. Revenue was approximately USD 146 million, while cash and cash equivalents at quarter-end were USD 24.8 million.

The release described the company's financial position at that reporting date. Subsequent restructuring and ownership announcements are separate developments; this historical result should not be treated as a current cash-balance statement.
