[Gausium](/brands/gausium) reported more than 90 cleaning robots installed across Latvia in a [September 29 case study](https://gausium.com/case/floor-cleaning-robots-latvia/). It attributes the two-year installed base to local distributor Viss visapkārt tīrs (VVT), rather than to a single new order.

Named users include automation manufacturer AE Partner, Liepkalni bakery and cleaning contractor Cleanhouse. The examples span industrial floors, carpeted offices and shopping-centre cleaning, using Beetle, Phantas and Omnie robots.

The figures come from company and distributor case material, adapted from a Delfi Brand Studio article. They illustrate local deployment breadth, rather than an independently audited market share.
