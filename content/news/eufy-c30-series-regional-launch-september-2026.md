[Eufy](/brands/eufy) announced Omni C30 and C30 Edge in a [September 21 Nordic release](https://api.kommunikasjon.ntb.no/pressemelding/19074225/anker-eufy-robot-vacuums-omni-c30-edge-og-omni-c30-gjor-grundig-rengjoring-enkelt-hjemme?lang=no&publisherId=17849449). Both use rotating mop pads and cyclone filtration; the Edge adds an extending mop guided by a side-facing laser.

The release scheduled C30 Edge sales for October 8 and standard C30 sales for October 31. These are regional dates, not a worldwide launch schedule.

**U.S. availability checked October 25:** the [official C30 edge listing](https://www.eufy.com/products/t210q111-eufy-robot-vacuum-omni-c30edge) still described preorder status, with shipping estimated before November 1. That estimate does not confirm completed deliveries.
