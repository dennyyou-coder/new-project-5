[BISSELL](/brands/bissell) announced on 22 September 2026 that Adam Madigan would become chief executive officer effective 1 October. He previously served as president and chief operating officer and had spent 14 years with the company.

Mark Bissell, who stepped down as CEO in September 2025, remains executive chairman. Max Bissell continues as president of global product, reporting to Madigan. The appointment places a longtime company executive in charge while the Bissell family retains an active role in leadership.
