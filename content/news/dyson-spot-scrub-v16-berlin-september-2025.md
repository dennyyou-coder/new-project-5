[Dyson](/brands/dyson) presented new and updated appliances at a Berlin event on September 4, 2025, with floorcare spanning robot cleaning, wet washing and cordless vacuuming. The [company presentation](https://www.dyson.co.uk/discover/news/latest/James-Dyson-unveils-problem-solving-products-in-Berlin) included Spot+Scrub Ai, Clean+Wash Hygiene and the [V16 Piston Animal](/products/dyson-v16-piston-animal).

Spot+Scrub Ai represented the wet-and-dry robot format, combining automated movement around the home with floor cleaning. Clean+Wash Hygiene addressed dedicated wet floorcare, while V16 Piston Animal belonged to the cordless dry-vacuum range. Their appearance together showed the breadth of the portfolio rather than a single cleaning system shared across all three products.

PencilVac also appeared at the event, following its earlier announcement in May. Its inclusion brought the slim cordless format into the Berlin showcase, but September was not its first introduction. The presentation mixed products at different points in their announcement and release cycles.

Dyson described the event as covering both available appliances and forthcoming releases. Each model therefore retained its own commercial schedule and regional package. For retailers and industry observers, the news was the combined portfolio presentation in Berlin; the event date alone did not establish that every displayed machine had reached stores or received the same worldwide launch date.
