[Dyson](/brands/dyson) presented a group of new and upgraded products at its Berlin event on September 4, 2025. Floorcare highlights included Spot+Scrub Ai, Clean+Wash Hygiene and the [V16 Piston Animal](/products/dyson-v16-piston-animal).

The portfolio covered a wet-and-dry robot, a dedicated wet floor cleaner and cordless dry vacuuming. PencilVac, announced earlier in May, also appeared in the presentation; its appearance in Berlin did not represent its first announcement.

Dyson described the event as including both available products and forthcoming releases. The September event date should therefore not be read as a common retail launch date for every model.
