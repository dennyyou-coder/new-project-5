The U.S. CPSC announced a [Rowenta](/brands/rowenta) cordless-vacuum recall on July 2, 2026, covering approximately 3,660 units because selected lithium-ion batteries could overheat and ignite.

The notice identifies X-Force Flex 14.60 Animal model RH99A2U1 and X-Force Flex 15.60 Animal model RH99F2U1. The affected battery is ZR0097U2 with a date code beginning with 23 or 24; batteries were sold separately or with the cleaners.

Owners should stop using affected vacuums, remove the recalled battery and register with Rowenta for a free replacement. The official notice provides verification and hazardous-battery disposal instructions. The recall does not apply to every Rowenta vacuum or battery.
