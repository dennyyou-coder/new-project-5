[Roborock](/brands/roborock) reported first-half 2026 revenue of CNY 10.084 billion on August 24, up 27.60% from a year earlier. Net profit attributable to shareholders reached CNY 986 million, an increase of 45.60%, according to China Securities Journal's account of the interim results.

Profit excluding non-recurring items rose faster, increasing 60.74% to CNY 803 million. Second-quarter revenue was CNY 5.857 billion. Together, the figures show earnings growing ahead of sales in the first half, following a 2025 reporting period in which revenue expansion had coincided with declining annual profit.

The report described continued development of overseas distribution and local service networks. Roborock combines third-party online marketplaces with direct consumer channels and physical retail partnerships. Its market approach includes premium products in established territories and broader price coverage in other markets, supported by local marketing and after-sales operations.

The results cover the consolidated company, including its expanding cleaning portfolio, rather than one robot-vacuum family. Roborock's [corporate information](https://global.roborock.com/pages/about-us) describes a business extending beyond robot vacuums into other home-cleaning products. The interim report provides a financial measure of that wider operation, while promotional market-share claims cited alongside the results refer to their own events and channel definitions.
