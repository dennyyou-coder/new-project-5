[Roborock](/brands/roborock) released its first-half 2026 results on 24 August, according to China Securities Journal. Revenue reached CNY 10.084 billion, up 27.60% from a year earlier. Net profit attributable to shareholders rose 45.60% to CNY 986 million.

Profit excluding non-recurring items increased 60.74% to CNY 803 million. The report described continued investment in overseas distribution and local service networks. These are consolidated company figures, rather than revenue or earnings for an individual robot vacuum model.
