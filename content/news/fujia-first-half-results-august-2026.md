Ningbo Fujia reported first-half 2026 revenue of approximately CNY 1.394 billion, down 5.80%, according to its results announcement covered by Zhitong Finance on 27 August. Attributable net profit fell 59.71% to about CNY 30.39 million.

Profit excluding non-recurring items was approximately CNY 2.95 million, down 95.68%. The company cited higher raw-material costs and increased foreign-exchange losses. The figures describe the listed manufacturer's consolidated results rather than the sales of any single customer brand.
