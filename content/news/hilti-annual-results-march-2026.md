[Hilti](/brands/hilti) released its 2025 results on 13 March 2026. Sales reached CHF 6.297 billion, down 2.1% in reported currency but up 1.9% in local currencies.

Operating profit declined 5.3% to CHF 728 million and net income fell 8.0% to CHF 516 million. The results distinguish underlying local-currency sales growth from the effect of translating international business into Swiss francs.
