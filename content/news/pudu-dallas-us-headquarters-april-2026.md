[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) reported on April 27, 2026 that it had opened a new U.S. headquarters in the Dallas area on April 23.

The company presented the facility as part of its Americas expansion and a step toward more structured regional operations. Pudu's portfolio covers service delivery, commercial cleaning and industrial delivery robots.

The opening date and the newsroom publication date are distinct: April 23 was the facility opening, while April 27 is the date of the company update recorded here. The announcement does not establish availability of every robot model across all U.S. markets.
