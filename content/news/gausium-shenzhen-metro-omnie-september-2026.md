[Gausium](/brands/gausium) reported 27 Omnie cleaning robots in service across Shenzhen Metro Lines 13 and 4 in a [September 9 case study](https://gausium.com/case/mtr-shenzhen-metro/). It dates formal handover on Line 13 to July 12 and the Line 4 upgrade to July.

Twenty robots cover ten Line 13 stations, where water and drainage connections support automated wet cleaning. Seven serve Line 4 stations in dry dust-mopping mode.

Gausium describes the fleet as leased and says further machines are contracted. The figures are the supplier’s deployment account, not a new 27-unit order placed in September.
