Techtronic Industries announced 2025 revenue of approximately USD 15.3 billion on 3 March 2026, up 4.4%. Net profit rose 6.8% to about USD 1.2 billion.

[Milwaukee](/brands/milwaukee) sales grew 7.9% in local currency and [RYOBI](/brands/ryobi) grew 5.4%. These tool-brand growth rates should be kept separate from the performance of TTI's floorcare businesses, which operate in different markets and product categories.
