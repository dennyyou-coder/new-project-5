[Makita](/brands/makita) reported revenue of JPY 206.55 billion for April–June 2026 in its 29 July results release. Sales rose 10.7% year on year, while operating profit increased 17.2% to JPY 30.565 billion.

Profit attributable to owners reached JPY 22.866 billion, up 18.6%. This is the first quarter of Makita's fiscal year ending March 2027. The reporting period therefore differs from companies that describe January–March as their first quarter.
