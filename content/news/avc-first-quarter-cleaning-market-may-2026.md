AVC figures reported by China Household Electrical Appliances on 21 May 2026 showed a 0.2% year-on-year decline in China's first-quarter cleaning-appliance retail sales. The small overall change concealed different category trends.

Robot-vacuum sales fell about 10% and conventional vacuum sales about 12%, while wet floor cleaners grew approximately 3%. These figures describe the monitored retail market, rather than factory shipments or the global revenue of Chinese manufacturers.
