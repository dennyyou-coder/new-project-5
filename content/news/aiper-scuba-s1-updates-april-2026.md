[Aiper](/brands/aiper) announced software and usability upgrades for its Scuba S1 cordless pool cleaner on April 9, 2026, ahead of the spring pool-opening season. The company said the model, introduced in 2024, had reached more than 500,000 users worldwide.

The update adds a customizable weekly cleaning schedule and changes to route planning. Aiper also described broader shallow-area coverage and an enhanced Eco Mode with a claimed runtime of up to four hours. Those features address routine maintenance between more intensive cleaning sessions, while the multi-layer filtration system handles debris of different sizes.

The S1 remains positioned as a more accessible model within Aiper's pool-cleaner range. The [April announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/aiper-celebrates-its-best-selling-scuba-s1-with-new-upgrades-just-in-time-for-pool-season-302738184.html) listed a promotional U.S. price of $579.99 through Aiper and Amazon. That was the price advertised for the announcement period; regional offers and subsequent promotions may differ.

The update concerns Scuba S1 specifically and should be read separately from the newer Scuba V3 family. Aiper had already [presented that family at CES in January](/news/aiper-smart-yard-scuba-v3-ces-january-2026), before announcing [V3 Ultra purchase availability in June](/news/aiper-scuba-v3-ultra-june-2026-availability). The April S1 release therefore documents continued support for an existing product alongside the rollout of the newer flagship range.
