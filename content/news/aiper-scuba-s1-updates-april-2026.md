[Aiper](/brands/aiper) announced upgrades to its Scuba S1 cordless pool cleaner on April 9, 2026, ahead of the spring pool-opening season. The company said the model had reached more than 500,000 users since its 2024 introduction.

The announcement presented the refreshed S1 as a value-focused option in Aiper's pool-cleaner range and listed a promotional U.S. price of $579.99 through its website and Amazon.

That price belongs to the April announcement and is not a current offer. The story concerns Scuba S1 specifically, rather than the later Scuba S3 or V3 models.
