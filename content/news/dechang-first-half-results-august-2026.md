Ningbo Dechang Electrical Machinery reported first-half 2026 revenue of CNY 2.310 billion, up 11.70%, in its half-year summary. Attributable net profit declined 69.67% to CNY 33.59 million.

Profit excluding non-recurring items fell 77.32% to CNY 22.40 million. The company operates across household appliances and motor-related businesses. The consolidated revenue growth therefore should not be interpreted as a sales increase for every vacuum-cleaner or motor product category.
