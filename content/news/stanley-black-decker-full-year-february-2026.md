Stanley Black & Decker released its fourth-quarter and full-year 2025 results on 4 February 2026. Annual revenue was approximately USD 15.1 billion, down 2%, while fourth-quarter revenue declined about 1% to USD 3.7 billion.

The release described softer North American retail demand within Tools & Outdoor. The results cover the group before the announced sale of its Consolidated Aerospace Manufacturing business was completed.
