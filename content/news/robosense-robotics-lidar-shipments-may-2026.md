RoboSense reported first-quarter 2026 results on 27 May, with media coverage the following day highlighting robotics LiDAR shipments of approximately 185,500 units. That represented year-on-year growth of 1,458.8% and 56.2% of total LiDAR shipments.

The figure measures sensors supplied for robotics applications, not completed lawn mowers or cleaning robots. It shows the growing contribution of robotics customers alongside automotive programs without establishing the shipment volume of any individual finished-device brand.
