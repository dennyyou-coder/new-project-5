On April 9, 2026, the U.S. CPSC announced a recall involving selected [BISSELL](/brands/bissell) Steam Shot OmniReach and Omni handheld steam cleaners with attachments. Approximately 1.7 million units were sold in the United States, with about 96,000 more in Canada.

The notice says attachments can unexpectedly detach and release hot water or steam, creating a serious burn hazard. BISSELL had received 206 reports of escaping hot water or steam, including 161 burn-injury reports.

Consumers should stop using the recalled attachments immediately and register through the official recall program for free replacements. The CPSC notice lists the affected 4155- and 4171-series model numbers and disposal instructions; owners should check their rating label against that list.
