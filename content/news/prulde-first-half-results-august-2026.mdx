Zhejiang Prulde reported first-half 2026 revenue of CNY 595.15 million, up 29.21% from a year earlier, in its [interim report summary](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-26/1225502485.PDF) dated August 26. The power-tool manufacturer recorded attributable net profit of CNY 37.91 million, a decline of 16.97%, despite the increase in sales.

The underlying profit comparison moved in the other direction. Profit excluding non-recurring items rose 8.64% to CNY 36.41 million. The gap between that measure and reported attributable profit was much smaller than in the corresponding period of 2025, when non-recurring items made a larger contribution. Revenue growth therefore did not translate directly into growth in the headline earnings figure.

Operating activities generated CNY 51.97 million in net cash, down 30.19% year on year. Total assets at the end of the period were approximately CNY 1.53 billion, while equity attributable to shareholders was CNY 1.29 billion. Both were slightly below their year-end levels.

The Shenzhen-listed company proposed no interim cash dividend or bonus shares. Its summary also recorded a restricted-share incentive program introduced during the period. These results cover Prulde's consolidated business, providing a broader manufacturing-company comparison rather than a standalone measure of cleaning-equipment demand.
