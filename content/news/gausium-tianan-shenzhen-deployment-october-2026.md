[Gausium](/brands/gausium) published a [TianAn Cyber Park case study](https://gausium.com/case/tianan-cyber-park-shenzhen-cleaning-robots/) on October 15, 2026, describing cleaning at two Shenzhen campuses. The publication date is not an installation date; the company does not specify when the robots entered service.

At Futian, one Omnie serves roughly 5,000 square metres of indoor common areas. At Longgang, one Scrubber 75 handles about 20,000 square metres of underground garage space. Each uses a workstation for charging and water management.

The case illustrates different machines serving indoor pedestrian areas and a larger garage. Its operating claims come from Gausium and the customer account, rather than an independent performance audit; no measured labour savings or payback period is disclosed.
