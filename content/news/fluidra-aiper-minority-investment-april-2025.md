[Fluidra](/brands/fluidra) announced an agreement on April 1, 2025, to invest $100 million in newly issued [Aiper](/brands/aiper) shares for a 27% interest.

The companies described a partnership combining pool-equipment expertise with cordless robot technology. Fluidra would continue focusing on professional distribution, while Aiper would concentrate on consumer retail channels.

A possible second phase envisaged majority ownership after specified revenue and profitability milestones. At announcement, the initial investment still required customary closing conditions and antitrust clearance, with completion expected in the second half of 2025. The April story therefore records the agreement, not a completed majority acquisition.
