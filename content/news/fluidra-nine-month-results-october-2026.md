[Fluidra](/brands/fluidra) reported January–September 2026 sales of EUR 1.780 billion, up 4% at constant exchange rates, in its [October 29 release](https://www.fluidra.com/press-room/press-release/fluidra-delivers-a-strong-nine-month-performance/). Adjusted EBITDA reached EUR 428 million, up 6% on the same currency basis, with a 24.1% margin.

The company said aftermarket demand offset weakness in new-pool construction. It narrowed its full-year sales-growth forecast to 3.5–5.5% at constant exchange rates and its adjusted EBITDA margin outlook to 23.3–23.5%.

These figures extend the group's [first-half update](/news/fluidra-first-half-hydrapro-agreement-july-2026). They cover the consolidated pool-equipment business, rather than robotic pool cleaners alone. The growth rates above exclude currency movements; the outlook remains a forecast.
