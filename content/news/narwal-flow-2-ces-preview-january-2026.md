[Narwal](/brands/narwal) previewed its [Flow 2 robot vacuum](/products/narwal-flow-2) for CES 2026 in a release published on January 5, ahead of the show's January 6 opening.

The announcement positioned Flow 2 as the company's 2026 flagship and introduced the V50 Series cordless vacuum and U50 Series mattress vacuum alongside it. Narwal described the range as an expansion from robotic floorcare into additional household cleaning tasks.

The source carries a January 5 publication timestamp and a January 6 dateline. WCB uses the publication date for this preview. [North American Flow 2 preorders were announced separately in April](/news/narwal-flow-2-north-america-launch-april-2026).
