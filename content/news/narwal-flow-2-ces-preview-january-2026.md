[Narwal](/brands/narwal) previewed [Flow 2](/products/narwal-flow-2) and two additional household-cleaning categories ahead of CES 2026. Its [January announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/narwal-unveils-next-generation-smart-cleaning-lineup-at-ces-2026-headlined-by-flagship-flow-2-robot-vacuum-302652813.html) introduced the V50 Series cordless vacuum and U50 Series mattress vacuum alongside the flagship robot, broadening the presentation beyond autonomous floorcare.

Flow 2 combined an updated track-mopping system with NarMind Pro, a camera-based navigation and recognition system. Narwal described using both onboard processing and a cloud vision-language model to interpret unfamiliar objects. The preview also outlined modes intended for households with pets or babies and notifications for valuable objects detected on the floor.

The FlowWash system continuously refreshes the moving mop with clean heated water and collects wastewater. Narwal specified 60°C water during mopping and 12 N of pressure in the preview. The station's new design included easier-to-lift tanks and a glass panel, making maintenance and appearance part of the product update.

The wire release was published January 5 with a January 6 dateline, ahead of the show's opening. That event was a preview, with [North American preorders announced separately in April](/news/narwal-flow-2-north-america-launch-april-2026). Keeping the two stages separate preserves the distinction between the CES presentation and the later regional sales offer.
