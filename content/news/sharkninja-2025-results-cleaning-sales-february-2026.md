SharkNinja, parent of [Shark](/brands/shark), reported its 2025 results on February 11, 2026.

Full-year Cleaning Appliances sales rose 6.9% to $2,205.8 million, supported by carpet extraction and cordless vacuum products. Total group sales increased 15.7% to $6,399.2 million across cleaning, cooking, food preparation, beauty and home-environment categories.

For the fourth quarter alone, Cleaning Appliances sales increased 3.4% to $669.9 million, with carpet extractors and robotics cited as growth drivers. The group growth rate therefore should not be used as a proxy for the performance of cleaning appliances or robot vacuums specifically.
