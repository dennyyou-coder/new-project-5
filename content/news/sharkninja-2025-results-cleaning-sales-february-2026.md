SharkNinja, parent of [Shark](/brands/shark), reported 2025 revenue of USD 6.399 billion on February 11, 2026, up 15.7% from the previous year. Cleaning Appliances sales increased 6.9% to USD 2.206 billion, with carpet extractors and cordless vacuums supporting the category's growth.

The [annual earnings release](https://ir.sharkninja.com/news/news-details/2026/SharkNinja-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx) shows different growth rates across the portfolio. Food Preparation and Beauty and Home Environment expanded faster than cleaning, while Cooking and Beverage also increased. As a result, the group's headline revenue growth reflects several businesses with different demand patterns, rather than robot vacuums alone.

In the fourth quarter, Cleaning Appliances sales rose 3.4% to USD 669.9 million. Carpet extractors and robotics were the cited drivers for that three-month period. Total quarterly sales increased 17.6% to USD 2.101 billion, with Beauty and Home Environment again making a larger growth contribution.

Full-year reported net income reached USD 701.4 million, while adjusted net income was USD 749.6 million. SharkNinja also announced a USD 750 million share-repurchase authorization and its initial 2026 outlook. The category detail provides the more direct view of cleaning-equipment trading, while the broader result shows how the company's expansion into additional household categories is changing its revenue mix.
