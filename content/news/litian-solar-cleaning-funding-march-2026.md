Solar-cleaning robotics company Litian Intelligent announced a pre-Series B financing round exceeding CNY 100 million, according to a 13 March 2026 report. The financing brought additional capital to a business focused on maintaining photovoltaic installations.

The announcement concerns company financing rather than a customer deployment or verified shipment volume. Solar-panel cleaning is a distinct commercial application from household floorcare, with different operating environments and service requirements.
