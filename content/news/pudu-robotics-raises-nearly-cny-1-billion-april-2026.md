[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) said on April 23, 2026, that it had raised nearly CNY 1 billion in a new funding round, valuing the Shenzhen company at more than CNY 10 billion. Chinese media reported the round the same day. It takes Pudu's total funding to more than CNY 2 billion.

Longgang Financial Holdings and AIC Capital led the round. BAIC Capital, Lens Technology, Honghui Fund and government guidance funds from the Pearl River Delta and Yangtze River Delta also took part.

Pudu said the money would go to embodied-AI research, new products, overseas expansion, manufacturing capacity and its supply chain. Reports described the round as preparation for a future listing. The company has not announced an IPO timetable.

The figures Pudu released with the round show how far cleaning now drives the business. Commercial cleaning robots made up more than 70% of revenue, the company said. Revenue more than doubled in 2025, cumulative shipments passed 120,000 units, and the company is close to positive EBITDA. Pudu serves more than 40,000 customers in over 80 countries and regions. These are the company's own figures and have not been independently audited.

Since the round, Pudu has opened a U.S. headquarters near Dallas and a European headquarters in Neuss, Germany. In November it announced TOMO S, its first robotic mower for homes.
