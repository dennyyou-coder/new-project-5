[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) announced the opening of its European headquarters in Neuss, Germany, on October 30, 2026. Its [company-issued announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pudu-robotics-opens-european-headquarters-in-germany-to-support-continued-growth-across-the-region-302923863.html) says the site's offices, showroom, warehouse, training, sales, repair and after-sales functions are operational.

The facility complements Pudu's existing European offices and partner network, together with warehousing in Germany and the Netherlands. It is a regional operating hub, rather than a relocation of the company's global headquarters.

For cleaning-robot customers, the relevant expansion is local demonstration, distributor training and service capacity. Buyers still need to confirm which local partner handles deployment, maintenance and spare parts for their specific site.
