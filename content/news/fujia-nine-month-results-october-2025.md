Ningbo Fujia's third-quarter filing, published on 28 October 2025, reported January–September revenue of CNY 2.291 billion, up 23.29%. Attributable net profit declined 19.19% to CNY 117.12 million.

For the third quarter alone, attributable profit rose 10.80% to CNY 41.70 million while revenue fell 3.31%. The standalone quarter therefore improved in earnings even though the nine-month total remained below the prior year.
