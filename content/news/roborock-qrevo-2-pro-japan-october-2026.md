[Roborock](/brands/roborock) introduced Qrevo 2 Pro through Yamada Denki stores and Yamada Webcom in Japan. Its [October 8 notice](https://jp.roborock.com/blogs/news/roborock-qrevo-2-pro-%E3%82%92%E7%99%BA%E5%A3%B2) gives October 2 as the sales date, following reservations from September 24.

The model combines a reversible side brush, an anti-tangle brush system and two rotating mop pads. Roborock positions the side-brush change around reaching room corners.

This is a Japanese retail launch. The announcement does not establish equivalent model names, specifications or availability in other countries.
