[Mammotion](/brands/mammotion) unveiled LUBA 4 AWD at IFA on September 4, 2026. Its [official model guide](https://us.mammotion.com/blogs/news/luba-4-awd) describes four cameras combined with 360-degree LiDAR and NetRTK, plus independent front-wheel steering and bidirectional driving.

The 3000F, 6000F and 12000F configurations target different lawn sizes. An optional edge-trimming module supports the 3000F and 6000F, but is not compatible with the 12000F.

Mammotion schedules preorders for Q4 2026. The guide leaves pricing and regional delivery dates to subsequent announcements, so the IFA reveal does not establish immediate retail availability.
