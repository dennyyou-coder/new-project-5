Samsung outlined an updated Bespoke AI robot-cleaner offering in South Korea on 11 February 2026, according to regional reporting. The planned March release continued the company's use of an integrated station for cleaning and mop maintenance.

The announcement concerned a Korean launch schedule. It did not establish identical release dates or configurations for other markets, where Samsung may use different model names and packages.
