TTI Floor Care announced plans to move operations from Charlotte, North Carolina, to Anderson, South Carolina, according to an 8 October 2025 economic-development announcement. The move was planned before 1 June 2026.

The floorcare business includes [Hoover](/brands/hoover), [Oreck](/brands/oreck) and [Dirt Devil](/brands/dirt-devil). The announcement set out a relocation plan; it did not establish on that date that every role or activity had already transferred.
