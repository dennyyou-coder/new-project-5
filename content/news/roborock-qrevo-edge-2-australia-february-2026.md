[Roborock](/brands/roborock) announced Qrevo Edge 2 Pro for Australia on February 17, 2026, with a recommended retail price of AUD 2,799. The [Australian launch release](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/roborock-launches-qrevo-edge-2-pro-in-australia--delivering-health-focused-cleaning-for-modern-homes-302688621.html) places the model in the company's premium robot-vacuum range and names its official online store and selected retail partners as sales channels.

A retractable LiDAR assembly supports a low-profile operating configuration of 7.98 centimeters, intended to improve access beneath beds and sofas. The robot pairs vacuuming with two rotating mop pads and an adaptive chassis for moving between rooms, rugs and raised transitions.

Roborock highlights an automated station that washes the mops, dries them and cleans its own base. The release advertises hot-water washing and warm-air drying as maintenance functions, alongside automatic servicing between cleaning sessions. It also describes brush features intended to reduce hair wrapping and obstacle recognition covering more than 200 household object types.

The Australian announcement uses the full Qrevo Edge 2 Pro name and says further models in the Edge 2 family are expected. That distinction matters when comparing retail listings: a shorter series name does not identify every package or specification. The February release establishes the Australian offer, with local pricing and channel arrangements tied to this particular model.
