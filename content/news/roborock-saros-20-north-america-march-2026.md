[Roborock](/brands/roborock) announced the North American launch of the Saros 20 on 23 March 2026. The model combines the company's StarSight navigation system with an AdaptiLift chassis intended to handle more demanding household transitions.

Roborock describes an 8.8 cm capability for a two-layer threshold configuration comprising 4.5 cm and 4.3 cm steps. That specification should not be interpreted as the ability to climb a single vertical 8.8 cm ledge.
