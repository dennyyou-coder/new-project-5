[Dreame](/brands/dreame) confirmed a strategic reorganization to Red Star Capital Bureau on 29 June 2026. It identified smart homes, outdoor yard care, smart mobility and embodied intelligence as its four core business directions.

The company said it would concentrate research, staffing and financial resources around those areas. The report also contained employee accounts of job reductions, but Dreame had not confirmed the widely circulated total. This brief therefore reports the confirmed business structure without presenting that estimate as an established headcount change.
