China's National Development and Reform Commission said on 30 September that all CNY 250 billion in 2026 central funding for consumer-goods trade-ins had been allocated. The final tranche amounted to CNY 62.5 billion.

The commission said supported purchases generated more than CNY 1.55 trillion in sales during January–August. The announcement concerns the wider consumer-goods program; it does not itself establish subsidy eligibility for an individual cleaning appliance.
