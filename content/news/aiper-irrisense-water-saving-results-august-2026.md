[Aiper](/brands/aiper) reported on August 27, 2026 that participants in its IrriSense 2 Water-Saving Initiative had recorded more than 3.1 million gallons of water savings. The ten-week campaign ran from May 7 to July 15 and involved 907 users, with results collected through the company's app.

Germany was the largest participating market, with 368 users and approximately 1.23 million gallons recorded. The United States contributed 248 users and about 674,000 gallons, while 69 participants in France recorded approximately 310,000 gallons. Those three countries represented most of the campaign's participation and measured savings.

IrriSense 2 combines mapped watering areas, weather-responsive schedules and app-based monitoring. Its mapping system is intended to keep irrigation away from paved surfaces, while forecast information helps users adjust watering when conditions change. The campaign was designed to make those adjustments visible through accumulated usage records.

The [company's results announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/aiper-irrisense-2-users-save-more-than-3-1-million-gallons-in-global-water-saving-initiative-302861750.html) follows an April commitment to report the initiative's outcome. Its totals are participant app records, rather than an independent audit or a guaranteed saving for every garden. The program also provides background to Aiper's broader [Smart Yard Ecosystem presentation at IFA](/news/aiper-smart-yard-ecosystem-ifa-2026), which brought irrigation and pool maintenance into the same portfolio.
