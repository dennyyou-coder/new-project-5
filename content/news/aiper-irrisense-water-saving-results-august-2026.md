[Aiper](/brands/aiper) published results from its IrriSense 2 Water-Saving Initiative on August 27, 2026. The company said 907 users participating from May 7 through July 15 recorded collective water savings of 3,103,150 gallons through the Aiper app.

Germany accounted for 368 participants, followed by 248 in the United States and 69 in France. The program followed an April commitment to report participation and impact.

The figures are company-reported app records from the defined campaign period. They should not be presented as an independently audited global water-saving total or a guaranteed saving for every irrigation installation.
