[Hilti](/brands/hilti) reported on 24 September 2026 that sales for January–August reached CHF 4.244 billion, up 1.9% in Swiss francs and 5.9% in local currencies. Operating profit increased 16.9% to CHF 533 million.

Local-currency growth was stronger in the Americas and Asia-Pacific than in Europe. Management pointed to investment in energy and data-center projects while describing traditional construction as weak. Hilti raised its full-year local-currency sales growth expectation to the mid-single-digit range.
