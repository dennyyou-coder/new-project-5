[Electrolux](/brands/electrolux) reported second-quarter 2026 sales of SEK 31.569 billion on 29 July, with organic sales growth of 2%. Operating income excluding non-recurring items was SEK 1.202 billion.

Including approximately SEK 2.2 billion in non-recurring charges, the reported operating result was a loss of SEK 1.005 billion and the net loss was SEK 1.641 billion. The release identified restructuring and the planned Midea partnership among the relevant changes. Reported and adjusted earnings consequently give different views of the quarter.
