eufy presented the Omni E35 robot vacuum at IFA 2026, according to Engadget's 3 September report. The product combines a self-cleaning roller mop with a multi-cyclone filtration system.

The report listed European and UK availability from 4 September, alongside U.S. preorders before a planned 25 September release. U.S. early pricing was about USD 1,000, compared with a regular price of about USD 1,300. eufy also previewed a robotic lawn mower, but its later availability should not be confused with the vacuum's launch schedule.
