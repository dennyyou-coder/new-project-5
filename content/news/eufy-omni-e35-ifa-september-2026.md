eufy presented the Omni E35 robot vacuum at IFA 2026, adding another roller-mop model to its cleaning range. Engadget's September 3 report described the product as combining a self-cleaning roller with a multi-cyclone filtration system.

The roller handles wet floor cleaning, while the filtration arrangement manages the airflow carrying collected dust. Their inclusion in one robot places attention on both washing and dry-debris collection. The report also described third-party certification marks associated with the product, although those certifications should be read according to their specific scope rather than as a universal performance ranking.

European and UK availability was scheduled for September 4. In the United States, the same date marked the opening of preorders ahead of a planned September 25 release. Engadget listed U.S. early pricing at about USD 1,000, compared with a regular price of about USD 1,300. Those figures describe the announced launch offer.

The IFA display also included security devices and a preview of the Robot Lawn Mower S2 Max. The outdoor robot had a different commercial timetable, so its appearance alongside the E35 did not establish a shared launch date. The E35 news concerned the vacuum's named regional rollout and cleaning system.
