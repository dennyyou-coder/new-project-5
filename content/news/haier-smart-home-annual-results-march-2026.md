Haier Smart Home reported 2025 revenue of CNY 302.347 billion on March 27, 2026, an increase of 5.71% from the previous year. Its [annual report](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-27/1225038336.PDF) put net profit attributable to shareholders at CNY 19.553 billion, up 4.39%.

Operating cash flow reached approximately CNY 26.003 billion. The report described continued work on domestic distribution and inventory management, including efforts to shorten the route from factories to consumers. Haier said its changes to warehousing, delivery and dealer support were intended to make the supply chain respond more directly to retail demand.

The company also described its position across major home-appliance categories, including refrigeration, laundry and air conditioning. Those operations, together with its international businesses, form the consolidated annual result. Revenue growth at group level does not mean that each category, country or brand grew at the same rate.

For cleaning-industry readers, the report provides the financial scale and operating context of a major appliance group active in laundry and related household equipment. It does not offer a standalone measure of the robot-vacuum or vacuum-cleaner market. The figures cover the completed 2025 calendar year and should remain separate from quarterly or half-year disclosures issued later in 2026.
