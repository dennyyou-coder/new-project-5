Haier Smart Home reported 2025 revenue of CNY 302.347 billion on 27 March 2026, up 5.71% year on year. Attributable net profit increased 4.39% to CNY 19.553 billion.

The results span Haier's international home-appliance operations. They provide a view of the broader appliance business, rather than a standalone measure of cleaning robots or vacuum cleaners.
