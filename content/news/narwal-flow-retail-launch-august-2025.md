[Narwal](/brands/narwal) announced consumer availability of the original Flow robot vacuum on August 7, 2025, following its CES preview earlier that year.

The product combined a moving-track FlowWash mopping system with edge cleaning, carpet-focused vacuuming and Twin-AI obstacle avoidance. Its multifunction dock automated dust collection, mop washing and drying, with a separate plumbing-connected dock option described for the series.

Narwal said beta-user and industry feedback had informed changes between the CES presentation and retail launch. The August date records this sales release, not the earlier preview or the later [Flow 2 launch in April 2026](/news/narwal-flow-2-north-america-launch-april-2026).
