[Narwal](/brands/narwal) announced retail availability of the original Flow robot vacuum and mop on August 7, 2025, following its CES presentation earlier that year. The [launch release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/narwal-flow-debuts-advanced-self-clean-track-mopping-ai-robot-vacuum-302523977.html) said feedback from beta users and industry participants had informed refinements before the consumer introduction.

Flow's defining feature was its moving-track FlowWash mop, refreshed with clean water while dirty water was collected separately. Narwal listed water at 45°C during mopping and 12 N of downward pressure. EdgeReach extended the cleaning surface toward walls, while a separate carpet-cleaning arrangement adjusted airflow around the vacuum brush.

The robot combined maximum suction of 22,000 Pa with dual-camera obstacle recognition. Its multifunction dock handled dust collection, hot-water mop washing and drying. Narwal also described a separate dock option connected to household water and drainage, with a more compact station form.

These arrangements gave the Flow series different installation choices as well as a new mopping mechanism. The August date identifies the original product's consumer release, rather than its earlier exhibition preview. It is also separate from the subsequent [Flow 2 launch in April 2026](/news/narwal-flow-2-north-america-launch-april-2026), which introduced a later generation with its own specifications and regional pricing.
