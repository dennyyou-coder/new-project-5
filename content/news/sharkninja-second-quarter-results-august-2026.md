[Shark](/brands/shark)Ninja reported second-quarter 2026 revenue of USD 1.766 billion on August 5, up 22.2% year on year. Cleaning Appliances sales rose 4.1% to USD 522.0 million, with carpet extractors and cordless vacuums cited as the main contributors within that category.

The [company's results release](https://ir.sharkninja.com/news/news-details/2026/SharkNinja-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx) shows faster expansion elsewhere in the portfolio. Cooking and Beverage sales increased 36.5%, while Beauty and Home Environment grew 65.3%. Cleaning remained a substantial business, but its more modest increase means the group's headline growth rate is not a direct measure of floorcare demand.

Reported net income declined 7.0% to USD 129.8 million. Adjusted net income, which excludes the company's specified adjustments, increased 29.3% to USD 178.2 million. Gross margin was 48.7%, with tariff-related costs, currency and retailer activity among the factors discussed in the release.

International sales rose 36.6%, compared with domestic growth of 15.5%, as SharkNinja continued entering markets and extending existing product categories. Management also raised its full-year outlook. The quarter therefore combined strong group revenue growth with different category and profit trends, giving cleaning-industry readers a reason to follow the separate floorcare line as well as the company's consolidated result.
