[Shark](/brands/shark)Ninja reported second-quarter 2026 net sales of USD 1.7655 billion on 5 August, a 22.2% increase from a year earlier. Cleaning Appliances revenue increased 4.1% to USD 522 million.

The company attributed cleaning-category growth to carpet extractors and cordless vacuums. Other categories grew faster, so the group-wide growth rate should not be used as a measure of floorcare demand. Reported net income fell 7.0%, while adjusted net income rose 29.3%.
