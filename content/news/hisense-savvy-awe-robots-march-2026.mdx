[Hisense](/brands/hisense) brought three types of service robot to AWE 2026 in Shanghai, extending its exhibition presentation beyond conventional home appliances. The lineup comprised the Savvy household robot, Moii companion robot and Harley humanoid, according to the [organizer's closing report](https://www.awe.com.cn/contents/30/19806.html) published on March 15.

The four-day exhibition ran from March 12 to 15 and attracted more than 1,200 participating companies and innovation businesses. Hisense's robots appeared within a wider display of artificial intelligence applied to domestic tasks, interaction and connected appliances. The company also presented updates to its laundry, food-preservation and air-management technologies using its Xinghai AI model.

Home-service robotics was a recurring theme across the show. The organizer described demonstrations from appliance and floorcare manufacturers involving cleaning, companionship and household work. Hisense's three named robots therefore represented distinct uses within the same presentation, rather than three versions of a robot vacuum.

For appliance channels, the display offers a view of the categories Hisense is exploring as robots become part of broader smart-home portfolios. The closing report documents their public appearance but supplies no retail price or firm consumer delivery schedule for Savvy. Commercial availability and final capabilities will depend on subsequent product announcements.
