[Dyson](/brands/dyson) announced the [W1 PencilWash](/products/dyson-w1-pencilwash) on 3 September 2026 as the next generation of its lightweight wet-cleaning range.

The updated cleaner uses a hybrid roller with an anti-tangle comb and a revised head that separates wet and dry debris. Dyson says its filter-free design reduces the maintenance associated with trapped dirt in filters and internal pathways.

The company lists a 2.4 kg machine weight and a 75 mm working height when laid flat. It retains the slim 38 mm handle format of the original PencilWash.

W1 PencilWash is a distinct model from the original PencilWash, so specifications and accessories should be matched to the exact name. The UK announcement does not provide a universal selling price; local pricing and availability need to be checked separately.
