[Aiper](/brands/aiper) announced purchase availability for [Scuba V3 Ultra](/products/aiper-scuba-v3-ultra) on June 15, 2026, with a listed price of $2,299.99. The announcement moved the flagship pool cleaner from its earlier CES presentation into a commercial release.

The model combines camera-based sensing with adaptive cleaning controls. Aiper says its Navium mode considers pool conditions and previous cleaning information when selecting a cleaning strategy, while a dual-camera system recognizes debris and obstacles. These are manufacturer-described capabilities rather than results from a WCB performance test.

Coverage includes the pool floor, walls, waterline and surface, as well as shallow sun shelves. The company specified operation on shelves at least 20 cm deep and described an adjustable chlorine chamber. Its buoyancy system uses air chambers to help the robot move between underwater cleaning and the surface, including retrieval after a cycle.

The [June release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-future-of-pool-care-has-arrived-with-aipers-scuba-v3-ultra-302800316.html) follows [Aiper's January CES showcase](/news/aiper-smart-yard-scuba-v3-ces-january-2026), where Scuba V3 Ultra appeared alongside other pool and irrigation products. The quoted price records that launch announcement, rather than a current retailer-stock check or a worldwide price. Together, the two announcements show the sequence from portfolio introduction to the availability of this specific model.
