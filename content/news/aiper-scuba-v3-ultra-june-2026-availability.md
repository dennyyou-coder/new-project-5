[Aiper](/brands/aiper) announced on June 15, 2026 that [Scuba V3 Ultra](/products/aiper-scuba-v3-ultra) was available for purchase at a listed price of $2,299.99.

The company described the flagship as a cognitive-AI pool-cleaning system with adaptive cleaning and multiple maintenance functions. The availability announcement followed [Aiper's January CES showcase](/news/aiper-smart-yard-scuba-v3-ces-january-2026) and promoted decision-making based on pool conditions.

The quoted price and purchase status are those of the June release. They should not be generalized to every country or treated as a live retailer-stock check. The V3 Ultra is the specific model covered here.
