[Sunseeker](/brands/sunseeker) used IFA 2026 to preview five LiDAR mower models for 2027. Its [September 8 announcement](https://sunseekertech.com/global/news/ifa-2026-sunseeker-debuts-2027-lidar-models-smart-garden-ecosystem) schedules availability from February 2027.

The lineup covers gardens of 500–3,000 square metres. S7 LiDAR Ultra AWD 3000 and S5 LiDAR Ultra Pro AWD 1800 use 360-degree LiDAR, while the S3 family uses solid-state LiDAR.

Smart Garden adds a plan to connect mowers with rain gauges and soil sensors. Irrigation integration is described as future development. The announcement broadens Sunseeker’s direction beyond mowing, but does not establish that the complete ecosystem is already on sale.
