[Dreame](/brands/dreame) announced the X60 Ultra Extreme for Singapore on August 11, 2026, with an early-bird campaign beginning August 17 and the official local launch scheduled for September 1. The [Singapore release](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/dreame-to-launch-x60-ultra-extreme-in-singapore-on-1-september-2026-302848025.html) focused on extending the machine's reach beyond open floor areas.

The model uses articulated brush and mop mechanisms that change their angles around furniture. Dreame says the side brush extends by up to 12 cm and the mop reaches up to 18 cm diagonally. Dual cameras, structured light and illumination support navigation and dirt detection, allowing the robot to adjust its cleaning method for dry debris or liquid messes.

The PowerDock handles dust emptying, water refilling, cleaning-solution dispensing and mop washing and drying. An optional water connection kit supports automatic filling and drainage where installation conditions permit. The release describes its high-temperature washboard system separately from the robot's work on the floor.

Singapore customers were directed to Dreame's website, official stores, COURTS Heeren and COURTS Megastore for product information and demonstrations. The announced features belong to the Ultra Extreme configuration, which is a distinct regional variant within the X60 family. The August statement provides its introduction schedule and local channels, rather than confirmation of stock at every outlet.
