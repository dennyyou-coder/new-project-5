[Dreame](/brands/dreame) announced the X60 Ultra Extreme for Singapore on August 11, 2026. The company scheduled early-bird activity from August 17 and the official local launch for September 1.

The named variant features extending side-brush and mop components, dual-camera navigation and an automated PowerDock, according to the manufacturer. Dreame listed its Singapore website, official stores and selected COURTS locations as places to experience the product.

X60 Ultra Extreme is a distinct regional variant. Its announced features should not be copied onto every X60 Ultra or Complete package, and the August release establishes a launch schedule rather than a current stock check.
