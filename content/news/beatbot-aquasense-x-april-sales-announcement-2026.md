[Beatbot](/brands/beatbot) announced an [AquaSense X](/products/beatbot-aquasense-x) sales program on April 13, 2026, bringing its pool robot and AstroRinse station closer to customers after their CES introduction. The release listed an MSRP of $4,250 and a trade-up offer for owners of older pool robots.

The system combines underwater cleaning with automated filter maintenance at the station. Beatbot described a rotating high-pressure nozzle that cleans the filter in about three minutes, with debris collected in a 22-litre container. Charging follows the maintenance process, reducing the number of separate filter-handling steps described for a conventional cleaner.

The workflow still requires the owner to place the robot on the station. Automated filter washing does not mean the cleaner can leave the pool and dock by itself. That distinction is useful when comparing the system with machines whose automation is limited to cleaning routes inside the water.

The [April announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/beatbot-announces-aquasense-x-open-sale-the-worlds-first-pool-robot-with-a-self-cleaning-dock-302740394.html) used “open sale” in its headline but described the pricing offer as presale in the body. Shipment timing cannot therefore be established from the headline alone. The promotion dates are historical. The earlier [January CES announcement](/news/beatbot-aquasense-x-ces-january-2026) records the product introduction; this April report records the subsequent sales program.
