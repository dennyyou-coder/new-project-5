[Tineco](/brands/tineco) showed its Station View wet floor cleaner at AWE 2026, combining a transparent wastewater section with a station intended to simplify maintenance. China Household Electrical Appliances described the product in its March 14 exhibition report.

The transparent section lets users observe dirty water moving through the cleaning system. Tineco also said the station occupied 18% less floor space than its predecessor. Those changes make the cleaning process visible while reducing the space needed for a dock near suitable plumbing.

The report describes a flushing arrangement that mixes air and water under pressure and detects the state of the pipework. Installation options include sharing a washing machine's water connections through a T-connector and using floor or wall drainage. Such arrangements depend on the layout of the home; connection details belong with the installation support available through [Tineco's official site](https://www.tineco.com/).

The washer also offers steam cleaning and a lay-flat position for reaching beneath furniture. After cleaning, the station washes the roller, flushes the tank and discharge path, and offers a five-minute rapid drying cycle or a twenty-minute quieter cycle. The AWE presentation brought these appliance and station functions together around reducing repeated handling of wastewater and wet cleaning components.
