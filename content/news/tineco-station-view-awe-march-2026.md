[Tineco](/brands/tineco) presented the Station View wet floor cleaner at AWE 2026, according to China Household Electrical Appliances' 14 March report. The product uses a transparent wastewater section and automatic station maintenance to make the cleaning process easier to inspect.

The company said the station footprint was 18% smaller than its predecessor and described a high-pressure air-and-water flushing system. Those are manufacturer claims; the exhibition report did not provide an independent long-term blockage test.
