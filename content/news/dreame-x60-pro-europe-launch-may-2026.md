[Dreame](/brands/dreame) announced its new European portfolio on 27 May 2026, setting 5 June as the launch date for the X60 Pro Ultra Complete at EUR 1,499. A EUR 200 introductory discount was scheduled through 11 June.

The company also outlined X60 Pro Ultra Matrix, Matrix Vision and Master models for August. Availability details for the Cyber X were to follow separately. The announcement distinguishes products with a defined retail schedule from devices still awaiting launch details.
