[Dreame](/brands/dreame) set out its next European cleaning portfolio on May 27, 2026, with the X60 Pro Ultra Complete scheduled to launch on June 5. The [company announcement](https://www.presseportal.de/pm/173033/6282735) listed a EUR 1,499 retail price and a EUR 200 introductory discount through June 11.

The X60 Pro family emphasized extending cleaning components. Dreame described a side brush reaching outward by up to 12 cm and a mop mechanism reaching up to 18 cm, together with revised obstacle recognition and faster charging. These are manufacturer-stated features aimed at cleaning around furniture and reducing interruptions across larger areas.

Other configurations were scheduled for August. The X60 Pro Ultra Matrix would switch between mop types for different rooms, with a Vision version adding a transparent station design. The Master model used a compact station with automatic water supply and drainage, giving it different installation requirements from a conventional tank-based package.

Dreame also presented Cyber X, a tracked stair-climbing system intended to work with the X60 Pro family, but said availability details would follow separately. The event included T-series wet cleaners, window cleaning and pet-care products. Its announced June and August schedules applied to the specified models, rather than every device shown on stage.
