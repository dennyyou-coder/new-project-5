[Kärcher](/brands/karcher) announced the B 85 R Classic on 14 September 2026, adding a ride-on scrubber dryer to its entry-level professional range.

The company describes the machine as robust and straightforward to operate, with a design intended for several cleaning applications. Its announcement places the B 85 R Classic in the ride-on segment rather than the smaller walk-behind category.

The public newsroom entry does not state a universal launch price or country-by-country availability. Those details need confirmation with the relevant Kärcher sales operation. WCB has not conducted a performance test of this model.
