[Gausium](/brands/gausium) described a fleet of seven Scrubber 75 robots at Chongqing East Railway Station in a [case study published on October 10, 2025](https://gausium.com/case/chongqing-east-railway-station/). The machines were assigned to major public areas, including the main waiting hall, integrated transfer level and metro interchange level.

The station's interconnected passages and heavy passenger flow created repeated floor-cleaning work across large spaces. Gausium said the robots were deployed to take on those regular tasks while moving around people and other obstacles. Their work included edges as well as open areas of the floor.

The case study described the seven machines working across the station's principal zones. A representative of Chongqing Jinhe Jieli Cleaning Equipment said the arrangement allowed staff to give more attention to detailed work and unexpected cleaning needs. That account illustrates the intended division of tasks between the fleet and the human team.

The reported benefits came from the equipment supplier and project participants, rather than an independent comparison of costs or cleaning quality. October 10 is the publication date of the case study, not a confirmed installation date. The report establishes a named seven-robot transport-site deployment without disclosing the contract value or a quantified return on investment.
