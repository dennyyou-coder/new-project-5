[Gausium](/brands/gausium) published a case study on October 10, 2025 describing the use of seven Scrubber 75 cleaning robots at Chongqing East Railway Station.

The company said the deployment addressed repetitive floor-cleaning work across busy public areas, including waiting halls and connecting corridors. The project illustrates the use of a fleet of autonomous scrubbers in a transport facility with heavy pedestrian traffic.

The date above is the publication date of Gausium's case study. The report does not establish October 10 as the robots' installation date; its description of operating benefits comes from the equipment supplier.
