[Roborock](/brands/roborock) reported revenue of CNY 12.066 billion for the first nine months of 2025 on October 30, a 72.22% year-on-year increase. Attributable net profit fell 29.51% to CNY 1.038 billion, producing a markedly different direction for sales and earnings.

The standalone third quarter was stronger on the profit comparison. Revenue reached CNY 4.163 billion, up 60.71%, while attributable profit increased 2.51% to approximately CNY 360 million. That quarterly improvement was not enough to reverse the decline accumulated across the first nine months.

China Securities Journal's coverage also reported research and development investment of CNY 1.028 billion through September, up 60.56%. The company continued extending its technology and distribution across cleaning categories, while building online and physical retail coverage in China and overseas.

The distribution network described in the report included major online marketplaces, appliance retailers and international retail partners. This provides operating context for a business whose [product portfolio](https://global.roborock.com/pages/about-us) extends beyond its original robot-vacuum category. The nine-month result measures the company as a whole. Its rapid revenue increase, modest third-quarter profit growth and lower cumulative earnings are distinct indicators, each covering a different part of the reporting comparison.
