[Roborock](/brands/roborock) reported revenue of CNY 12.066 billion for the first nine months of 2025 on 30 October, up 72.22% year on year. Attributable net profit declined 29.51% to CNY 1.038 billion.

The figures show a substantial increase in sales alongside lower reported earnings. They cover January–September and should not be substituted for either the standalone third quarter or the full calendar year.
