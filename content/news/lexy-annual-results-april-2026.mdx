LEXY Electric reported almost flat revenue and a substantial fall in profit for 2025 in its [annual filing](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-16/1225106543.PDF) disclosed on April 16, 2026. Revenue increased 1.05% to CNY 9.867 billion, while net profit attributable to shareholders declined 35.04% to CNY 799.34 million.

The cleaning, health-appliance and garden-tool business generated CNY 5.751 billion of revenue, up 1.44%. Its gross margin fell by 2.91 percentage points to 22.14%. Motors, precision automotive parts and other components accounted for a further CNY 3.949 billion in the principal-business breakdown.

The report described higher sales-service costs and continued research spending, alongside lower interest income and foreign-exchange gains. Operating cash flow reached CNY 1.476 billion, down 8.36% from the previous year. These additional measures show that the earnings decline was much larger than the movement in total sales.

LEXY's board proposed no cash dividend or bonus-share distribution for 2025, saying retained funds would support business development; that proposal required shareholder approval. The results cover the consolidated group, including manufacturing and branded products. They should not be treated as a direct measure of any single overseas customer or individual floorcare brand supplied by the company.
