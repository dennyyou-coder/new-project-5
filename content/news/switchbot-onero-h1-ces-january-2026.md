SwitchBot announced its Onero H1 household robot ahead of CES in a release dated 4 January 2026. The company presented a 22-degree-of-freedom platform intended to carry out a broader range of home tasks.

The demonstration sits alongside SwitchBot's existing smart-home devices. A CES presentation is not confirmation that every demonstrated task is available in a shipping consumer product; commercial availability remains subject to the company's release schedule.
