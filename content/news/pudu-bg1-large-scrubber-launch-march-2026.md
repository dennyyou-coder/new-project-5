[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) announced the BG1 series on March 25, 2026, targeting large facilities such as warehouses, industrial plants, retail complexes and transport hubs.

Pudu described the range as combining 3D perception with AI-based assessment of floor conditions and cleaning tasks. The larger scrubber-dryer series joined its existing CC1 and MT1 families, extending the sizes and applications covered by its commercial cleaning range.

The March announcement also set out plans to exhibit BG1 at MODEX in Atlanta and Interclean Amsterdam in April. Those exhibition plans were separate from the product announcement; the release did not establish a universal country-by-country delivery schedule.
