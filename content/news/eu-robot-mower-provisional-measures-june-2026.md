The European Commission said on 19 June 2026 that it would not impose provisional anti-dumping measures in its investigation into robotic lawn mowers from China. The investigation would continue.

The Commission cited the complexity of the case and the need for further analysis. The decision concerned the provisional stage; it was not a finding that dumping had or had not occurred, and it did not determine the eventual outcome of the investigation.
