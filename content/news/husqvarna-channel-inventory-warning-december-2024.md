[Husqvarna](/brands/husqvarna) issued a trading update on December 9, 2024, citing heavier promotional activity, particularly in North America, and a less favorable product mix.

Retailers and servicing dealers were reducing inventory, which the company said had lowered production volumes and factory utilization. At the time, it expected fourth-quarter organic sales to decline about 5% and an operating loss of SEK 700 million to SEK 800 million, excluding items affecting comparability.

Those numbers were forecasts issued before the year-end results. The update also said cost reductions were continuing as Husqvarna prepared its product launches for the 2025 gardening season.
