Guangdong Xinbao Electrical Appliances, also known as Donlim, disclosed first-half 2026 revenue of CNY 7.420 billion, down 4.91%. Net profit attributable to shareholders fell 74.86% to CNY 136.44 million.

Profit excluding non-recurring items declined 77.72% to CNY 118.55 million. Operating cash flow was positive at approximately CNY 134.62 million. These results cover the company's broader small-appliance manufacturing business and should not be treated as a direct measure of the cleaning-appliance market alone.
