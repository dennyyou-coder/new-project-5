[Tennant](/brands/tennant) released second-quarter 2026 results on August 5. Net sales were $324.0 million, up 1.7% year on year, while adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, declined to $35.3 million from $51.0 million.

The company said autonomous mobile robot sales were approximately $31 million, up 37%. It cited residual ERP inefficiencies in North America and pricing and cost pressure in EMEA as factors affecting profitability.

Tennant raised its 2026 net-sales guidance to $1.270–$1.310 billion and lowered adjusted EBITDA guidance to $155–$170 million. These were the outlook ranges issued in August, rather than achieved full-year results.
