The FCC added two more companies to its list of conditional approvals for foreign-produced advanced robotic devices on November 18, 2026. Syncrobotic Co., Ltd. was approved for its "Jeff" and "Adventure" quadruped robotic platforms, and Bluepath Robotics, Inc. for its O1000 platform autonomous mobile robot and OM1500 tunnel AMR, according to [FCC Public Notice DA 26-1243](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-26-1243A1.pdf).

That brings the number of companies with robot approvals to 17 since [Husqvarna](/brands/husqvarna) received the first on September 9. Cleaning and lawn care are still represented by only two of them: Husqvarna's four Automower platforms, and Matic's robotic floor cleaner and dock, approved on October 2. No other robot vacuum or robotic mower maker has been approved.

The same notice approved a drone from FlyingBasket and routers from EnGenius and Zyxel. Router approvals run for about 18 months, while robot approvals carry no end date as long as each company follows the onshoring plan in its application and passes updated vetting. A false statement or a broken onshoring plan can return the products to the Covered List.

Foreign-produced advanced robotic devices were added to the Covered List on July 28, 2026. Models authorised before that date can still be sold.
