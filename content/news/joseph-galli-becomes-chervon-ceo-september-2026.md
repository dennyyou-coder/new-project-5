[Chervon](/brands/chervon) appointed Joseph Galli Jr. as chief executive with effect from September 10, 2026. Founder Pan Longquan handed over the CEO role and stays on as executive director and chairman. Chervon is listed in Hong Kong and owns the [EGO](/brands/ego-power-plus), FLEX, SKIL and DEVON brands.

Galli is one of the best-known executives in power tools. He joined Black & Decker in 1980, rose to president of its worldwide power tools and accessories business during about 19 years there, and founded the DeWalt brand. He later held senior roles at Amazon and Newell Rubbermaid. He joined Techtronic Industries (TTI) in 2006 and was its chief executive from 2008 until he retired in 2024. Under him, Milwaukee became one of the leading professional power tool brands and Ryobi's tool and outdoor business grew.

The appointment puts TTI's longtime leader in charge of one of its direct competitors. Chervon's EGO competes with Ryobi in battery outdoor equipment, and FLEX competes with Milwaukee and DeWalt for professional users. Chervon's revenue fell 8.2% in 2025 and rose 12.8% in the first half of 2026.

Within weeks of taking over, Galli began bringing in his own management team. WCB has covered those moves in a [separate article](/blog/galli-chervon-new-team).
