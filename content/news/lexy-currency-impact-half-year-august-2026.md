LEXY Electric's half-year report summary, published on 27 August 2026, identified foreign-exchange losses of CNY 388.81 million, compared with gains of CNY 17.46 million a year earlier.

The company said reported attributable net profit declined, but its calculation excluding the currency effect showed a 36.20% increase, assuming a 15% income-tax rate. That is a company-provided adjusted comparison, not the reported profit result. The disclosure highlights why reported and currency-adjusted earnings need to remain separate.
