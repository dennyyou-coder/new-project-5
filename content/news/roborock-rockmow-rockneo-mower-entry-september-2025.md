[Roborock](/brands/roborock) introduced its RockMow and RockNeo robotic mower families in Berlin on September 4, 2025. The range comprised RockMow Z1, RockMow S1 and RockNeo Q1, extending the company's navigation and cleaning-automation business into lawn maintenance.

The [official introduction](https://newsroom.roborock.com/gl/news/roborock-expands-outdoor-portfolio-with-next-generation-rockmow-and-rockneo-series-of-lawnmowers-for-every-lawn-and-every-challenge) positioned Z1 as the flagship for more demanding gardens. It combines all-wheel drive, independent steering at the front wheels and a suspension system intended to maintain traction on uneven ground. Its environmental sensing combines RTK positioning with visual mapping, while the cutting arrangement reaches toward lawn edges.

S1 focuses on easier mapping and access through narrower garden passages. Q1 brings automated mowing to more routine lawn maintenance, with scheduling by zone and a setting intended to avoid nighttime operation around nocturnal wildlife. The three models therefore share a brand platform but address different layouts and operating needs.

The launch gives Roborock a distinct outdoor category alongside robot vacuums and wet-cleaning appliances. For users, the relevant comparison is between the named mower models and their terrain, navigation and area specifications. The September event established the product-line introduction; local sales schedules and service coverage remained part of the subsequent commercial rollout in each market.
