[Roborock](/brands/roborock) presented its RockMow and RockNeo mower families in Berlin on September 4, 2025, expanding from indoor cleaning into robotic lawn care.

The announced models were RockMow Z1, RockMow S1 and RockNeo Q1. The flagship Z1 used all-wheel drive and an active steering system, while the wider range addressed different garden sizes and layouts through navigation, obstacle avoidance and configurable mowing.

The event marked the product-line introduction. The features belonged to these named 2025 mower models, rather than Roborock's robot vacuums or later pool-cleaning range; local commercial availability was a separate rollout question.
