[Midea](/brands/midea) and [Electrolux](/brands/electrolux) announced on 1 October 2026 that all three joint ventures under their North American partnership were operating. The companies originally announced the arrangement on 23 April.

The partnership combines manufacturing, product development, supply-chain and commercial activities. The companies said they planned to roughly double their combined North American production capacity for food preservation and fabric care over time. That capacity ambition is a future target, distinct from the confirmation that the joint ventures have begun operating.
