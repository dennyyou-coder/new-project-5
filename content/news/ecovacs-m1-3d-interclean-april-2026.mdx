[ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) presented DEEBOT PRO M1 3D at Interclean Amsterdam, alongside its M1 scrubber and K1 VAC vacuuming robot. The [April 16 announcement](https://www.ecovacs.com/global/newsroom/official-news/interclean-amsterdam-2026) describes the new model as a machine for factories, warehouses and other complex spaces.

M1 3D adds 3D LiDAR navigation, multi-sensor obstacle detection and targeted cleaning of dirty areas, according to the company.

ECOVACS also reported strategic agreements with European customers during its first Interclean appearance. It did not identify order volumes or establish a market-wide delivery date.
