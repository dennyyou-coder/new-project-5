[iRobot](/brands/irobot) announced Roomba Combo 2 Essential and Roomba Vac 2 Essential on October 21, 2024. They were the first models in its Essential family to empty their onboard dustbins into an AutoEmpty dock.

The North American announcement listed launch prices of $424.99 for the vacuum-and-mop Combo model and $399.99 for the vacuum-only version. Both added recharge-and-resume operation, while the Combo retained a removable microfiber mopping pad.

The release also reported Combo 2 Essential availability in EMEA and Asia-Pacific. The quoted dollar prices belong to the 2024 North American launch and are not current retail offers.
