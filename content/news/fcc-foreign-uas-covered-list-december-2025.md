The FCC announced on 22 December 2025 that foreign-produced unmanned aircraft systems and specified critical components were being added to its Covered List. The notice followed a national-security determination.

The measure concerns the equipment-authorization framework for the defined UAS category. Its scope should not be transferred automatically to household cleaning robots; those devices and any later regulatory actions require separate assessment.
