[Dreame](/brands/dreame) announced the X50 Ultra robot vacuum and Z1 Pro cordless pool cleaner on January 6, 2025, as part of its CES product presentation.

X50 Ultra introduced the ProLeap obstacle-crossing mechanism and VersaLift navigation system. The wider cleaning lineup included H12 Pro FlexReach wet-and-dry cleaning and the Z30 cordless vacuum, extending the announcement beyond robot floorcare.

For the North American X50 Ultra launch, Dreame listed a $1,699.99 price and February 14 sales date, with a presale program starting January 7. These were the regional plans published at CES, distinct from later X60 products.
