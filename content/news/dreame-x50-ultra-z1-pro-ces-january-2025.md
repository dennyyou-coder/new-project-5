[Dreame](/brands/dreame) announced the X50 Ultra robot vacuum and Z1 Pro cordless pool cleaner on January 6, 2025, as part of its CES presentation. The [company release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/all-dreams-in-one-dreame-dreame-technology-unveils-next-generation-smart-home-ecosystem-at-ces-2025-302342783.html) brought indoor and outdoor cleaning into the same product showcase while giving the North American X50 Ultra a defined sales schedule.

The X50 Ultra introduced ProLeap, an obstacle-crossing mechanism intended to help the machine negotiate changes in floor level. Its VersaLift navigation system addresses a different challenge: adapting the robot's sensing arrangement for access beneath furniture. Together, those systems focused on where the robot could travel as well as the cleaning work it performed.

Dreame listed a North American launch price of USD 1,699.99 for the X50 Ultra and a February 14 sales date, following a presale programme beginning January 7. Those were the terms announced at CES, rather than a current offer or a uniform price for every country.

The wider lineup included the H12 Pro FlexReach wet-and-dry cleaner and Z30 cordless vacuum, alongside the Z1 Pro pool product. The announcement therefore covered several distinct cleaning tasks and product formats. It belongs to the 2025 X50 launch cycle and should be read separately from Dreame's later X60-series releases.
