[Electrolux](/brands/electrolux) reported first-quarter 2026 net sales of SEK 29.543 billion on 24 April. Organic sales declined 0.5%, with growth in EMEA, Asia Pacific and Latin America offset by an 11.6% decline in North America.

Operating income excluding non-recurring items was SEK 198 million, a 0.7% margin. The company identified weaker North American demand and increased U.S. tariff costs among the pressures on the region.
