[Husqvarna](/brands/husqvarna) reported third-quarter 2024 net sales of SEK 9.739 billion on October 23, down 7% from a year earlier. Organic sales declined 4% as consumer spending remained subdued.

Within Forest & Garden, the company said robotic mowers grew in both professional and residential markets, alongside battery-powered equipment. Europe expanded while North America declined. Those product trends differed from the overall group result.

The report also outlined further cost-saving measures targeting SEK 500 million. The figures describe the quarter ending in September 2024; the news is filed under the October results announcement.
