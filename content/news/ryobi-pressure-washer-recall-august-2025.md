The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced a [RYOBI](/brands/ryobi) pressure-washer recall on August 28, 2025. It covered specified serial ranges of models RY142300, RY142300VNM and RY142711VNM.

The notice said a capacitor could overheat and burst, ejecting parts and causing impact injuries. Approximately 764,000 units were affected in the United States, with another 16,000 sold in Canada.

CPSC instructed owners to stop using recalled units and check the official recall instructions for a free repair kit. Model and serial-number checks are essential: the notice does not apply to every RYOBI pressure washer. The linked source carries the detailed affected ranges and remedy.
