[GE Appliances](/brands/ge-appliances) and IUE-CWA announced a USD 1 billion investment plan for Appliance Park in Louisville on 2 September 2026. The company said more than USD 400 million would support moving dryer production from Mexico to Kentucky.

The announcement described the investment as strengthening U.S. appliance manufacturing and supporting approximately 4,700 production jobs at the site. It is a manufacturing commitment with work still to be implemented, rather than confirmation that all relocated production lines are already operating.
