[Shark](/brands/shark)Ninja reported third-quarter 2025 net sales of USD 1.6302 billion on 6 November, up 14.3% year on year. Cleaning Appliances sales increased 12.4% to USD 592.9 million.

Group net income rose 42.6% to USD 188.7 million. The category result provides a more direct view of floorcare trading than the group total, which also includes cooking and other household products.
