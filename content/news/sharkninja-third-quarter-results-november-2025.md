[Shark](/brands/shark)Ninja reported third-quarter 2025 net sales of USD 1.630 billion on November 6, up 14.3% from the same quarter a year earlier. On a constant-currency basis, growth was 13.0%, separating underlying trading from the effect of exchange-rate movements.

Cleaning Appliances revenue increased 12.4% to USD 592.9 million, compared with USD 527.5 million in the prior-year period. The [quarterly earnings release](https://ir.sharkninja.com/news/news-details/2025/SharkNinja-Reports-Third-Quarter-2025-Results/default.aspx) identified carpet extractors and robotics as contributors to that increase, giving a more specific view of the floorcare business within the wider group.

Reported net income rose 42.6% to USD 188.7 million. That profit figure covers SharkNinja's full operations, including cooking, beverage, food-preparation, beauty and home-environment products as well as cleaning. It should therefore be considered alongside, rather than substituted for, the category sales comparison.

The results arrived during a period in which appliance suppliers were responding to U.S. tariff costs and changing product demand. SharkNinja's figures show double-digit growth in both total sales and Cleaning Appliances for the quarter, while the mix of products determines how that growth reaches the group total. The report covers July through September 2025 and remains separate from the later full-year result.
