[Nilfisk](/brands/nilfisk) became part of Freudenberg Home and Cleaning Solutions on April 15, 2026, following completion and settlement of the takeover offer. Its [announcement](https://www.nilfisk.com/global/professional/news-and-articles/nilfisk-enters-a-new-phase-as-part-of-freudenberg-after-completion-of-takeover-offer/) marked the ownership milestone after the [offer first announced in December 2025](/news/nilfisk-freudenberg-takeover-offer-december-2025).

Freudenberg's separate completion notice said it held more than 90% of Nilfisk's share capital and voting rights, excluding treasury shares. The buyer intended to acquire the remaining shares through a compulsory acquisition and seek delisting. Those subsequent steps were distinct from settlement of the accepted offer on April 15.

The companies described a combination of Nilfisk's professional cleaning machines with Freudenberg's manual cleaning systems. Nilfisk was to continue as a standalone brand within the larger group. Management presented the industrial fit as a basis for a broader customer offering while building on each business's existing capabilities.

Nilfisk entered the group with 2025 revenue of EUR 996.3 million and a business serving professional customers in more than 100 countries. The immediate event was a completed takeover offer and new ownership structure. Removal of the shares from Nasdaq Copenhagen came later, in May, and should not be folded into the April completion date.
