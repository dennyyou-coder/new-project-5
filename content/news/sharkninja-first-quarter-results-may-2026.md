[Shark](/brands/shark)Ninja reported first-quarter 2026 net sales of USD 1.4128 billion on 6 May, up 15.6% year on year. Net income increased 3.1% to USD 121.5 million, while adjusted net income rose 25.1% to USD 154.8 million.

Management raised its full-year net-sales growth forecast to 11.5%–12.5%. The forecast was issued with the quarterly results and was not a completed full-year outcome. Group sales include cooking and other household categories as well as cleaning appliances.
