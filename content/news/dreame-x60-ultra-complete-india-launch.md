[Dreame](/brands/dreame) announced the X60 Ultra Complete for India on September 21, 2026, listing a price of INR 134,999. Its [local launch release](https://www.prnewswire.com/in/news-releases/dreame-technology-launches-flagship-x60-ultra-complete-robot-vacuum-in-india-redefining-whole-home-cleaning-with-smarter-intelligence-and-powerful-performance-302884549.html) scheduled sales through Dreame's Indian website from September 22 and Amazon India from September 27, with Croma and selected stores also named as channels.

The Indian configuration combines a 7.95 cm body with a retractable navigation sensor. Dual cameras support obstacle recognition, while extending brush and mop components are intended to reach edges around furniture. Dreame states a maximum suction figure of 35,000 Pa for this model.

Its obstacle-crossing system is designed for single-layer steps up to 4.2 cm and eligible double-layer transitions up to 8 cm. Those are different obstacle conditions, rather than interchangeable height ratings. The station automates mop maintenance, and the robot adjusts its cleaning approach according to detected dirt and floor conditions.

Dreame said the package included a one-year warranty, with service coverage in more than 160 Indian cities and installation or collection support at eligible locations. The release establishes the local package, channels and announced sales dates. The X60 name also appears in other regional lineups, where accessories, capabilities and pricing may differ from this Indian offer.
