[Dreame](/brands/dreame) announced the X60 Ultra Complete robot vacuum for India on September 21. The company listed a price of INR 134,999, with sales scheduled to begin through its Indian website on September 22 and Amazon India on September 27. It also named Croma and selected retail stores as sales channels.

For this Indian model, Dreame describes a 7.95 cm body, a retractable navigation sensor and binocular AI cameras for obstacle recognition. Its announcement claims 35,000 Pa suction and a system designed to negotiate single-layer steps up to 4.2 cm and double-layer transitions up to 8 cm. Those figures come from the manufacturer and should be read within the stated test conditions.

The announcement concerns the X60 Ultra Complete package sold in India. Specifications, accessories and prices should not be assumed to apply to every X60 variant or regional bundle. The September dates are the company's announced launch schedule, rather than confirmation of current retailer stock.
