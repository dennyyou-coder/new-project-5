Empower Brands, the home and personal care business jointly owned by Spectrum Brands and funds managed by Oaktree Capital Management, made public on November 4 a non-binding, all-cash proposal to buy Helen of Troy for USD 37.00 per share. The offer implies an enterprise value of about USD 1.6 billion.

Empower owns [Russell Hobbs](/brands/russell-hobbs), Remington, George Foreman and PowerXL, and sells BLACK+DECKER household appliances. Helen of Troy owns OXO, Hydro Flask and Osprey.

The [press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000109177/000095010326016905/dp256289_ex9901.htm) set out the history. Empower first proposed USD 34.00 per share on August 19. Helen of Troy rejected it on September 3 without further engagement. Empower raised the offer to USD 37.00 on October 12, and on October 20 Helen of Troy's management called the increased proposal inadequate. Empower said the price was a 31% premium to Helen of Troy's 30-day volume-weighted share price, and that an affiliate already owns about 4.5% of Helen of Troy's shares.

The deal would be paid for with Empower's cash, new equity from Spectrum Brands and Oaktree, and third-party debt, with no financing contingency. Spectrum's [8-K filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000109177/000095010326016905/dp256289_8k.htm) said neither it nor Empower has had access to Helen of Troy's books. No agreement has been signed.

Oaktree took about 27% of Spectrum's home and personal care business in May 2026. For the ownership chain behind Russell Hobbs, see [who owns Russell Hobbs](/blog/who-owns-russell-hobbs-spectrum-brands).
