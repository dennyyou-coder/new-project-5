[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) outlined an expanded European support network on September 14, 2026, after exhibiting at IFA Berlin. The company said its business covered more than 20 European countries and announced plans for a new European headquarters in Germany.

The [regional update](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pudu-robotics-deepens-european-presence-following-ifa-2026-advancing-a-scaled-and-localized-growth-strategy-302877494.html) describes offices in Germany and the Netherlands, with staff covering sales, deployment, technical assistance and after-sales service. Pudu combines those direct teams with authorized local partners for consultation, installation, training and maintenance. Regional warehousing supports equipment delivery and service work across the network.

The announcement also identified cleaning installations already operating in the region. Pudu said Swiss supermarket chain Denner had deployed 200 CC1 robots across its stores. Shaoke Logistics in the Netherlands uses CC1 and MT1 machines for warehouse cleaning, while other named customers span hospitality, healthcare and retail. These examples show different operating settings for the same broader commercial robotics portfolio.

Pudu's planned German headquarters is intended to coordinate regional management, partner support and solution delivery. It was described as a future organizational step, with no opening date or address given. The update therefore combines existing offices and deployments with a further expansion plan, rather than announcing that the new headquarters had already begun operating.
