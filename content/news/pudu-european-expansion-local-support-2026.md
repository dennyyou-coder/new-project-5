[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) outlined an expanded European sales and service network on September 14, following its appearance at IFA Berlin. The company says its business now covers more than 20 European countries.

Pudu described offices in Germany and the Netherlands, local teams covering sales, deployment and after-sales service, and regional warehousing. Its support model combines direct teams with authorized partners for consultation, installation, training and maintenance.

The update also cited commercial-cleaning deployments. According to Pudu, Swiss retailer Denner has deployed 200 CC1 cleaning robots across its stores, while Shaoke Logistics in the Netherlands uses CC1 and MT1 machines for warehouse cleaning.

Pudu plans to establish a new European headquarters in Germany to coordinate regional operations and partner support. The announcement describes that headquarters as a future step; it does not confirm an opening date or address.
