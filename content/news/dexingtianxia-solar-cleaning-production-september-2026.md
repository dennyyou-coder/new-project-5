Wuhan Economic and Technological Development Zone reported on 10 September 2026 that Wuhan Dexingtianxia had started batch production of its second-generation handheld solar-panel cleaning equipment. The initial batch comprised 220 units, with some already being shipped to customers in the Philippines.

The equipment is intended for distributed solar installations, including rooftops and smaller or irregular arrays. It is a handheld cleaning system; the announcement should not be interpreted as a deployment of 220 fully autonomous mobile robots.
