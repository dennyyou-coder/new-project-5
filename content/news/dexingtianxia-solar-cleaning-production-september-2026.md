Wuhan Dexingtianxia began batch production of its second-generation handheld solar-panel cleaning equipment, according to a September 10, 2026 report from the Wuhan Economic and Technological Development Zone. The first batch comprised 220 machines, with some already dispatched to customers in the Philippines.

The batch included three configurations: 20 units with a 5.5-metre roller brush, 100 with a 5.5-metre disc arrangement and 100 with a 7.5-metre disc arrangement. The equipment is intended for distributed solar installations, including commercial rooftops, smaller arrays and areas that are difficult for large automated systems to reach.

The company said the second generation incorporated changes to core components and overall durability, usability and safety. The [local production report](https://www.whkfq.gov.cn/xwzx/yw/kfqyw/qnxw/202609/t20260910_2846104.html) described a handheld system used by an operator, even though the product is marketed with robot terminology. The shipment count should therefore not be read as a deployment of 220 fully autonomous mobile cleaners.

Dexingtianxia was established in 2023 as a robotics business within its parent group. The production milestone adds a manufacturing and export update to the solar-cleaning segment, with the Philippines shipment providing a specific example of the destinations planned for the initial batch.
