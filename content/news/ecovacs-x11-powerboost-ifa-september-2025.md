[ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) detailed DEEBOT X11 in an IFA update published on September 7, 2025. Its PowerBoost system was designed to add charge during the robot's normal mop-cleaning stops.

The company said a three-minute stop could restore 6% of battery capacity under its stated conditions. The approach combined frequent top-ups with power scheduling, aiming to reduce long interruptions during cleaning. That is a manufacturer claim, rather than a WCB endurance test.

The Berlin display also included WINBOT and GOAT products. This story is dated to the September 7 source update and should not be treated as the first announcement or a universal on-sale date for X11.
