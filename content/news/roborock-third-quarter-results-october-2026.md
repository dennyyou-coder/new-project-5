[Roborock](/brands/roborock) reported third-quarter revenue of CNY 5.61 billion, up 34.85% year on year, and attributable net profit of CNY 479.2 million, up 33.01%. Its quarterly report, dated October 31, covers the three months to September 30, 2026.

Across January–September, revenue reached CNY 15.70 billion and attributable net profit CNY 1.47 billion, increases of 30.10% and 41.23%. Profit excluding non-recurring items rose 44.50% to CNY 1.21 billion over the nine-month period. These are consolidated company results, rather than sales figures for an individual robot vacuum.

The company attributed growth to domestic and overseas market expansion, improved operating efficiency and lower expense ratios. It also disclosed CNY 213.8 million of IEEPA tariff refunds recognized in nine-month earnings. That contribution should be distinguished from revenue generated by product sales.

Operating cash flow remained negative at approximately CNY 301.7 million for the first nine months, despite a narrower outflow. Roborock cited stocking and payment settlement effects in the third quarter. The update extends the company's [first-half results](/news/roborock-first-half-results-august-2026), with earnings growth continuing alongside working-capital demands.
