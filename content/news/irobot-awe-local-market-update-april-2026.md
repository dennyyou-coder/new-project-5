[iRobot](/brands/irobot)'s Italian press office highlighted the company's participation at AWE 2026 in an update dated 13 April. The statement linked its exhibition presence and Roomba Mini introduction with product development following the Picea transaction.

The company said it would retain its Bedford headquarters and core U.S. engineering, product-development and marketing functions, with manufacturing concentrated in Picea facilities. The update describes its operating direction rather than a separately confirmed China-only model launch.
