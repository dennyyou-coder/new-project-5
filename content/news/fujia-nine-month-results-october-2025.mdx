Ningbo Fujia reported first-nine-month 2025 revenue of CNY 2.291 billion, up 23.29%, in its [third-quarter filing](https://money.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=11538253&stockid=603219) published on October 28. Net profit attributable to shareholders declined 19.19% to CNY 117.12 million over the same January-to-September period.

The third quarter alone showed a different earnings direction. Attributable profit increased 10.80% to CNY 41.70 million, while revenue fell 3.31% compared with the same quarter a year earlier. That means the latest quarter improved in profit even though the cumulative result remained below the previous year's level.

The quarterly and nine-month figures answer different questions. The former records trading during July through September; the latter includes the first six months as well. The third-quarter profit is already part of the CNY 117.12 million total and should not be added to it a second time.

Fujia's disclosure covers the listed manufacturing company rather than one consumer brand. It shows that higher cumulative sales did not translate into higher cumulative attributable earnings, while the latest quarter followed its own pattern. The report does not establish the retail performance of individual customers or provide a standalone market-growth measure for vacuum cleaners.
