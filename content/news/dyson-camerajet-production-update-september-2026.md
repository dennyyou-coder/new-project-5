[Dyson](/brands/dyson) said a manufacturing issue in one CameraJet jet-tube batch created potential water seepage and affected some early products. Its [September 30 statement](https://www.dyson.co.uk/discover/inside-dyson/statements/dyson-camerajet-update) says isolating those products caused a temporary stock interruption.

The company said CameraJet technology launched globally on September 1 and that it was now in full production and shipping products. The statement gives no affected-unit count or model-level breakdown.

The notice describes a production and availability problem; it does not announce a general product recall.
