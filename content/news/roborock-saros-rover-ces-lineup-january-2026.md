[Roborock](/brands/roborock) used CES 2026 to show Saros Rover, a robot-vacuum design with independently controlled wheel-leg mechanisms. In its January 12 [show recap](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ces-2026-roborock-releases-the-worlds-first-robotic-vacuum-with-wheel-leg-architecture-as-it-joins-hands-with-real-madrid-football-club-302658214.html), the company described a system intended to negotiate stairs and slopes while extending cleaning beyond a single flat floor.

Rover's demonstration centers on mobility. Instead of treating a staircase as the end of a cleaning area, the design seeks to coordinate lifting, balance and cleaning as the machine moves between levels. Roborock identified it as a product under development and had not confirmed a launch date, so the CES appearance was a technology demonstration.

The company's wider lineup included [Saros 20](/products/roborock-saros-20), [Saros 20 Sonic](/products/roborock-saros-20-sonic), Qrevo Curv 2 Flow and F25 ACE Pro. Those announcements covered different floorcare formats, from autonomous vacuuming and mopping to handheld wet-and-dry cleaning, rather than one shared product specification.

Roborock also outlined entry into the U.S. robotic lawnmower market, including RockMow X1 LiDAR, and promoted its partnership with Real Madrid. The show combined near-term product programs with longer-term robotics development. The January 12 date refers to the company's recap of that showcase, with individual retail launches following their own regional schedules.
