[Roborock](/brands/roborock) summarized its CES 2026 announcements in a January 12 release, including Saros Rover, a robot vacuum with independently controlled wheel-leg mechanisms intended to navigate and clean stairs.

The company explicitly described Saros Rover as a product in development with an unconfirmed launch date. Its demonstrations therefore did not establish retail availability.

The broader lineup included [Saros 20](/products/roborock-saros-20), [Saros 20 Sonic](/products/roborock-saros-20-sonic), Qrevo Curv 2 Flow and F25 ACE Pro. Roborock also announced plans to enter the U.S. robotic lawnmower market, including RockMow X1 LiDAR. January 12 is the date of this company recap of the CES showcase.
