[Mammotion](/brands/mammotion) named Shingu Shoko (新宮商行) as its general distributor in Japan on September 24, 2026. The [company release](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000002.000183827.html) says the partnership will use Shingu’s agricultural and forestry machinery experience and dealer network to support local sales and service.

Mammotion also announced plans to exhibit LUBA 3 AWD and LUBA mini 2 AWD at GARDEX, scheduled for October 7–9 at Makuhari Messe. Maston was listed as a development-stage commercial mower for reference display. The announcement establishes the channel appointment and exhibition plan; it does not confirm commercial deliveries of Maston.
