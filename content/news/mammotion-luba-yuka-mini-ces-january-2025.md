[Mammotion](/brands/mammotion) expanded its robotic mower portfolio at CES 2025 with LUBA mini AWD and YUKA mini, alongside updates to the larger LUBA 2 AWD and YUKA families. The [announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mammotion-unveils-next-generation-robotic-mowers-with-new-ai-vision-technology-at-ces-2025-302342426.html) was published on January 5 with a January 6 Las Vegas dateline and opened preorders from January 5.

The two mini lines extended the range toward smaller lawns. Mammotion described LUBA mini AWD as suitable for areas up to 1,500 square metres, with a stated weight of 15 kg. YUKA mini targeted lawns up to 800 square metres and was listed at 10 kg. These were range-level specifications for the original 2025 products.

UltraSense AI Vision was a central part of the presentation. The company described automatic lawn mapping, visual boundary recognition and navigation support where satellite reception is weak. The larger YUKA retained a combined mowing-and-sweeping proposition, while LUBA emphasized all-wheel-drive terrain handling.

The resulting lineup offered distinct choices by lawn size and work type rather than one mower scaled uniformly across every garden. These original mini models should also be kept separate from subsequent mini 2 and LUBA 3 generations, whose sensing systems and configurations were announced later.
