[Mammotion](/brands/mammotion) announced LUBA mini AWD and YUKA mini alongside updates to LUBA 2 AWD and YUKA in its CES 2025 release. The wire release was published on January 5, with a January 6 Las Vegas dateline; January 5 is used here as the source publication date.

The new mini families extended Mammotion's range toward smaller lawns. Its UltraSense AI Vision system was presented as supporting automatic mapping, boundary recognition and navigation where satellite signals are weak.

The company said preorders would begin January 5. These are the original 2025 mini models, separate from the later mini 2 and LUBA 3 generations.
