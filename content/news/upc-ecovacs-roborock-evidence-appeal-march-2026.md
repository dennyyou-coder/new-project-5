The UPC Court of Appeal upheld the revocation of an [ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) evidence-preservation order against [Roborock](/brands/roborock) on 16 March 2026, according to EPLAW's account of case UPC_CoA_3/2026.

The decision addresses disclosure obligations when seeking an order without hearing the other party. It concerns the procedure used to obtain evidence at IFA 2025, not a final determination that the products did or did not infringe a patent.
