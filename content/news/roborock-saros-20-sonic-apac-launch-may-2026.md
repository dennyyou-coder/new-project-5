[Roborock](/brands/roborock) presented six products led by [Saros 20 Sonic](/products/roborock-saros-20-sonic) at a Tokyo event on May 18, 2026. The company reported the launch in a May 26 release covering a two-day Asia-Pacific program with media, regional partners and customers.

The [event announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/roborock-unveils-new-flagship-product-lineup-and-hosts-where-the-greatest-meet-football-event-in-partnership-with-real-madrid-302781605.html) highlighted VibraRise 5.0 mopping and AdaptiLift Chassis 3.0 on Saros 20 Sonic. The presentation focused on improved mopping coverage and movement across household floor transitions. Approximately 200 guests attended the product session, according to Roborock.

The program also included a demonstration of Saros Rover, the wheel-leg robot first shown at CES. Roborock described Rover as still under development, with no launch date announced. Its appearance alongside retail products therefore illustrated a development direction rather than confirming that the concept was ready for purchase.

On May 19, a football clinic organized with Real Madrid brought together users and fans from several APAC markets. The same release listed Saros 20 Sonic at VND 29,990,000 through brand stores and e-commerce in Vietnam. That price belonged to the Vietnamese offer, while the Tokyo event served a broader regional audience. May 18 remains the product-event date, distinct from the later publication date.
