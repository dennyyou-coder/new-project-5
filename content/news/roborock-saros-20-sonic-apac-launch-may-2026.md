[Roborock](/brands/roborock) introduced six products led by [Saros 20 Sonic](/products/roborock-saros-20-sonic) at a Tokyo event on May 18, 2026. The company reported the event in a May 26 release.

Roborock highlighted the model's VibraRise 5.0 mopping system and AdaptiLift Chassis 3.0. Its APAC program also included a football clinic with Real Madrid on May 18–19 and a demonstration of the Saros Rover concept previously shown at CES.

The release listed Saros 20 Sonic availability in Vietnam through brand stores and e-commerce at VND 29,990,000. That is a dated regional listing, not a worldwide price. The news date here is the May 18 product event.
