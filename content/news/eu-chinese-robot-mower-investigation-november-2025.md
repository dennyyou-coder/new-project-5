The European Commission opened an anti-dumping investigation into imports of robotic lawn mowers originating in China on 19 November 2025. The notice followed a complaint lodged on 3 October by Husqvarna Manufacturing CZ.

Opening an investigation is a procedural step to examine the complaint and evidence. It does not itself impose a duty or establish a final finding of dumping or injury.
