[Nilfisk](/brands/nilfisk) reported 2025 revenue of EUR 996.3 million on 19 February 2026. Organic growth was 0.2%, while reported revenue declined 3.1%.

EBITDA reached approximately EUR 129.2 million, equivalent to a 13.0% margin. The result concerns the cleaning-equipment group's annual trading performance and is separate from announcements about its ownership transaction.
