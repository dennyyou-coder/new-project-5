The Unified Patent Court's Munich division dismissed Papst Licensing's infringement claim against [Roborock](/brands/roborock) concerning EP 3 030 943, JUVE Patent reported on September 29, 2026. The report placed the decision in mid-August without specifying an exact day.

The [published patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3030943B1/en) concerns how a self-moving floor cleaner uses room information when executing a cleaning plan. It describes storing a map and assigning a task to a particular room, then using sensor information to check whether the robot is actually in that room.

A central part of the first claim is the response when the room does not match: the device does not begin, interrupts or ends the associated cleaning task. Further claims describe possible user notifications and changes to the task sequence. The technical subject is therefore room identification linked to task control, rather than the suction or mopping hardware alone.

The reported dismissal concerns Papst's claim under this specific patent. It does not resolve unrelated patents or other disputes involving the manufacturer. September 29 records when this outcome was reported; the earlier August period refers to the court event. Keeping that distinction preserves the sequence without supplying a decision date that this report did not establish.
