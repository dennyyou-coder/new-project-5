Chervon reported lower revenue and profit for 2025, as weaker power-tool sales offset a steadier outdoor-equipment business. Results announced on March 25, 2026 put group revenue at approximately USD 1.628 billion, down 8.2%, and annual profit at USD 97.69 million, down 13.3%.

The two main business areas moved differently. Outdoor power equipment revenue was broadly unchanged, rising 0.1%, while power-tool revenue fell 18.3%. The report linked more cautious customer purchasing to uncertainty around U.S.–China tariffs. North American sales declined, while Europe recorded a small increase.

Profitability also weakened. Gross margin fell to 32.9%, and adjusted net profit declined more sharply than reported profit. Operating cash flow nevertheless increased to about USD 229 million, showing that the cash result followed a different path from accounting earnings.

Chervon's brands include EGO, FLEX and SKIL, spanning outdoor equipment and power tools. The annual figures describe the consolidated business before the [EGO AURA-R2 mower availability announcement in 2026](/news/ego-aura-r2-march-availability-february-2026). They provide the preceding full-year baseline for the company's later [first-half 2026 results](/news/chervon-first-half-results-august-2026), rather than a standalone result for robotic mowing.
