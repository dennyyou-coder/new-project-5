Chervon reported 2025 revenue of approximately USD 1.628 billion on 25 March 2026, down 8.2% year on year. Net profit declined 13.3% to approximately USD 97.69 million.

The group's portfolio includes power tools and outdoor power equipment, including EGO. The annual figures describe the consolidated business; they do not identify a separate profit result for the robotic-mower range introduced in the following year.
