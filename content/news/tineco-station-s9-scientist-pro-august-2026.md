[Tineco](/brands/tineco) announced the [FLOOR ONE STATION S9 Scientist Pro](/products/tineco-floor-one-station-s9-scientist-pro) on August 7, 2026.

The hard-floor washer introduces HydroBurst water-jet cleaning, a sensing scraper intended to collect water on the backward pass, and a Smart Refresh Station for automated maintenance. Tineco specified 25,000 Pa suction and an 11 cm lay-flat design, with performance dependent on the stated operating conditions.

The release listed availability through Tineco, Amazon and selected retailers at a U.S. MSRP of $1,199. That is the price and sales announcement from August, not confirmation of today's offers or stock.
