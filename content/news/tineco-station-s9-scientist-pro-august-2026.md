[Tineco](/brands/tineco) introduced the [FLOOR ONE STATION S9 Scientist Pro](/products/tineco-floor-one-station-s9-scientist-pro) on August 7, 2026, combining a hard-floor washer with a station designed to automate more of its upkeep. The U.S. announcement listed a suggested retail price of $1,199 through Tineco, Amazon and selected retailers.

The machine adds HydroBurst, a water-jet function aimed at stubborn dirt and grease. Tineco specifies maximum suction of 25,000 Pa and uses its iLoop sensing system to adjust suction and water delivery. A sensing scraper changes position with the cleaning direction, with the backward pass intended to collect residual water from the floor.

According to the [company's launch release](https://en.prnasia.com/releases/global/tineco-launches-its-most-advanced-floor-washer-to-date-the-floor-one-station-s9-scientist-pro-543345.shtml), the washer can lie flat to reach spaces as low as 11 cm. Powered movement assistance, edge cleaning and lighting for revealing dust form part of the handling package. Tineco lists up to 100 minutes of runtime in Quiet mode and 75 minutes in Auto mode, so those figures describe different operating settings.

The Smart Refresh Station replenishes hot water and handles cleaning and drying after use. A five-minute FlashDry option and a quieter drying setting give users different maintenance choices. The launch addresses floor cleaning and preparation for the next session.
