[Dyson](/brands/dyson) introduced PencilVac in an announcement dated May 21, 2025. The cordless vacuum used a 38 mm body and a compact Hyperdymium motor to reduce the size of the main assembly.

Its Fluffycones head combined four conical rollers intended to release long hair during cleaning. Dyson also described a linear dust-separation system and illumination at the front and rear of the head, with movement designed for cleaning in several directions.

PencilVac is a dry vacuum. It should be distinguished from the later [PencilWash wet floor cleaner](/news/dyson-pencilwash-announcement-february-2026); their names, cleaning systems and release dates differ.
