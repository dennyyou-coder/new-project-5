[Narwal](/brands/narwal) announced the Freo Z10 Turbo on 12 May 2026, with U.S. availability scheduled from 18 May. The company positioned the model below its top-priced offerings while bringing selected cleaning features to a broader price tier.

Narwal lists maximum suction of 25,000 Pa and its CarpetFocus cleaning system. Launch promotions and regular pricing are time-sensitive; the announcement's feature claims are manufacturer specifications rather than results from a WCB performance test.
