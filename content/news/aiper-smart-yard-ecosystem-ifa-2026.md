[Aiper](/brands/aiper) presented an expanded Smart Yard Ecosystem at IFA Berlin in September 2026, connecting pool cleaning and garden maintenance through a shared control platform. The presentation extended the company's outdoor-care story beyond a standalone pool robot.

The announced ecosystem spans pool cleaners, irrigation, robotic mowers, soil sensors, weather stations and solar panels. Aiper says compatible devices can coordinate routines using schedules, weather information and soil conditions. The aim is to let a household organize several maintenance tasks together rather than manage each device in isolation.

Product highlights included the IrriSense 2 SE irrigation system and [Scuba V3 Ultra](/products/aiper-scuba-v3-ultra). The company described routines for daily upkeep, changing weather and periods when residents are away. These examples explain the planned user experience, although device compatibility and availability remain dependent on the market and hardware involved.

The [IFA announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/smart-yard-ecosystem-aiper-unveils-integrated-outdoor-care-solutions-at-ifa-berlin-2026-302869823.html) follows Aiper's [CES portfolio introduction in January](/news/aiper-smart-yard-scuba-v3-ces-january-2026) and [V3 Ultra purchase announcement in June](/news/aiper-scuba-v3-ultra-june-2026-availability). It also follows the company's [August irrigation campaign results](/news/aiper-irrisense-water-saving-results-august-2026). Read together, the releases show a progression from individual product announcements toward coordinated outdoor maintenance; the Berlin presentation does not mean every device went on sale worldwide at the show.
