[Aiper](/brands/aiper) presented an expanded Smart Yard Ecosystem at IFA Berlin in September 2026, bringing pool cleaning and garden maintenance into a shared app-controlled platform.

The company says compatible devices can coordinate tasks using weather information, schedules and soil-sensor data. The announced ecosystem spans pool robots, irrigation systems, robotic mowers, soil sensors, weather stations and solar panels.

Product highlights included the IrriSense 2 SE irrigation system and the [Scuba V3 Ultra](/products/aiper-scuba-v3-ultra) pool cleaner. Aiper described routines for daily maintenance, changing weather and periods when owners are away.

The announcement outlines compatibility and automation plans; it does not establish that every device is available in every market. Retail availability and the exact combinations supported by the local app and hardware should be checked separately.
