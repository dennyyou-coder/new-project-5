[Electrolux](/brands/electrolux) reported third-quarter sales of SEK 33.919 billion on October 23, 2026, with organic growth of 3.5%. Its [results release](https://www.electroluxgroup.com/en/electrolux-group-interim-report-q3-2026-47822/) recorded operating income of SEK 607 million, and SEK 912 million before non-recurring items. The net result was a SEK 340 million loss.

The report dates the Anderson fabric-care manufacturing venture with [Midea](/brands/midea) to September 1. The U.S. food-preservation sales venture and Juarez manufacturing venture became operational October 1, when the Juarez asset sale also completed.

The figures cover the household-appliance group, not cleaning products alone. They provide updated financial context for the [previously reported partnership](/news/midea-electrolux-north-america-jvs-operational-october-2026).
