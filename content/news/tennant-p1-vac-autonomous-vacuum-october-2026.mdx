[Tennant](/brands/tennant) introduced the P1 VAC on October 20, 2026, describing it as its first autonomous robotic vacuum and first P-Series model. The [company announcement](https://investors.tennantco.com/news/news-details/2026/Tennant-Company-Introduces-Its-First-Autonomous-Robotic-Vacuum-the-P1-VAC/default.aspx) said the machine was available to order.

P1 VAC combines vacuuming and dust mopping, with a side brush guiding debris into its path and a dual-compartment, 10-litre collection system. Tennant positions it for routine commercial cleaning and identifies a Wisconsin school district as a testing partner.

The launch adds soft-floor cleaning to a robotics portfolio previously centered on hard-floor equipment.
