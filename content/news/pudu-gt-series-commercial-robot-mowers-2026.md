[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) launched the GT Series on September 1, 2026, entering professional grounds care with three robotic mowers. GT3, GT5 and GT7 address different jobs, from mixed commercial properties to large lawns and precision sports turf.

The [company's launch release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pudu-robotics-launches-pudu-gt-series-a-three-model-lineup-of-commercial-robotic-mowers-for-professional-grounds-and-turf-management-302866160.html) positions GT3 as a compact machine for fragmented sites such as campuses and business parks. It combines mowing and grass collection with light sweeping on paved areas, allowing grounds teams to cover turf and adjoining paths with one platform.

GT5 is a larger rotary mower for repeated work across extensive commercial lawns. Pudu describes a 60-inch cutting width and collection of clippings, leaves and light debris during mowing. GT7 uses three floating reel units for golf courses and professional sports fields, with configurable mowing patterns and cutting-height adjustment. Its positioning combines RTK, LiDAR and camera vision.

The models share autonomous navigation and obstacle detection, while manual driving lets operators reposition equipment or intervene. Collection and automatic dumping depend on deployment configuration. Pudu scheduled the range's public debut at GaLaBau in Nuremberg in September, bringing its commercial robotics offer into outdoor maintenance alongside its established indoor cleaning and delivery products.
