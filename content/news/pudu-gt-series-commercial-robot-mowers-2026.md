[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) announced the GT Series of commercial robotic mowers on September 1, 2026. The lineup comprises GT3, GT5 and GT7.

The company described three distinct use cases: integrated grounds care around commercial properties, high-efficiency maintenance of larger turf areas, and precision work for golf courses and professional sports fields.

Pudu's announcement extends its robotics portfolio into professional grounds management. The models should be assessed individually; the series description does not establish one shared mowing capacity, price or delivery schedule for all three machines.
