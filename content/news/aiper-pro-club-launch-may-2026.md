[Aiper](/brands/aiper) launched its Pro Club initiative on May 21, 2026, presenting an educational content hub and community for pool, lawn and irrigation maintenance.

The company said the program brought together more than 20 experts to contribute tutorials, seasonal checklists and advice on using smart yard equipment. Named contributors included Chip Wade and pool- and lawn-care creators.

The initiative expands Aiper's customer-support content around its outdoor products. It is an education and community announcement; the release does not identify a new pool-robot model or disclose commercial performance results for the platform.
