[Aiper](/brands/aiper) introduced Pro Club on May 21, 2026, adding a pool-and-yard education platform to its outdoor-equipment business. The program brings together more than 20 contributors to help homeowners plan maintenance and make better use of connected backyard products.

The content covers pool opening, seasonal lawn care, irrigation and everyday equipment use. Planned formats include practical tutorials, maintenance checklists and advice from specialists. Aiper positioned the initiative around recurring ownership questions, including how to prepare a pool after winter and how to avoid common spring lawn-care mistakes.

Named participants include designer and television host Chip Wade, pool-care creator thep00lguy and lawn-care contributor Pest and Lawn Ginja. Their roles span different parts of the backyard, reflecting the company's expansion from cordless pool cleaners into irrigation and other outdoor categories.

According to [Aiper's launch announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/aiper-builds-expert-led-backyard-care-community-through-new-aiper-pro-club-initiative-302779106.html), Pro Club will operate as both an educational resource and a community. The release did not announce a new robot or publish commercial results for the platform. It adds a service and guidance layer around the equipment portfolio, alongside the company's [CES smart-yard presentation](/news/aiper-smart-yard-scuba-v3-ces-january-2026). For owners, its immediate offering is help with maintenance routines across the season.
