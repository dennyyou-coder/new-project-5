[Gausium](/brands/gausium) announced on November 14, 2025 that its Mira autonomous floor-cleaning robot had received Innovation of the Year in the Automation & Equipment category at ISSA Show North America.

The company said the award was presented at the Mandalay Bay Convention Center in Las Vegas. Its exhibit paired Mira with Gausium Leaves and highlighted the robot's ability to sweep and scrub in a single pass.

The recognition applies to the named award category. It is an exhibition award rather than a comparative field test of cleaning performance or operating costs.
