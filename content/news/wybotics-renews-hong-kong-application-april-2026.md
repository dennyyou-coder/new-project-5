Tianjin [WYBOT](/brands/wybot)ICS submitted a renewed Hong Kong listing application on 24 April 2026, according to exchange disclosures reported by Zhitong Finance. CSC International was identified as sole sponsor.

The company supplies pool-cleaning robots under its own brands and through manufacturing partnerships. A renewed application updates the listing process; it does not establish regulatory approval, an offer price or a confirmed trading date.
