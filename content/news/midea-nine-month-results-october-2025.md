[Midea](/brands/midea) reported revenue of CNY 363.056 billion for the first nine months of 2025 on 30 October, up 13.82%. Attributable net profit rose 19.51% to CNY 37.883 billion.

Third-quarter revenue alone was CNY 111.933 billion, up 10.06%. The report covers the diversified group's consolidated operations rather than a standalone sales figure for floorcare products.
