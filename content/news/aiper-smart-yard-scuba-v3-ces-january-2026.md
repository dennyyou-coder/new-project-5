[Aiper](/brands/aiper) outlined a broader pool-and-garden portfolio on January 6, 2026, ahead of its January 7 CES launch event. The announcement brought new pool-cleaning robots together with surface skimming and irrigation equipment under the company's smart-yard strategy.

The Scuba V3 family included Scuba V3, Scuba V3 Pro and [Scuba V3 Ultra](/products/aiper-scuba-v3-ultra). Aiper presented adaptive route selection and operating adjustments as core features of the range. The lineup gave the company several models within the same generation, rather than a single replacement for every existing cleaner.

Alongside the underwater cleaners, Aiper introduced EcoSurfer Senti for pool-surface maintenance and IrriSense 2 for irrigation. Those products address different jobs: removing floating debris and managing water delivery in the garden. Their inclusion made the CES presentation an outdoor-equipment portfolio announcement as well as a pool-robot launch.

The [January release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/aiper-is-pioneering-the-carefree-and-reliable-smart-yard-ecosystem-at-ces-2026-302654094.html) introduced the products and their intended functions. Commercial milestones followed later, including [Scuba V3 Ultra purchase availability in June](/news/aiper-scuba-v3-ultra-june-2026-availability) and an [expanded ecosystem presentation at IFA in September](/news/aiper-smart-yard-ecosystem-ifa-2026). Keeping those dates separate makes clear what was shown at CES and what was subsequently announced for sale.
