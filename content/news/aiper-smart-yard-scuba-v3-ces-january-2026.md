[Aiper](/brands/aiper) announced its expanded pool-and-garden portfolio on January 6, 2026, ahead of a CES launch event scheduled for January 7.

The lineup included Scuba V3, Scuba V3 Pro and [Scuba V3 Ultra](/products/aiper-scuba-v3-ultra), alongside the EcoSurfer Senti surface skimmer and IrriSense 2 irrigation system. Aiper described automatic adjustment of cleaning routes and operating settings as a central feature of the Scuba V3 family.

The CES presentation introduced the portfolio and its intended functions. [Scuba V3 Ultra purchase availability was the subject of a later June announcement](/news/aiper-scuba-v3-ultra-june-2026-availability), distinct from this January showcase.
