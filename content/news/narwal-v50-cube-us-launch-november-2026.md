[Narwal](/brands/narwal), best known for its self-cleaning robot vacuums, launched the V50 Cube in the United States on November 2. The company calls it its first cordless dry vacuum for the US market.

The V50 Cube is a cordless stick vacuum that docks into a compact base. According to Narwal's [announcement](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/narwal-launches-the-v50-cube-in-the-us-debuting-its-first-cordless-dry-vacuum-with-a-compact-auto-empty-base-station-302925955.html), the base empties the dustbin into a sealed 0.8-gallon bag, which Narwal says holds up to 100 days of debris, and charges a spare battery. The vacuum weighs 3.1 lb and is rated at 210 AW of suction from a 450 W motor. Narwal claims up to 110 minutes of runtime with two batteries, in Eco mode with the main brush, based on lab testing.

The list price is USD 649.99, with a launch price of USD 499.99 from November 2 to November 30. It is sold on Narwal's website and Amazon.

The launch puts Narwal into the auto-empty stick vacuum segment, where Shark, Dyson and Samsung already sell models with self-emptying bases. See [who owns Narwal](/blog/who-owns-narwal-yunjing-investors-manufacturing).
