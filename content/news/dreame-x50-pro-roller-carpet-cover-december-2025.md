[Dreame](/brands/dreame)'s X50 Pro Roller uses a retractable cover to separate its wet-cleaning mechanism from carpet, according to a New Express report published on December 31, 2025. The feature addresses a practical problem for roller-mop robots that move between hard flooring and rugs during one cleaning run.

The cover is intended to shield the wet roller when the machine encounters carpet. Instead of relying only on lifting a damp cleaning surface, the design adds a physical barrier. The report describes this as part of the manufacturer's approach to managing different floor types within one route.

For hard floors, the X50 Pro Roller uses a 27 cm roller supplied continuously with clean water, together with recovery of dirty water. The arrangement keeps washing and wastewater collection active while the robot travels, rather than treating the roller solely as a damp pad that returns to its dock between rooms.

The carpet-protection mechanism and water circuit belong to the named X50 Pro Roller configuration. The report's claims about dry carpets describe the intended operation of that system; they are not independent results across every rug material or pile height. The December coverage focused on the cleaning design rather than establishing a retail schedule.
