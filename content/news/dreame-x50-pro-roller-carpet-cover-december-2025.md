[Dreame](/brands/dreame)'s X50 Pro Roller model was featured in a 31 December 2025 report by New Express. The product uses a retractable cover intended to shield the wet roller when the robot moves onto carpet.

The report also describes a 27 cm roller with continuous clean-water supply and dirty-water recovery. Claims about keeping carpets dry are the manufacturer's description of the mechanism, rather than an independent result for all carpet types.
