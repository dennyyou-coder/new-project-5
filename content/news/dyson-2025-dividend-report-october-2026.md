Bloomberg reported on 2 October 2026 that [Dyson](/brands/dyson) Holdings distributed GBP 750 million to Weybourne Holdings in 2025, citing Singapore registry filings. The distribution compared with GBP 200 million in 2024.

The report concerns the family holding company's dividend receipts. Separately, Dyson's previously released operating results showed 2025 revenue of GBP 6.13 billion and EBITDA of GBP 1.11 billion. Those operating measures should not be confused with the dividend payment.
