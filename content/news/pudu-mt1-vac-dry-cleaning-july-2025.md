[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) launched MT1 Vac on July 15, 2025, adding a dedicated dry-cleaning option to its commercial portfolio.

The robot combined sweeping, vacuuming and dust mopping for sites such as airports, hotels, offices and metro stations. Pudu positioned it between the CC1 family, which emphasized wet cleaning, and the original MT1 sweeper, which targeted larger debris.

The announcement highlighted stronger vacuuming capability for commercial and industrial applications. MT1 Vac is a separate model from the original MT1, so its functions and specifications should not be transferred automatically to the rest of the family.
