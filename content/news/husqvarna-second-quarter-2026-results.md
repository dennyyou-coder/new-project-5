[Husqvarna Group](/brands/husqvarna) published its second-quarter 2026 report on July 17. Net sales were SEK 14,383 million, down 6% year on year, including exchange-rate effects; organic sales declined 4%.

The group described challenging European conditions alongside growth in North America. Operating income was SEK 1,600 million. Excluding items affecting comparability, operating income was SEK 1,950 million, with a 13.6% margin.

Free operating cash flow reached SEK 3,903 million. These are group-wide quarterly figures for garden and construction operations, not separate sales results for robotic mowers alone.
