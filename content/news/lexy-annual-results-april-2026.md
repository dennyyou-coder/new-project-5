LEXY Electric disclosed its 2025 results on 16 April 2026. Revenue increased 1.05% to CNY 9.867 billion, while attributable net profit fell 35.04% to CNY 799.34 million.

The figures cover LEXY's consolidated manufacturing and branded-product businesses. The limited movement in revenue and larger earnings decline are separate measures of performance and should not be read as the sales result of an individual overseas customer.
