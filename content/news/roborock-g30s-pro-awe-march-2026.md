[Roborock](/brands/roborock) presented the G30S Pro at AWE on 12 March 2026. The China-market announcement highlighted a 7.98 cm body, its DuoDivide brush system and an adaptive lifting chassis. The water-tank version was listed at CNY 5,499.

The presentation also promoted RRmindGPT voice interaction. Body height is not a guarantee of access beneath every piece of furniture: clearance depends on the robot's operating configuration and the space it must enter.
