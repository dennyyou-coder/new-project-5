[Roborock](/brands/roborock) presented G30S Pro at AWE on March 12, 2026, highlighting a slimmer body, adaptive chassis and voice interaction for the China market. China Household Electrical Appliances reported a listed price of CNY 5,499 for the water-tank version.

The presentation focused on reaching areas that conventional robot bodies can struggle to enter. The reported 7.98-centimeter body works with a retractable laser-navigation assembly, while the lifting chassis adjusts for thresholds and changes in floor height. Actual access beneath furniture still depends on the robot's operating configuration and the available approach space.

G30S Pro also uses the DuoDivide brush arrangement to manage hair and combines vacuuming with vibrating mopping. The exhibition coverage described extending edge-cleaning components and an automated station for washing and drying the mop, bringing floor treatment and maintenance functions together in the same system.

RRmindGPT voice interaction was another part of the demonstration, with conversational commands intended to make cleaning requests easier to issue. The model appears within [Roborock's China product range](https://cn.roborock.com/). The AWE presentation concerns that domestic configuration and price, rather than establishing identical naming, specifications or bundles for overseas markets where the company sells related but separately marketed products.
