Haier Smart Home's first-half 2026 report recorded revenue of CNY 152.12 billion, down 2.80% year on year. Net profit attributable to shareholders fell 14.27% to CNY 10.32 billion.

Profit excluding non-recurring items was CNY 9.30 billion, down 20.54%, while operating cash flow declined 12.45%. The report covers Haier Smart Home's global consolidated appliance operations, including businesses beyond cleaning and laundry. It does not provide a standalone result for cleaning robots.
