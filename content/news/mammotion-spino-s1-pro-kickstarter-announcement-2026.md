[Mammotion](/brands/mammotion) announced a Kickstarter campaign for SPINO S1 Pro on April 27, 2026, with the campaign scheduled to open on April 28 and run through May 28.

The Pro system includes an AutoShoreCharge dock with an articulating arm intended to lift the robot out of the pool for recharging. A separate SPINO S1 option was announced without that self-docking station.

The release listed limited-quantity early-backer prices, including $1,699 for S1 Pro and $899 for S1. These were crowdfunding reservations, not confirmation of immediate retail shipment, and the quoted campaign terms belong to April 2026.
