[Maytronics](/brands/maytronics) previewed its 2027 range of Dolphin pool products at Piscine Global in Lyon on November 17, 2026. According to its [release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/17/3404299/0/en/maytronics-unveils-its-dolphin-pool-care-line-up-for-2027.html), prices and availability will be announced in early 2027.

The most unusual item is the DualPower Hybrid Kit. It lets compatible Dolphin EON 100 and EON 120d robots run either on their battery or on a cable. Owners can use cordless mode for everyday cleaning and switch to corded mode for long sessions, back-to-back cycles and scheduled cleaning without being limited by battery life. Most of the industry has been moving from corded to cordless robots; Maytronics is offering both on the same machine.

The second-generation EON 100 and EON 120d add a dedicated mode for sun ledges, the shallow platforms common in newer pools, building on the 2026 models, which Maytronics says were the first pool robots to scrub sun ledges in water as shallow as 20 cm. Maytronics says the new models also climb and turn better on walls, waterlines and steps, and that improved battery management extends cleaning cycles to up to three and four hours. The EON 120d keeps the DebrisLock closed filter chamber with a built-in backwash, introduced last year.

The line-up also includes ClearPod, a ceramic cartridge that clips into compatible robots and traps fine particles to make the water look clearer; the solar-powered Skimmi 300 surface skimmer; and a range of automatic pool covers, some battery-powered. Maytronics also introduced a new identity for the Dolphin brand.

Chief executive Rafi Benami said the company was reinforcing "the Dolphin difference" with innovation aimed at real pool-owner needs. Maytronics, founded in 1983, sells through five subsidiaries and more than 100 distributors in over 65 countries, and faces growing competition from Chinese cordless brands such as [Beatbot](/brands/beatbot) and Aiper, both of which also showed new robots at this year's pool shows.
