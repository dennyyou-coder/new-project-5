Fairland submitted an application for a Hong Kong Main Board listing on 17 April 2026, China Securities Journal reported the following day. Its business includes intelligent pool equipment and an expanding lawn-equipment portfolio.

The filing is an application rather than confirmation of listing approval, pricing or a trading date. The company described an integrated yard-equipment strategy spanning energy management, connected devices and water treatment.
