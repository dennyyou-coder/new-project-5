[Beatbot](/brands/beatbot) introduced the [AquaSense X](/products/beatbot-aquasense-x) and AstroRinse Cleaning Station in a January 5, 2026 announcement for CES.

The company described a pool robot combining camera-based vision, infrared and ultrasonic sensing, with cleaning coverage extending to the water surface. Its AstroRinse station was designed to backflush the filter, collect debris and recharge the robot after the user placed it on the station.

That manual placement is an important part of the announced workflow: automatic filter cleaning did not mean the robot could independently climb out of the pool and dock. Beatbot issued a separate [April sales announcement](/news/beatbot-aquasense-x-april-sales-announcement-2026).
