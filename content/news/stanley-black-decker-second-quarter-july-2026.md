Stanley Black & Decker reported second-quarter 2026 revenue of approximately USD 3.96 billion on 29 July. Reported sales were broadly flat, while organic sales increased 3%. Adjusted earnings per share were USD 1.57.

The company raised its full-year adjusted earnings guidance to USD 5.20–5.80 per share. That outlook was management's estimate at the time of the release. The results include power tools, outdoor products and fastening operations, rather than a standalone cleaning-appliance segment.
