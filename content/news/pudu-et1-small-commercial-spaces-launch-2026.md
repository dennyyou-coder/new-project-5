[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) launched ET1 on August 12, 2026, as the first product in its E Series. The company positions the compact scrubber-dryer robot for cleaning areas of 100–800 m², including shops, restaurants, smaller hotels and offices.

ET1 combines sweeping, scrubbing, vacuuming and dust-mopping. Pudu described autonomous mapping, stain recognition and adaptive cleaning, alongside hot-water scrubbing supported by its dock.

The new model extends Pudu's cleaning range toward smaller commercial premises. The announcement does not provide a universal price or confirm installation suitability for every site; deployment still depends on the floor plan and local support arrangement.
