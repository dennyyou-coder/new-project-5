[ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) announced a UK product rollout at IFA on September 4, 2026, naming Amazon, Argos, Currys and Very among its intended sales channels. The [regional release](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/ecovacs-robotics-unveils-next-generation-of-intelligent-cleaning-at-ifa-2026-302869519.html) set separate prices and dates for three DEEBOT floor-cleaning robots and a WINBOT window cleaner.

The [DEEBOT X12S OmniCyclone](/products/ecovacs-deebot-x12s-omnicyclone) was listed at GBP 1,199, with availability from September 9. The [T90 MAX PRO OMNI](/products/ecovacs-deebot-t90-max-pro-omni) received the same date at GBP 549, while the [T90S PRO OMNI](/products/ecovacs-deebot-t90s-pro-omni) was scheduled for September 24 at GBP 699. The lineup shared roller-mopping technology but used different packages and positioning.

For windows, the [WINBOT W2S PRO OMNI](/products/ecovacs-winbot-w2s-pro-omni) was announced at GBP 529 from September 9. ECOVACS described an updated edge-cleaning system, spray arrangement and navigation software. Its operating requirements remain separate from the floor robots, despite the shared emphasis on automating household cleaning.

The company also previewed GOAT lawn-mowing and ULTRAMARINE pool-cleaning products for 2027. Those previews were future portfolio items rather than part of the September retail schedule. The published prices were UK recommended launch prices; the X12S and T90S names should be kept distinct from similarly named earlier regional models.
