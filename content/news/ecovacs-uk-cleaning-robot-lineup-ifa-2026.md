[ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) used its 4 September IFA announcement to outline a new UK retail lineup, naming Amazon, Argos, Currys and Very among its intended sales channels.

The company listed the [DEEBOT X12S OmniCyclone](/products/ecovacs-deebot-x12s-omnicyclone) at a £1,199 recommended price and the [T90 MAX PRO OMNI](/products/ecovacs-deebot-t90-max-pro-omni) at £549, both with announced availability from 9 September. The [T90S PRO OMNI](/products/ecovacs-deebot-t90s-pro-omni) was listed at £699 for 24 September.

For window cleaning, the [WINBOT W2S PRO OMNI](/products/ecovacs-winbot-w2s-pro-omni) was announced at £529 from 9 September. ECOVACS also previewed lawn and pool products for 2027.

These are UK launch prices and dates from the company’s release, rather than current stock checks. The similarly named X12 and X12S, or T90 and T90S, should be treated as separate models when comparing regional offers.
