[LionsBot](/brands/lionsbot) announced a collaboration agreement with German facility-services provider WISAG on April 8, 2026. WISAG contributed experience from commercial cleaning operations to the development of LionsBot's R5 robot.

The companies said practitioner feedback helped refine the machine's usability, reliability and readiness for deployment. LionsBot planned to demonstrate the R5 at Interclean Amsterdam, which ran on April 14–17.

The announcement describes a product-development collaboration and exhibition showcase. It does not disclose an order quantity or claim that the entire WISAG cleaning fleet has been automated.
