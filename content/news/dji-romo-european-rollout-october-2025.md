[DJI](/brands/dji-romo) announced the European rollout of its ROMO robot vacuum series on October 28, 2025, extending its consumer technology business into home floorcare.

The announcement highlighted obstacle sensing adapted from DJI's drone technology, a self-cleaning base station and the ROMO P's transparent robot and dock panels. DJI said European availability would begin in phases through its store and authorized retail partners.

This news records the European rollout announced in October. Regional availability was subject to the local sales channel, and the announcement should not be read as a simultaneous worldwide launch.
