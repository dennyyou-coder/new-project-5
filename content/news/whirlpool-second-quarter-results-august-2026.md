[Whirlpool](/brands/whirlpool) reported second-quarter 2026 net sales of USD 3.517 billion on 3 August, down 6.8% from a year earlier. Organic net sales declined 1.7%. The prior-year reported comparison included its previously owned India business.

Net earnings attributable to common shareholders were USD 75 million, while ongoing EBIT was USD 62 million, equal to a 1.8% margin. The difference between reported and organic revenue reflects business-scope adjustments as well as trading performance.
