[Shark](/brands/shark) introduced Rocket Turbo and Detect + Reveal corded stick vacuums on October 7, 2026. The [announcement](https://newsroom.sharkninja.com/shark-introduces-two-lightweight-stick-vacuums-with-nonstop-corded-power/) adds two plug-in options alongside the brand's cordless and robotic cleaners.

Both weigh under ten pounds, convert to handheld use and include self-cleaning brushrolls. Detect + Reveal adds movement-sensitive suction adjustment and a blue LED beam intended to reveal debris. These are manufacturer-described features; the release does not provide independent comparative testing.

The announced U.S.-dollar prices were $199.99 for Rocket Turbo and $249.99 for Detect + Reveal. For retailers, the launch creates two feature levels within corded stick cleaning, with the higher-priced model adding detection technology. Regional availability and bundles should be checked separately.
