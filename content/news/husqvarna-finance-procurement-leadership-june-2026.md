[Husqvarna Group](/brands/husqvarna) announced management appointments on June 5, 2026. Patrik Johnson was named Chief Financial Officer, with a scheduled September 11 start, succeeding Terry Burke.

The group also appointed Lily Guo as Chief Procurement Officer, a newly created group-management position, with an August 1 start. She was to be based in Hong Kong. Anders Candell was named Chief Information Officer with a planned start in early December.

These are the appointments and timing reported in the June announcement. The company linked the changes to financial performance, procurement and digital transformation across its garden and construction equipment businesses.
