[STIHL](/brands/stihl) announced more than 90 outdoor products and accessories at Equip Expo on October 21, 2026. Its [U.S. release](https://www.stihlusa.com/en/support-events/about-stihl/press/equip-expo-2026) includes the SFA 120, the company’s first snow thrower, and AZ Series gas-powered stand-on zero-turn mowers.

The showcase also features ALLPRO battery technology for professional AP tools, alongside additions to the homeowner AK range. The snow thrower broadens the seasonal use of battery equipment, while the stand-on mowers extend STIHL’s professional landscaping offer.
