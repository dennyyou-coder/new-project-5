[DEWALT](/brands/dewalt) expanded its cordless carpentry range on August 11, 2026, adding cutting, sanding and fastening tools for professional users. The Stanley Black & Decker brand said the products were available through its sales channels at the time of the announcement.

The additions included a 20V MAX XR 6½-inch plunge track saw, five- and six-inch random orbital sanders, an oscillating multi-tool and a 16-gauge straight finish nailer. The lineup covers several stages of a carpentry job, from cutting material to surface preparation and final fixing.

Specifications and package contents vary by item. One track-saw kit included an 8Ah battery, charger and two connecting tracks, while the sanders were listed with batteries and chargers sold separately. The [official announcement](https://dewalt.mediaroom.com/2026-08-11-DEWALT-R-Unleashes-a-Major-Expansion-of-Cordless-Carpentry-Tools-for-the-Toughest-Jobsites) also identified individual product codes, which distinguish bare tools from kit configurations.

The release extends DEWALT's cordless platform within professional carpentry rather than announcing a separate cleaning-appliance range. Its relevance to the wider equipment market is the continued addition of task-specific tools around a common battery platform. For purchasers comparing the new products, the model code and included accessories are more precise identifiers than the shared family name.
