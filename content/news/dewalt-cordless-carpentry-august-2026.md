[DEWALT](/brands/dewalt) announced an expansion of its cordless carpentry range on 11 August 2026. The Stanley Black & Decker brand introduced products aimed at carpenters, remodelers and general contractors.

The announcement includes a 20V MAX XR plunge track saw, random orbital sanders, an oscillating multi-tool and a finish nailer. Packages differ in their inclusion of batteries, chargers and track accessories, so buyers should check the individual product code rather than assume that every announced item is supplied as a complete kit.
