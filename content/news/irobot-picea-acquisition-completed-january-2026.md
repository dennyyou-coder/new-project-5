[iRobot](/brands/irobot) announced on January 23, 2026 that Picea had acquired 100% of its equity, completing the court-supervised transaction [announced in December 2025](/news/irobot-picea-restructuring-agreement-december-2025).

The company became privately held and wholly owned by Picea. iRobot said it would retain its Bedford, Massachusetts headquarters, with engineering, product development, marketing and other corporate functions anchored in the United States.

The announcement also described iRobot Safe, a separate U.S.-based subsidiary responsible for protecting U.S. consumer data under an independent governance structure. Picea had previously served as iRobot's main contract manufacturer and secured lender; the January transaction marked the change in ownership.
