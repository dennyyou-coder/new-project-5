[Midea](/brands/midea)'s first-half 2026 report recorded operating revenue of CNY 260.04 billion, up 3.55%, and attributable net profit of CNY 26.45 billion, up 1.66%. Profit excluding non-recurring items fell 25.31% to CNY 19.60 billion.

The company explained that foreign-exchange losses on monetary items were included in recurring results while gains on derivatives used to manage currency exposure were classified as non-recurring. The two profit measures therefore need to be read together rather than treating the decline as a standalone measure of operating demand.
