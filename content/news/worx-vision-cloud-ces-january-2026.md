Worx announced its Vision Cloud robotic mowing platform on 6 January 2026 at CES. The company highlighted stereo vision and a cloud RTK approach designed to reduce the need for a dedicated positioning antenna at the property.

The announcement described the navigation approach and platform direction. Model availability, local connectivity requirements and installation conditions remain market-specific rather than uniform across every garden or country.
