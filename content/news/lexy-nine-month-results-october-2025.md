LEXY Electric reported first-nine-month 2025 revenue of CNY 7.313 billion on 29 October, up 0.92%. Attributable net profit declined 29.32% to CNY 621.10 million.

For the third quarter alone, attributable profit was CNY 193.41 million, down 29.99%. The company-wide figures describe a different reporting scope from the individual brands and manufacturing customers supplied by LEXY.
