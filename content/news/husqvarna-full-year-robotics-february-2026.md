[Husqvarna](/brands/husqvarna) reported 2025 net sales of SEK 46.613 billion on 4 February 2026, down 4% in reported terms but up 1% organically. Management highlighted the introduction of 13 boundary-wire-free robotic mower models.

Operating income excluding comparability items was SEK 2.901 billion, a 6.2% margin. The company said professional robotic mowers had momentum, while consumer demand remained cautious, particularly in North America.
