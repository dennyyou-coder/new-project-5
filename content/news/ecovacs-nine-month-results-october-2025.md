[ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) reported first-nine-month 2025 revenue of CNY 12.877 billion on 24 October, up 25.93%. Attributable net profit reached CNY 1.418 billion, up 130.55%.

For the third quarter alone, revenue was CNY 4.201 billion and attributable profit was approximately CNY 438 million. The company identified revenue growth, better gross margin, a lower expense ratio and increased investment income among the factors behind the improvement.
