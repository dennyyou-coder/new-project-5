AVC data published on 30 June 2026 showed online cleaning-appliance retail sales of CNY 8 billion during the 618 promotion period, up 5% year on year. Unit sales rose 10% to 4.54 million. The measured window ran from 11 May to 21 June.

Robot vacuums contributed CNY 4.35 billion, up 7.1%, while wet floor cleaners generated CNY 2.71 billion, up 8.2%. Conventional vacuum-cleaner sales declined 19.1%, illustrating different results across cleaning categories.
