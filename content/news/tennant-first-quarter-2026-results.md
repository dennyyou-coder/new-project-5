[Tennant](/brands/tennant) published its first-quarter 2026 results on May 4. Net sales for the quarter ended March 31 were $297.9 million, up 2.7% from a year earlier.

Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, was $29.1 million compared with $41.0 million in the prior-year quarter. Reported net income was $0.2 million. The company said order growth reached 10% and operational execution improved as its ERP recovery progressed.

Tennant reaffirmed its full-year outlook in this May release. That historical guidance should be read alongside subsequent updates, including the company's August second-quarter announcement.
