[iRobot](/brands/irobot) unveiled the Roomba Duo prototype at IFA Berlin on September 4. The concept combines a main robot for vacuuming and steam-assisted floor washing with a smaller companion for confined spaces.

The main unit carries a mechanical arm designed to place the companion robot in areas it cannot reach itself. The two machines use a shared home map and coordinate tasks through the Roomba Home App, alongside an autonomous mobile dock.

iRobot said its IFA demonstration used the Roomba Plus 575 Combo as the companion robot. The main unit incorporates a continuously cleaned roller mop and hot-air floor drying, according to the company's announcement.

Roomba Duo was presented as a prototype, rather than a product with confirmed retail availability. The release does not establish a consumer launch date or selling price for the complete system.
