[Dyson](/brands/dyson) was proposing changes affecting a small number of creative roles, the Gazette and Herald reported on 26 February 2026. The report described a move toward a different creative and agency structure, including work in London.

The account concerned a proposal, not confirmation that all affected staff had left. Dyson said its overall UK headcount was expected to remain unchanged. This update is separate from the larger Malmesbury back-office proposal reported in July.
