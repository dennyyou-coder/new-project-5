Zhejiang Prulde reported first-half 2026 revenue of CNY 595.15 million, up 29.21%, in its interim report summary dated 26 August. Attributable net profit fell 16.97% to CNY 37.91 million.

Profit excluding non-recurring items increased 8.64% to CNY 36.41 million. Operating cash flow remained positive at approximately CNY 51.97 million but declined from the prior-year period. The report covers the power-tool manufacturer's consolidated operations.
