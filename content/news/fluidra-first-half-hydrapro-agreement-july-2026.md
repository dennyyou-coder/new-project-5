[Fluidra](/brands/fluidra) [reported first-half 2026 sales of EUR 1.258 billion](https://www.fluidra.com/press-room/press-release/fluidra-delivers-a-strong-first-half-of-2026-and-maintains-positive-momentum-in-a-dynamic-environment/) on July 30, up 5% at constant exchange rates. Adjusted EBITDA reached EUR 321 million, up 6% on the same currency basis.

The group also signed an agreement to acquire French pool-chemical manufacturer Hydrapro. Its release expected completion in early 2027, subject to closing conditions; this was an agreement announcement, not a completed acquisition.

Fluidra reported 21% first-half sales growth at [Aiper](/brands/aiper), in which it owns a 27% stake. The minority investment should not be described as full ownership.
