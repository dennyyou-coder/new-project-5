[iRobot](/brands/irobot) announced Roomba Electro Plus on July 7, 2026, describing it as the company's first non-robot floor-cleaning product. The cordless cleaner combines vacuuming and mopping with an electrolyzed-water system.

The release also introduced five Roomba robot models to North America, extending the company's navigation, cleaning and dock options across its range. It followed [Picea's completed acquisition of iRobot in January](/news/irobot-picea-acquisition-completed-january-2026).

iRobot said Electro Plus and the expanded robot lineup were available for presale through its website, with selected retailers expected later in July. Any disinfecting-performance claim belongs to the manufacturer's stated test conditions; this announcement does not establish independent testing by WCB.
