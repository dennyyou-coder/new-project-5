[ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) presented DEEBOT X8 PRO OMNI on January 6, 2025, with its OZMO ROLLER mopping system. The design washed the roller during floor cleaning, extending the company's robot-vacuum range beyond its previous mop formats.

Its CES portfolio also included GOAT A3000 LiDAR lawn care and WINBOT W2 PRO OMNI and WINBOT MINI window-cleaning products. The announcement covered several household robot categories with different deployment needs.

ECOVACS set February 18 as the planned launch date for X8 PRO OMNI and T50 MAX PRO, with pricing to follow. The January date records the announcement rather than the subsequent retail release.
