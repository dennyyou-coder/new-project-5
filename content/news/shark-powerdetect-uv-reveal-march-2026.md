[Shark](/brands/shark) announced the PowerDetect UV Reveal robot vacuum and mop on 3 March 2026. The company said its UV-assisted detection system identifies otherwise hard-to-see messes and adjusts cleaning to the areas detected.

The announcement describes a stain-detection feature. UV detection should not be confused with a claim that the robot disinfects floors using ultraviolet light. Cleaning outcomes remain dependent on the surface and type of soil.
