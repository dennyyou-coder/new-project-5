[Maytronics Australia](/brands/maytronics) [announced Chris Papageorgiou’s departure on October 8, 2026](https://www.maytronics.com.au/chris-leaving-maytronics/). The company said he was leaving to focus on personal and family life.

The notice says Dan Kwaczynski will continue leading in his existing role and directs customers to their sales contacts or leadership team for support.

This announcement concerns the Australian operation. It does not announce a replacement group chief executive.
