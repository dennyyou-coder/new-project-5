[Electrolux](/brands/electrolux) and [Midea](/brands/midea) announced a strategic partnership on 23 April 2026 involving three appliance joint ventures. The plans included manufacturing cooperation and a commercial venture, with different ownership arrangements for the separate businesses.

The announcement set out an agreement and proposed operating structure. It did not mean every venture was already operational on that date. The partners subsequently provided a separate update on implementation, making the signing and operating launch distinct news events.
