[Narwal](/brands/narwal) announced the North American launch of [Flow 2](/products/narwal-flow-2) on April 13, 2026, following its CES preview earlier in the year.

The company described a vision-language model for interpreting household objects and spaces, alongside an upgraded FlowWash track-mopping system. Announced features included 31,000 Pa suction, a 7,000 mAh battery and an automated maintenance dock. These are manufacturer specifications.

Preorders were scheduled for April 13–28 on Narwal's website and Amazon. The release listed a $1,499.99 MSRP and a $1,099.99 introductory price for that period; the historical promotion should not be treated as today's selling price.
