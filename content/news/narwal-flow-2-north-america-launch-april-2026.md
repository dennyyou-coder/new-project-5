[Narwal](/brands/narwal) announced North American preorders for [Flow 2](/products/narwal-flow-2) on April 13, 2026, moving the flagship robot beyond its [January CES preview](/news/narwal-flow-2-ces-preview-january-2026). The [launch release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/narwal-launches-flow-2-robot-vacuum-with-vision-language-modelvlm-and-upgraded-flowwash-mopping-system-302740026.html) listed a USD 1,499.99 suggested price and a USD 1,099.99 introductory offer for April 13–28 through Narwal's website and Amazon.

The updated FlowWash system uses a moving track mop with 60°C water, 12 N of pressure and a 16-nozzle rinse arrangement. A scraper removes debris while wastewater is collected internally. The design aims to refresh the cleaning surface during a run, rather than waiting for the robot's return to its station.

Flow 2 combines dual RGB cameras and onboard processing with a cloud-based vision-language model. Narwal described object recognition, pet-related functions and quieter operation around cribs. It also listed maximum suction of 31,000 Pa, a 7,000 mAh battery and a dock handling dust collection and mop maintenance.

The station received a redesigned exterior with a glass surface and status lighting. For the North American launch, the central changes were the hotter track-mopping system, updated recognition and maintenance package. The introductory price records the original preorder promotion and is not a current retail quotation.
