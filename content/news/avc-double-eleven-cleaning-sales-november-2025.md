AVC data reported on 26 November 2025 put online cleaning-appliance retail sales during Double 11 at CNY 9.5 billion, down 17% year on year. Unit sales declined 1% to 5.28 million.

The monitored period covered weeks 41–46. The larger decline in value than volume indicates a lower average sales value across the measured mix; these promotion-period figures are not the same as full-year market performance.
