[Roborock](/brands/roborock) reported first-quarter 2026 revenue of CNY 4.227 billion in its quarterly filing published on 23 April. Revenue rose 23.31% and attributable net profit increased 20.83% to CNY 323.20 million.

Operating cash flow remained negative at approximately CNY 393.75 million. The quarter therefore combined growth in accounting earnings with a cash outflow from operations. These figures are distinct from Roborock's 2025 annual results released in the same reporting season.
