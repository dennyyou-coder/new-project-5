[Kärcher](/brands/karcher) announced 2025 sales of EUR 3.483 billion on 29 January 2026, up 1.1% in reported terms. Adjusted for currency movements, growth was 3.2%.

Business outside Germany accounted for approximately 86% of revenue. The figures cover Kärcher's consumer and professional cleaning portfolio, providing a group-level result rather than the sales performance of a single machine category.
