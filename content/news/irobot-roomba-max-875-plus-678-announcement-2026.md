[iRobot](/brands/irobot) announced Roomba Max 875 Combo and Plus 678 Combo on September 2, 2026, each paired with an AutoWash dock.

Both use SealForce technology, which the company describes as a mechanical skirt intended to concentrate airflow when cleaning carpet. Max 875 adds ThermaMist heated-mist roller mopping and a carpet-protection roller shield. Plus 678 uses a different SprayForce AI pretreatment and roller-mopping system.

These are model-specific features from the September launch release. The robots are separate from the Roomba Duo prototype shown later at IFA; the prototype's two-robot architecture should not be attributed to the 875 or 678.
