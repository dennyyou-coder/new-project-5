[Midea](/brands/midea) reported third-quarter 2026 operating revenue of CNY 107.32 billion on October 30, down 4.12% from a year earlier. Net profit attributable to shareholders rose 25.93% to CNY 14.95 billion, while profit excluding non-recurring items fell 15.70% to CNY 9.19 billion.

For January to September, revenue rose 1.19% to CNY 367.36 billion and attributable net profit rose 9.27% to CNY 41.39 billion. Profit excluding non-recurring items fell 22.49% to CNY 28.79 billion, and operating cash flow fell 29.58% to CNY 40.19 billion.

The [quarterly report](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-10-30/1225645132.PDF) gives the same explanation as in the [first half](/news/midea-first-half-results-august-2026). As the renminbi kept rising, exchange losses on foreign-currency balances were counted in recurring profit, while gains on the derivatives used to manage that risk were counted as non-recurring. Non-recurring items added CNY 5.75 billion to third-quarter profit, including CNY 7.67 billion of fair-value and disposal gains on financial assets and derivatives before tax. The report also records higher goodwill impairment losses for the nine months.

Midea also disclosed nine-month revenue for three businesses outside home appliances: building technology at CNY 30.9 billion, up 10%; robotics and automation at CNY 24.9 billion, up 10%; and industrial technology at CNY 18.3 billion, down 13%. The report does not give a separate figure for cleaning appliances.
