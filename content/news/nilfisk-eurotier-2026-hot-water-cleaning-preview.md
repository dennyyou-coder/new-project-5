[Nilfisk](/brands/nilfisk) announced its EuroTier 2026 exhibit on September 29, with the MH45M-200/960 PAX hot-water pressure washer as a central product. The agricultural trade show takes place in Hannover on November 10–13; Nilfisk lists Hall 13, Stand F26.

The company specifies a working pressure of 200 bar and a water flow of 960 litres per hour for the machine. It describes a biofuel-compatible heat exchanger, multilingual digital controls and a steam setting reaching up to 150°C. These are manufacturer specifications for the named equipment.

The planned display also includes other hot-water and cold-water pressure washers, battery-powered vacuums, and equipment for wet and dry floor cleaning in livestock and agricultural facilities.

This is an advance exhibition announcement. The November show has not yet taken place, and the announcement does not provide a universal sales price or worldwide delivery schedule.
