[Nilfisk](/brands/nilfisk) previewed its agricultural cleaning display for EuroTier 2026 on September 29, led by the MH45M-200/960 PAX hot-water pressure washer. The [exhibition announcement](https://www.nilfisk.com/de-de/professional/neuigkeiten-und-fachartikel/eurotier-2026/) lists Hall 13, Stand F26 at the Hannover event, scheduled for November 10–13.

The company specifies working pressure of 200 bar and water flow of 960 litres per hour for the featured machine. It combines a biofuel-compatible heat exchanger with multilingual digital controls, stored cleaning modes and protected settings. Nilfisk lists water temperatures up to 90°C and a separate steam setting up to 150°C.

The broader stand is planned to include the MH50M-220/1100 PAX for higher-volume work, the MC2C-150/650 XT cold-water pressure washer and a lithium-ion version of the SW3000 ride-on sweeper. A VU200 cordless vacuum is intended for smaller jobs such as offices, staff areas and vehicle cabins.

Together, the display covers several different agricultural tasks, from washing equipment and livestock facilities to dry floor cleaning. The announcement is an advance preview: EuroTier had not yet taken place at publication. Its specifications describe the named machines and planned exhibition range, with purchasing configurations and local availability to be established through the relevant sales channel.
