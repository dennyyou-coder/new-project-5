[iRobot](/brands/irobot) introduced Roomba Mini to Europe and the United Kingdom on 10 March 2026. The Robot Vacuum & Mop package with AutoEmpty Dock was announced at EUR 399 and GBP 379 through iRobot's online channels.

The company describes it as its smallest Roomba and said it had already launched in Japan. The March announcement establishes European availability, rather than the first release of the product anywhere in the world.
