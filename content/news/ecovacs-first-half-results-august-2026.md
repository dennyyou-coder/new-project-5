[ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs) reported first-half 2026 revenue of CNY 10.341 billion, up 19.18%, in results released on 21 August. Net profit attributable to shareholders increased 27.40% to CNY 1.248 billion.

Profit excluding non-recurring items fell 6.37% to CNY 805 million. A subsequent securities-research summary cited component-cost and currency pressures alongside overseas growth. The different profit measures matter: the rise in reported net profit did not also occur in the measure excluding non-recurring gains and losses.
