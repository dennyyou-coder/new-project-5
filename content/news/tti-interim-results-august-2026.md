Techtronic Industries reported first-half 2026 sales of approximately USD 8.3 billion, up 5.9% on a reported basis. Its interim report records a 9.9% EBIT margin and growth in its main cordless-tool businesses.

[Milwaukee](/brands/milwaukee)'s underlying local-currency growth was 10.5%. Noncore businesses, representing 6.6% of group revenue, declined 19.4% in local currencies, reflecting the HART exit and softer consumer operations. That noncore measure includes several activities and should not be described as a floorcare-only sales decline.
