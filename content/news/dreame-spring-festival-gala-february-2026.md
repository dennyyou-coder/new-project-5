[Dreame](/brands/dreame) featured its smart-home ecosystem during the 2026 CCTV Spring Festival Gala, Nantong's government news service reported on 24 February. The coverage described product appearances across several performance formats.

The event provided a large broadcast platform for the company's expanding appliance range. The source does not quantify sales generated by the appearance, so the broadcast should be treated as a marketing event rather than evidence of commercial conversion.
