[Mammotion](/brands/mammotion) announced its 2026 robotic mower range at CES on January 5, led by the LUBA 3 AWD family. The [launch release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mammotion-takes-stage-at-ces-2026-with-its-game-changing-robotic-lawn-mowing-lineup-ushering-in-the-next-era-of-autonomous-lawn-mastery-302652397.html) combined an updated sensing system with the company's all-wheel-drive platform and additions to its compact mower range.

LUBA 3 AWD uses Mammotion's Tri-Fusion approach, bringing together 360-degree LiDAR, dual-camera AI vision and RTK positioning correction. The company assigns different roles to those inputs: mapping the surroundings, recognizing objects and correcting position. NetRTK connectivity is available only on selected models, so it should not be assumed across the entire family.

The drive system uses four independently powered wheels. Mammotion lists a maximum slope capability of 80%, equivalent to approximately 38.6 degrees, as a manufacturer specification. This describes the platform's stated terrain capability rather than a recommendation to use every model on any steep site.

Mammotion said the range was intended to serve different lawn sizes and layouts, with the exact navigation and cutting equipment matched to each model. WCB's [LUBA 3 AWD 1500 profile](/products/mammotion-luba-3-awd-1500) covers one family member. Capacity, connectivity and regional configuration remain important when moving from the CES range announcement to a specific mower purchase.
