[Mammotion](/brands/mammotion) announced its 2026 robotic mower range on January 5, led by the LUBA 3 AWD series.

The company said the flagship used an updated navigation system combining 360-degree LiDAR, AI vision and RTK correction with an all-wheel-drive platform. The release specified that NetRTK connectivity was available on selected LUBA 3 AWD models, making variant and regional configuration relevant when comparing products.

Mammotion also announced additions to its compact mower range. WCB's [LUBA 3 AWD 1500 profile](/products/mammotion-luba-3-awd-1500) covers one specific member of the family; capacities and equipment should be checked for the exact model under consideration.
