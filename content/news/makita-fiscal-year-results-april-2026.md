[Makita](/brands/makita) released results for the year ended 31 March 2026 on 28 April. Revenue increased 3.2% to JPY 777.6 billion, while operating profit declined 2.2% to JPY 104.705 billion.

Profit attributable to owners was JPY 79.414 billion, broadly unchanged with a 0.1% increase. The results cover a March-ending fiscal year and the company's wider tool and outdoor-equipment operations.
