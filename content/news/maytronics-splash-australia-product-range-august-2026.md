[Maytronics Australia](/brands/maytronics) outlined its SPLASH! 2026 introductions in an [August 26 recap](https://www.maytronics.com.au/maytronics-unveils-biggest-product-launch-splash-2026/). The lineup included Dolphin EON cordless robots and a new corded Dolphin S-Line.

The company also displayed Skimmi surface skimmers, Wave commercial robots and regional water-care products. Together, the products cover pool-floor cleaning, floating debris and commercial applications.

The recap describes the Australian showcase. It does not establish identical launch dates or dealer availability for every model worldwide.
