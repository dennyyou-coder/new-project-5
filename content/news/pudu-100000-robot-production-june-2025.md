[Pudu Robotics](/brands/pudu-robotics) reported completion of its 100,000th robot on June 10, 2025, at its Jianhu manufacturing site in Jiangsu, China. The milestone machine was a [CC1 Pro cleaning robot](/news/pudu-cc1-pro-launch-may-2025).

The company said the factory had begun operations in August 2024 and was designed for annual capacity of 100,000 units. It also reported that overseas markets generated more than 80% of company revenue at the time.

The cumulative milestone covers Pudu's service-robot business across categories. It is not a claim that 100,000 cleaning robots were sold, nor that the factory had already produced its full annual design capacity.
