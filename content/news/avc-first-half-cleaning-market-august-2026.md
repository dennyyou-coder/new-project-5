AVC's half-year market review put China's cleaning-appliance retail sales at CNY 22.7 billion for January–June 2026, according to a report published on 3 August. The wider home-appliance market reached CNY 425 billion, down 9.9%.

Cleaning appliances were one of several categories tracked in the review. The retail-market figures describe purchases in China and should be distinguished from manufacturers' worldwide revenue and export shipments.
