[Segway Navimow](/brands/segway-navimow) announced five robotic mower ranges at CES on January 6, 2026: X4, i2 AWD, i2 LiDAR, H2 and Terranox. The expanded portfolio stretches from smaller residential lawns to commercial grounds, with different navigation and drive systems across the lineup.

The [company's CES release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/segway-navimow-expands-robotic-mower-lineup-with-smarter-more-effortless-lawn-care-solutions-debuting-at-ces-2026-302653790.html) positioned X4 for larger home properties. Its named models included [X430](/products/segway-navimow-x430) and X450, with announced U.S. prices of USD 2,499 and USD 2,999 respectively. The smaller i2 families separate an all-wheel-drive approach from a LiDAR-led navigation option, while H2 combines multiple positioning technologies for more complex gardens.

Navimow also announced a relationship with RoboSense to incorporate solid-state LiDAR into selected products. That sensor arrangement applies to the relevant models; it is not a specification shared by every mower introduced at the show.

Terranox extends the range into professional maintenance, supported by software for monitoring and managing multiple machines. The two announced commercial models address different area capacities. The CES program therefore expanded both the product ladder and its intended users, giving dealers distinct residential and commercial offers. Availability and package details remained tied to individual models and markets rather than one universal release configuration.
