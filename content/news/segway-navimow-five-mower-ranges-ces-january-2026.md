[Segway Navimow](/brands/segway-navimow) announced five robotic mower ranges at CES on January 6, 2026: X4, i2 AWD, i2 LiDAR, H2 and Terranox.

The release positioned X4 for larger residential properties and Terranox for commercial applications. It also announced a partnership with RoboSense to integrate solid-state LiDAR into selected models.

Within X4, Navimow named the [X430](/products/segway-navimow-x430) and X450, quoting U.S. prices of $2,499 and $2,999 respectively at the time. Navigation systems, drive layouts and area capacities varied across the five ranges; the shared launch did not imply identical hardware or capabilities.
