[Roborock](/brands/roborock) announced its Saros flagship range on January 5, 2025, ahead of CES. The Saros Z70 featured OmniGrip, a folding five-axis arm intended to move selected small objects so the robot could clean underneath.

The initial supported items included socks, small towels, tissues and sandals weighing less than 300 grams. The company expected Z70 sales in the first half of 2025; this announcement was not a confirmation of immediate worldwide availability.

The same release introduced Saros 10 with retractable laser navigation and Saros 10R with a tower-free navigation system, plus the F25 wet-and-dry floor-cleaner family.
