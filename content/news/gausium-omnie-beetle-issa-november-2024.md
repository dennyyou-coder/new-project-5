[Gausium](/brands/gausium) presented Beetle, Omnie and an updated Phantas at ISSA Show North America on November 19, 2024.

Beetle targeted industrial sweeping, including larger debris and indoor or outdoor work. Omnie introduced AI-based detection of dry and wet dirt, a pre-sweeping function and camera coverage intended to support remote service. These features were described by the manufacturer at the exhibition.

Phantas gained compatibility with a workstation for charging, clean-water supply and wastewater removal, with a mobile water tank option. The announcement marked an expansion across both cleaning tasks and automatic servicing, without setting one delivery date for all markets.
