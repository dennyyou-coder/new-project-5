[Gausium](/brands/gausium) introduced Beetle, Omnie and an updated Phantas at ISSA Show North America on November 19, 2024. Its [exhibition announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/gausium-sets-new-trends-in-cleaning-technology-with-next-generation-autonomous-solutions-at-issa-show-north-america-2024-302309939.html) combined new cleaning machines with servicing equipment and a detergent format for commercial operators.

Beetle was positioned as an industrial sweeper for indoor and outdoor work, including collection of larger debris. Omnie focused on detecting and cleaning both dry and wet dirt, using an updated spot-cleaning system. Its pre-sweeping function was intended to reduce preparation before scrubbing, while broad camera coverage supported monitoring and remote service.

The Phantas update added compatibility with a workstation for charging, clean-water refilling and wastewater drainage. A mobile water tank could work with the station where a more flexible water arrangement was needed. That development addressed the support equipment around the robot, rather than only its cleaning mechanism.

Gausium also presented Leaves, a concentrated detergent-paper product intended to make chemical dosing easier in its machines. Taken together, the lineup covered industrial sweeping, mixed-soil cleaning and automatic servicing between runs. The November release described the products at the trade show but did not establish one delivery date for all markets or all configurations, leaving commercial arrangements to individual regional channels.
