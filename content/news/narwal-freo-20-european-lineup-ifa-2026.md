[Narwal](/brands/narwal) expanded its track-mopping technology into the Freo range at IFA on September 3, 2026. The [autumn lineup announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/narwal-expands-ai-powered-cleaning-across-the-home-as-ifa-2026-returns-to-berlin-302868793.html) introduced [Freo 20](/products/narwal-freo-20) and [Freo 20 Edge](/products/narwal-freo-20-edge), alongside [Flow 2](/products/narwal-flow-2) and the [V50 Cube](/products/narwal-v50-cube) cordless vacuum.

Both Freo products adopt FlowWash, which refreshes the moving mop with hot water during cleaning. Their navigation equipment differs. Freo 20 uses dual RGB cameras and NarMind Pro recognition, while Freo 20 Edge combines hidden dToF LiDAR with structured light for a simpler configuration without the same AI clutter-recognition system.

The V50 Cube brings a compact automatic-emptying station to a manually operated cordless vacuum. Narwal also previewed wet-and-dry vacuum products planned for Europe in 2027. Those additions show the company's move into several cleaning formats, rather than only expanding the number of robot models.

The release described a European rollout beginning in September and separately listed U.S. availability for Freo 20 Edge from September 7. U.S. pricing and timing for V50 Cube and the wet-and-dry range were left for later announcements. The IFA display therefore combined products entering channels with previews for future markets and seasons.
