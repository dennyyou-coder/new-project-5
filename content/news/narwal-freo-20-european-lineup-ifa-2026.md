[Narwal](/brands/narwal) announced its autumn cleaning lineup at IFA on September 3, extending its FlowWash track-mopping system from the Flow family to [Freo 20](/products/narwal-freo-20) and [Freo 20 Edge](/products/narwal-freo-20-edge).

The company describes Freo 20 as a model with dual RGB cameras and AI obstacle recognition. Freo 20 Edge uses a different navigation configuration, including hidden dToF LiDAR and structured light. Both were included in the announced European rollout starting in September.

The display also featured [Flow 2](/products/narwal-flow-2) and the [V50 Cube](/products/narwal-v50-cube) cordless vacuum with a compact auto-empty dock. Narwal previewed a separate wet-and-dry vacuum range planned for Europe in 2027.

For the United States, the September release listed Freo 20 Edge availability from September 7. It left U.S. pricing and launch timing for V50 Cube and the wet-and-dry range to a later announcement. European and U.S. schedules should therefore be checked separately.
