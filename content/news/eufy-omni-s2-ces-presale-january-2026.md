Anker Innovations announced the [eufy Omni S2](/products/eufy-omni-s2) in its [CES release](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/anker-innovations-showcases-iconic-lineup-of-charging-smart-home-and-entertainment-devices-during-ces-2026-302653788.html) on January 6, 2026, opening presales that day. The company scheduled the full retail launch for January 20 and listed a U.S. launch price of USD 1,599.99 through eufy and Amazon.

The [eufy](/brands/eufy) robot combines HydroJet mopping with a suction system rated by the manufacturer at 30 kPa. Its cleaning arrangement brings dry-debris collection and roller-based washing into the same machine. The pressure figure is a product specification, rather than a direct measure of how every type of dirt will be removed from a floor.

Anker described CleanMind AI controls that adapt cleaning mode, suction, scrubbing force and wheel height according to floor types and room layouts. That approach treats navigation and cleaning settings as connected decisions, rather than asking the robot to use an identical routine throughout a home.

Omni S2 appeared within a wider Anker group presentation covering charging, entertainment and smart-home products. Its January presale and retail dates applied to the named launch programme, while other markets received their own introductions. The quoted U.S. price records that announcement and should be distinguished from later promotions or regional packages.
