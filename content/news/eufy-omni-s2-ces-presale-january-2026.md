Anker Innovations announced the [eufy Omni S2](/products/eufy-omni-s2) in its January 6, 2026 CES release, with presales beginning that day and a full retail launch scheduled for January 20.

The [eufy](/brands/eufy) robot combined HydroJet mopping with a manufacturer-rated 30 kPa suction system. Anker described CleanMind AI controls that adjust cleaning mode, suction, scrubbing force and wheel height based on floor types and room layouts.

The release quoted a U.S. launch price of $1,599.99 through eufy and Amazon. That figure records the announced January launch price, rather than a current offer or a price applicable to every market.
