EGO's AURA-R2 robotic mower range was reported on 25 February 2026 ahead of March availability. The lineup covers gardens up to 1,500, 3,000 and 6,000 square metres, with UK starting prices of GBP 1,799.

EGO identifies PATH IQ navigation and app-based zone scheduling as core features. Wire-free refers to the absence of a perimeter boundary wire; it does not mean that every installation is free of positioning hardware.
