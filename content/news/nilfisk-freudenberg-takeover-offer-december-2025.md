[Nilfisk](/brands/nilfisk) announced on December 11, 2025 that its board unanimously supported Freudenberg's planned all-cash takeover offer following a strategic review and competitive sales process.

The proposed price was DKK 140 per share, valuing the outstanding equity at approximately DKK 3.798 billion. Nilfisk said this represented a 35.9% premium to the previous day's closing share price. Completion remained subject to the offer's conditions and required approvals.

The December announcement began the public transaction timeline. WCB separately covers [completion of the takeover offer in April 2026](/news/nilfisk-freudenberg-takeover-completion-2026) and [Nilfisk's May delisting](/news/nilfisk-nasdaq-copenhagen-delisting-may-2026).
