Bain Capital announced an investment in Positec Group on April 10, 2026, in a release datelined Hong Kong. Positec, founded in 1994 and based in Suzhou, owns the [Worx](/brands/worx) and Kress brands of power tools, outdoor power equipment and robotic mowers.

Neither side disclosed the size of the stake, the amount invested or which Bain Capital fund made the investment. The release also gave no closing conditions. Positec described Bain as a strategic partner rather than a buyer, and founder Don Gao remains president.

According to the release, the money will support Positec's international expansion, product development and growth in new categories, as well as its shift toward electrification and robotics. It is also meant to strengthen distribution, brand building and operations, and to give the company more financial flexibility. Positec employs more than 4,000 people and sells in nearly 70 countries and regions, with North America and Europe as its main markets.

Michael Hui, a partner at Bain Capital, credited Positec's three decades of brand building and product development. Gao said the backing would speed up work in robotics and electrification and widen the company's global footprint.

The deal brings a large private-equity investor into one of the main Chinese-owned groups in cordless outdoor equipment. Worx's Landroid and Kress's commercial mowers compete in the wire-free robotic mower market, where Chinese makers face a European Union anti-dumping investigation and, in the United States, a Federal Communications Commission approval requirement for new foreign-made robots. Positec is privately held and does not publish revenue figures.
