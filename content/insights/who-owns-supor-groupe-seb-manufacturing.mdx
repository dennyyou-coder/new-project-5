**SUPOR is ultimately controlled by French appliance group Groupe SEB.** More precisely, SUPOR's operating platform is Shenzhen-listed **Zhejiang Supor Co., Ltd.** Its 2025 annual report identifies **SEB INTERNATIONALE S.A.S.** as the direct parent and **SEB S.A.**, the listed head of Groupe SEB, as the ultimate parent.

This structure matters because SUPOR is not merely a label placed on generic Groupe SEB products. It has its own listed company, subsidiaries, manufacturing network, procurement, sales channels and brand identity.

## Is SUPOR Owned by Groupe SEB?

Yes. Groupe SEB's official history records acquiring a majority stake in SUPOR in 2007. Zhejiang Supor's 2025 reporting identifies SEB INTERNATIONALE S.A.S. as its parent company and SEB S.A. as its ultimate parent.

The legal chain is therefore:

**SUPOR consumer brand → Zhejiang Supor operating business → SEB INTERNATIONALE direct parent → SEB S.A. ultimate parent.**

This is more precise than saying only that “a French company owns SUPOR.” The listed Zhejiang company continues to operate procurement, production and sales through its own corporate structure and subsidiaries.

For the parent-level portfolio, manufacturing and governance view, see [Who Owns Groupe SEB? Brands, SUPOR, Manufacturing and Family Control Explained](/blog/who-owns-groupe-seb-brands-supor).

## What Is Zhejiang Supor Co., Ltd.?

Zhejiang Supor is the listed Chinese company closely associated with the SUPOR brand. Official company history traces the business to 1994 and records its Shenzhen listing in 2004.

The company is not identical to the SUPOR trademark in every jurisdiction, nor is it necessarily the contracting entity for every overseas sale. Subsidiaries, factories, exporters, regional Groupe SEB companies, distributors, importers and retailers can each hold a specific role.

When reviewing a product or supply agreement, use the exact legal name and registration number rather than writing “SUPOR” as the counterparty.

## What Products Does SUPOR Make?

SUPOR's portfolio is broader than cookware. Official materials include:

- pressure cookers, woks, frying pans, steamers and kitchen tools;
- rice cookers, electric pressure cookers, induction cookers and kettles;
- blenders, air fryers and other small kitchen appliances;
- vacuum cleaners, air-treatment products and garment steamers; and
- selected large kitchen appliances.

The brand is strongest in China in cookware and small domestic appliances. Its cleaning and home-environment products are relevant to World Clean Biz readers, but a category appearing in company materials does not prove that every model is current in every export market.

## Who Makes SUPOR Products?

Zhejiang Supor's 2025 annual reporting describes the company and its subsidiaries as operating procurement, production and sales systems. The official foreign-trade page identifies R&D and manufacturing locations in Hangzhou, Wuhan, Shaoxing, Yuhuan and Vietnam, with category specialization across the network.

This supports a substantial internal industrial base. However, the foreign-trade page includes operating figures tied to an earlier period, and neither it nor the annual report provides a current public model-to-factory table for every product.

![SUPOR model, factory, exporter and warranty verification flow](/images/blog/supor-product-supply-verification-map.svg)

For a specific SUPOR SKU, verify:

1. the full model number and market version;
2. the legal manufacturer named on the label and declaration;
3. the current production site and country of origin;
4. the exporter and destination-market importer;
5. food-contact, electrical and safety compliance; and
6. the authorized seller, parts route and warranty provider.

## Are SUPOR Products Made in China?

Many SUPOR products are associated with the company's Chinese manufacturing network, but the accurate answer is model-specific. SUPOR also reports a Vietnam site, and production allocation can vary by category, destination and year.

“Chinese brand,” “Chinese listed company” and “made in China” answer different questions. The origin declaration, factory record and shipping documents are the evidence for an exact product.

## Is SUPOR the Same as Groupe SEB?

No. SUPOR is a consumer brand and Zhejiang Supor is a distinct listed operating company within Groupe SEB. Groupe SEB is the wider French-headed corporate group that also includes brands such as Tefal, Rowenta, Moulinex, Krups and WMF.

Portfolio membership can create shared technology, procurement, distribution or management resources. It does not mean a Rowenta factory makes every SUPOR vacuum, that every SUPOR product is sold by a French entity, or that warranties transfer across brands and countries.

## Does Groupe SEB Own All of Zhejiang Supor?

Official reporting establishes parent and ultimate-parent control, but Zhejiang Supor remains a separately listed company with public reporting and minority shareholders. “Controlled by Groupe SEB” is therefore more accurate than describing it as an unlisted brand division.

Ownership percentages can change and should be checked in the latest annual report or exchange filing when a transaction depends on the exact figure. The enduring practical point is the controlling relationship, not a rounded percentage copied from an older source.

## How Is SUPOR Distributed Internationally?

SUPOR's official materials describe international distribution through Groupe SEB's network and publish regional foreign-trade contacts. That does not automatically prove that a proposed seller is authorized or that a product is registered for the destination market.

International buyers should confirm the exporter, importer of record, trademark authorization, destination compliance, local voltage and service route. A product purchased from a China channel may not carry the same warranty or certification as an officially distributed overseas model.

## What About SUPOR Vacuum Cleaners and Floor-Care Products?

SUPOR's broader home-environment portfolio includes vacuum cleaners and related appliances. In China, the brand's scale and retail reach can support category entry, but ownership alone says little about the platform, motor, battery, filtration, factory or service network of a particular floor-care product.

Cleaning-industry buyers should request the exact bill of materials, battery-cell documentation, filter and consumable plan, product certification, factory audit and warranty parts forecast. Kitchen-appliance brand strength should not substitute for floor-care product diligence.

## How Does the SUPOR Warranty Work?

SUPOR publishes China-facing customer-service and after-sales routes. Overseas arrangements can involve a Groupe SEB company, distributor, importer or retailer and may differ by product category.

Before purchase, identify the entity named in the written warranty, the territory covered, proof-of-purchase requirements, commercial-use exclusions, service locations and responsibility for freight. Cross-border purchases are especially likely to fall outside local coverage.

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

1. Separate the SUPOR brand from Zhejiang Supor and Groupe SEB parent entities.
2. Confirm the counterparty and its authority for the target territory.
3. Lock the exact model, specifications and intended sales channel.
4. Obtain current factory, origin and compliance documents.
5. Validate food-contact or electrical requirements by category.
6. Confirm exporter, importer and product-registration responsibilities.
7. Document local parts, repair and warranty obligations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is the parent company of SUPOR?

Zhejiang Supor's official reporting identifies SEB INTERNATIONALE S.A.S. as its direct parent and SEB S.A. as its ultimate parent. Both sit within Groupe SEB's structure.

### Is SUPOR a Chinese or French brand?

SUPOR is a Chinese-origin consumer brand operated through a Chinese listed company, while the company is ultimately controlled by French-headed Groupe SEB. Brand origin and current corporate control can both be true.

### Is SUPOR publicly traded?

Zhejiang Supor Co., Ltd. is listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange. The SUPOR consumer brand itself is not a separately traded security.

### Does Groupe SEB manufacture SUPOR products?

Zhejiang Supor and its subsidiaries report their own production network within the wider Groupe SEB structure. The exact legal manufacturer and factory must be checked for each product.

### Does SUPOR make vacuum cleaners?

Official SUPOR materials include vacuum cleaners within its broader home-environment portfolio. Availability, manufacturer, specifications and support vary by market and model.

## Final Answer

SUPOR is ultimately controlled by Groupe SEB through a structure in which SEB INTERNATIONALE is the direct parent of listed Zhejiang Supor and SEB S.A. is the ultimate parent. Zhejiang Supor remains a distinct listed Chinese operating company with its own subsidiaries, production and sales systems.

For buyers, corporate ownership is only the first layer. Verify the exact model's legal manufacturer, factory, origin, exporter, importer, seller and warranty before making a sourcing or distribution decision.
