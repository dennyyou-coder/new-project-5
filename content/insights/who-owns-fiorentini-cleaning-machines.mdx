**Fiorentini is owned by Ing. O. Fiorentini S.p.A., the company founded by Osvaldo Fiorentini.** Fiorentini does not publish its current shareholders or name a parent company.

Fiorentini is Italian. Its history goes back to 1961, when it distributed cleaning equipment, and it has made industrial cleaning machines since 1978. It is based in Piancaldoli, Firenzuola, near Florence.

## Where are Fiorentini machines made?

In Italy. Fiorentini says design, production and assembly stay in Italy, at its Piancaldoli and Castel San Pietro plants. Castel San Pietro handles processing, assembly and final checks.

## What does Fiorentini make?

Scrubber dryers, including the compact ICM 38 ride-on first shown in 1980; sweepers and urban cleaning machines; vacuums, pressure washers and textile cleaners; autonomous and specialist equipment.

## Where does Fiorentini sell?

Besides Italy, Fiorentini has operations or local structures in the Middle East, the UAE and Morocco. It offers purchase or rental and after-sales support from its own technicians.
