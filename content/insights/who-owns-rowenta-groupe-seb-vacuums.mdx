**Rowenta is owned by Groupe SEB, the French household-equipment group headed by listed SEB S.A.** Groupe SEB acquired the German company Rowenta and its eight subsidiaries in 1988. Rowenta continues as a distinct consumer brand focused on floorcare, linen care, home comfort and selected personal-care products.

Ownership does not make SEB S.A. the legal manufacturer or warranty provider for every Rowenta product. Rowenta France S.A.S., other Groupe SEB companies, distributors and retailers can hold different roles depending on the model and country.

## Is Rowenta a German or French Company?

Rowenta began with Weintraud GmbH, founded by Robert Weintraud in Offenbach, Germany, in 1884. The Rowenta name was adopted in 1909. That history makes Rowenta a German-origin brand.

Today, however, Rowenta sits within France-based Groupe SEB. The most accurate description is therefore **a German-origin brand owned by a French listed appliance group**. Neither statement tells a buyer where a current vacuum cleaner or iron was made.

## Which Company Owns the Rowenta Trademark?

Official Groupe SEB sources establish the group relationship, but public product and legal documents show several operating layers. Groupe SEB's listed parent is SEB S.A. Rowenta France S.A.S. appears in the group's reporting perimeter and on selected declarations. SEB Développement publishes the global Rowenta website, while regional companies or distributors can handle sales and service.

The reviewed official sources do not identify one trademark-holding entity for every jurisdiction. That is why a distributor needing chain-of-title evidence should check the relevant trademark registry and authorization documents, not rely only on the Rowenta logo or group website.

## What Products Does Rowenta Sell?

Rowenta's current offering varies by market. Official catalogues commonly include:

- bagged and bagless canister vacuums;
- cordless stick and handheld vacuums;
- wet-and-dry floor cleaners and steam cleaners;
- robot vacuums in selected markets;
- irons and garment-care products; and
- fans, air treatment and selected personal-care appliances.

Market variation matters. A model sold on a European Rowenta site may not have the same suffix, plug, accessories, software or warranty as a similar-looking model offered elsewhere.

## Who Makes Rowenta Vacuum Cleaners?

There is no reliable one-factory answer for the entire brand. A December 2024 EU declaration for specified X-Plorer S70 and S70+ robot-vacuum references names Rowenta France S.A.S. – Groupe SEB and says the products are sold by Groupe SEB subsidiaries. That is strong evidence for those codes, not for every Rowenta vacuum.

Groupe SEB has also published a historical account of its home-cleaning organization. It described the Vernon site in France as a floorcare expertise and high-end vacuum hub, alongside production in China and selected outsourcing. Because that account reflects the mid-2010s, it should not be used as a current model-to-factory map.

1. the complete commercial reference and regional suffix;
2. the declaration of conformity and legal manufacturer;
3. the product label and country of origin;

5. the destination-market importer or responsible operator; and
6. the authorized seller, parts route and warranty terms.

## Are Rowenta Vacuums Made in France?

Some Rowenta and Groupe SEB floorcare production has been documented in France, but that does not mean every Rowenta vacuum is French-made. Groupe SEB uses an international industrial and sourcing network. Model allocation can differ between canister, cordless, robot and wet-and-dry platforms.

Claims such as “Made in France” should be supported by the exact product label and current documents. A French company address on a declaration identifies a responsible entity, not necessarily the assembly location.

## Are Rowenta and Tefal the Same Company?

They are related brands within Groupe SEB, but they are not the same brand. They may share corporate resources, technology or channels while retaining separate catalogues and market positioning.

Shared ownership also does not prove battery, filter, brush, charger or spare-part compatibility. Compatibility should be confirmed using the exact official part number.

## How Do Rowenta Warranty and Repair Work?

Rowenta uses regional websites, authorized repairers and local warranty terms. Its global warranty information directs consumers either to authorized service or the original point of sale, with conditions varying by product range and country.

Groupe SEB promotes long-term repairability for many electrical appliances, including Rowenta products. Buyers should treat that as a group support policy with defined scope—not as a universal promise for every product, country or commercial-use case.

Parallel imports deserve particular attention. A seller can offer a genuine Rowenta product whose voltage, model suffix, included accessories or warranty route does not match the destination market.

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

1. Separate Groupe SEB ownership from Rowenta's operating entities.
2. Confirm the exact regional model and official-market listing.
3. Match the model to its declaration, factory and origin.
4. Validate seller and distributor authorization.
5. Check filters, brushes, batteries and consumable availability.
6. Confirm whether commercial use is covered.
7. Retain the local invoice and written warranty route.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is Rowenta owned by?

Rowenta is owned by Groupe SEB, whose listed parent is SEB S.A. Groupe SEB acquired Rowenta in 1988.

### Is Rowenta a German brand?

Yes, by origin. Rowenta traces its roots to Offenbach, Germany. It is now owned by the French Groupe SEB.

### Are all Rowenta vacuums made in France?

No. Groupe SEB has documented production in France and China as well as external sourcing. Check the exact model's label and documents.

### Does Rowenta make robot vacuums?

Yes, Rowenta sells robot vacuums in selected markets, including X-Plorer ranges. Availability and legal responsibility vary by model and country.

### Are Rowenta and Tefal the same?

No. They are separate Groupe SEB portfolio brands. Ownership does not make their products or parts interchangeable.

## Final Answer

Rowenta is a German-origin appliance brand owned by France-based Groupe SEB since 1988. The corporate parent is listed SEB S.A., while Rowenta France and other regional entities may handle product, sales, compliance or warranty roles.

Rowenta vacuum cleaners are not all made in one country or by one factory. For a reliable sourcing answer, verify the exact reference, declaration, production site, origin, seller and local repair terms.
