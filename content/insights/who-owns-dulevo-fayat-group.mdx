**Dulevo is owned by FAYAT Group.** Dulevo's own history says FAYAT, the French construction and equipment group, acquired it in 2021. Dulevo now operates within FAYAT Environmental Solutions, while Dulevo International S.p.A. remains the Italian company named on its website.

Dulevo is Italian. It developed its first sweeper prototype in 1976, has operated as Dulevo International since 1991, and is based in Fontanellato, near Parma.

## Where are Dulevo sweepers made?

In Italy. Dulevo describes a 20,000-square-metre production footprint, and in 2020 it acquired Tecnosystem as a production hub for truck-mounted products.

## What does Dulevo make?

Industrial sweepers, street sweepers such as the Dulevo 6000, scrubber-dryers and combination machines, street washers and specialist vehicles for commercial, industrial and urban cleaning. Dulevo marked 50 years in 2026.
