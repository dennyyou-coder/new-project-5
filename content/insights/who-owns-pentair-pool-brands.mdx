**Pentair is owned by its public shareholders.** The group's parent company, Pentair plc, is listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker PNR, and no other company owns it. Its management office is in Golden Valley, Minnesota, and its corporate history begins in 1966.

Pentair is a water-solutions company. Its Pool segment designs, makes and sells residential and commercial pool equipment, including robotic pool cleaners.

## Is Pentair a public company?

Yes. Pentair plc's shares trade on the NYSE under PNR. The plc is the top company of the group; there is no outside owner.

## What pool brands does Pentair own?

Pentair's 2025 annual report names Pentair Pool, Kreepy Krauly, Pleatco and Sta-Rite as its primary Pool brands. The segment covers pumps, filters, heaters, water treatment, automation and lighting, plus robotic, suction-side and pressure-side pool cleaners.

## Where are Pentair pool cleaners made?

The annual report says the Pool segment designs, manufactures and sells its equipment, but it does not assign one factory to every brand or cleaner model. Check the label and documents of the specific model.

## How does Pentair sell?

Pentair's Pool customers are mainly wholesale and retail distribution businesses in residential and commercial markets. Pentair directs homeowners to pool professionals and dealers, and provides warranty and registration online.
