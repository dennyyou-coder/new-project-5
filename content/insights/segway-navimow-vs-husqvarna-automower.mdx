**Choose Segway Navimow if you want a consumer-first, wire-free setup and its specific RTK-, vision-, LiDAR- or AWD-equipped model fits your lawn. Choose Husqvarna Automower if you value a mature dealer route, professional installation, wired and EPOS choices, published spare-parts support or commercial fleet capability.** Neither brand wins every lawn: the decisive variables are signal exposure, boundary geometry, slope at the edge, installation quality and the local repair path.

This is a family comparison, not a single-model shootout. Navimow ranges from compact i-series machines to X3/X4 large-lawn platforms. Automower ranges from boundary-wire residential products to iQ/NERA EPOS and professional AWD/EPOS machines. Compare models with similar area, cutting-height range and intended duty.

The maximum capability shown anywhere in a brand’s range does not describe its entry model. A Navimow X4 slope claim cannot be applied to an i105. A professional Automower 535 AWD EPOS is not a fair substitute for a residential unit in a price comparison.

## Match Comparable Models First

Start with the property, then select the product family.

### Small residential lawns

Navimow i105/i110 and smaller Automower products address compact residential areas. Navimow’s i-series emphasizes app mapping, EFLS positioning and camera-based obstacle recognition. Husqvarna’s small models vary by region: some use physical boundary wire, while newer NERA or iQ products can support virtual boundaries.

### Medium and large residential lawns

Navimow X3/X4 and higher-capacity Automower iQ/NERA models address larger, more complex properties. In the United States, Husqvarna currently lists the 440 iQ at about two acres and the 435 iQ AWD at about 1.3 acres. Navimow’s current US store locator shows X4 models for roughly one to 1.5 acres, while earlier X3 configurations extend across other sizes and markets.

### Steep or commercial sites

This is where platform differences become larger. Navimow X4 uses four-wheel drive and markets very high test-grade capability. Husqvarna separates residential AWD from professional models such as the 535 AWD EPOS and 560/580 EPOS families. The professional products add fleet-oriented operation, dealer installation and commercial service logic.

Do not buy by acreage alone. A flat, open two-acre lawn is easier than a one-acre property split by buildings, tree canopy, gates, steep banks and narrow transport paths.

## RTK, Vision, LiDAR and EPOS

Both brands increasingly sell virtual-boundary mowing, but their system architecture and fallback behavior differ.

### Navimow positioning

Navimow’s EFLS family combines satellite correction with other sensors. The X3 platform’s EFLS 3.0 combines RTK coverage with visual simultaneous localization and mapping (VSLAM) and visual-inertial odometry (VIO). Its wide camera system also supports obstacle detection.

Newer Navimow platforms add different routes:

- Network RTK can remove the need for a local antenna where coverage is available;
- i2 LiDAR uses laser-based localization to reduce dependence on open-sky satellite reception;
- H2 combines LiDAR, network RTK and vision;
- i2 AWD pairs network RTK and vision with a driven steering wheel;
- X4 combines its positioning system with four-wheel-drive terrain hardware.

These are not software-only variants. Sensor suite, drive system and supported lawn size change by model.

### Husqvarna EPOS

Husqvarna EPOS uses satellite positioning and correction data to define work areas, stay-out zones and transport paths. In covered US locations, correction data can come through Husqvarna Cloud. Otherwise, compatible packages can use a local reference station with an open view of the sky.

Husqvarna says a reference station needs approximately a 135-degree clear sky view. Moving it after mapping can require rebuilding the map. Cloud-based EPOS also needs suitable internet connectivity across the operating area.

EPOS Support by Wire can reinforce selected sections with poor satellite reception. It is a hybrid tool, not a way to create a completely separate work area outside the virtual boundary. Husqvarna also notes that the docking point still needs adequate satellite conditions.

### What this means in practice

RTK accuracy is only one layer. Before purchase, mark:

- tree canopy and seasonal leaf density;
- walls, tall hedges and metal structures;
- narrow passages between buildings;
- front-to-back routes;
- charging-station sky view;
- Wi-Fi and cellular coverage;
- places where a stopped mower would block access;
- pools, roads, retaining walls and drops that need a physical safety margin.

Vision or LiDAR can improve continuity, but no sensor removes the need for a safe site design.

## Lawn Area and Productive Capacity

Manufacturers publish a recommended area, but actual completion time depends on mowing frequency, growth rate, slope, turns, obstacles, charging and travel between zones.

Use four capacity numbers:

1. recommended maintained area;
2. cutting width and speed;
3. mowing time versus charging time;
4. number of complete cuts possible in the available schedule.

Navimow X3 was promoted for lawns up to 2.5 acres in the US launch, while model capacities vary internationally. The current X4 lineup is segmented by area. Husqvarna ranges from compact residential machines to two-acre iQ models and larger professional systems.

A buyer should reserve capacity. If the lawn is at the model’s maximum rating, spring growth, rain delays and extra zones can leave no recovery window. Commercial operators should calculate weekly hectares or acres actually completed—not just the nameplate area.

## Slopes, Traction and Boundary Safety

Slope claims are among the easiest robot-mower specifications to misuse.

Navimow’s compact i105/i110 platform lists a 30% (17-degree) maximum operating slope but only 10% (6 degrees) at the boundary. X3 lists up to 27 degrees. X4’s much higher headline was measured under specific dry-grass, grass-height and remote-control test conditions.

Husqvarna’s professional 535 AWD EPOS lists up to 70% (35 degrees), while 560 EPOS lists 50% inside the work area and 20% at the boundary. Residential models have their own limits.

Inspect at least three slope conditions:

- straight uphill and downhill;
- lateral travel across the grade;
- turning or stopping near the boundary.

Wet turf, loose soil, moss, roots, holes and wheel contamination reduce grip. A robot may climb a slope yet slide during a turn. Near a pool, road, wall edge or drop, do not treat the laboratory maximum as the design target.

For difficult terrain, compare driven wheels, tire tread, vehicle weight, ground clearance, pivot behavior and how easily wheels or body panels can be replaced.

## Cutting, Edges and Lawn Finish

Navigation does not determine cut quality by itself. Compare:

- cutting width;
- number and position of blade discs;
- cutting-height range;
- electronic versus manual height adjustment;
- systematic versus irregular patterns;
- edge offset;
- turn radius and turf disturbance;
- blade access and replacement interval.

Navimow’s newer large-lawn platforms emphasize wider cutting systems, systematic routes and edge features. Husqvarna offers both systematic EPOS mowing and established random-pattern platforms, depending on model.

“Edge cutting” never means zero trimming on every property. Blade offset can reduce the uncut strip, but raised borders, walls, fences and sharply mapped virtual boundaries still leave different results. A level paved border is easier than a vertical retaining wall.

For dealers, show a real boundary demonstration. Measure the uncut strip at a wall, a flush path and a curved bed rather than repeating one edge-cutting label.

## Installation: DIY Convenience vs Site Engineering

Removing buried boundary wire reduces one type of labor, but wireless installation still involves:

- charging-station placement;
- correction-data or reference-station setup;
- mapping work areas and stay-out zones;
- defining transport paths;
- testing edges, gates and slopes;
- setting schedules and cutting heights;
- validating weak-signal recovery.

Navimow’s consumer proposition is strongly app-led. Automatic or assisted mapping on newer models can reduce manual driving. This is attractive for a straightforward lawn and a confident owner.

Husqvarna now supports DIY installation on its iQ family, but it also publishes a professional service covering property walk-through, station placement, physical wire or virtual mapping, firmware, app pairing, operating settings and follow-up. That route can be valuable on complex properties or when the buyer wants one accountable installer.

The right question is not “wire or no wire?” It is “who owns the map, signal survey and recovery visit if the mower misses a zone?”

## Connectivity, Apps and Firmware

Both ecosystems use mobile apps for mapping, schedules, zones, status and updates. Connectivity can include Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and cellular service, but the included term and regional availability vary by model.

Before purchase, confirm:

- which functions work over Bluetooth only;
- whether remote status requires Wi-Fi or 4G;
- whether correction data depends on paid or included connectivity;
- what happens when the internet is unavailable;
- how many users or properties the account supports;
- how maps are backed up after service or replacement;
- whether a dealer can access diagnostics with owner consent;
- how long firmware and app support is expected.

Husqvarna says Automower can still operate independently of the Connect app on supported models. Its professional EPOS products can integrate with Husqvarna Fleet Services. Navimow provides app-based diagnostics, mapping and anti-theft functions, with exact features varying by platform.

For multi-property operators, account roles, fleet overview and technician access can outweigh a small difference in cutting speed.

## Dealers, Parts and Service

Husqvarna’s strongest structural advantage is its published service system. The US site provides a dedicated Automower support center, servicing-dealer locator, professional installation, spare-parts pages and annual-service guidance. Dealers can inspect hardware, update firmware, replace blades or batteries and store equipment for winter where those services are offered.

Navimow is not online-only. Its current store locator includes authorized dealers and retail partners, and its warranty process starts with online or in-app diagnosis before directing repair or return. The practical network is still market- and postcode-specific.

Audit the local route before buying:

1. call the nearest listed seller;
2. ask whether it installs and repairs, or only sells;
3. request normal diagnostic turnaround;
4. confirm stock for blades, wheels, charging contacts, power supplies and body parts;
5. ask whether batteries are replaced locally or shipped;
6. confirm winter-storage and spring-start services;
7. identify who pays freight during warranty.

A dealer logo near your postcode does not prove workshop capability. Conversely, a capable independent installer may offer better response than a distant national network.

## Warranty, Wear Parts and Winter Storage

Navimow’s US limited-warranty page lists three years for the covered i and X3 mower bodies and two years for battery packs and power adapters. Blades are consumables. Newer model terms and other countries must be checked separately.

Husqvarna warranty coverage depends on product, purchaser type and market. Warranty service generally runs through an authorized dealer or workshop and requires proof of purchase plus model and serial information.

Budget for items outside the headline warranty:

- blades and screws;
- wheel wear or traction accessories;
- cleaning and corrosion prevention;
- battery aging;
- station or reference-unit mounting;
- damaged low-voltage cables;
- collection, freight or technician travel;
- annual inspection;
- indoor winter storage where freezing, snow or prolonged inactivity applies.

Clean the deck safely, replace blades as a set under the manual, store the mower dry and follow the specified charging routine. Do not pressure-wash components unless the exact manual permits it.

## Total Cost of Ownership

Use:

`mower + station/reference hardware + installation + connectivity + blades + repairs + battery + seasonal service + downtime`

Navimow can produce a lower installed cost when the owner completes a reliable map without an installer or local antenna. The advantage disappears if repeated signal troubleshooting, freight repair or unsupported slopes create downtime.

Automower can cost more as a complete installed system, but a qualified local dealer may reduce site risk and repair time. Professional models can justify that premium across multiple lawns or high-value turf.

Ask for a three-year ownership quote, not only a mower price. For dealers, separate hardware margin from installation, map changes, annual service, winter storage and emergency call-outs.

## FAQ

### Is Segway Navimow better than Husqvarna Automower?

Navimow is often the better starting point for a tech-comfortable DIY buyer who validates the exact model and site. Automower is often stronger when installation, dealer service or professional fleet support is part of the decision.

### Does either brand need a boundary wire?

Compatible Navimow and Automower EPOS models can use virtual boundaries. Some Automower models still use physical wire, and EPOS Support by Wire can reinforce selected weak-signal areas.

### Which works better under trees?

It depends on model and site. Navimow uses vision, VSLAM, VIO or LiDAR on selected platforms. Husqvarna can combine EPOS with Support by Wire. Test dense canopy and narrow corridors rather than assuming “wire-free” guarantees coverage.

### Which is better on steep slopes?

Compare matched AWD models and separate the maximum work-area grade from the boundary limit. X4 and Automower AWD platforms can address steep terrain, but wet grass, lateral turns and edge safety determine the usable margin.

### Do I need professional installation?

Not for every lawn. A simple property can be mapped by the owner. Complex boundaries, weak signals, steep drops or multiple transport paths make a professional survey and commissioning more valuable.

### Which has better dealer support?

Husqvarna publishes a broader, long-established outdoor-power-equipment dealer and service structure. Navimow has authorized stores and partners, but capability should be verified by location. Call the actual workshop before buying.

### What costs are commonly missed?

Reference or mounting hardware, installation, connectivity after any included term, blades, wheel wear, battery replacement, shipping, map revisions, annual service and winter storage.

## Bottom Line

Navimow’s advantage is fast-moving navigation and terrain technology packaged for wire-free residential adoption. Automower’s advantage is a broad system spanning physical wire, EPOS, AWD, professional installation, parts and commercial service.

Choose the model that completes your real lawn with safe boundaries and a credible repair route. The better robot is not the one with the highest isolated specification; it is the one whose navigation, traction, installation and service system still work together in year three.
