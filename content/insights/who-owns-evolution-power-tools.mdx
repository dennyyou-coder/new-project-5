## Who Owns Evolution Power Tools?

Evolution Power Tools Ltd is company number 03442268 in the United Kingdom. Companies House lists it as an active private limited company incorporated on September 30, 1997, with a registered office in Sheffield.

The current persons-with-significant-control register identifies **Matthew Gavins** as holding more than 50% but less than 75% of shares. It also records corresponding voting-rights and board-appointment control bands.

The precise language matters. The filing establishes majority control, not complete ownership. Other shareholders may hold the remaining equity. Evolution is therefore accurately described as a privately held, founder-controlled specialist tool company.

The live filing history shows group accounts through December 31, 2024 were filed in September 2025 and includes a charge-satisfaction filing dated March 2026. Because ownership and finance records can change, Companies House should be checked again before using the conclusion in a transaction or investment decision.

## Is Evolution Owned by a Large Tool Group?

Current public records do not show Evolution Power Tools Ltd as a brand of Bosch, Stanley Black & Decker, TTI, Chervon or a major home-improvement retailer.

That distinguishes it from group-owned tool brands such as [Einhell](/blog/who-owns-einhell-power-x-change) and retailer-controlled programs such as [Kobalt](/blog/who-makes-kobalt-tools-lowes-suppliers). Evolution presents its own company identity, develops a specialist cutting proposition and operates international sales and support entities.

Retailers can distribute Evolution products without owning the company. Likewise, a contract factory can manufacture a product without owning the Evolution trademark.

## Who Manufactures Evolution Power Tools?

Evolution controls its brand, product specifications and commercial system, but no current primary source establishes one universal factory for its entire catalogue.

Historical company material describes a Shanghai operation and a manufacturing facility in Zhejiang, China. That is useful evidence about the company's development, but it is not enough to label every current saw, drill, vacuum, pressure washer or accessory as coming from the same site.

Current manuals identify Evolution Power Tools Ltd and regional entities as manufacturers or responsible economic operators for regulatory purposes. A responsible entity is accountable for placing the product on the market; it is not automatically the physical assembly plant.

![Evolution manufacturing evidence and multi-material cutting boundaries](/images/blog/evolution-manufacturing-cutting-map.svg)

For an exact product, verify:

1. the model and revision number;
2. the origin marking on the tool and carton;
3. the current manual and declaration of conformity;
4. the named manufacturer, importer or responsible party;
5. blade specifications and approved materials; and
6. date code and warranty market.

This model-level method avoids two common errors: assuming a British headquarters means all tools are made in Britain, or assuming historical Chinese manufacturing means every current product is made at one Chinese factory.

## What Is Evolution's Multi-Material Cutting Technology?

Evolution is best known for saws and blades designed to cut multiple specified materials. A current R355CPS chop-saw page, for example, describes cutting steel, copper, aluminium, wood and plastic with the supplied multi-material blade.

The system uses a tungsten-carbide-tipped blade and a controlled, lower-speed cutting approach for suitable models. Compared with abrasive cutting in mild steel, Evolution emphasizes a colder cut, fewer sparks and a cleaner finish.

The commercial idea is straightforward: one correctly matched saw-and-blade combination can reduce blade changes when a project moves among compatible timber, metal and plastic materials.

## Does One Evolution Blade Really Cut Everything?

No. “Multi-material” is not the same as unlimited.

Material type, wall thickness, cross-section, blade diameter, tooth geometry, feed rate and saw speed all matter. Mild steel, stainless steel, aluminium, timber with nails, plastics and composites can require different blades or operating limits even when several are supported by one platform.

Before cutting, confirm:

- the exact saw model;
- the installed blade's part number and approved-material list;
- maximum section dimensions and wall thickness;
- clamping and workpiece support;
- required eye, hearing and respiratory protection; and
- whether dust or chips require extraction.

Do not use a marketing phrase as a substitute for the manual. Never cut a material that the current blade and saw documentation exclude.

## What Else Does Evolution Sell?

Evolution's range extends beyond chop saws. Its current store includes mitre saws, circular saws, table saws, magnetic drills, concrete cutting, cordless tools, vacuums, dust accessories and an Evo-System pressure-washer configuration.

The adjacent cleaning categories are relevant to facilities and construction-cleaning buyers. Dust-port size, antistatic hose needs, filtration, wet pickup, replacement bags and local service can matter as much as saw power.

The company is also growing an 18V cordless range. A shared cordless identity should still be checked at the exact battery, charger and destination-market level.

## What Warranty Does Evolution Offer?

Evolution's current UK warranty page separates categories:

- single circular-blade saws and magnetic drills carry a three-year limited warranty; and
- other Evolution power tools carry a two-year limited warranty.

Coverage depends on the product, market, proof of purchase and stated conditions. Registration deadlines, wear items, misuse and commercial duty can affect a claim. Buyers outside the UK should use the warranty page for their own country rather than importing the UK headline term.

Consumables such as blades are not equivalent to the power tool itself. Blade wear or damage caused by an unsupported material should not be assumed to fall under the tool warranty.

## What This Means for Buyers and Distributors

Evolution is not a retailer label hiding an unrelated brand owner. It is a distinct private tool company with a documented controlling shareholder and a focused cutting proposition.

But independence does not remove supply-chain diligence. Distributors should request current conformity documents, responsible-party data, origin evidence, spare-parts arrangements and blade availability for every market and model.

Professional users should evaluate the full cutting system:

- saw duty cycle and accuracy;
- approved materials and section sizes;
- blade life and replacement cost;
- chip and spark control;
- guarding, clamping and extraction; and
- repair turnaround and warranty eligibility.

For higher-duty alternatives, compare not only ownership but service models and dust-control systems.
