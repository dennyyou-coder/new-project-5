**Procter & Gamble owns the Braun trademark, but De’Longhi operates Braun-branded household appliances under a perpetual licence.** The agreement covers small kitchen appliances, ironing and selected household-appliance categories. It does not mean De’Longhi bought the Braun trademark or controls all Braun products.

This explains why a Braun hand blender or steam iron can sit within De’Longhi’s appliance business, while Braun shavers and other personal-care products follow a different P&G operating structure.

## Braun Appliances at a Glance

## What Did P&G License to De’Longhi?

In April 2012, De’Longhi and P&G announced an agreement for perpetual licensing of the Braun brand in small kitchen appliances, ironing and selected household-appliance categories. The transaction also included certain production assets, related inventory and movement of some employees.

De’Longhi’s later reporting records the perpetual Braun licence as an intangible asset. The licence gives the group long-term rights to design, manufacture and distribute products within the agreed scope. Ownership of the Braun brand remains with P&G.

“Perpetual” describes the duration of the licensed right. It does not convert a licence into trademark ownership.

## Which Braun Products Are Operated by De’Longhi?

Braun Household’s current portfolio includes:

- hand blenders, food processors, jug blenders and juicers;
- coffee makers, kettles and toasters;
- selected cooking and food-preparation appliances; and
- steam irons and ironing systems.

The exact assortment differs by country. Product names and retailer listings should be checked against the official destination-market catalogue.

## Are Braun Shavers and Oral-B Appliances Included?

No. Braun personal-care products such as electric shavers and grooming devices are outside De’Longhi’s small-household-appliance licence. Oral-B products are also a separate P&G business context.

This is the most important category boundary. The same Braun wordmark can appear on products with different operating companies, support sites and warranty routes.

![Braun product category, factory, seller and warranty verification flow](/images/blog/braun-appliance-responsibility-map.svg)

Before contacting support, identify whether the product is:

1. Braun Household kitchen or garment care;
2. Braun personal care or grooming; or
3. Oral-B dental care.

The serial label, model number and product category determine the correct service organization.

## Where Are Braun Household Appliances Made?

De’Longhi Group reports a mix of owned plants and manufacturing or assembly partners across its household-appliance portfolio. The 2025 annual report identifies six household plants in Italy, Romania and China, but it does not publish a complete Braun model-to-factory map.

It would therefore be inaccurate to assign all Braun Household products to Germany, Italy or China. Corporate heritage, engineering offices and factory origin are separate facts.

For procurement, request the exact model’s rating label, origin statement, manufacturer, factory record, conformity file and food-contact documentation.

## Who Sells and Warrants Braun Household Products?

Braun Household uses regional De’Longhi companies and market-specific channels. The reviewed US privacy policy identifies De’Longhi America, Inc. as the operator for that website and related data context. Other markets can use different companies or distributors.

A retailer return policy is not the same as the manufacturer warranty. Cross-border models may use different voltage, plugs, accessories and service eligibility.

For commercial kitchens, offices or hospitality, verify that the warranty covers the intended use. Many small appliances are sold primarily for domestic duty cycles.

## Can Braun Household Parts Be Used Across Product Lines?

Only when the official parts documentation confirms compatibility. Hand-blender shafts, bowls, whisks and processor attachments can look similar while using different couplings, model generations or regional references.

Trademark ownership and group affiliation provide no technical compatibility guarantee. Use the full type and model number when ordering replacements.

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

1. Confirm that the product falls inside the Braun Household licence.
2. Do not describe De’Longhi as owner of the Braun trademark.
3. Match the exact model to its regional catalogue and electrical specification.
4. Verify factory, origin and conformity documents at SKU level.
5. Identify the invoice seller, importer and warranty provider.
6. Confirm attachment, consumable and spare-parts compatibility in writing.
7. Keep personal-care and Oral-B support routes separate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Braun appliances?

P&G owns the Braun trademark. De’Longhi operates Braun household appliances under a perpetual licence covering defined small-appliance categories.

### Did De’Longhi buy Braun?

No. De’Longhi acquired a perpetual licence and certain operating assets for Braun household appliances; trademark ownership remained with P&G.

### Who makes Braun hand blenders?

Braun hand blenders are part of De’Longhi’s licensed household-appliance business. The exact legal manufacturer and factory depend on the model.

### Does De’Longhi make Braun shavers?

No. Braun personal care is outside the De’Longhi household-appliance licence.

### Is Braun a German brand?

Braun has German origins and design heritage. Current trademark ownership, category operating rights and model manufacturing locations are separate questions.

## Final Answer

P&G owns Braun, while De’Longhi holds a perpetual licence to use the brand for small kitchen appliances, ironing and selected household products. De’Longhi operates that household business but does not own the Braun trademark or Braun personal-care categories.

For a particular appliance, verify the category, exact model, factory, origin, regional seller and warranty provider. The logo alone cannot identify all of those responsibilities.
