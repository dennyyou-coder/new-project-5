**Lavor is owned by Comet Group.** Lavor's own 50th-anniversary account says the brand became part of Italy's Comet Group in 2017. The cleaning-equipment business is run through Lavorwash S.p.A.

Lavor is Italian. Lavorwash was established in Pegognaga, in the province of Mantua, in 1975.

## What is Comet Group?

Comet is an Italian maker of pumps and pressure washers, founded in Reggio Emilia in 1959. Lavor joined it in 2017, and Lavorwash S.p.A. operates under the direction and coordination of Comet S.p.A.

## What does Lavor make?

Professional and home pressure washers, scrubber dryers and sweepers, professional and industrial vacuums, and home and outdoor cleaning machines.

## Where is Lavor sold?

Lavor says its group is present in more than 130 countries, with branches in Europe, the Americas and China, and sells through dealers.
