**Shop-Vac is owned within the GreatStar group. The current US operating company is Shop-Vac USA, LLC; its parent is Great Star Tools, USA, Inc.; and the ultimate listed-company parent is Hangzhou GreatStar Industrial Co., Ltd. of China. GreatStar acquired substantially all operating assets of the former Shop-Vac Corporation in late 2020.**

That answer is more precise than saying either “Shop-Vac is American” or “Shop-Vac is Chinese.” The brand retains a US operating presence and Williamsport address, while ownership sits inside a Chinese-listed tool group with a global supply chain.

## The Current Shop-Vac Ownership Chain

Shop-Vac’s [official privacy policy](https://www.shopvac.com/policies/privacy-policy), updated in 2025, identifies the website operator as Shop-Vac USA, LLC and names Great Star Tools, USA, Inc. as its parent company. A [2022 Hangzhou GreatStar filing](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2022-04-12/1212884034.PDF) describes Shop-Vac USA, LLC as a wholly owned indirect subsidiary of the listed company.

The practical chain is:

| Layer | Entity | What it tells buyers |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | Shop-Vac | Product identity, trademarks and market reputation |
| US operator | Shop-Vac USA, LLC | Website, warranty, customer service and US commercial activity |
| Immediate parent | Great Star Tools, USA, Inc. | US holding and operating link inside GreatStar |
| Ultimate listed parent | Hangzhou GreatStar Industrial Co., Ltd. | Group ownership, capital allocation and global tool strategy |

These layers answer different questions. The brand on a tank does not automatically identify the legal seller, factory, importer or warranty obligor in every market.

## What Happened in 2020

The former Shop-Vac Corporation abruptly shut its Williamsport operation in September 2020. Hangzhou GreatStar’s [official acquisition announcement](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2020-12-24/1208978404.PDF) said the company planned to capitalize Great Star Tools USA with $50 million and use the US subsidiary to purchase Shop-Vac operating assets for $41.86 million.

The disclosed assets included:

- real estate connected to the business;
- machinery and equipment;
- intellectual property;
- inventory;
- accounts receivable.

That structure matters. GreatStar did not merely buy shares in the old corporation and leave every legal relationship unchanged. It acquired a defined operating-asset package and rebuilt the business through its own US structure.

The same filing said the old Shop-Vac had entered an asset-disposal process after it could not repay a JPMorgan Chase loan on time. GreatStar’s transaction announcement said the Williamsport factory would reopen and that some employees separated during the closure would be rehired.

## Was Shop-Vac Founded in 1953 or 1965?

Shop-Vac’s own current website gives two answers. An [official Shop-Vac article](https://www.shopvac.com/blogs/whats-new/the-best-wet-dry-vac-for-home-work-and-wherever-life-happens) says the company has made vacuums since 1953. Current [Shop-Vac product pages](https://www.shopvac.com/products/shop-vac%C2%AE-12-gallon-6-0-peak-hp-stainless-steel-wet-dry-vacuum) use “Since 1965,” and GreatStar’s 2020 filing also describes the business as founded in 1965.

Without a primary corporate-history record reconciling those dates, the defensible answer is that Shop-Vac’s official materials are inconsistent. A serious ownership page should disclose the discrepancy instead of copying whichever date appears first in search.

## Brand Owner, Operating Company, Trademark and Factory

Four identities are often collapsed into “who makes Shop-Vac?”

1. **Brand owner:** the group controlling the Shop-Vac brand and business.
2. **Operating company:** the entity selling products, running the website or providing warranty support.
3. **Trademark owner:** the entity recorded for specific marks in a particular jurisdiction.
4. **Manufacturer:** the plant and legal manufacturer responsible for an exact model.

The first two are well supported by Shop-Vac and GreatStar disclosures. The fourth requires model-level evidence.

The 2020 acquisition filing said the former Shop-Vac business had manufacturing bases in the United States, China and Vietnam. That is evidence about the network at the transaction date. It does not prove that every 2026 model is made in all three countries, or that a specific current SKU comes from the Williamsport plant.

Check the rating label, carton, manual, customs documents, certificate applicant, declaration, factory audit and purchase contract for the exact model. “American brand,” “owned by a Chinese company” and “made in China” are separate statements.

## Why GreatStar Wanted Shop-Vac

GreatStar entered the transaction with a large tool and global-supply-chain business. Shop-Vac brought a recognized wet/dry vacuum name, North American retail relationships, an accessory ecosystem, intellectual property, equipment, real estate and local service capacity.

The acquisition extended GreatStar beyond hand tools into powered cleanup equipment. It also provided a brand whose name is widely used as a category shorthand in North America. For GreatStar, restoring the business could combine US brand equity with its sourcing, manufacturing and retailer-management capabilities.

The strategy is visible in Shop-Vac’s current assortment. The [official store](https://www.shopvac.com/) still centers on wet/dry vacuums, but it also carries cordless handhelds, stick vacuums, spot cleaners, blower/vacuum combinations, air movers and cleaning accessories.

## Wet/Dry Vacuum Strategy: The Parts Ecosystem Matters

A wet/dry vacuum is a platform business. Tank capacity and motor claims attract the initial sale, but filters, bags, hoses, adapters, nozzles and replacement parts determine long-term usability.

Shop-Vac maintains an [official filter guide](https://www.shopvac.com/pages/official-shop-vac-filter-guide) and a [parts-schematic search](https://www.shopvac.com/pages/product-search) covering current and selected older models. Its [home-use warranty](https://www.shopvac.com/pages/the-shop-vac%C2%AE-home-use-warranty) is issued by Shop-Vac USA, LLC and directs customers to model-specific parts information.

For distributors, this creates both value and responsibility. A broad installed base supports repeat parts sales, but model proliferation can produce filter and hose confusion. Inventory planning should link every vacuum SKU to compatible filters, collection bags, hose diameters, adapters and service documents.

## Current Manufacturing Evidence—and Its Limits

The safest current statement is:

- Shop-Vac is owned through GreatStar;
- Shop-Vac USA, LLC operates from Williamsport, Pennsylvania;
- GreatStar acquired US real estate, equipment and other assets in 2020 and announced the factory’s reopening;
- the pre-acquisition network included US, China and Vietnam manufacturing;
- current model origin must be checked individually.

Do not infer the factory from website ownership or a historical press release. Product lines can move, suppliers can change and regional variants may differ.

A B2B buyer should request:

- exact legal manufacturer and production site;
- country of origin for the ordered SKU;
- label and carton artwork;
- current safety and performance reports;
- critical-component and factory lists;
- warranty owner by market;
- parts availability and change-control commitments.

## Channels, Warranty and Service

Shop-Vac sells through its direct website and external retailers. The [official return policy](https://www.shopvac.com/pages/shipping-and-returns-policy) says direct purchases return to Shop-Vac USA, LLC, while items bought from another retailer return to that retailer. This division affects customer experience and dealer obligations.

Distributors should clarify:

- who invoices and imports the product;
- who handles dead-on-arrival units;
- whether warranty is replacement, repair or parts-based;
- which consumables are excluded;
- where returned units and parts are stocked;
- whether commercial use changes coverage;
- how legacy models are supported.

The brand’s home-use warranty excludes accessories and filters and excludes products used commercially. Professional buyers must not assume a household warranty applies to workshop fleets or cleaning contractors.

## What the Ownership Means for Distributors and OEM Buyers

GreatStar ownership gives Shop-Vac access to a broader tool-group supply chain and retail infrastructure. The Shop-Vac name brings category recognition and an established parts vocabulary. Neither advantage eliminates model-level due diligence.

For distributors, the questions are assortment separation, channel protection, regional warranty, spare parts and inventory continuity. For OEM buyers benchmarking GreatStar or competing wet/dry platforms, the useful comparison is not corporate nationality. It is the evidence chain from design control and component approval through factory, end-of-line test, origin, parts and warranty.

## Verification Checklist

Before relying on any ownership or manufacturing claim, verify:

1. the current operating company in official legal and privacy pages;
2. the parent-company chain in listed-company filings;
3. whether the historic transaction was a share or asset purchase;
4. trademark ownership in the target jurisdiction;
5. exact model, region and country of origin;
6. factory and certificate identity;
7. warranty issuer and commercial-use exclusions;
8. compatible filters, bags, hoses and replacement parts;
9. importer, retailer and return responsibilities;
10. production and component change control.

## Final Answer

Shop-Vac is owned within Hangzhou GreatStar Industrial’s group through Great Star Tools USA and Shop-Vac USA, LLC. GreatStar bought the operating assets of the former Shop-Vac Corporation in 2020 after the old business entered an asset-disposal process.

The acquisition preserved and restarted a US operating presence while placing the brand inside a global Chinese-owned tool company. That ownership answer does not establish the origin of every vacuum. Model labels, compliance files and contracts remain the right evidence for manufacturing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Shop-Vac an American company?

Shop-Vac USA, LLC is a US operating company with a Williamsport, Pennsylvania address. Its parent group is controlled by Hangzhou GreatStar Industrial, a Chinese listed company.

### Did GreatStar buy Shop-Vac Corporation?

GreatStar’s disclosed 2020 transaction was an asset acquisition covering real estate, equipment, intellectual property, inventory and receivables. The current business operates through Shop-Vac USA, LLC.

### Are Shop-Vac vacuums made in the United States?

Some US manufacturing assets were acquired and the Williamsport factory was announced for reopening. The country of origin must still be checked on the exact current model; historical network statements are not proof for every SKU.

### Who handles Shop-Vac warranty claims?

Shop-Vac USA, LLC issues the official US home-use warranty. Retailer returns and warranty processes can differ, and commercial use has separate implications.
