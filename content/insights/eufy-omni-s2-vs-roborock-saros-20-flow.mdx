**The eufy Omni S2 and Roborock Saros 20 Flow are both roller-mop robots, and they differ most in how they handle carpets and thresholds.** eufy's S2 has a wide 29 cm roller and lifts it up to 2.8 cm over carpet. Roborock's Saros 20 Flow is slimmer and better at climbing tall transitions.

## Carpets

A 2.8 cm mop lift helps the Omni S2 keep a wet roller off thicker rugs. If your home mixes carpets and hard floors, ask how each handles your rug height.

## Thresholds and furniture

The Saros 20 Flow's lifting chassis and 89.8 mm body are its strengths. Note that eufy's MarsWalker stair-climbing hardware is not part of the S2 purchase.

## Which should you buy?

**Omni S2** for mixed carpet homes and a do-everything station. **Saros 20 Flow** for low furniture and tall thresholds.
