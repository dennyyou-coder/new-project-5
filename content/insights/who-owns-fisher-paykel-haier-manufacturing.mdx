**Fisher & Paykel Appliances is owned by Haier Smart Home, the listed appliance company within the wider Haier Group.** Haier first acquired a 20% stake in 2009, gained control in 2012 and completed the acquisition process that took Fisher & Paykel private.

The ownership change did not erase Fisher & Paykel's identity. It remains a premium appliance business with New Zealand design roots, its own product organization, regional companies and a manufacturing network that official Haier material currently identifies across Thailand, China, Italy and Mexico.

## How Did Haier Acquire Fisher & Paykel?

The relationship began before the full takeover.

In 2009, Haier participated in Fisher & Paykel Appliances' recapitalization and agreed to take a 20% stake. The companies also announced cooperation covering markets, design, technology, manufacturing resources, sourcing and after-sales service.

In November 2012, Haier announced that it had crossed the 90% control threshold and would begin compulsory acquisition of the remaining shares under New Zealand takeover rules. That process made Fisher & Paykel part of Haier's global appliance business.

The sequence matters because older pages can describe Haier only as a cornerstone shareholder. That was accurate in 2009 but is no longer the current ownership answer.

Today, Haier Smart Home includes Fisher & Paykel in the same global portfolio as Haier, GE Appliances, Candy, AQUA, Casarte and Leader. Our [Haier appliance ownership guide](/blog/who-owns-haier-appliances-brand-portfolio) maps the full group structure.

## Is Fisher & Paykel Still Independent?

It is not independent in the ownership sense: Haier controls the business through Haier Smart Home. It remains distinct in the operating and market sense.

Fisher & Paykel continues to use its own brand, product architecture, design language, websites, regional entities and service channels. Its current positioning emphasizes premium kitchen and laundry systems and “human-centred” design rooted in New Zealand.

This is a common portfolio structure. A parent company can own a business without replacing every local company or integrating all products into one factory. For buyers, the practical question is not only who owns the brand, but which Fisher & Paykel entity signs the contract, imports the product, provides the warranty and controls the relevant factory or supplier program.

## Where Are Fisher & Paykel Appliances Made?

Haier's current Fisher & Paykel company profile says the business manufactures in **Thailand, China, Italy and Mexico**. It also describes a global company operating across 50 countries.

Those country-level facts establish a multinational manufacturing network. They do not assign one product category or model to a particular site.

Fisher & Paykel's current supply-chain disclosure adds another important boundary: appliances sold by the business can be made in its own factories **or made on its behalf by suppliers**. Direct material suppliers include other Haier group companies and long-term original-equipment manufacturers.

![Fisher & Paykel manufacturing countries and model-level responsibility checks](/images/blog/fisher-paykel-manufacturing-responsibility-map.svg)

Therefore, claims such as “all Fisher & Paykel appliances are made in New Zealand,” “all are made in China,” or “Haier makes every model in a Haier factory” are too broad.

To establish origin, use the rating plate, model code, carton, declaration of conformity, importer document and product manual for the actual unit. A current corporate factory list is useful context, not a substitute for SKU evidence.

## What Products Does Fisher & Paykel Make?

Fisher & Paykel's portfolio centers on premium kitchen and laundry appliances. Depending on the market, it includes:

- integrated and freestanding refrigeration;
- ovens, cooktops, ranges and ventilation;
- DishDrawer and conventional dishwashers;
- washing machines and dryers;
- outdoor cooking products under relevant regional ranges.

The brand's commercial value comes partly from presenting these categories as a coordinated suite. Designers, developers and retailers can specify appliances around a consistent premium kitchen and home environment rather than treating each product as an isolated box.

That design proposition does not establish common manufacturing. A refrigerator, dishwasher and oven sold as one suite may use different factories, legal manufacturers, software platforms or spare-parts systems.

## How Does Fisher & Paykel Relate to Other Haier Brands?

Fisher & Paykel sits beside, rather than underneath, consumer-facing brands such as GE Appliances in Haier Smart Home's portfolio.

The brands can share group investment, sourcing knowledge, technology and regional infrastructure. Fisher & Paykel also has historical distribution relationships with Haier in Australia, New Zealand and China. But a Fisher & Paykel authorization is not automatically a GE Appliances or Haier brand authorization.

For suppliers, this distinction is critical. A program may be managed by Fisher & Paykel Appliances Ltd., a Thailand manufacturing company, a regional sales subsidiary, another Haier group entity or an approved OEM. The parent-company chart identifies ultimate ownership; the purchase order and quality agreement identify operational responsibility.

## Who Makes Fisher & Paykel Appliances?

The company-level answer is Fisher & Paykel Appliances within Haier Smart Home. The product-level answer depends on the model.

Some products are manufactured in Fisher & Paykel group facilities. Others can be made on the company's behalf by suppliers. A security declaration or user manual may name several Fisher & Paykel manufacturing companies or a responsible regional entity, and that named record should take priority over a general brand claim.

Buyers should avoid assuming that premium positioning means every component is made internally. Modern appliances combine cabinets, motors, compressors, electronics, software and accessories from multiple supply chains even when final assembly is in a company-owned factory.

## How Does Fisher & Paykel Warranty Work?

Warranty is regional. Current Fisher & Paykel pages show why a single worldwide number is misleading.

In the United States and Canada, the standard limited warranty for covered consumer appliances is commonly two years, with additional terms for selected systems. In the United Kingdom, the current site advertises a five-year manufacturer warranty on appliances. The warrantor and detailed exclusions differ by country.

Warranty coverage also changes for commercial use, mobile installations and grey-market purchases. The local warranty booklet and purchasing channel should be checked before a distributor promises coverage.

The correct due-diligence set is:

1. destination-country product page;
2. model-specific warranty booklet;
3. named regional warrantor;
4. authorized dealer or direct seller;
5. service network and parts route.

| Check | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| Model and serial label | Exact product identity and production record |
| Country-of-origin marking | Origin of the unit rather than the brand generally |
| Manufacturer declaration | Company responsible for the covered model |
| Importer and seller | Entity placing the appliance on the local market |
| Brand authorization | Territory and product scope of distribution rights |

| Parts documentation | Availability of filters, pumps, boards, seals and other repair parts |

For project buyers, also verify whether a connected feature depends on a regional cloud service, mobile application or account. Ownership by a global group does not guarantee identical software availability in every country.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Fisher & Paykel owned by Haier?

Yes. Fisher & Paykel Appliances is owned within Haier Smart Home's global appliance portfolio.

### When did Haier buy Fisher & Paykel?

Haier acquired a 20% stake in 2009, crossed the 90% control threshold in 2012 and completed the takeover process that year.

### Is Fisher & Paykel made in New Zealand?

The brand has New Zealand design roots, but current official sources identify manufacturing in Thailand, China, Italy and Mexico. Check the exact product label for origin.

### Does Haier manufacture Fisher & Paykel appliances?

Haier owns the business, and group resources can support production. Fisher & Paykel also uses its own factories and products made on its behalf by suppliers. The model documents identify the correct maker.

### Is Fisher & Paykel a luxury brand?

It is positioned as a premium appliance brand, especially in integrated kitchen, refrigeration, cooking, dishwashing and laundry categories.

### Does Fisher & Paykel have the same warranty worldwide?

No. Warranty periods, warrantors and exclusions differ by country and product. Use the local model-specific terms.

## Final Answer

Fisher & Paykel is owned by Haier Smart Home after Haier's investment and takeover between 2009 and 2012. The company remains a distinct premium appliance brand with New Zealand design roots, regional operating companies and a global manufacturing network.

Current official sources identify manufacturing in Thailand, China, Italy and Mexico and acknowledge both company-factory and supplier production. Buyers should therefore separate parent ownership from the exact legal manufacturer, factory, country of origin, importer and regional warranty provider for each model.
