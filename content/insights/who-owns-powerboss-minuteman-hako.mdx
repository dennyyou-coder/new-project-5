**PowerBoss is owned by Minuteman International, part of Hako Group.** Hako's group page lists PowerBoss Inc. as a subsidiary of Minuteman, and Hako has been wholly owned by Germany's Possehl Group since 2007.

PowerBoss is American. It says it has made industrial cleaning equipment in Pingree Grove, Illinois, since 1985.

## Is PowerBoss part of Minuteman?

Yes. PowerBoss shares Minuteman's Pingree Grove site and its manuals, parts and service systems. See [who owns Minuteman](/blog/who-owns-minuteman-hako-powerboss) and [who owns Hako](/blog/who-owns-hako-possehl-group).

## Where are PowerBoss machines made?

PowerBoss says it has manufactured industrial cleaning equipment in Pingree Grove, Illinois, since 1985, and its site carries a Made in the USA statement.

## What does PowerBoss make?

Heavy-duty machines for manufacturing, warehousing, food and beverage, recycling, utilities, construction and parking: rider sweepers such as the Armadillo, rider scrubbers, sweeper-scrubbers, walk-behind machines and HEPA sweepers. Service and parts come through factory-trained distributors.
