**Ozito is owned by the Einhell Group and operated in Australia through Einhell Australia Pty Ltd.** Einhell acquired Ozito Industries and related companies in 2013. Bunnings is the brand's most important retail and warranty-return channel, but it does not own Ozito and should not be described as the manufacturer of every Ozito tool.

The manufacturing answer is product-specific. Einhell controls the business and the Power X-Change platform, while individual tools can come through different factories and supply arrangements. A model label, manual and current conformity or import documentation are stronger evidence than the logo alone.

## Does Einhell Own Ozito?

Yes. Einhell's 2013 annual report says the group acquired 100% of Ozito Industries Pty Ltd and affiliated companies under an agreement dated July 1, 2013. The transaction was carried out through Einhell Holding Australia Pty Ltd, a wholly owned Einhell Germany AG subsidiary.

Einhell's 2024 financial report lists the current Australian subsidiary as **Einhell Australia Pty Ltd (formerly Ozito Industries Pty Ltd)**. Ozito's own website also uses Einhell Australia Pty Ltd in its current footer and legal information.

This makes the corporate chain clear: Ozito is an Einhell group brand, not an independent Australian company owned by Bunnings. The brand still has a distinct Australian market identity and product program.

Ozito says it began in Australia in 1993. Its history before the acquisition explains the local brand recognition; the 2013 transaction explains today's ownership and platform connection. For the parent-company structure, see our [Einhell ownership and Power X-Change guide](/blog/who-owns-einhell-power-x-change).

## Is Ozito a Bunnings Brand?

Ozito is strongly associated with Bunnings because Bunnings is the key place Australian customers see, buy and return the products. That retail relationship is commercially important, but it is not the same as owning the trademark or operating company.

Ozito's warranty instructions tell customers to return a product to the nearest Bunnings store with proof of purchase. Bunnings product pages also explain the Ozito PXC battery ecosystem. Those facts establish a retail and service channel—not that Bunnings designs or manufactures every product.

This distinction also appears with other retailer-focused tool programs. [Kobalt is owned by Lowe's](/blog/who-makes-kobalt-tools-lowes-suppliers), while [Parkside is a Lidl private-label range](/blog/who-makes-parkside-tools-lidl-suppliers). Ozito differs because the disclosed corporate owner is Einhell.

## Who Manufactures Ozito Tools?

There is no reliable evidence that one factory makes the entire Ozito catalogue.

Ozito spans drills, saws, sanders, garden tools, compressors, pumps, vacuums and accessories. These categories use different components, certifications and production capabilities. Einhell's group-level sourcing disclosures help explain the broader system, but they do not identify the factory for a particular Ozito mower or drill.

For a specific model, verify:

1. the exact Ozito model and product number;
2. the country-of-origin marking on the product and carton;
3. the manufacturer, importer or responsible party in the manual;
4. the production or date code;
5. the battery and charger compatibility table; and
6. the current warranty document for the Australian market.

Two products can share a battery interface without sharing a factory. Conversely, a supplier can build tools for several brands without owning any of them.

## What Is Ozito Power X-Change?

Ozito launched its Power X-Change range in Australia in 2014. The current Ozito site describes PXC as one 18V battery-and-charger system covering more than 80 devices across workshop and garden categories.

Bunnings likewise states that one Ozito PXC battery can power the Ozito PXC range. That is the useful customer promise: buy compatible Ozito PXC tools and reuse the same regional battery system, subject to each tool's capacity and pack requirements.

Some higher-demand products use two 18V packs. Battery capacity can also affect runtime and performance. “Fits the platform” does not mean every pack is the best choice for every tool.

## Are Ozito and Einhell Batteries Interchangeable?

Do not assume that an Ozito PXC pack bought in Australia is approved for every Einhell PXC tool sold overseas.

Ozito and Einhell share group ownership and related Power X-Change technology, but regional products can differ in casing details, electrical approvals, charger input, documentation and warranty rules. Ozito's warranty page specifically says its PXC products are covered only when used with Ozito-branded PXC batteries and chargers.

The safe rule is simple: rely on the compatibility statement for the exact market, model and battery code. Physical fit, online demonstrations or common ownership are not substitutes for the manufacturer's regional documentation.

## What Warranty Does Ozito Offer?

Ozito's current Australian warranty page lists:

- a five-year replacement warranty for Power X-Change tools; and
- a three-year replacement warranty for Power X-Change batteries and chargers.

Conditions apply. Proof of purchase is required, claims are handled through Bunnings, and the PXC coverage depends on using documented Ozito batteries and chargers. Other Ozito categories may have different terms, so the model page and warranty document control.

Commercial buyers should pay particular attention to domestic-versus-trade-use exclusions. A long headline term does not automatically mean continuous commercial use is covered.

## What This Means for Buyers and Importers

For Australian DIY users, Ozito's value is the combination of a broad PXC ecosystem and a convenient Bunnings return channel. For importers, service teams and professional buyers, the key questions are different:

- Is the exact product approved for the destination market?
- Are batteries, chargers and replacement parts locally available?
- Does the intended use qualify under the warranty?
- Who is the responsible supplier for this model and production run?
- Can the retailer support the claim if the product was bought through resale or export?

These checks matter for cleaning-adjacent products such as blowers, wet-and-dry vacuums, pressure equipment and dust-producing tools. Filters, hoses, nozzles and consumables can determine lifetime value more than the bare-tool price.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Ozito tools?

Einhell Group owns Ozito. The Australian operation is Einhell Australia Pty Ltd, formerly Ozito Industries Pty Ltd.

### Does Bunnings own Ozito?

No. Bunnings is the principal retail and warranty-return channel, while Einhell is the disclosed corporate owner.

### Are Ozito tools made by Einhell?

Einhell owns and operates the brand, but individual products can be made through model-specific suppliers. Check the exact label and manual before naming a factory or country.

### Can I use an Einhell battery in an Ozito tool?

Do not rely on ownership or physical similarity. Use only combinations documented for the exact product and market; Ozito's warranty requires Ozito-branded PXC batteries and chargers.

### How long is the Ozito PXC warranty?

The current Australian warranty page lists five years for PXC tools and three years for PXC batteries and chargers, subject to its terms.

## Final Answer

Einhell owns Ozito and operates it through Einhell Australia. Bunnings is the central Australian sales and warranty-return channel, not the brand owner or universal manufacturer. Ozito's PXC range is a regional 18V ecosystem, while cross-market compatibility, manufacturing origin and warranty coverage must be confirmed for the exact model.
