**Erbauer is a Kingfisher own-exclusive tool brand, not an independent manufacturer with one disclosed factory.** Kingfisher controls the brand, develops the product offer and sources products globally. Its retail banners—including Screwfix and B&Q—sell Erbauer in their respective markets.

That structure answers the ownership question but not the factory question. Erbauer tools can be produced by different approved suppliers. The model number, manual, responsible-party statement and origin label are required before assigning a product to a particular manufacturer.

## Does Kingfisher Own Erbauer?

Kingfisher classifies Erbauer as one of its **own exclusive brands**. In retail terminology, that is a private-label or controlled-brand program: Kingfisher owns or controls the customer proposition and makes it available through its banners rather than treating it as an unrelated national brand.

Kingfisher's current brand page lists Erbauer alongside other group-exclusive names. Its FY 2025/26 results describe Erbauer as the number-one tools brand sold across the group and report the launch of a second-generation range with improvements to power, runtime, durability and guarantees.

Kingfisher is the parent retail group behind businesses that include Screwfix and B&Q in the UK and Castorama and Brico Dépôt in parts of continental Europe. Availability, product names and warranty routes can differ by banner and country.

## Is Erbauer a Screwfix Brand or a B&Q Brand?

Erbauer is best understood as a Kingfisher group brand sold through Kingfisher banners. Calling it only a Screwfix brand or only a B&Q brand misses the parent-company structure.

Screwfix currently operates a dedicated Erbauer brand range, while B&Q also lists Erbauer products. The retailer handles the transaction and often the first stage of support. Kingfisher's group functions coordinate the own-brand offer and sourcing.

This resembles other controlled retail programs, but the owners differ. [Kobalt is tied to Lowe's](/blog/who-makes-kobalt-tools-lowes-suppliers), while [Parkside is a Lidl private-label range](/blog/who-makes-parkside-tools-lidl-suppliers). A shared retail model does not imply a shared supplier.

## Who Actually Manufactures Erbauer Tools?

Kingfisher says its Offer & Sourcing organisation sources own-exclusive-brand products from across the globe. The group develops products in-house or with suppliers and uses a vendor network rather than presenting Erbauer as the output of one named factory.

That is the most defensible catalogue-level answer: **Kingfisher controls the brand and sourcing system; approved suppliers manufacture specific products.**

For a current Erbauer product, check:

1. the full model and retail SKU;
2. the manual's manufacturer or responsible-party section;
3. the country-of-origin label;
4. the declaration of conformity;
5. the battery compatibility list; and
6. the warranty attached to that listing.

One current Erbauer battery page identifies Kingfisher International Products Ltd and its EU counterpart as the manufacturer or responsible economic operator for that product. That legal statement is useful for the named battery; it does not prove those entities physically manufacture every Erbauer tool in one company-owned plant.

Visual resemblance to another brand is also insufficient. Contract factories can use shared component families, but tooling, electronics, quality specifications and commercial ownership can still differ.

## What Is the Erbauer EXT Battery Platform?

Screwfix describes EXT as Erbauer's cordless platform. Current 18V battery listings say the pack is compatible with Erbauer 18V EXT products, allowing multiple tools to share batteries and chargers within the documented system.

The catalogue also includes voltage and pack variations. Some products use 12V architecture, while a current 18/36V dual-voltage battery listing covers specified 18V, 36V and 72V applications using documented pack configurations.

![Erbauer EXT compatibility and warranty verification map](/images/blog/erbauer-ext-warranty-map.svg)

The safe compatibility sequence is:

- match the Erbauer platform name;
- match the voltage family;
- check whether the tool requires one or more packs;
- confirm the battery code on the current product page or manual; and
- use the destination-market charger and warranty terms.

A nominal voltage printed on two unrelated brands does not make their batteries interchangeable. Do not modify rails, terminals or battery electronics to force compatibility.

## How Long Is the Erbauer Warranty?

There is no single warranty term for every Erbauer product.

A current-generation Erbauer power-tool manual reviewed for this guide states a three-year guarantee for the power tool and two years for the battery and charger. At the same time, a current Screwfix listing for an individual 18V EXT battery displays a one-year manufacturer guarantee. Hand tools and other categories can have still different terms.

The apparent variation is why buyers should retain the exact product page and manual from the time of purchase. Registration requirements, proof of purchase, commercial-use exclusions and the retailer's repair or replacement process can all affect a claim.

Kingfisher's recent results say the new Erbauer range has strengthened guarantees, but that group statement should not overwrite the model-specific document.

## Is Erbauer a Professional Tool Brand?

Kingfisher positions Erbauer above basic entry-level ranges and sells it for demanding DIY and trade-oriented use. The second-generation product messaging emphasizes higher power, runtime and durability.

Positioning is not a technical specification. A professional buyer should still compare duty cycle, vibration, dust extraction, service access, consumable availability and warranty treatment for business use.

For cleaning and facilities work, relevant Erbauer categories can include wet-and-dry vacuums, blowers, pressure-related equipment and cutting tools with dust ports. Compatibility between a tool, hose and extractor is often adapter-specific.

## What This Means for Importers and Buyers

An Erbauer purchase involves three separate layers:

- **Brand layer:** Kingfisher controls the Erbauer market identity and product program.
- **Supply layer:** model-specific vendors and production sites make the goods.
- **Service layer:** the selling Kingfisher banner applies the local return, repair and warranty process.

For consumers, the key question is whether the local retailer supports the exact product. For commercial sourcing, supplier identity, conformity files, factory audit evidence and spare-parts continuity must be collected at SKU level.

The brand name alone cannot substitute for that documentation. This is the same core boundary that applies across [retailer private-label cleaning products](/blog/private-label-cleaning-products-from-china): the commercial brand and the physical factory are related but legally distinct roles.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Erbauer tools?

Kingfisher plc identifies Erbauer as one of its own-exclusive brands.

### Does Screwfix manufacture Erbauer tools?

No catalogue-wide evidence supports that statement. Screwfix is a Kingfisher retail banner; specific products are sourced through Kingfisher's vendor system.

### Who is the OEM for Erbauer?

There is no verified universal OEM for the full current range. Use the exact model's manual, responsible-party information and origin label.

### Are all Erbauer EXT batteries compatible?

Compatibility is broad within the documented platform, but 12V, 18V, 36V and multi-pack configurations require exact model matching.

### Does Erbauer have a three-year warranty?

Many current power-tool manuals state three years, but batteries, chargers, hand tools and older products can differ. The exact product document controls.

## Final Answer

Erbauer is Kingfisher's own-exclusive tool brand, sold through group banners such as Screwfix and B&Q. Kingfisher controls the offer and global sourcing, while product-specific suppliers manufacture the range. EXT compatibility and warranty coverage are real buying benefits, but both must be confirmed by voltage family, model and current local documentation.
