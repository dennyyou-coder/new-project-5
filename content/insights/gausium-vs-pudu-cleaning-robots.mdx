**Gausium and PUDU are China's two best-known commercial robot makers in cleaning, but they come from different directions.** Gausium has built cleaning robots since 2013 and sells little else. PUDU started with restaurant delivery robots in 2016 and has grown into cleaning, with a broad service-robot range. For a cleaning-only deployment both are credible; the right choice depends on your floors, building and local support.

## Which is better for large public buildings?

Gausium has published deployments in metros, airports and offices, such as 27 Omnie robots on the Shenzhen Metro, and its Scrubber 75 is built for large open floors. PUDU's BG1 series covers large-scrubber jobs and its CC1 family suits retail and offices.

## Which is better if you also need other robots?

PUDU. Its delivery and hospitality robots, and since 2026 its GT robotic mowers, let one supplier and one fleet app cover several jobs.

## Support

Ask each for the local partner, response time, spare robots and software-update terms in writing. PUDU opened a European headquarters in Neuss, Germany, in October 2026 for demonstrations, training, warehousing and repairs. Gausium works through global offices and dealers. In the US, check FCC status for new models; see the [FCC robot restrictions tracker](/topics/us-fcc-robot-restrictions).
