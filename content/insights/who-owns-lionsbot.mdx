**LionsBot is owned by LionsBot International Pte. Ltd., an independent Singapore company.** It was founded in 2018 by Dylan Ng, Michelle Seow and robotics professor Mohan Rajesh Elara. Its official materials name no parent company. Co-founder Dylan Ng is chief executive officer.

LionsBot is from Singapore, where it has its headquarters and its factory. It also has offices in the Netherlands, the United States and India.

## Does Nilfisk own LionsBot?

No. LionsBot and Nilfisk worked together on the Nilfisk SC25, introduced in 2024, which LionsBot makes at its Singapore factory. A product collaboration is not ownership. Nilfisk itself belongs to Freudenberg, as covered in [who owns Nilfisk](/blog/who-owns-nilfisk-freudenberg-manufacturing).

## Where are LionsBot robots made?

In Singapore. LionsBot opened an expanded 4,908-square-metre manufacturing and R&D facility in April 2024, with component warehousing, sub-assembly, final assembly, calibration and testing. The company says the plant can make up to 4,000 robots a year on a single shift.

## What does LionsBot make?

- **Scrubbing robots**: the R3 Scrub Pro, the compact R5, developed with feedback from German facility-services provider WISAG, and the larger R12 Rex Scrub.
- **Vacuum robots**: the R3 Vac.
- **LionsOS and fleet services** for scheduling and remote support.

LionsBot sells through local dealers, backed by remote support from headquarters and certified dealer technicians.
