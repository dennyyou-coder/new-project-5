**AEG Power Tools is operated by Techtronic Industries under license from AB Electrolux, which owns the AEG trademark.** TTI acquired the AEG brand license and the power-tool and accessories business in 2005 and continues to include AEG in its tool portfolio.

**Who manufactures AEG Power Tools?** TTI is the industrial operator and manufacturing group behind the current AEG power-tool business; Electrolux is the trademark owner, not the tool manufacturer. Regional TTI companies can act as seller or warranty provider, while the exact factory, legal manufacturer and country of origin remain model-specific and must be verified from the label, declaration and shipment documents.

So the short claim “TTI owns AEG” is incomplete. TTI controls the licensed power-tool operation, while Electrolux remains the trademark owner. AEG household appliances and AEG power tools can therefore carry the same name while sitting in different operating structures.

## Who Owns AEG Power Tools?

The accurate answer has two layers.

AB Electrolux owns the AEG trademark. TTI's own AEG brand page states that AEG is a registered trademark of AB Electrolux (publ) and is used under license. The same notice appears on current AEG Power Tools pages and in TTI corporate reporting.

TTI operates the power-tool business. Its corporate history says that in 2005 the group acquired the AEG brand license and the power-tool and accessories business. TTI now presents AEG alongside Milwaukee and Ryobi in its portfolio.

This is a licensed-brand operating structure: the trademark owner authorizes another group to use the mark in a defined category. A license can carry substantial product, marketing, manufacturing and distribution control without transferring the trademark itself.

## Does Electrolux Own AEG?

Yes, Electrolux owns the AEG trademark and operates major AEG appliance activities. Electrolux's corporate history says it acquired AEG's household-appliance business in 1994 and later obtained broader worldwide rights to the AEG brand and corporate name.

But “Electrolux owns AEG” does not answer who runs every product carrying the mark. Licensed categories must be checked separately. For power tools, the current official evidence identifies TTI as the operator under license.

Our separate guide to [AEG and Electrolux](/blog/who-owns-aeg-electrolux-relationship) focuses on appliances, floor care and the broader brand relationship. This article focuses on power tools, TTI's license, PRO18V and tool-specific manufacturing and warranty checks.

## When Did TTI Take Over AEG Power Tools?

TTI's corporate history dates the acquisition to 2005. The transaction covered the AEG brand license and the relevant power-tool and accessories business, together with other tool assets acquired in that period.

The wording matters. It supports current operational control of the tool business and an ongoing right to use the AEG name. It does not support saying that TTI purchased the entire AEG trademark from Electrolux.

Current TTI and AEG pages still carry the Electrolux license notice, showing that the distinction remains active rather than being only historical background.

## Who Manufactures AEG Power Tools?

AEG Power Tools sits inside TTI's product-development, sourcing and manufacturing system. TTI is a global tool manufacturer with multiple brands and facilities, but ownership of an operating business does not identify the factory for every AEG SKU.

![AEG Power Tools model, platform, origin and warranty verification map](/images/blog/aeg-power-tools-manufacturing-verification-map.svg)

TTI group factory information establishes manufacturing capability at corporate level. A specific AEG drill, grinder, vacuum, dust extractor, battery or accessory can follow a product- and market-specific route. Suppliers and production allocations can also change over a model's life.

For a commercial sourcing decision, obtain:

1. full model and revision;
2. country-of-origin marking;
3. manufacturer and importer on the declaration;
4. production date and serial number;
5. destination-market certification;
6. warranty entity and service route; and
7. factory evidence for the covered shipment if origin is material.

The Electrolux trademark notice proves the license boundary, not the physical place of manufacture.

## Where Are AEG Power Tools Made?

There is no reliable single-country answer for the complete current and historical range. AEG Power Tools participates in TTI's international manufacturing and sourcing network, while exact origin must be read from the product and its documents.

Avoid three common errors:

- treating AEG's German industrial heritage as proof that every tool is made in Germany;
- treating TTI's headquarters or group factory list as the origin of every model; and
- using the trademark owner's address as the legal manufacturer of a licensed tool.

An authorized product can be made outside the brand's country of origin. The useful procurement question is not “Where is AEG made?” but “Who made this model, where, for which market and under which declaration?”

## What Is the AEG PRO18V System?

PRO18V is AEG's current 18V cordless platform. AEG says PRO18V batteries are compatible with PRO18V tools, allowing users to share batteries within the documented system.

That compatibility statement has boundaries. It should not be extended to older AEG platforms, other voltages, products sold under different regional specifications or other TTI brands. TTI also owns Milwaukee and licenses or operates other tool brands, but batteries are not interchangeable merely because the brands share a parent group.

Before buying a battery or tool, match the product code, voltage, battery generation, charger and local manual. For the parent-company comparison, see [TTI's Milwaukee, Ryobi, Hoover, Vax and Oreck portfolio](/blog/who-owns-tti-milwaukee-ryobi-hoover-vax-oreck).

## Does AEG Power Tools Make Cleaning Equipment?

AEG's professional range includes site vacuums, dust extractors and other cleanup equipment alongside drills, saws, grinders and outdoor tools. These products are relevant to contractors and facility operators, but they are not the same business as Electrolux-operated AEG household vacuum cleaners.

For a site vacuum or extractor, verify wet or dry approval, filtration class, dust-exposure application, antistatic requirements, power-tool activation, consumables and local service. A shared trademark does not make household and construction-cleaning products technically interchangeable.

## How Does the AEG Power Tools Warranty Work?

AEG's reviewed European warranty page describes a standard one-year tool warranty with an additional two years available after qualifying registration within 30 days. It describes a standard one-year battery and charger warranty with an additional year after registration.

Those are regional terms, not a worldwide promise. Other countries can publish different periods or promotional extensions. Coverage can depend on purchase channel, product category, consumer or commercial use, registration timing and proof of purchase.

Check the local warranty page at the purchase date, register within the stated deadline if required and keep the confirmation. Do not convert “three-year tool warranty” into a claim about every battery, charger, accessory or market.

## What This Means for Buyers and Distributors

AEG Power Tools demonstrates why brand ownership needs more precision than a single parent-company label. Electrolux owns the trademark; TTI holds the power-tool license and operates the business; a regional company sells or warrants the product; and a specific factory or supplier makes the model.

For due diligence, record all those layers separately. Confirm that a seller is authorized for the destination market, that PRO18V compatibility covers the named models and that the warranty registration route is available before stock is placed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does TTI own AEG Power Tools?

TTI operates the AEG power-tool business and holds the brand license for that category. AB Electrolux owns the AEG trademark, so “TTI owns AEG” is incomplete without the license distinction.

### Is AEG Power Tools owned by Electrolux?

Electrolux owns the AEG trademark, but TTI operates the power-tool business under license. Electrolux's AEG appliance business is a separate operating context.

### When did TTI acquire AEG Power Tools?

TTI's corporate history dates its acquisition of the AEG brand license and power-tool and accessories business to 2005.

### Are AEG and Milwaukee batteries interchangeable?

Do not assume so. Both tool businesses sit in TTI's portfolio, but each uses its own documented battery systems and compatibility rules.

### Are all AEG tools made in Germany?

No universal German-origin claim is supported. Verify the exact model's label, declaration and shipment evidence.

## Final Answer

AB Electrolux owns the AEG trademark, while Techtronic Industries operates AEG Power Tools under a license acquired with the power-tool business in 2005. TTI manages the tool brand and supply system, but exact factory, origin, PRO18V compatibility and warranty remain product- and market-specific.
