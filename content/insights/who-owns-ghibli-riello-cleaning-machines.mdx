**Ghibli is owned by Riello Group.** The Ghibli brand is run by Riello Cleaning Machines S.p.A., which Riello Industries Group has owned outright since 2021. Until July 2025 the company was called Ghibli & Wirbel; the new name applies to the company, while Ghibli stays a product brand.

Ghibli is Italian. It was founded in 1968 and makes its machines in Dorno, in the province of Pavia. The company's registered office is in Verona.

## Where are Ghibli machines made?

In Dorno, Italy. The company runs separate assembly lines for vacuums, industrial vacuums, single-disc machines, steam generators and scrubber dryers. It moulds most of the components it uses on its own automated injection-moulding lines.

## What does Ghibli make?

Professional and industrial vacuums, scrubber dryers, sweepers, single-disc machines, and carpet, steam and pressure-cleaning equipment. It sells through dealers and its own sales offices.
