**Chervon Holdings Limited is a publicly traded power-tool and outdoor-power-equipment group.** It is listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange under stock code 2285. Current HKEX disclosure identifies founder, chair and CEO Pan Longquan as the controlling shareholder, with 50.92% of voting shares as of March 20, 2026.

EGO, FLEX and SKIL do not own Chervon. They are distinct brands inside the group. Chervon is both the corporate parent and an integrated developer and manufacturer, but the exact factory, seller and warranty still depend on the brand, model and country.

## Who Owns Chervon?

Chervon Holdings Limited is the listed parent. It began trading on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on December 30, 2021, under stock code 2285.

Public listing means investors can own shares in Chervon Holdings. It does not mean the company lacks a controlling shareholder. The HKEX Disclosure of Interests substantial-shareholder list showed Pan Longquan with 50.92% of voting shares on March 20, 2026. A November 2025 board filing identified him as chairperson and chief executive officer.

That percentage is a dated, high-sensitivity fact. Investors should consult the current HKEX register rather than assuming it remains unchanged after the article date. Other disclosed interests can overlap through family, trust, controlled-company or attribution rules and should not be added mechanically.

## Is Chervon the Same Company as Chevron?

No. Chervon, without the second “e,” is a power-tool and outdoor-equipment group. Chevron is a separate energy company. The similar English spelling creates search confusion but no ownership relationship.

## Which Brands Does Chervon Own?

Chervon's portfolio includes EGO, FLEX, SKIL, DEVON and X-TRON, among other current group identities. The important business point is segmentation rather than a single badge applied to the same product.

- **EGO** focuses on cordless outdoor power equipment and related battery systems.
- **FLEX** is positioned as a professional power-tool brand with German heritage.
- **SKIL** covers consumer and professional tools with its own PWRCORE platforms.
- **DEVON** and **X-TRON** address additional professional and regional segments.

The portfolio can change, and each brand has its own market strategy. Common parent ownership does not prove common battery interfaces, identical specifications, shared warranties or the same authorized retail channels.

## How Did FLEX and SKIL Join Chervon?

Chervon acquired FLEX-Elektrowerkzeuge GmbH in 2013. The German business retained its name and professional-tool identity while joining Chervon's wider development, manufacturing and distribution network.

Chervon completed its acquisition of the SKIL businesses in 2017. SKIL therefore moved from its earlier Bosch ownership into Chervon's portfolio. Historical articles that call Bosch the current SKIL owner are outdated.

EGO has a different history inside the group and should not be described as an acquisition simply because FLEX and SKIL were acquired. The broader lesson is to document each brand transition separately.

## Is Chervon a Manufacturer or Just a Brand Owner?

Chervon is both a brand group and an integrated product company. Its listing announcement describes a value chain spanning research and development, sourcing, manufacturing, testing, sales, distribution and after-sales service.

![Chervon integrated value chain and model-level verification map](/images/blog/chervon-manufacturing-network-map.svg)

The group reports major manufacturing operations in Nanjing, China, and Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Chervon's April 2026 opening of an additional Vietnam campus shows that the network continues to expand.

This supports a stronger manufacturing conclusion than merely calling Chervon a marketing parent. It still does not prove that every EGO mower, FLEX grinder or SKIL drill came from the same campus. Product generation, category, destination market, external components and capacity allocation can change the answer.

## Where Are Chervon Tools Made?

Chervon has substantial production in China and Vietnam, while individual brands can retain additional engineering or manufacturing capabilities. FLEX, for example, retains its German corporate and industrial heritage.

For an exact product, verify:

1. brand, model, SKU and revision;
2. product and packaging origin markings;
3. manufacturer and importer on the declaration;
4. destination-market certification;
5. factory evidence for the covered production lot; and
6. warranty entity and authorized service route.

A Chervon annual report or campus announcement can establish group capability. It cannot replace the label and documents for a particular commercial shipment.

## Are EGO, FLEX and SKIL Batteries Interchangeable?

Do not assume so. Each brand markets named battery systems with different voltage, mechanical, electronic and warranty requirements.

Within one brand, compatibility also has a defined scope. A statement about the SKIL PWRCORE 20 platform does not necessarily apply to PWRCORE 12 or to an older SKIL generation. FLEX 24V in North America should not be converted into a claim about every FLEX system worldwide. EGO's outdoor-equipment batteries remain part of the EGO system.

Only use an official compatibility statement that names the tool, battery, charger and market. Group ownership is not technical compatibility.

## How Are Chervon Products Distributed?

Distribution differs by brand and country. Chervon combines its own commercial entities with home centers, professional dealers, ecommerce and specialist outdoor-power channels.

Retail relationships can also change. A launch partner or home-center exclusive arrangement is not ownership of the brand and may not cover every channel, country or future contract. Buyers should check the current official dealer locator and warranty eligibility before purchasing from a marketplace seller.

## How Do Chervon Warranties Work?

There is no universal “Chervon warranty” that safely replaces brand and product terms. FLEX, SKIL and EGO publish their own regional policies, and the named warrantor can be a Chervon regional subsidiary rather than the listed parent.

Coverage can depend on category, commercial or consumer use, registration, proof of purchase and authorized sellers. Batteries and chargers frequently have terms different from tools. Use the exact market policy and keep a dated copy.

## What This Means for Buyers and Distributors

Chervon offers a combination of public-company access, differentiated brands and integrated manufacturing scale. That structure can support product development and international supply, but it also creates several layers that procurement teams must keep separate.

Record the listed parent and current control date, brand owner, contracting seller, factory or supplier, origin, importer and warranty entity. That evidence is more useful than saying every group product is “made by Chervon” without a model boundary.

### Is Chervon a Chinese company?

Chervon Holdings is a Hong Kong-listed group with roots and major operations in China, plus an expanding global network including Vietnam. Legal domicile, listing venue, headquarters and factory location are separate facts.

### Is Chervon publicly traded?

Yes. Chervon Holdings Limited trades on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange under 2285.

## Final Answer

Chervon Holdings Limited is the public parent behind EGO, FLEX and SKIL. Founder, chair and CEO Pan Longquan was the disclosed controlling shareholder at the dated 2026 HKEX check.
