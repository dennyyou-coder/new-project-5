**GE Appliances is owned by Haier Smart Home Co., Ltd.** The U.S. operating company is **Haier US Appliance Solutions, Inc.**, which does business as GE Appliances. General Electric sold the appliance business to Haier in 2016 and no longer owns it.

The familiar GE name requires a second answer: GE Appliances continues to use the GE brand under a long-term trademark license. Company ownership, trademark ownership, manufacturing location and warranty responsibility are therefore related but different facts.

## What Company Actually Owns GE Appliances?

GE Appliances is a wholly owned business within **Haier Smart Home**, the publicly listed global appliance company. GE Appliances' own corporate disclosure identifies the legal U.S. organization as Haier US Appliance Solutions, Inc. and says it does business as GE Appliances.

That structure places the business inside the same listed portfolio as Haier, Fisher & Paykel, Candy and other appliance brands. It does not turn GE Appliances into an informal label for all Haier products. The Louisville-based company maintains its own U.S. leadership, brands, manufacturing operations, distribution and service system.

This is also why the most accurate ownership chain is not simply “GE is owned by Haier.” It is:

1. Haier Group is the wider corporate group.
2. Haier Smart Home is the listed appliance platform.
3. Haier US Appliance Solutions is the legal U.S. subsidiary.
4. GE Appliances is the business name used in the market.

Our [Haier ownership guide](/blog/who-owns-haier-appliances-brand-portfolio) explains the parent-company portfolio in more detail.

## Did General Electric Sell GE Appliances?

Yes. General Electric agreed in January 2016 to sell the appliance business to Qingdao Haier for $5.4 billion, and the transaction closed in June 2016. GE's later corporate materials state plainly that GE no longer owns the appliance business.

The sale transferred the operating business, its people, plants and product organization. Louisville remained the headquarters. Since then, GE Appliances has expanded under Haier ownership while continuing to present itself as an American appliance company.

This history matters because some shoppers assume the current GE Appliances company is one of the three public companies that emerged from the former General Electric. It is not part of GE Aerospace, GE Vernova or GE HealthCare. It belongs to Haier Smart Home.

## Who Owns the GE Appliances Name?

The operating company and the trademark are not owned in the same way.

As part of the 2016 transaction, General Electric entered into a long-term agreement allowing Haier's appliance business to continue using the GE Appliances brand. GE's own shareholder materials describe Haier as a separate company with a trademark license to use the GE brand on appliance products.

For buyers and suppliers, that creates four distinct layers:

- **General Electric** remains the relevant trademark owner and licensor.
- **Haier Smart Home** owns the appliance business.
- **Haier US Appliance Solutions** operates the U.S. company.
- **GE Appliances** is the customer-facing business and brand family.

A trademark license does not make General Electric the manufacturer or warranty provider for a new refrigerator. Conversely, Haier ownership does not mean the GE name has transferred outright.

## Where Are GE Appliances Made?

GE Appliances has a substantial U.S. production base. Its current company overview says employees at **nine manufacturing plants across five states** build appliances for the U.S. market. The network includes Appliance Park in Louisville, Kentucky, and operations in Alabama, Georgia, South Carolina and Tennessee.

Official examples show how product assignments vary:

- Appliance Park is the headquarters and the company's largest manufacturing complex.
- Roper Corporation in LaFayette, Georgia produces cooking products.
- Camden, South Carolina was converted into a water-heater manufacturing center.
- The Selmer, Tennessee operation makes selected high-end refrigeration products and packaged terminal air conditioners.
- GE Appliances announced a major 2025 investment to move production of selected front-load washers and combination washer-dryers to Louisville.

![GE Appliances U.S. manufacturing footprint and model-level origin boundary](/images/blog/ge-appliances-us-manufacturing-map.svg)

These facts support a strong conclusion that GE Appliances is a major U.S. manufacturer. They do not support the claim that every GE, Café, Monogram, GE Profile, Hotpoint or Haier-branded product is made in the United States.

The exact origin can change by model, generation and market. Buyers should check the rating plate, carton, customs documentation and country-of-origin statement for the actual unit.

## Does Haier Manufacture Every GE Appliance?

Haier Smart Home owns GE Appliances, but “made by Haier” is too imprecise for product-level due diligence.

Many products are made in GE Appliances' own U.S. operations. Other models or components can come through other facilities and approved suppliers. Even within one product category, two models can have different final-assembly locations or component sources.

The responsible company may also change by transaction. A U.S. retail product can name GE Appliances as the brand and service organization, while a regional importer or project distributor appears on commercial paperwork elsewhere.

For a reliable answer, match the model number to:

1. the manufacturer or responsible-company statement;
2. the country-of-origin label;
3. the importer and seller;
4. the declaration or certification record;
5. the product warranty.

## Which Brands Are Part of GE Appliances?

GE Appliances says it manufactures and sells products under **Monogram, Café, GE Profile, GE, Haier and Hotpoint** in its U.S. operation.

Those names serve different positions. Monogram and Café emphasize premium design and built-in kitchen applications; GE Profile sits above the core GE range; Hotpoint addresses value-oriented categories; Haier is also sold through the same U.S. operating platform.

The portfolio should not be confused with worldwide trademark ownership. Hotpoint, for example, has had regional ownership differences outside the United States. The correct parent and warranty answer must match the country and product.

## How Does GE Appliances Handle Warranty and Service?

GE Appliances' U.S. support material says new products include a manufacturer's limited warranty, typically one year, while certain products and components can carry different or longer terms. The owner's manual for the exact model is the controlling document.

The company now presents much of its service experience under the **Bodewell** name, using factory-trained and authorized service channels. That service branding does not replace the written product warranty or prove coverage for a grey-market unit.

Commercial buyers should verify the warranty period, covered labor and parts, installation exclusions, commercial-use rules, authorized territory and parts availability before purchase. A warranty statement on a U.S. website should not be copied to another country.

| Check | What it establishes |

| Exact model and serial number | Product identity, generation and production code |
| Rating plate and carton | Legal markings, voltage and stated country of origin |

| Factory evidence | Site responsible for the model, not merely a group facility |
| Warranty manual | Coverage, provider, period, exclusions and service route |

For suppliers, confirm that a request comes from GE Appliances or an authorized contractor and identify the plant, program, purchasing entity and quality agreement. A Haier group name alone does not establish purchasing authority for a GE Appliances project.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is GE Appliances owned by China?

GE Appliances is owned by Haier Smart Home, a China-headquartered listed appliance company. The operating business remains headquartered in Louisville and has a large U.S. workforce and manufacturing network.

### Does General Electric still own GE Appliances?

No. General Electric sold the appliance business to Haier in 2016. It remains connected through the long-term GE trademark license.

### Is GE Appliances the same company as Haier?

No. GE Appliances is the business name of Haier US Appliance Solutions, a subsidiary of Haier Smart Home. It is part of the Haier portfolio but retains a distinct U.S. operation.

### Are all GE Appliances made in America?

No. GE Appliances operates nine U.S. manufacturing plants across five states and makes many products domestically, but origin remains model-specific.

### Who handles GE appliance warranties?

GE Appliances and its designated service channels handle covered products in the applicable market. The exact owner's manual and warranty terms determine coverage.

### Does Haier own the GE trademark?

No. The appliance business uses the GE name under a long-term trademark license associated with the 2016 sale.

## Final Answer

GE Appliances is owned by Haier Smart Home and operated in the United States through Haier US Appliance Solutions, Inc. General Electric sold the business in 2016 but licenses the GE name for appliance use.

GE Appliances is also a significant American manufacturer, with nine plants across five states. That footprint should not be generalized to every model. Buyers, dealers and suppliers should verify the exact legal company, brand license, factory, country of origin, seller and warranty document for the product and market involved.
