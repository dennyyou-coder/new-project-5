**GE Appliances is owned by Haier Smart Home, the publicly listed home-appliance company within the wider Haier Group.** Haier completed the acquisition of the U.S. appliance business in June 2016. The operating company is **Haier US Appliance Solutions, Inc., doing business as GE Appliances**, and it remains headquartered at Appliance Park in Louisville, Kentucky.

The familiar name needs one important qualification: GE Appliances uses the GE trademark under a long-term license. The appliance company, the trademark rights and the wider General Electric corporate identity are not the same thing. Ownership also does not make every GE-branded appliance Chinese-made. GE Appliances operates a large U.S. manufacturing system, while the origin and responsible entity remain model-specific.

## What Company Actually Owns GE Appliances?

The direct group-level owner is **Haier Smart Home Co., Ltd.**, the listed appliance platform controlled within Haier Group. Haier announced completion of the GE Appliances acquisition on June 6, 2016, with the U.S. headquarters remaining in Louisville.

The U.S. legal identity is more specific. GE Appliances' corporate reporting identifies **Haier US Appliance Solutions, Inc.** as the company doing business under the GE Appliances name. That distinction matters in contracts, compliance documents and supplier onboarding. A buyer should not replace the legal entity on an invoice with “Haier” or “GE” simply because those names are better known.

The relationship therefore has several layers:

1. **Haier Group** is the wider corporate group.
2. **Haier Smart Home** is the listed global home-appliance company.
3. **Haier US Appliance Solutions, Inc. dba GE Appliances** is the principal U.S. operating identity.
4. Individual subsidiaries, plants, brands and local sellers can carry narrower responsibilities.

Our broader [Haier appliance ownership guide](/blog/who-owns-haier-appliances-brand-portfolio) explains how GE Appliances sits alongside Fisher & Paykel, Candy and other portfolio businesses without becoming the same operating company.

## Is GE Appliances Still Part of General Electric?

No. General Electric sold the appliance business in 2016. The company that makes and sells GE Appliances products is now part of Haier Smart Home, not the modern GE corporate group.

The continued GE name comes from a trademark arrangement. Haier's completion announcement said GE Appliances would continue using the GE Appliances brand under a long-term license, with an initial 40-year term including extensions. That is why the brand can retain more than a century of U.S. recognition after the corporate ownership changed.

This is not just a branding technicality. A distributor should distinguish among:

- ownership of the appliance operating company;
- ownership or licensing of a trademark;
- the legal seller for a country or channel;
- the factory responsible for an exact product; and
- the company providing the local warranty.

A trademark license does not make every GE business a Haier company, and Haier ownership does not transfer the wider GE corporate identity into the appliance portfolio.

## Which Brands Belong to GE Appliances?

GE Appliances describes a U.S. portfolio that includes **Monogram, Cafe, GE Profile, GE, Haier and Hotpoint**. The brands cover different price positions, channels and product propositions.

- **Monogram** addresses the luxury built-in appliance segment.
- **Cafe** emphasizes customizable premium design and finishes.
- **GE Profile** combines mass-premium appliances with connected features.
- **GE** is the broad core appliance brand.
- **Hotpoint** serves value-oriented major-appliance categories in the U.S. context.
- **Haier** gives the company an additional compact and mainstream appliance route in selected channels.

This portfolio should not be confused with universal worldwide trademark ownership. Hotpoint, for example, has different corporate paths by region, much as [Hoover ownership is divided between the Americas and Europe](/blog/who-owns-hoover-tti-haier-candy). The country, product class and contracting entity remain essential.

## Where Are GE Appliances Made?

GE Appliances has one of the most visible U.S. manufacturing footprints in the home-appliance sector. Its current company page describes manufacturing across five states, with the largest operation at **Appliance Park in Louisville, Kentucky**.

The official operations material identifies several product-focused locations:

- **Louisville, Kentucky:** washers, dryers, dishwashers, bottom-freezer and multi-door refrigerators, plus components and molding operations;
- **Decatur, Alabama:** top-freezer refrigerators sold under GE and Hotpoint;
- **LaFayette, Georgia:** ranges, cooktops and wall ovens;
- **Selmer, Tennessee:** selected Monogram, Cafe and GE Profile refrigeration and air-conditioning products;
- **Camden, South Carolina:** water heaters;
- **Stamford, Connecticut:** selected small-batch small appliances through the CoCREATE microfactory.

The company has continued to change this network. In 2025 it announced a major investment in Louisville washer production, including the transfer of additional models. In January 2026 it announced first-time water-filter manufacturing at Appliance Park as part of a wider U.S. investment program.

These facts demonstrate substantial American manufacturing. They do not prove that every GE Appliances model is made in the United States. GE Appliances' own “Made in America” qualification applies to designated products and content thresholds. Refrigeration, cooking, laundry, air-conditioning, water and small-appliance ranges can use different sites and supply chains.

## Is GE Appliances an American Company or a Chinese Company?

The most accurate short description is **a U.S.-headquartered and U.S.-manufacturing appliance company owned by China-based Haier Smart Home**.

Calling it only American omits the current corporate owner. Calling it only Chinese omits its legal operation, headquarters, workforce, factories and market identity in the United States. Both dimensions matter, and neither settles a product's country of origin.

For sourcing work, separate at least four questions:

1. Where is the parent company based?
2. Which legal company signs the contract?
3. Where is the exact model assembled?
4. Where do key components and software responsibilities sit?

The answer can span more than one country without contradiction.

## Does GE Appliances Manufacture Every Product It Sells?

GE Appliances is a major manufacturer, but no brand-level statement should be used as proof that one company-owned plant makes every finished product or component.

Its catalogue includes major appliances, air conditioners, water products, RV appliances and small appliances. Different categories can use different facilities, subsidiaries and approved supply partners. A product may be designed and quality-controlled within GE Appliances while using externally sourced components or a manufacturing arrangement specific to that model.

The reliable evidence is the model rating plate, carton, country-of-origin marking, safety certification, declaration, factory audit and purchase agreement. A general corporate manufacturing map provides context; it does not replace SKU-level documentation.

## How Does GE Appliances Connect to Cleaning and Small Appliances?

GE Appliances is relevant to cleaning-industry suppliers even though it is best known for large appliances. Laundry and dishwashing are major cleaning categories, and the company also sells water-filtration products, disposers and selected small appliances.

For a vacuum, motor, pump, filter, brush, electronics or plastics supplier, a GE Appliances relationship may offer access to buyers who manage adjacent home-appliance categories. But the portfolio is not a promise that one purchasing team controls every category. Small-batch innovation at FirstBuild or CoCREATE, a major Appliance Park program and a brand-specific premium project can have different technical owners and qualification routes.

This is why the opportunity should be framed by product program: exact brand, category, model, factory, engineering owner, purchasing entity and destination market.

## What Buyers and Suppliers Should Verify

| Document or check | What it should establish |
|---|---|
| Legal company name | Whether the counterparty is Haier US Appliance Solutions, a subsidiary, distributor or another entity |
| Brand and territory | Whether GE, GE Profile, Cafe, Monogram, Hotpoint or Haier rights apply in the destination market |
| Model and serial label | Exact SKU, electrical rating, production code and responsible-company information |
| Country-of-origin marking | Origin for the actual product, not an inference from the parent company |
| Factory evidence | Plant responsible for the model and whether production is owned or contracted |
| Trademark notice | Licensed-name scope and any regional limitation |
| Compliance file | Standards, importer, responsible signatory and model coverage |
| Warranty terms | Local provider, duration, exclusions, labor and parts responsibility |
| Supply agreement | Tooling, approved sites, quality control, change notification and recall obligations |

Supplier fraud checks should also compare the company domain, physical address, bank account and purchasing contact. Similar-looking Haier and GE Appliances names are not interchangeable authorization.

Haier Smart Home owns GE Appliances. Haier completed the acquisition in June 2016.

### What is the legal company behind GE Appliances?

Corporate materials identify Haier US Appliance Solutions, Inc., doing business as GE Appliances. A specific transaction can still name a subsidiary or local entity.

### Is GE Appliances owned by General Electric?

## Final Answer

GE Appliances is owned by Haier Smart Home, the listed appliance company within Haier Group. The principal U.S. operating identity is Haier US Appliance Solutions, Inc., doing business as GE Appliances, headquartered in Louisville, Kentucky.

The company is no longer part of General Electric, but it continues using the GE name under a long-term trademark license. It sells Monogram, Cafe, GE Profile, GE, Haier and Hotpoint appliances and operates substantial U.S. manufacturing across several states. Those group and factory facts do not establish the origin or responsible company for every SKU. Buyers and suppliers should verify the exact model, legal seller, plant, importer, trademark scope and warranty provider before making a product-level claim.
