**Electrolux is owned through AB Electrolux (publ), a Swedish public company listed on Nasdaq Stockholm. It is not a subsidiary of Whirlpool, Bosch, Miele or another appliance group.** At the end of 2025, Investor AB was the largest shareholder, holding 17.9% of share capital and 30.4% of voting rights, while other institutions and tens of thousands of registered shareholders held the rest.

AB Electrolux is the parent of the household-appliance group behind the Electrolux, AEG and Frigidaire brands. Electrolux Professional is no longer part of this group: it was distributed to shareholders and separately listed in March 2020.

## What Company Owns Electrolux?

The corporate parent is **AB Electrolux (publ)**, headquartered in Stockholm. Its Class A and Class B shares trade on Nasdaq Stockholm, with one vote for an A share and one-tenth of a vote for a B share.

The dual-class structure means capital ownership and voting power are not identical. Electrolux's [2025 annual report](https://www.electroluxgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/02/Annual-Report-2025.pdf) reported 283,077,393 registered shares at year-end and tens of thousands of registered shareholders.

At 31 December 2025:

| Shareholder | Share capital | Voting rights |
|---|---:|---:|
| Investor AB | 17.9% | 30.4% |
| Causeway Capital Management LLC | 12.8% | 10.1% |

Investor AB was the largest shareholder, but it did not own all of Electrolux. The accurate answer is that AB Electrolux is publicly owned, with Investor AB holding the largest voting position disclosed at that date.

## Is Electrolux a Swedish Company?

Yes. AB Electrolux is a Swedish public limited company with headquarters in Stockholm and a corporate history dating to 1919. The company operates globally, so Swedish ownership and headquarters do not mean every Electrolux appliance is made in Sweden.

This distinction applies to almost every global appliance group:

- country of corporate origin;
- stock-exchange listing;
- brand heritage;
- factory location;
- product country of origin; and
- local seller or warranty provider

are separate facts.

## What Brands Does Electrolux Own?

Electrolux Group identifies **Electrolux, AEG and Frigidaire** as its three main appliance brands. Its official reporting says the group sells household products in around 120 markets.

The main-brand structure has different positions:

| Brand | Group role | Primary market context |
|---|---|---|
| Electrolux | Global group brand associated with Scandinavian design and household-appliance systems | Broad international presence |
| AEG | Premium appliance brand associated with precision performance and German engineering heritage | Particularly important in Europe |
| Frigidaire | Major North American appliance brand with mass-market through premium extensions | Primarily North America |

The group also has regional brand histories and licensing arrangements. A brand list does not prove that two products share the same specification, factory or service provider.

## Does Electrolux Own Frigidaire and AEG?

Yes. AEG and Frigidaire are current main brands inside Electrolux Group.

AEG has German industrial and appliance heritage. Electrolux acquired AEG Hausgeräte in 1994 and later obtained worldwide AEG brand and corporate-name rights. Frigidaire is the group's central North American brand, with Frigidaire Gallery and Frigidaire Professional extensions used in higher-value segments.

“Electrolux owns the brand” should not be translated into “AB Electrolux is the seller on every invoice.” Regional Electrolux companies can operate local websites, import products, sell directly or administer service. Licensed categories can add another operator outside the core factory route.

For the Frigidaire-specific ownership history, North American factory context and model-level origin checks, see the separate guide to [who owns Frigidaire](/blog/who-owns-frigidaire-electrolux-manufacturing).

## Is Electrolux Professional the Same Company?

No. Electrolux Professional AB is a separate listed company today.

AB Electrolux distributed the shares of its professional-products subsidiary to Electrolux shareholders, and [Electrolux Professional began trading separately](https://www.electroluxgroup.com/en/distribution-of-electrolux-professional-30752/) on Nasdaq Stockholm on 23 March 2020.

The two businesses share history and a name, but the current boundary is clear:

- AB Electrolux focuses on household appliances and the consumer group discussed here.
- Electrolux Professional operates professional food-service, beverage and laundry solutions as a separate company.

For a commercial laundry or kitchen project, the product documentation and contracting entity determine which company is involved. The Electrolux wordmark alone is not enough.

## Who Manufactures Electrolux Appliances?

Electrolux Group manufactures through a global network of factories and suppliers. The group does not use one country or one plant for every refrigerator, washer, dishwasher, cooker, vacuum or air-care product.

Current official examples illustrate the network:

- In October 2025, Electrolux announced an expansion of its **Kinston, North Carolina dishwasher factory**, describing it as the North American center of excellence for dish production.
- In 2025, the group announced the first phase of a new factory in **São José dos Pinhais, Brazil**, its fifth plant in the country.
- The 2025 annual report describes manufacturing and restructuring across business areas rather than assigning one origin to the Electrolux brand.

These examples prove that Electrolux operates real manufacturing facilities in multiple regions. They do not establish the origin of a specific appliance without a model-level record.

## Does Electrolux Make Every Component?

No global appliance manufacturer needs to make every component internally. An appliance combines cabinets, motors, compressors, pumps, electronics, batteries, sensors, software and bought-in materials. Electrolux can control design and final assembly while sourcing selected parts from approved suppliers.

The buyer's question should be more precise:

- Which legal entity owns the specification?
- Which factory assembles the exact model?
- Which suppliers provide safety-critical components?
- Which company signs the declaration of conformity?
- Who controls engineering changes?
- Who supplies parts and pays warranty claims?

Those answers matter more than a blanket claim that the product is “made by Electrolux.”

## Is Electrolux Owned by Whirlpool or Beko?

No. AB Electrolux is an independent listed company.

Whirlpool Corporation is a separate U.S.-listed appliance company. Beko and Beko Europe belong to the Arçelik/Beko corporate structure. Market transactions involving factories, brands or regional businesses do not turn AB Electrolux into their subsidiary.

This distinction is especially important after large appliance-industry transactions. A company can sell a factory, exit a country, license a trademark or reorganize a regional business without transferring ownership of its global listed parent.

For comparison, see World Clean Biz's guide to [Whirlpool and Beko Europe](/blog/who-owns-whirlpool-appliances-beko-europe).

## Does Electrolux Still Own Eureka?

No. Electrolux sold the North American Eureka vacuum-cleaner brand and most related assets to Midea in 2016. Eureka's older association with Electrolux is historical, not current ownership evidence.

The [current Eureka ownership guide](/blog/who-owns-eureka-midea-electrolux-manufacturing) explains that separate path. This example shows why old product manuals and corporate histories must be dated before they are used in a current brand chart.

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

| Check | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| Exact brand and model | Product identity rather than group-level description |
| Rating plate and country-of-origin mark | Stated origin and electrical specification for the unit |
| Legal manufacturer | Entity responsible for production and technical documentation |
| Regional seller or importer | Company placing the product on the destination market |
| Declaration of conformity | Applicable standards and responsible signatory |
| Factory evidence | Production site for the exact model or contract program |
| Trademark or licensing agreement | Permission to use Electrolux, AEG or another group mark |
| Warranty terms | Provider, coverage, labor, exclusions and authorized service route |
| Connected-service terms | App provider, data handling, updates and regional feature availability |
| Electrolux Professional entity check | Confirms whether a professional product belongs to the separate company |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Electrolux?

Shareholders own AB Electrolux, the Nasdaq Stockholm-listed parent of Electrolux Group. Investor AB was the largest shareholder by votes at the end of 2025.

### Is Electrolux owned by Investor AB?

Investor AB is the largest disclosed shareholder, not the sole owner. It held 17.9% of capital and 30.4% of votes at 31 December 2025.

### Is Electrolux a public company?

Yes. AB Electrolux has Class A and Class B shares listed on Nasdaq Stockholm.

### What brands does Electrolux own?

The group's three main appliance brands are Electrolux, AEG and Frigidaire. It also has regional brand and licensing arrangements that should be checked by market.

### Who makes Electrolux appliances?

Electrolux Group makes appliances through a global factory and supplier network. The manufacturer and country of origin must be verified for the exact model.

### Is AEG owned by Electrolux?

Yes. AEG is a main Electrolux Group appliance brand with German heritage and Swedish corporate ownership.

### Is Frigidaire owned by Electrolux?

Yes. Frigidaire is Electrolux Group's main North American appliance brand.

### Is Electrolux Professional owned by Electrolux Group?

No. Electrolux Professional has been a separate Nasdaq Stockholm-listed company since March 2020.

### Is Electrolux owned by Whirlpool or Beko?

No. AB Electrolux is an independent public company and not a subsidiary of Whirlpool Corporation or Beko.

## Final Answer

Electrolux Group is owned through publicly traded AB Electrolux. Investor AB held the largest voting position at the end of 2025, but it was one shareholder among many and did not own the whole company.

AB Electrolux operates the household-appliance group behind Electrolux, AEG and Frigidaire. It manufactures through a global network, while the exact factory, importer, seller and warranty provider remain product- and market-specific. Electrolux Professional must be kept outside this ownership chart because it has been an independent listed company since 2020.
