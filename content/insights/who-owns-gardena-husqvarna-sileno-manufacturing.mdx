**GARDENA is owned by Husqvarna Group.** The German garden-equipment company says it has been a member of the Swedish group since 2007 and operates as a separate Gardena Division. Husqvarna AB (publ) is the listed parent behind the group.

**Who manufactures GARDENA products?** GARDENA manufactures through its own factory network in Germany and the Czech Republic, while additional products and components come from Husqvarna Group plants in Sweden, England and the United States and from qualified global suppliers. That network does not identify the legal manufacturer, factory or country of origin of every hose fitting, pump, battery tool or SILENO mower, so those details must be checked for the exact model and market.

GARDENA remains a distinct consumer brand and operating system for residential watering, lawn care, garden tools and smart products. Group ownership does not mean every GARDENA product is a relabeled Husqvarna machine or that batteries, robot-mower parts and warranties are interchangeable.

## When Did Husqvarna Acquire GARDENA?

GARDENA’s official history states that the company became part of Husqvarna Group in 2007. Founded in Ulm in 1961, GARDENA had developed from a garden-tool trading business into a manufacturer of connected garden-care systems.

Husqvarna Group’s current reporting separates three divisions: Husqvarna Forest & Garden, Gardena and Husqvarna Construction. The Gardena Division focuses on residential customers, particularly watering, lawn, tree, hedge, surface care and smart gardening.

For the broader parent-company structure, see [Who Owns Husqvarna? Automower, Motorcycles and the Company Structure Explained](/blog/who-owns-husqvarna-automower-motorcycles).

## Is GARDENA a German or Swedish Company?

Both descriptions can be correct when properly qualified. GARDENA is a German-founded brand and operating business headquartered in Ulm. Its ultimate listed parent, Husqvarna AB, is Swedish.

Corporate nationality does not settle product origin. A German brand owned by a Swedish group can manufacture through plants in several countries and external suppliers.

## Where Are GARDENA Products Made?

GARDENA’s official help center says most products and spare parts continue to come from its factory network in Germany and the Czech Republic. Other products and components are made in Husqvarna Group plants in Sweden, England and the United States. GARDENA also works with external suppliers operating globally and sources some services and components from Asia.

This is a transparent group-level answer, not a factory list for every hose connector, pump, battery tool or mower. A model’s packaging, rating label, declaration and import documents remain the best evidence of origin.

For a commercial order, request:

1. the full article number and regional version;
2. country of origin and legal manufacturer;
3. factory and supplier documentation;
4. conformity declarations and battery transport files;
5. importer and responsible operator; and
6. warranty, spare-parts and service commitments.

## Who Makes GARDENA SILENO Robot Mowers?

SILENO is GARDENA’s robotic-lawnmower family within Husqvarna Group. Current official product documentation covers wired and wire-free models, including SILENO minimo, city, life, free, flex, sense and pro/max references depending on market and model year.

The portfolio relationship does not prove that every SILENO shares the same factory, navigation stack or component set with Husqvarna Automower. Robot mowers can share group engineering knowledge while retaining different product positioning, software, accessories and service channels.

Buyers should verify boundary-wire or satellite requirements, connectivity, data processing, replacement batteries, blades, charging stations and local dealer support for the exact model.

## Is GARDENA Part of POWER FOR ALL?

GARDENA participates in the POWER FOR ALL Alliance for compatible 18V garden products. The alliance is associated with a shared consumer battery platform across participating brands.

Compatibility applies only to products officially marked for that platform. It does not mean every GARDENA battery, legacy tool or SILENO robot mower uses the same pack. Nor does it make a Bosch or other alliance member responsible for GARDENA’s product warranty.

## Who Sells and Warrants GARDENA Products?

GARDENA uses regional companies, retailers and online channels. Its German legal notice, for example, distinguishes GARDENA Deutschland GmbH from GARDENA Manufacturing GmbH, which operates the reviewed online shop.

Other countries may use different group companies or distributors. The local invoice, terms and warranty identify the responsible parties. Parallel imports can complicate product registration, spare parts and warranty eligibility.

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

1. Separate Husqvarna Group ownership from GARDENA’s operating entities.
2. Match the product to the destination-market catalogue and article number.
3. Verify factory and origin from current model documents.
4. Confirm the exact battery platform rather than assuming group compatibility.
5. For SILENO, document navigation, connectivity, data and installation responsibilities.
6. Identify the invoice seller, importer and warranty provider.
7. Confirm blades, batteries, charging equipment and spare-parts availability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns GARDENA?

Husqvarna Group owns GARDENA. The brand has been part of the group since 2007.

### Is GARDENA owned by Husqvarna or Bosch?

GARDENA is owned by Husqvarna Group. Its participation in the POWER FOR ALL battery alliance does not make Bosch its owner.

### Is GARDENA made in Germany?

Many products and spare parts come from GARDENA’s German and Czech factory network, but other Husqvarna plants and external suppliers also contribute. Check the exact model’s origin.

### Does Husqvarna make GARDENA robot mowers?

SILENO sits within the Gardena Division of Husqvarna Group. Exact manufacturing, platform and service responsibilities must still be confirmed by model.

### Are GARDENA and Husqvarna batteries interchangeable?

Only use batteries and chargers that the official documentation identifies as compatible. Shared ownership alone is not sufficient.

## Final Answer

GARDENA is a German-founded garden-equipment brand owned by Sweden’s Husqvarna Group since 2007. It operates as a separate division, with residential watering, garden tools, smart products and SILENO robotic mowers.

The parent-company answer is simple; the supply chain is not. GARDENA uses German and Czech factories, other group plants and external suppliers. Buyers should verify each model’s origin, battery or robot platform, regional seller and warranty route.
