**Lindhaus is a family business.** Lindhaus S.r.l. describes itself as a family company, with a new generation now working alongside its long-standing team. It does not publish its shareholders or name a parent company.

Lindhaus is Italian. It was founded in Padua in 1987.

## Where are Lindhaus machines made?

In Italy. Lindhaus says all its machines are designed and manufactured in Italy and that it controls development, testing and quality. Its 2026 brochure names Rotafil as the family company's electric-motor manufacturer.

## What does Lindhaus make?

Professional vacuums and upright cleaners, scrubber dryers and floor washers, sweepers and carpet-cleaning equipment, plus domestic floorcare products.

## Where does Lindhaus sell?

Worldwide. It runs international websites and opened a US office in Minneapolis in 1992 to distribute its domestic machines.
