**No, Dyson is not publicly traded.** Dyson is not listed on the London, New York, Singapore or any other stock exchange, and it has no ticker symbol. It is a private company controlled by its founder, Sir James Dyson. You cannot buy Dyson shares through a broker, and any website or message offering "Dyson stock" or "Dyson pre-IPO shares" should be treated with suspicion.

## Who owns Dyson if it is not public?

Sir James Dyson. The UK register of persons with significant control lists him as the only controlling person of Dyson UK Group Limited, with 75% or more of the shares and voting rights and the right to appoint or remove directors. Dyson UK Group's 2024 accounts name Weybourne Holdings Pte. Ltd. in Singapore as the ultimate holding company and Sir James as the ultimate controlling party.

Because the company is private, there are no public shareholders, no quarterly earnings calls and no share price. For the full ownership chain, see [who owns Dyson](/blog/who-owns-dyson-james-dyson-singapore-manufacturing).

## Did moving to Singapore make Dyson a Singapore-listed company?

No. Dyson opened its global headquarters at St James Power Station in Singapore in March 2022, and several holding companies are registered there. A headquarters and holding companies are not a stock listing. Dyson is not listed on the Singapore Exchange.

## Does Dyson publish financial results?

Dyson is not required to report like a listed company. Its UK and Singapore companies file statutory accounts, and the UK filings can be read on Companies House. These are company accounts, not market disclosures for investors.

## Can you invest in Dyson's market another way?

Not in Dyson itself. Several of its competitors are listed, and their shares trade publicly:

None of these companies owns Dyson, and this list is not investment advice. It only shows which companies in the same categories are publicly traded.
