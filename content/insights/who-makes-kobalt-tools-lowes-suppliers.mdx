**Kobalt is a Lowe's private brand, but Lowe's does not operate one factory that makes every Kobalt tool.** Lowe's controls the retail brand and sells the range through its stores and website. The physical products are sourced through category- and model-specific manufacturing relationships.

That distinction explains why there is no accurate one-company answer to “who makes Kobalt tools?” A drill, mechanics tool set, air compressor, shop vacuum and lawn mower can have different manufacturers, importers, countries of origin and service routes. Public records identify Greenworks or related Globe companies for some Kobalt outdoor equipment, but that evidence must not be extended to all Kobalt power tools.

## Does Lowe's Own Kobalt?

The practical answer is yes: Kobalt is a Lowe's private brand.

Lowe's corporate materials explicitly group Kobalt with its private brands. Its 2025 SpringFest release distinguishes private brands such as Kobalt from national brands and says the assortment is available through Lowe's. Lowe's responsible-sourcing policy also uses Kobalt tools as an example of products supplied by “Lowe's Private Brand Vendors.”

This structure is different from Lowe's merely stocking an independent manufacturer. Lowe's directs the brand proposition, assortment and retail route while outside vendors participate in product development, production, import or after-sales obligations under their agreements.

The trademark and operating entities behind a private brand can be more detailed than a shopper-facing ownership answer. For sourcing, litigation or licensing work, check the current trademark record and contract party. For a buyer deciding who stands behind the shelf brand, the useful answer is that Kobalt is controlled and retailed by Lowe's.

## Who Manufactures Kobalt Tools?

Kobalt uses multiple product-specific suppliers. Public evidence does not support naming one manufacturer for the complete range.

The catalogue crosses cordless power tools, hand tools, mechanics tools, storage, air equipment, outdoor power equipment and cleanup products. Those categories require different factories, certifications, components and service systems. A supplier documented for a mower therefore cannot automatically be assigned to a drill or wet/dry vacuum.

Regulatory records provide some of the clearest examples:

- A July 2026 U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recall for specified Kobalt 24V and 48V outdoor tools named Greenworks North America as importer. The covered products were manufactured in China and Vietnam.
- A 2020 CPSC recall for specified Kobalt 40V pole saws named Changzhou Globe Co. Ltd. as manufacturer and Hongkong Sun Rise Trading as distributor.
- A separate 2020 chainsaw recall identified the same manufacturer and distributor for the covered Kobalt 40V models.
- A 2017 mower recall covered both Kobalt and Greenworks-branded products and named Hongkong Sun Rise Trading as manufacturer for those models.

These records verify a Greenworks/Globe relationship in parts of Kobalt outdoor power equipment across particular periods. They do not prove that the relationship covers every current outdoor SKU, much less Kobalt's whole tool catalogue.

## Does Greenworks Make Kobalt Tools?

Greenworks makes or supports some documented Kobalt outdoor products, but “Greenworks makes Kobalt” is too broad.

The latest direct evidence is the 2026 CPSC battery recall. It covers listed trimmers, blowers, mowers, chainsaws, pruning saws, a bristle-brush kit and a Kobalt 24V power cleaner using specified USB-C batteries. Greenworks is the recall contact and named importer. Only the listed products and batteries are included.

That record is especially useful because it shows how private-brand responsibility works. A product can carry the Kobalt name, be sold by Lowe's, be imported or supported by Greenworks and be made in China or Vietnam. Each role is real, but none replaces the others.

For any unlisted drill, saw, compressor, vacuum or hand tool, find model-level evidence before naming Greenworks or another supplier.

## Where Are Kobalt Tools Made?

Kobalt tools are made in multiple countries. There is no reliable brand-wide country-of-origin answer.

Current regulatory evidence identifies China and Vietnam for the specified 2026 outdoor tools and batteries. Historical CPSC notices identify China for covered Kobalt mowers, pole saws and chainsaws. Other product families can follow different sourcing routes, and a model's origin may change with a new generation or supplier.

Check the exact product for:

1. the model, item number and date code;
2. country-of-origin marking on the tool and package;
3. manufacturer or importer named in the manual or certification;
4. current recall and safety notices;
5. the legal seller on the invoice; and
6. warranty terms for the destination market.

The headquarters of Lowe's, the nationality of a supplier and the country where a tool is assembled are separate facts.

## What Is the Kobalt 24V MAX Platform?

Kobalt launched its modern 24V MAX brushless platform at Lowe's in June 2016 with seven tools: a drill/driver, impact driver, reciprocating saw, circular saw, two impact wrenches and an LED light. Lowe's described that release as the first phase of a multi-year platform plan.

The current Kobalt brand page says one 24V MAX battery can power more than 75 tools. The assortment now reaches beyond core workshop tools into outdoor equipment and cleanup. Lowe's product listings include 24V vacuums and the 2026 recall list includes a 24V power cleaner, making the platform relevant to both tool and cleaning-equipment buyers.

“24V MAX” identifies the battery family, not the factory. Tools sharing a battery interface can still come from different product programs or suppliers. It also does not mean the battery is compatible with another brand that uses similar voltage language.

Buyers should match the exact tool, battery and charger model. Kobalt also sells 40V and 80V outdoor platforms, while some newer outdoor products use two 24V batteries and are marketed as 48V. Similar blue housings or voltage numbers are not a substitute for documented compatibility.

## Kobalt in Cleaning and Outdoor Equipment

Kobalt matters to the cleaning industry because its ecosystem includes products that cross from construction into debris collection and exterior cleaning.

The current Lowe's range includes portable wet/dry vacuums and shop-vacuum products, while the 24V system reaches blowers and a power cleaner. These products can share customers and, for documented models, batteries with workshop tools.

The commercial opportunity is an integrated tool-and-cleanup purchase. The operational risk is assuming one supplier, filter standard, hose connection, service center or warranty rule applies across the range. A cordless vacuum can sit inside the same branded ecosystem without sharing the same manufacturing route as a mower or impact wrench.

## How Does the Kobalt Warranty Work?

Warranty coverage must be checked by product and purchase date.

At the 2016 platform launch, Lowe's stated that the original Kobalt 24V MAX brushless tools carried a five-year limited tool guarantee and the batteries carried a three-year limited guarantee, alongside a one-year satisfaction program. Current product pages still commonly show five-year tool and three-year battery or charger coverage for specified 24V items, but a historical launch statement should not be treated as universal current terms.

Hand tools, storage, compressors, outdoor equipment, batteries and vacuums may have different warranties and exclusions. Commercial use can also affect coverage. The product page, package, manual and warranty document in force when the item was purchased should control the claim.

Keep the receipt, item number, model, serial or date code, battery identity and the original warranty text. Lowe's may be the first return point, while a supplier or dedicated service channel handles technical diagnosis, parts or a recall remedy.

## What the 2026 Kobalt Battery Recall Means

The July 9, 2026 recall is important but narrowly defined.

It covers listed Kobalt 24V and 48V outdoor tools supplied with specified Kobalt USB-C batteries. The reported hazard involves charging the battery through its USB-C port while it remains inserted in the tool. CPSC says only products with the identified USB-C batteries are included and directs affected consumers to Greenworks for replacement batteries and related materials.

The notice does **not** mean all Kobalt 24V batteries, every Kobalt tool or the entire platform has been recalled. Owners should compare the exact tool and battery model against the current CPSC notice and follow its remedy instructions. Used-equipment buyers should make the same check before resale or deployment.

## What Buyers, Dealers and Suppliers Should Verify

Before relying on a Kobalt manufacturing claim, record:

1. **Product identity:** Model, Lowe's item number, serial or date code and kit configuration.
2. **Category:** Power tool, hand tool, outdoor equipment, vacuum, compressor, storage or accessory.
3. **Battery system:** 24V, 40V, 80V or a two-battery 48V configuration.
4. **Manufacturer:** Company named on the model label, declaration, test report or regulatory notice.
5. **Importer or distributor:** Legal entity responsible for bringing that SKU into the market.
6. **Factory and origin:** Physical production site, country and production period.
7. **Seller:** Lowe's entity and market shown on the invoice.
8. **Warranty:** Term, exclusions, commercial-use conditions and claim route.
9. **Recall status:** Exact match of model, battery and date code against current notices.
10. **Parts and service:** Batteries, chargers, filters, hoses, blades and repair support for the model.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Kobalt tools?

Kobalt is a Lowe's private brand. Lowe's controls the brand and is its primary retail channel.

### Does Lowe's manufacture Kobalt tools?

Not as one universal factory. Lowe's manages the private brand and retail program, while product-specific suppliers manufacture or support different Kobalt models.

### Who makes Kobalt power tools?

There is no verified single manufacturer for every Kobalt power tool. Identify the exact model and use its label, manual, certification and current regulatory records.

### Are Kobalt tools made by Greenworks?

Some Kobalt outdoor equipment has documented Greenworks or Globe-related manufacturing, import, distribution or recall support. That evidence does not cover the full Kobalt catalogue.

### Are Kobalt tools made in the USA?

Kobalt is not an all-USA-made brand. Current and historical regulatory records identify China and Vietnam for specified products. Check the exact model's origin label.

### How many tools use Kobalt 24V batteries?

Lowe's currently says the Kobalt 24V MAX system includes more than 75 tools. Compatibility still needs to be confirmed for the exact tool, battery and charger.

### Are Kobalt 24V batteries compatible with other brands?

Do not assume cross-brand compatibility from voltage alone. Use only combinations documented by Kobalt and Lowe's for the exact models.

### Is every Kobalt 24V battery recalled?

No. The July 2026 CPSC notice applies to listed products using specified USB-C batteries. Compare the model numbers and follow the official remedy if matched.

## Final Answer

Kobalt is Lowe's private tool brand, but Lowe's is not one universal Kobalt factory. The range is built through product-specific supplier relationships. Greenworks and Globe-related companies are documented for certain outdoor products, while other Kobalt categories require their own model-level evidence.

The 24V MAX platform links more than 75 current tools across workshop, outdoor and cleanup applications, but it does not identify the manufacturer. For a reliable answer, verify the exact model's supplier, origin, importer, battery compatibility, warranty and recall status.
