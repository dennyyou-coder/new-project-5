**EGO Power+ is owned by Chervon, the Hong Kong-listed power-tool and outdoor-power-equipment group.** EGO is one of Chervon's own brands alongside FLEX and SKIL, but it has a separate market position, 56V battery system, channel network and warranty policy.

Chervon also develops and manufactures products. That does not mean every EGO mower, blower, battery or charger comes from one factory. Product origin, importer and warranty responsibility remain model- and market-specific.

## Is EGO a Chervon Brand?

Yes. Chervon's official EGO page places the brand inside the group and says the EGO POWER+ platform was introduced in 2014. The parent presents EGO as its cordless outdoor-equipment proposition for North America, Europe and Australia.

This answer is narrower than the existing [Chervon ownership guide](/blog/who-owns-chervon-ego-flex-skil). That article explains the listed parent, controlling shareholder and wider brand portfolio. This page answers the brand-level questions buyers ask about EGO: the 56V ecosystem, manufacturing boundary, channel role and product warranty.

EGO should not be described as owned by Lowe's, Ace Hardware, Amazon, a dealer or another retailer. A sales relationship can be commercially important without transferring brand ownership.

## Is EGO the Same Company as Chervon?

Not exactly. EGO is the consumer-facing brand and product platform. Chervon is the corporate group behind it. Regional legal entities can then appear as seller, importer, app provider or warrantor.

In the United States and Canada, the current EGO warranty names **Chervon North America, Inc.** as the issuer. The EGO app agreement also names that entity. Those records support Chervon's operating role, but they do not turn the North American subsidiary into the owner of every global EGO right or prove North American manufacture.

For contracts, distinguish at least four layers: Chervon Holdings or the relevant group parent, the EGO brand, the regional operating company and the exact product manufacturer or importer shown in product documents.

## What Is the EGO 56V Battery Platform?

EGO's current outdoor-equipment system is built around 56V ARC Lithium batteries. Chervon's brand page says every current 56V ARC Lithium battery powers the 100-plus tools in the POWER+ system, while EGO's product pages present battery interchangeability across the documented platform.

That is a strong ecosystem claim, but it has limits:

- it applies to the EGO POWER+ platform described by the current official source;
- battery size, output and recommended use can still differ by tool;
- commercial systems, legacy generations and destination-market products require their own documentation; and
- common Chervon ownership does not make EGO batteries compatible with FLEX, SKIL or private-label products.

The safest compatibility check uses the exact tool model, battery model, charger and current manual. A shared 56V label alone is not a universal technical approval.

## Who Manufactures EGO Power+ Products?

Chervon is more than a trademark holding company. Its power-tools business describes a full value chain covering R&D, sourcing, manufacturing, testing, marketing, distribution and after-sales service. The group identifies production facilities in Nanjing, China, and Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

Chervon's EGO materials also describe an international manufacturing business established in the 1990s and large annual tool output. These statements support the conclusion that EGO sits inside an integrated product group.

They do not identify the origin of every EGO product. A mower, battery, charger, pressure washer or lifestyle product may follow a different production route. Components and final assembly can also involve different locations. Buyers should use the rating label, carton, customs documents, declaration and model-specific recall or warranty records.

## Are EGO Tools Made in China or Vietnam?

Chervon discloses major production in both China and Vietnam. That makes both countries relevant to the group manufacturing footprint, but it is not a catalogue-wide origin statement for EGO.

The exact answer may change by category, model revision, market and production period. A corporate campus announcement proves capacity; a product label and shipment documents prove the origin of a particular unit. Procurement teams should not assign a factory simply because another Chervon brand or earlier EGO model was made there.

## Who Sells and Services EGO Products?

EGO uses regional retailers, authorized dealers, ecommerce and specialist outdoor-power channels. Its U.S. site maintains a store locator and warns that warranty eligibility is tied to authorized retail purchase where law permits.

Retail distribution is separate from ownership and manufacture. A retailer may have a launch arrangement, broad shelf presence or selected exclusive SKUs while Chervon remains the brand owner and a regional Chervon company remains the warranty issuer.

For distributors, verify territory, online-sales rights, spare-parts obligations, demo stock, service tools and warranty reimbursement. For end users, retain proof of purchase and check that the seller appears in the current authorized network.

## How Does the EGO Warranty Work?

The current U.S. and Canadian policy states different terms for consumer and professional use. It lists five years for many EGO outdoor-power tools in personal household use and three years for standard batteries and chargers, with product-specific registration extensions and separate commercial terms.

Those headline periods are not universal. Riding mowers, commercial products, reconditioned equipment, maintenance parts and certain battery models have distinct conditions. The policy can also change after this article date.

The controlling document is the current regional warranty for the exact model and use case. Buyers outside the United States and Canada should use the applicable country terms rather than importing North American coverage.

## What Buyers and Suppliers Should Verify

1. Confirm the exact EGO model, revision, battery and charger.
2. Record the legal seller, manufacturer, importer and warranty issuer separately.
3. Check country-of-origin markings and destination-market certification.
4. Use only current EGO compatibility documents for the battery combination.
5. Confirm authorized-retailer or dealer status before purchase.
6. Separate household, professional, rental and institutional warranty use.
7. Check current recall and service notices by model number.
8. For distribution, document territory, ecommerce, parts and service obligations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns EGO Power+?

Chervon owns and operates EGO as one of its group brands.

### Is EGO owned by Lowe's?

No. Retail availability is a distribution relationship, not brand ownership.

### Are EGO and SKIL batteries interchangeable?

Do not assume so. EGO and SKIL are separate Chervon brands with distinct documented battery platforms.

### Where are EGO tools made?

Chervon has manufacturing operations in China and Vietnam, but the exact EGO model's label and documents determine its origin.

### Who provides the EGO warranty in North America?

The current U.S. and Canadian policy is issued by Chervon North America, Inc.

### Do all EGO 56V batteries fit all EGO tools?

Current official materials state platform-wide compatibility across the EGO POWER+ system. Confirm the exact models and recommended battery requirements in the current manual.

## Final Answer

EGO Power+ is a Chervon brand. Chervon provides the corporate ownership, product-development and manufacturing platform behind EGO, while the brand has its own 56V battery ecosystem, regional channels and warranty rules.

Chervon's factories in China and Vietnam establish group capability, not the origin of every EGO SKU. For a product-level decision, verify the exact model, battery, charger, factory origin, importer, authorized seller and regional warranty issuer.
