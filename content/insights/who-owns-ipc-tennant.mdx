**IPC is owned by Tennant Company.** Tennant, the Minnesota floor-cleaning company listed on the New York Stock Exchange under TNC, completed its acquisition of IPC Group on April 6, 2017. The brand now appears as IPC by Tennant Company.

IPC is Italian in origin. IPC traces its integrated group to 2005, and official product documents still name Italian operating companies, such as IP Cleaning S.r.l. in Summaga di Portogruaro, near Venice.

## Is IPC part of Tennant?

Yes. IPC is a Tennant brand. Tennant itself is an independent public company with no parent, as explained in our guide to [who owns Tennant](/blog/who-owns-tennant-company-ipc-robotics). IPC's whistleblowing policy identifies IP Cleaning S.r.l. and IPC Tools S.p.A. as companies within the Tennant Group.

Ownership does not mean Tennant is the manufacturer or warranty company named on every IPC machine. Product sheets and compliance documents name the specific IPC entity responsible.

## What does IPC make?

- **Floor care**: scrubber-driers, sweepers and floor machines, including ride-on models such as the CT231.
- **Vacuums**: commercial and industrial vacuums.
- **Pressure washers, window and manual cleaning tools.**

## Where are IPC machines made?

IPC describes itself as a manufacturer of cleaning equipment and tools. A current IPC industrial-vacuum product sheet names IP Cleaning S.r.l. in Summaga di Portogruaro, Italy, as the company behind the product. Check the serial plate and declaration of conformity for a specific model.

## How does IPC sell?

IPC runs regional websites across Europe, the Americas, India and other markets, and sells through partners. IPC says it supports partners with floor-care know-how, programmes and after-sales service.
