**Triton Tools is now owned and managed globally by Timbecon Pty Ltd, an Australian-owned woodworking business.** Timbecon acquired the brand after former owner Toolstream Ltd entered administration in December 2025. Triton's official site announced the new ownership and its return to an Australian headquarters in 2026.

**Who manufactures Triton Tools?** Timbecon controls the brand, product direction and global supply chain, and its wholly owned Timbecon Global subsidiary says it supplies Triton Tools worldwide. Current official disclosures do not name Timbecon—or any other company—as the single physical manufacturer of every Triton router, sander, planer, saw or work-support product. The legal manufacturer, factory and country of origin therefore need to be checked on the exact model, rating plate and current compliance documents.

This is a recent change, so older pages that connect Triton only with Toolstream are now historical. Kreg does not own Triton; Kreg sells router-table products that are compatible with selected Triton routers.

## Who Owns Triton Tools Now?

Timbecon Pty Ltd owns and manages the global Triton brand. Triton's June 5, 2026 announcement says Timbecon acquired the brand after Toolstream Ltd, its former owner, entered administration in December 2025.

Timbecon is an Australian woodworking machinery and accessories business with more than 35 years of industry experience. Its other managed brands include Sherwood, Torquata and Melbourne Tool Company. The acquisition therefore places Triton inside a woodworking-focused operator rather than a general retail conglomerate.

The legal and timing distinction is important:

- **Timbecon is the current global brand owner and manager.**
- **Toolstream is the former owner.** Its historic manuals, catalogues and distributor records may still appear online.
- **Regional distributors sell and support the range.** Distribution rights do not transfer ownership of the global brand.

Because the change is recent, buyers should date-stamp supplier files and replace old Toolstream ownership descriptions when they rely on them for current commercial decisions.

## Does Kreg Own Triton Tools?

No. Current official Triton material identifies Timbecon—not Kreg—as the owner.

The confusion comes from the router-table market. Kreg markets router tables and insert plates that can accommodate selected Triton router models. That compatibility is useful to woodworkers, but it is a product-interface relationship, not an acquisition or corporate-control relationship.

This same rule applies across the tool industry: a battery, blade, rail, plate or dust-hose connection can link two brands commercially without linking their ownership.

## What Happened to Toolstream?

Toolstream Ltd previously owned Triton and distributed a broad portfolio of tool brands. According to Triton's official announcement, Toolstream entered administration in December 2025. Timbecon subsequently acquired Triton and committed to rebuilding product availability, support, warranties and spare-parts continuity.

Administration does not mean the Triton brand disappeared. The intellectual property and global brand operation moved to a new owner. It also does not mean every historical warranty or distributor obligation automatically remained unchanged in every country. The current regional support path and proof of purchase matter.

For existing tools, record:

1. model and serial number;
2. purchase date and seller;
3. destination market;
4. any product-registration confirmation; and
5. the current authorized service contact.

## Who Manufactures Triton Tools?

The ownership answer does not identify one universal factory.

Timbecon owns and manages Triton, sets the brand direction and coordinates distribution. Its [current privacy notice](https://www.tritontools.com/pages/privacy-notice) identifies Timbecon Global Pty Ltd as a wholly owned Timbecon subsidiary that supplies woodworking machinery, tools and accessories to Australian customers and Triton Tools globally. That establishes the operating and supply role, but current public sources do not establish that Timbecon physically manufactures every router, sander, planer, saw, sharpener and work-support product in one company-owned facility.

![Triton model-level manufacturing and buyer verification map](/images/blog/triton-manufacturing-verification-map.svg)

For a specific Triton tool, use the product itself as the evidence chain:

- exact model and revision;
- country-of-origin marking on the rating plate or carton;
- current manual and declaration of conformity;
- named manufacturer, importer or responsible economic operator;
- serial or batch code; and
- regional warranty and service contact.

Do not convert “Australian-owned” or “headquartered in Australia” into “made in Australia.” A brand owner can control engineering, specifications, quality and service while using different production partners or countries for different products.

## Triton's Australian Woodworking History

Triton's official history begins in Melbourne in 1976. George Lewin created the original Triton Workcentre to give woodworkers a more controlled way to use a handheld circular saw in a workbench system.

Several milestones shaped the brand:

- **1976:** Triton was established in Melbourne around the original Workcentre.
- **1992:** SuperJaws introduced a portable work-support and clamping format.
- **2001:** the TRA001 dual-mode plunge router arrived, combining plunge operation with above-table height adjustment.
- **2026:** ownership and headquarters returned to Australia under Timbecon.

These products explain Triton's market identity better than a generic “power-tool brand” label. The system is primarily associated with woodworking, routing, work support, sharpening, sanding and material preparation.

## What Products Does Triton Sell Today?

Triton's current official range is organized around Workcentre systems, routers, sanders, planers, power saws, joinery tools, whetstone sharpeners and SuperJaws work support.

For workshop and cleaning-industry buyers, the most relevant questions are often downstream of the tool itself:

- router and sander dust-port dimensions;
- hose and adapter compatibility;
- replacement filters or extraction requirements;
- abrasive and blade availability;
- noise and dust controls; and
- regional spare-parts support.

Triton has sold cordless products under T20 and XT18 naming in past and regional ranges. That history should not be treated as proof that every current cordless listing belongs to one universally supported battery ecosystem. Match the tool, battery, charger, connector and market documentation exactly.

## How Is Triton Distributed After the Acquisition?

Timbecon is rebuilding Triton through regional partners. Current official announcements identify:

- **TradeSafe Products Ltd** as exclusive UK distributor;
- **sauter GmbH** as exclusive distributor and licensed brand partner in Europe; and
- **Crimson Tool** as exclusive North American distributor for core Triton products.

Availability is being restored in phases, so a model shown on a global site may not yet be stocked or serviced in every market. Buyers should confirm local inventory, warranty handling and genuine spare parts before importing from another region.

Regional distribution also explains why two valid Triton pages may show different assortments. It does not mean those distributors own separate versions of the brand.

## What Warranty Does Triton Offer?

Triton's current site promotes a three-year guarantee. The detailed conditions still control the claim.

An official TRA001 manual, for example, requires online registration within 30 days to extend coverage to three years and excludes commercial use, normal wear and misuse. Other products, dates and regions may use different wording.

Before relying on the headline term, check:

- whether registration is required and its deadline;
- whether the tool is eligible in the purchase country;
- consumer versus commercial-use exclusions;
- which parts count as consumables;
- proof-of-purchase requirements; and
- the current service route after the ownership transition.

Timbecon's acquisition announcement says the new structure is committed to existing warranties, service and genuine replacement parts. That is an important continuity statement, but an individual claim still depends on its product and documentation.

## What This Means for Buyers and Distributors

The ownership transition is strategically positive for Triton's woodworking focus: the brand is now managed by an Australian woodworking specialist and has named partners for the UK, Europe and North America.

For distributors, the acquisition does not remove normal diligence. Confirm territorial authorization, current wholesale terms, conformity responsibility, product origin, warranty administration and spare-parts supply for each proposed range.

For professional buyers, compare the complete system rather than the badge alone:

- tool specification and duty cycle;
- accessories and consumables;
- table, plate and extraction compatibility;
- local repair capacity;
- warranty eligibility for commercial use; and
- long-term availability of model-specific parts.
