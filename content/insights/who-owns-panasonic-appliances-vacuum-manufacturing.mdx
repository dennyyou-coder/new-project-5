**Panasonic appliances ultimately sit within Panasonic Holdings Corporation, the Japanese group holding company. Since 1 April 2026, the reorganized Panasonic Corporation has operated the group's consumer major-appliance, small-appliance and audio-visual businesses.** Its published organization explicitly places refrigerators and laundry appliances in a Major Appliances division and vacuum cleaners and kitchen appliances in a Small Appliances division.

This current structure replaces older descriptions that may assign the same businesses to previous operating units. It still does not mean Panasonic Holdings or Panasonic Corporation is the named manufacturer, importer or warranty provider for every regional model.

## What Changed in Panasonic's 2026 Reorganization?

Panasonic Corporation announced that it began operations under a new business structure on 1 April 2026. The company now covers Major Appliances, Small Appliances and AVC businesses within one operating company.

The official organization page identifies:

- Major Appliances Business Division, including refrigerators and laundry appliances;
- Small Appliances Business Division, including vacuum cleaners and kitchen appliances; and
- AVC Business Division, including televisions and related consumer products.

Panasonic Holdings remains the parent group. This distinction is important for readers using older articles: the Panasonic brand did not change ownership, but the internal operating arrangement changed.

## Who Makes Panasonic Vacuum Cleaners?

Panasonic Corporation's Small Appliances Business Division is the current business unit responsible for vacuum cleaners at group-organization level. Panasonic also has a long floorcare history: its official consumer history records a cylinder vacuum in 1958 and the RULO robotic vacuum in 2015.

Those historical and organizational facts do not identify the factory for a current cordless, canister or robotic model. Panasonic's country websites carry different ranges. For example, its Malaysian consumer site lists the MC-SB53 cordless stick vacuum with local specifications and purchase routes.

The safe model-level answer requires the rating label, manual, conformity documents and local warranty. A group division can own product strategy while a specific affiliate or factory handles production and a regional sales company handles the customer.

## Where Are Panasonic Appliances Made?

Panasonic Corporation describes its business as developing, manufacturing and selling household appliances, beauty and health products, and AVC products. It operates internationally, with manufacturing and sales arrangements that differ across categories and markets.

No single country-of-origin answer covers all Panasonic appliances. Refrigerators, washing machines, microwave ovens, rice cookers and vacuum cleaners may use different sites and supply chains. Product origin can also change between model generations.

![Panasonic model, factory, importer and warranty verification workflow](/images/blog/panasonic-model-verification-map.svg)

Before making a sourcing claim, check:

1. the complete model number and regional suffix;
2. the country-of-origin statement on the unit and packaging;
3. the named manufacturer and conformity-document holder;
4. the importer or responsible economic operator; and
5. the local warranty issuer and authorized service network.

## Is Panasonic Still a Japanese Company?

Yes. Panasonic Holdings Corporation is headquartered in Japan, and the group's history begins with the business founded by Konosuke Matsushita in 1918. The Panasonic brand name was first used in 1955.

Japanese ownership and brand heritage do not mean every appliance is manufactured in Japan. Corporate nationality, engineering responsibility and factory location are three separate facts.

## Are National Panasonic, Technics and Panasonic Appliances the Same?

Panasonic's brand history includes the earlier National and National Panasonic marks. Current branding varies by product category, and Technics is associated with premium audio rather than a universal appliance sub-brand.

Historical marks should not be used to infer current warranty or parts compatibility. Identify the exact product and current local support channel.

## How Do Panasonic Sales and Warranties Work?

Panasonic operates through regional legal companies and country-specific consumer sites. A product may be designed within the global appliance organization but sold and warranted by a local Panasonic company or authorized distributor.

The UK and Ireland corporate profile, for example, identifies a regional legal company and territory. Other markets use their own structures. Online marketplace sellers may offer parallel imports that fall outside the official local warranty.

For distributors, written territory authorization, spare-parts supply and service obligations are more useful than a generic statement that the product is “from Panasonic.”

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

1. Use the post-April 2026 Panasonic operating structure for current ownership descriptions.
2. Distinguish Panasonic Holdings from Panasonic Corporation and local sales companies.
3. Match the exact model to the destination-country catalogue.
4. Verify factory and origin from model documents rather than brand heritage.
5. Confirm voltage, plug, energy labels and radio approvals.
6. Identify the local invoice seller, importer and warranty issuer.
7. Secure parts and service commitments before cross-border or commercial purchasing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Panasonic appliances?

Panasonic Holdings Corporation is the group holding company. The reorganized Panasonic Corporation operates the group's consumer appliance businesses from 1 April 2026.

### Does Panasonic still make appliances?

Yes. Panasonic Corporation officially defines its business as developing, manufacturing and selling household and related consumer appliances.

### Who makes Panasonic vacuum cleaners?

Vacuum cleaners sit within Panasonic Corporation's Small Appliances Business Division. The exact legal manufacturer and factory depend on the model.

### Are Panasonic appliances made in Japan?

Some products may be made in Japan, but the portfolio uses a global manufacturing system. Check the exact product label for origin.

### Is Panasonic owned by another electronics company?

No outside appliance group is identified as Panasonic's owner. Panasonic Holdings is the parent of the Panasonic group.

## Final Answer

Panasonic appliances belong to the Panasonic group led by Panasonic Holdings Corporation. Under the structure effective 1 April 2026, Panasonic Corporation operates major appliances, small appliances—including vacuum cleaners—and consumer AVC businesses.

The corporate chart explains current business responsibility, not every model's supply chain. Buyers should separately verify the factory, country of origin, regional seller, importer, parts route and warranty provider.
