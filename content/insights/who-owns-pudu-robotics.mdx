**Pudu Robotics is owned by Shenzhen Pudu Technology Co., Ltd., a Chinese company that is not publicly listed.** Pudu has raised several rounds of venture funding, but its official materials name no parent company and publish no table of who controls it. Funding from investors does not make any of them the owner.

Pudu is a Chinese company. It was founded in 2016 and has its global headquarters in Shenzhen. It makes service robots for restaurants, hotels, retail, offices and industry, and has grown into commercial cleaning robots and robotic mowers.

## Is Pudu Robotics a Chinese company?

Yes. The legal company behind PUDU products is Shenzhen Pudu Technology Co., Ltd. and its subsidiaries and affiliates. It runs overseas subsidiaries, including a US headquarters in the Dallas area, opened in April 2026, and a European headquarters in Neuss, Germany, announced in October 2026 for demonstrations, training, warehousing and repairs.

## What does Pudu make?

- **Cleaning robots**: the CC1 and CC1 Pro, the MT1 family and the BG1 series of larger scrubbers.
- **Delivery and hospitality robots**: such as BellaBot and FlashBot.
- **Robotic mowers**: the GT3, GT5 and GT7 for professional grounds care, launched in September 2026.
- **Fleet software and services**: PUDU Link and remote support.

## Where are Pudu robots made?

Pudu reports three factories in China, in Jiangsu, Zhejiang and Jiangxi. In June 2025 it said its 100,000th robot, a CC1 Pro, came off the line at its Jianhu factory in Jiangsu. By its own count it has now shipped more than 130,000 robots to more than 85 countries and regions.
