**Henry vacuum cleaners are made by Numatic International Limited, a UK cleaning-equipment manufacturer. Numatic operates the official MyHenry business and identifies its main trading and manufacturing location in Chard, Somerset.** Henry is a Numatic product family, not an unrelated appliance brand licensed to another universal manufacturer.

Numatic's current disclosures support a strong UK manufacturing answer, but they do not mean every motor, electronic part, plastic resin or raw material is UK-made. The company sources materials and parts through an international supply chain and assembles final products at its UK site.

## What Company Is Behind Henry?

The company is **Numatic International Limited**, registered in England and Wales under company number 773331. Numatic's official About page lists the company identity, while the MyHenry terms say the consumer website is operated by Numatic International Ltd.

The practical structure is:

1. Numatic International Limited is the UK manufacturer and company behind the products.
2. Henry is the best-known name in Numatic's HomeCare range.
3. MyHenry is Numatic's UK consumer website and direct-commerce operation.
4. Overseas Numatic subsidiaries and independent distributors handle regional sales and support.
5. A local dealer or service provider can be responsible for a particular transaction without owning the Henry product family.

This is why an invoice may name a distributor or retailer while the product and warranty documentation still point back to Numatic.

This article focuses specifically on the Henry consumer product family and manufacturing question.

## Where Are Henry Vacuum Cleaners Made?

Numatic says its equipment is designed, engineered and made in the United Kingdom. Its 2025 anti-slavery statement provides a more detailed operating boundary: the company has its head office and **sole manufacturing plant in the UK**, with the UK business based in Chard, Somerset.

The same statement says Numatic's overseas subsidiaries do not manufacture or assemble goods; they provide distribution channels. It also says parts made from sourced raw and processed materials are assembled into final products at the UK site.

That supports three conclusions:

- Numatic is a real manufacturer, not only a brand owner.
- Chard is the central final-production location described in the current company disclosure.
- Global component sourcing remains part of the manufacturing system.

The safe wording is therefore: **Henry vacuum cleaners are made by Numatic in the UK, with internationally sourced materials and components feeding final production at Chard.**

## Does “Made in the UK” Mean Every Component Is British?

No. Numatic's own supply-chain statement says it works with companies around the world and sources raw and processed materials used to make parts before final assembly at the UK site.

This is normal for modern vacuum production. A machine can involve:

- plastic resins and moulded parts;
- electric motors and carbon brushes;
- cables, plugs and switches;
- filters and dust bags;
- batteries and chargers for cordless models;
- metal tubes, hoses and floor tools; and
- packaging and printed documentation.

Country-of-origin and customs rules can treat final manufacturing differently from component origin. Buyers who need a specific local-content threshold should request a bill-of-material origin declaration or supplier statement rather than relying on the brand's general UK manufacturing claim.

## How Did the Henry Vacuum Begin?

Numatic was founded in 1969 in Crewkerne, Somerset, initially producing commercial vacuum-cleaner designs. The company later moved through Yeovil and Beaminster as demand grew.

Numatic's official history dates Henry's inception to **1981** at the Beaminster operation. The company acquired its current Chard location in 1990 and expanded its production capacity and manufacturing expertise there.

This timeline helps explain Henry's design language. The product emerged from Numatic's commercial vacuum background rather than from a stand-alone consumer-appliance startup. The familiar character became the public face of a broader engineering and cleaning-equipment company.

## What Products Are in the Henry Family?

Henry is not one unchanging vacuum. Numatic and MyHenry currently use the family across several cleaning formats.

| Family type | Examples named in current support or warranty materials | Buyer boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Corded cylinder vacuums | Henry, Hetty, Harry and James | Check capacity, filtration, tools and market plug |
| Wet-and-dry or extraction machines | Charles and George | Do not treat them as ordinary dry-only cylinders |
| Quick range | Henry Quick, Hetty Quick and related variants | Battery, pods, charger and warranty differ from corded machines |
| Accessories and consumables | Bags, filters, floor tools and specialist kits | Compatibility must match the exact machine and generation |

Names, colours and faces are useful for family recognition but do not replace the model code. A “Henry” sold in one market can have different voltage, cable, filtration, tool kit or warranty terms from a similarly named product elsewhere.

## Is Henry the Same as Numatic's Professional Vacuums?

Henry belongs to Numatic, and the company's domestic and professional vacuum experience is connected. However, a Henry HomeCare model is not automatically identical to a Numatic CleanCare or ProCare machine.

Professional products can use different:

- filtration classes;
- dust or hazardous-material controls;
- tank materials and capacities;
- cables, batteries and charging systems;
- accessories and tool standards;
- duty-cycle expectations; and
- commercial warranty terms.

A smiling canister shape should not be used as the technical specification. Facilities managers and distributors should compare model data and certifications instead of assuming that a domestic Henry and a professional Numatic vacuum are interchangeable.

## Who Owns Numatic?

The reviewed official pages identify Numatic International Limited as the company behind Henry and provide its registered company details. They do not identify another appliance group as a parent.

That supports saying **Henry is a Numatic product family**. It does not justify guessing private shareholders or describing an undisclosed ownership structure. For procurement, the relevant verified entity is Numatic International Limited and the regional seller or distributor named in the contract.

## How Does the Henry Warranty Work?

The current MyHenry UK warranty page says Numatic International Limited warrants products to the original purchaser and lists product-specific periods. It currently states two years for cylinder vacuum cleaners and Henry/Hetty Quick products, with a possible additional year for eligible direct purchases and registration.

The same page says claims normally pass through the dealer from whom the product was purchased. That means the manufacturer, dealer and warranty-processing route are connected but not identical.

Warranty terms can differ outside the United Kingdom. Before relying on a headline period, verify:

- the exact model;
- the country and authorized seller;
- the purchase date and registration requirements;
- whether the item is a machine, accessory or consumable;
- excluded wear parts; and
- who supplies parts and labor locally.
