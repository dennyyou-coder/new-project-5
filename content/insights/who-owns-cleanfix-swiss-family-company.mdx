**Cleanfix is a family-run Swiss company.** Cleanfix describes itself as a family-run company, and its imprint names Cleanfix Reinigungssysteme AG as the company behind the brand. It does not name a parent company or publish its shareholdings.

Cleanfix is Swiss. It was founded as Siprotech in Henau, St. Gallen, in 1976, took the Cleanfix name in 1995, and is still based in Henau-Uzwil.

## Where are Cleanfix machines made?

In Switzerland. Cleanfix says it develops and manufactures its machines in Henau-Uzwil, with its own plastic injection moulding, pump production, mechanical production and assembly. Most parts it does not make itself come from regional suppliers, and its factories in Switzerland and India run almost entirely on solar power.

## What does Cleanfix make?

Vacuum and spray-extraction cleaners, scrubber dryers, single-disc machines, sweepers and autonomous cleaning robots. In 2023 it joined the Cordless Alliance System battery platform.

## How does Cleanfix sell?

Through dealers, listed in its dealer directory, with service for spare parts, maintenance and repair.
