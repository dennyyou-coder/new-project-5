**Beko appliances sit within the global home-appliance group that now uses Beko as its corporate identity, while current exchange and legal records continue to identify the listed company as Arçelik A.Ş. In Europe, Beko Europe B.V. is the regional operating company and has been wholly owned through Beko B.V. since June 2026.**

Beko Europe's portfolio also includes the Whirlpool brand under a licence limited to specified jurisdictions. That licence survived Beko's purchase of Whirlpool Corporation's remaining 25% interest. Beko owns Beko Europe, but it does not own the Whirlpool trademark globally.

## Is Beko a Brand or a Company?

It is both a consumer brand and a corporate identity, which is why records can look inconsistent.

Beko's corporate website describes Beko as an international home-appliance company operating through subsidiaries in more than 55 countries. It presents a portfolio of owned and licensed brands and a global manufacturing and research network.

At the same time, the Turkish Public Disclosure Platform and current corporate legal notices identify **Arçelik A.Ş.** as the listed legal entity. Beko's website privacy notice says references to “Beko” mean Arçelik A.Ş. and relevant affiliated companies. The corporate contact page similarly uses “Beko / Arçelik A.Ş.”

For brand storytelling, Beko is the global company name. For contracts, filings, bank details and ownership records, the exact legal name in the document still controls.

## Who Controls Arçelik A.Ş.?

Arçelik A.Ş. is a publicly traded Turkish appliance company within the wider Koç Group. Its shareholding, board and legal identity should be checked in current exchange filings rather than inferred from the Beko consumer brand.

This produces three separate layers:

1. the Beko consumer brand on products;
2. Beko as the group's global corporate identity; and
3. Arçelik A.Ş. as the listed legal issuer in current regulatory records.

A regional subsidiary can add a fourth layer as seller, importer, manufacturer or warrantor.

## What Is Beko Europe?

Beko Europe B.V. is the European home-appliance operating company created by combining Arçelik's European businesses with Whirlpool Corporation's European major-domestic-appliance business.

At the April 2024 closing, Beko/Arçelik owned 75% and Whirlpool Corporation owned 25%. Beko Europe brought together manufacturing, supply chains, distribution and a portfolio of owned or licensed brands, including Beko, Whirlpool, Hotpoint, Indesit and Bauknecht.

Current Beko Europe materials describe a large regional network with approximately 15,000 employees and 11 production sites in Italy, Poland, Romania and Slovakia. Figures can change with industrial restructuring, so buyers should use the latest company disclosure for capacity planning.

## Does Whirlpool Corporation Still Own 25% of Beko Europe?

No.

On 19 June 2026, Arçelik announced that wholly owned Beko B.V. would acquire Whirlpool EMEA Holdings LLC's 25% stake in Beko Europe for EUR 71.45 million. The regulatory entry dated 23 June records completion.

After the closing, Beko B.V. owns 100% of Beko Europe and the shareholder relationship with Whirlpool EMEA Holdings ended. Crucially, the disclosure says the trademark licence agreement remains in force.

This is the current boundary:

- Beko owns Beko Europe outright;
- Whirlpool Corporation owns the Whirlpool trademark;
- Beko Europe can continue using Whirlpool under the licence in specified jurisdictions; and
- Whirlpool Corporation retained KitchenAid rather than transferring it into Beko Europe.

## Does Beko Own Whirlpool Appliances?

Only the regional operating company and licence rights should be associated with Beko—not global trademark ownership.

Beko Europe's official brand page lists Whirlpool and marks the licence as limited to certain jurisdictions. The company can manufacture, market and service covered Whirlpool-branded appliances through its European operation. That does not make Beko the owner of Whirlpool Corporation or of the Whirlpool brand worldwide.

The detailed [Whirlpool ownership guide](/blog/who-owns-whirlpool-appliances-beko-europe) maps the licensed European business against Whirlpool Corporation's retained operations.

## Why Is KitchenAid Outside the Beko Europe Deal?

Whirlpool Corporation's official April 2024 closing announcement says it retained its EMEA KitchenAid small- and major-domestic-appliance business. KitchenAid therefore provides a clear limit to the transaction scope.

Beko Europe received the contributed European major-appliance operation and relevant regional Whirlpool brand rights. It did not receive ownership of every Whirlpool Corporation brand present in Europe.

## Which Brands Are in the Beko Portfolio?

Beko's corporate site reports a portfolio of 22 brands owned or used under limited licences. It names Beko, Arçelik, Whirlpool, Grundig, Hotpoint, Arctic, Ariston, Leisure, Indesit, Blomberg, Defy, Dawlance, Hitachi, Voltas Beko and others.

The list contains different legal relationships:

- owned consumer brands;
- regional brands;
- joint-venture brands; and
- brands used under limited licences.

The portfolio should not be converted into a single “Beko owns all these brands” statement. A star or licence note on the official list is an important legal qualifier.

## What Products Does Beko Sell?

Beko's core portfolio includes refrigeration, laundry, cooking, dishwashing, air conditioning, televisions and small domestic appliances, with range differences by country.

The brand also has a direct cleaning-appliance connection. Current official Beko country sites list cordless vacuums, canister vacuums, wet-and-dry cleaners and robot vacuums in selected markets. This supports Beko's presence in both Home Appliances & Small Appliances and Floorcare & Home Cleaning.

It does not prove a universal global vacuum range. A product offered in Romania, Mauritius, Egypt or the Philippines may use a different model, app region, importer and service network from another country.

## Where Are Beko Appliances Made?

Beko's corporate overview reports 38 production facilities across multiple regions. Beko Europe separately reports 11 European production sites in Italy, Poland, Romania and Slovakia.

These disclosures establish scale and regional capability. They do not establish the factory for one Beko refrigerator, washer or robot vacuum.

The Beko group also operates through subsidiaries, joint ventures and licensed relationships. A product may be made by a group plant, a joint venture or an approved external supplier. Buyers should not assign a factory solely because it makes the same category or is geographically close to the sales market.

## How Should Buyers Verify a Beko Product?

| Evidence | What to confirm |
|---|---|
| Country-specific official page | Whether the model is officially ranged in the destination market |
| Rating plate | Model, voltage, serial code and legal manufacturer disclosures |
| Carton and customs documents | Country of origin, exporter and importer |
| Declaration of conformity | Standards, exact model and responsible signatory |
| Trademark notice | Owned, licensed or joint-venture brand status |
| Warranty certificate | Local guarantor, period, exclusions and service contacts |
| Factory declaration | Exact production site and quality-system scope |
| App listing | Region, data controller, radio approval and long-term support |

For a distributor appointment, also verify territory, online-channel rights, spare-parts obligations and whether the agreement is with Arçelik A.Ş., Beko B.V., Beko Europe B.V. or a local subsidiary.

## Is Every Beko Europe Factory a Beko Factory?

Not in the product-brand sense.

Beko Europe operates an industrial network that came from both Arçelik and Whirlpool Corporation's contributed European business. Plants can support several brands, including licensed Whirlpool products. Calling every output “made by the Beko brand” hides the distinction between operating company, plant and consumer trademark.

For suppliers, the correct customer may be Beko Europe or a subsidiary even when the production program carries another brand. For buyers, the model plate and warranty identify the responsible product chain more reliably than the group logo on a presentation.

## Final Answer

Beko is a consumer brand and the global corporate identity used by the appliance group whose current legal issuer is Arçelik A.Ş. Beko B.V. owns 100% of Beko Europe after completing the acquisition of Whirlpool Corporation's remaining 25% interest in June 2026.

Beko Europe continues to use the Whirlpool brand under a limited regional licence, while KitchenAid remained with Whirlpool Corporation. Beko's factory network is large, but product origin, manufacturer, importer and warranty must still be verified for the exact model and country.
