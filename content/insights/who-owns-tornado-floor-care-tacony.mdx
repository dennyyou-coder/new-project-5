**Tornado is owned by Tacony Corporation.** Tacony, based in Fenton, Missouri, presents Tornado in its commercial floor-care brand family. Tornado Industries, Inc. runs the equipment and service business.

Tornado is American. Its floor-care history goes back to 1927.

## Who is Tacony?

Tacony is a US company with several commercial floor-care brands. It also owns the UK maker [Truvox](/blog/who-owns-truvox-tacony).

## Where are Tornado machines made?

Tornado says it has manufacturing operations at its product-management and development centres in St. James, Missouri, and Fort Worth, Texas, where its development, engineering and assembly teams work.

## What does Tornado make?

Automatic scrubbers, vacuums and carpet equipment, floor machines, burnishers and sweepers. Tornado sells through dealers and offers quotes, demonstrations, technical support and a service-centre locator.
