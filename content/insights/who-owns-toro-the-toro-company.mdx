**Toro is owned by The Toro Company, an independent public company.** The Toro Company is listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker TTC and has no parent. It is American: founded in 1914, it is headquartered in Bloomington, Minnesota.

The Toro brand covers residential mowers, snow throwers, robotic mowers, and professional turf, golf and irrigation equipment. The company also owns other brands, including Exmark, Ditch Witch, BOSS, Ventrac and Hayter.

## Is Toro owned by another company?

No. Toro is not a subsidiary. The Toro Company is the corporate entity behind the Toro brand, and its shares trade on the NYSE. In fiscal 2025 it reported net sales of about USD 4.5 billion and a presence in more than 125 countries.

## Who runs Toro?

Edric C. Funk became president and chief executive officer on November 1, 2026. Richard M. Olson, the previous CEO, is executive chairman. See [Edric Funk takes over as Toro CEO](/news/toro-edric-funk-takes-over-as-ceo-november-2026).

## What brands does The Toro Company own?

Besides Toro, the group's brands include Exmark commercial mowers, Ditch Witch, HammerHead and American Augers underground construction equipment, BOSS snowplows, Ventrac, Spartan mowers, Hayter in the UK, Irritrol irrigation and Perrot.

## Where are Toro mowers made?

The Toro Company's 2025 annual report lists manufacturing operations in the United States, Mexico, the United Kingdom, Italy, Romania, Germany, Poland and China. It also buys components and some finished products from outside suppliers. The country of manufacture for a specific mower is on its label and packaging.

## Does Toro make robotic mowers?

Yes. The Toro Haven is a vision-based robotic mower that entered the Toro range in 2025. Toro sells through dealers, mass retailers and distributors, and directs warranty service to authorised service dealers.
