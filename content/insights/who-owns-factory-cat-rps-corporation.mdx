**Factory Cat is owned by RPS Corporation, a family business.** Factory Cat's website says its machines are built by RPS Corporation, a Wisconsin company founded in 1986 that describes itself as a second-generation, family-owned manufacturer.

Factory Cat is American. RPS makes the machines in Racine, Wisconsin.

## Where are Factory Cat machines made?

In Racine, Wisconsin. RPS says it does design, prototyping, testing, fabrication and rotational moulding in-house, and that more than 95% of its components come from American suppliers.

## What does Factory Cat make?

Walk-behind and ride-on floor scrubbers, sweepers and specialty configurations for industrial and commercial floors.

## How does Factory Cat sell?

Through distributors. Factory Cat offers demonstrations, distributor training, parts and technical support.
