**FEIN Tools is owned by an independent, family-run German company: C. & E. Fein GmbH.** It is not a Bosch subsidiary. Bosch is a battery-technology partner through AMPShare, while FEIN remains a separate company with its own engineering, production and sales organization.

FEIN also makes an unusually strong manufacturing claim: more than 90% of its products are manufactured in-house. That does not mean every product comes from one German factory, but it clearly distinguishes FEIN from a brand that outsources its entire catalogue.

## Who Owns FEIN Tools?

C. & E. Fein GmbH describes itself as an independent, family-run business. The company was founded by Wilhelm Emil Fein in 1867 and remains headquartered in Germany.

“Family company” is the correct current ownership description. Public official material reviewed for this guide does not provide a precise shareholder table or percentage for individual family members, so those details should not be guessed.

FEIN operates internationally through 16 subsidiaries and more than 50 offices and representations. It reports about 800 employees worldwide. Those numbers describe operating scale, not a change in ownership.

## Is FEIN Owned by Bosch?

No. The confusion comes from AMPShare, the multi-brand 18V battery alliance powered by Bosch Professional technology.

FEIN was one of the founding expert brands in AMPShare. Current FEIN tools carrying the AMPShare interface can use AMPShare batteries and compatible Bosch Professional 18V batteries covered by the system. Bosch supplies the shared battery technology; it does not own FEIN.

The distinction mirrors other industrial alliances: a common battery interface can connect separate companies without combining their trademarks, factories, warranties or service networks.

## Who Manufactures FEIN Tools?

FEIN says more than 90% of its products are manufactured in-house. Its largest production site is at Schwäbisch Gmünd-Bargau, where specialists develop and produce tools. The company also works with production sites in Asia and North America.

![FEIN model, battery and warranty verification map](/images/blog/fein-product-verification-map.svg)

This supports describing FEIN as a vertically integrated manufacturer. It does not support saying every FEIN SKU is made in Germany. For a specific drill, grinder, magnetic core drill, oscillating tool or accessory, verify:

1. exact model and revision;
2. rating-plate and carton origin;
3. current manual and conformity declaration;
4. named manufacturer or responsible entity; and
5. market-specific warranty terms.

Accessories and consumables may follow different supply arrangements from power tools. A German-designed product can also be manufactured at another FEIN site without becoming a contract-manufactured private label.

## FEIN's Oscillating-Tool History

FEIN's oscillating-tool lineage began in 1967 with a plaster-cast saw. Its blade oscillated through a small arc so clinicians could cut rigid casts with less risk to the patient beneath.

The same operating principle later moved into automotive glass removal. FEIN then expanded it into renovation and workshop applications, creating the product family now associated with MultiMaster.

The broader company history is older. FEIN was founded in 1867, and Emil Fein and colleagues developed an early electric hand drill in 1895. These milestones explain why FEIN presents itself as an inventor-led manufacturer rather than only a modern tool label.

## What Is the FEIN MultiMaster?

MultiMaster is FEIN's oscillating multitool system for sawing, cutting, sanding, scraping and related work with the correct accessory. Current product tiers include MultiMaster 500 and 700 models, including AMPShare versions.

Performance depends on the complete interface: tool, oscillation specification, accessory mount, blade geometry and work material. A universal marketing term does not make every accessory suitable for every model or task.

Dust-intensive sanding and cutting also require suitable extraction. Check the FEIN dust-extraction accessory, hose adapter, vacuum class and material hazard rather than assuming that an open tool port provides compliant capture.

## How Does AMPShare Work on FEIN Tools?

AMPShare is an 18V multi-brand system built on Bosch Professional battery technology. FEIN says AMPShare-interface tools can use the alliance batteries and Bosch Professional 18V batteries covered by the documented interface, including Bosch Professional batteries dating from 2008.

That statement applies to tools carrying the AMPShare interface. It does not make every historical FEIN 18V battery or charger interchangeable. Before combining products, match:

- AMPShare marking;
- voltage and connector;
- tool and battery manuals;
- charger region and input voltage; and
- any high-current battery requirement.

## What Warranty Does FEIN Offer?

In the United States, FEIN's standard manufacturer's warranty is one year. Eligible power tools, lithium-ion batteries and chargers can receive the three-year FEIN PLUS warranty when the first purchaser registers them within three months.

The program has exclusions, including certain high-frequency, pneumatic, pipe-processing and metal-cutting products. Wear, misuse, overload, unauthorized modification and other conditions can also exclude a claim.

Warranty pages vary by country. Use the purchase market's current terms and keep the receipt and registration certificate.

## What This Means for Buyers

FEIN combines family ownership, substantial in-house production and specialist application engineering. That structure is attractive where process repeatability, metalworking support, accessory depth and repair matter more than the lowest initial price.

Buyers should still separate four questions: who owns the company, which FEIN site or partner made the model, whether its battery/accessory interface matches existing equipment, and who handles warranty service locally.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns FEIN power tools?

C. & E. Fein GmbH is an independent, family-run German company.

### Is FEIN owned by Bosch?

No. Bosch Professional provides the technology behind AMPShare, but FEIN remains independently owned.

### Are all FEIN tools made in Germany?

No catalogue-wide claim is justified. FEIN says more than 90% is made in-house and operates production in Germany, Asia and North America; check the model label for exact origin.

### Did FEIN invent the oscillating tool?

FEIN traces its oscillating technology to a 1967 plaster-cast saw, which evolved through automotive and renovation applications into the MultiMaster family.

### How long is the FEIN warranty?

The US standard term is one year. Eligible products can reach three years through FEIN PLUS registration within three months, subject to exclusions and local terms.

## Final Answer

FEIN Tools is owned by the independent, family-run C. & E. Fein GmbH. The German company says it manufactures more than 90% of its products in-house across a network led by its German headquarters and largest production site. FEIN pioneered oscillating-tool technology and now participates in Bosch-powered AMPShare without becoming a Bosch-owned brand.
