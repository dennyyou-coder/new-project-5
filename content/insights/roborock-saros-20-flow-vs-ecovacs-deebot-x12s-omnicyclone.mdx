**The Roborock Saros 20 Flow and ECOVACS DEEBOT X12S OmniCyclone are the two most direct 2026 rivals among roller-mop robot vacuums.** Both use a roller about 27 cm wide that is rinsed while it cleans. Choose the Saros 20 Flow if low furniture and tall thresholds are your problem; choose the X12S if a bagless dock matters more.

## Mopping

Both rollers are continuously rinsed, so neither drags dirty water across the floor the way a dock-washed pad can. Compare roller access for cleaning and how each lifts the roller on carpet.

## Furniture and thresholds

Roborock lists a 89.8 mm body and a lifting chassis for double thresholds up to 4.5 + 4.3 cm. That is a two-step figure, not a single vertical step. If your home has tall transitions, this is the Saros 20 Flow's main advantage.

## Dock and running costs

The X12S's bagless PureCyclone dock removes the cost of dust bags but still needs emptying and filter cleaning. Roborock's dock washes the roller with hot water. Compare consumable prices locally.

## Which should you buy?

**Saros 20 Flow** for low furniture, tall thresholds and Roborock's app ecosystem. **X12S OmniCyclone** for bagless convenience and quick top-up charging. Suction figures are measured differently (Pa vs AW) and should not decide it.
