**HART remains a brand in Techtronic Industries' portfolio, but TTI discontinued the HART business by the end of 2025.** That is the central fact for buyers in 2026.

Walmart was HART's exclusive retail channel in the United States. Current Walmart listings and an active HART support website may reflect remaining inventory, installed-base support and warranty obligations; they do not by themselves prove that TTI is running a normal new-product program.

## What Did TTI Announce?

In a voluntary announcement dated December 11, 2025, TTI said it was “voluntarily discontinuing the HART business by the end of 2025, while still retaining the HART brand in the portfolio.”

TTI's 2025 annual report repeated the planned exit and reported HART business revenue of US$156 million for 2025. These are direct group disclosures. They outweigh inferences drawn from a product page remaining online.

The wording matters. TTI did not say Walmart acquired the brand, and it did not say the HART trademark was abandoned. It separated the discontinued business from the retained brand asset.

## Who Owns and Operates the HART Name?

TTI's 2025 corporate-structure disclosure lists Hart Consumer Products, Inc. as a wholly owned group company. Current HART pages state that HART trademarks belong to Techtronic Cordless GP and identify HART Consumer Products, Inc. or affiliates in the site footer and terms.

These entities support the conclusion that HART remains inside TTI. They do not establish the factory origin of every tool. A trademark owner, website operator, importer, retailer and physical manufacturer can all be different.

## What Was Walmart's Role?

HART launched its broad modern tool program through Walmart as an exclusive retail relationship. Walmart gave the brand national shelf and online reach across power tools, lawn and garden, hand tools, storage and cleaning.

Walmart does not own HART merely because it was the exclusive seller. In 2026, a listing can represent existing inventory or ongoing fulfillment. Buyers should check whether the exact item is sold by Walmart or a marketplace seller, whether it is new or refurbished, and whether the receipt qualifies for warranty service.

## Who Makes HART Tools?

HART belongs to TTI's cordless-tool group, but the current evidence does not justify assigning every HART SKU to one factory or country. Check the product label, manual, origin marking, importer and compliance documents.

The business exit also makes time part of the manufacturing question. A tool sold in 2026 may have been produced earlier. Record the model, serial/date code and seller rather than assuming the sale year is the production year.

## HART 20V and 40V Battery Systems

HART's current support and product pages describe separate 20V and 40V systems. The 20V platform spans power tools, lights, vacuums and selected outdoor equipment. The 40V platform serves higher-demand lawn and garden products.

Compatibility is within the documented platform, not across 20V and 40V. It also does not extend to Milwaukee or Ryobi merely because TTI owns those brands. Each brand has its own mechanical, electrical and warranty system.

For existing fleets, inventory batteries and chargers by exact model. A discontinued business can make replacement-pack price and availability more important than the original tool price.

## HART Cleaning Products and Installed-Base Risk

HART's range includes wet-dry vacuums, stick and hand vacuums, scrubbers, pressure washers and carpet-cleaning products. These products create recurring needs for filters, brushes, hoses, nozzles, cleaning formulas and batteries.

Before adding discounted inventory to a commercial fleet, calculate the usable support horizon. Verify consumables, repair routes and replacement tools. A low clearance price may be attractive for a self-contained task, but platform expansion is riskier when the business has been discontinued.

## What Happens to HART Warranties?

The official HART warranty page remains active. It currently states two years for specified 4V products, three years for power tools and outdoor products, and five years for specified 40V outdoor tools with three years for their batteries. Hand tools can have a limited lifetime warranty.

Those are category summaries, not a promise for every product or use. The page requires proof of purchase, and exclusions and commercial-use rules matter. HART also maintains a service-center page. Existing owners should save a dated copy of the applicable terms, receipt, model and serial number and confirm the current service route before shipping a tool.

## Buyer Checklist for 2026

1. Confirm model, platform, serial/date code and country of origin.
2. Identify the actual seller and whether the item is marketplace stock.
3. Check battery, charger, filter and consumable availability.
4. Obtain the current written warranty and service-center confirmation.
5. Separate a one-off discounted purchase from a long-term fleet decision.
6. Do not interpret an active webpage as evidence of resumed business operations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does TTI still own HART in 2026?

TTI said it retained the HART brand in its portfolio while discontinuing the HART business by end-2025.

### Is HART owned by Walmart?

No. Walmart was the exclusive retail channel, not the disclosed brand owner.

### Is HART going out of business?

TTI's precise statement is that it discontinued the HART business by the end of 2025 while retaining the brand. That is more accurate than speculating about bankruptcy or a trademark sale.

### Can I still buy HART tools?

Listings may remain available. Confirm seller, stock condition, production date, return rights, warranty and parts before purchasing.

### Are HART batteries compatible with Ryobi or Milwaukee?

Do not assume compatibility. HART 20V and 40V are separate documented systems, and TTI's other brands use their own platforms.

### Will HART honor existing warranties?

Current warranty and service pages remain active, but coverage depends on the exact product, proof of purchase, use and terms. Confirm the current route before relying on it.

## Final Answer

HART remains a TTI-owned brand, but TTI discontinued the HART business by the end of 2025. Walmart's continuing listings and HART's support pages should be read as inventory and installed-base evidence, not proof of a normal 2026 product program. Existing owners should protect receipts and verify batteries, consumables, service and written warranty; new fleet buyers should price the platform-exit risk explicitly.
