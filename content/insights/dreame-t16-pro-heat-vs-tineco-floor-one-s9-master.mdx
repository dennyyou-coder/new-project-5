**The Dreame T16 Pro Heat and Tineco FLOOR ONE S9 Master are hot-water floor washers aimed at greasy kitchens and sticky spills.** The T16 stands out for its detachable battery; the S9 Master for its low lay-flat profile. Check heated runtime against your floor area: heat drains batteries fast.

## Heat and runtime

Dreame lists 70 minutes in Eco mode but only 22 minutes with hot water. Tineco's 65-minute figure is for Quiet mode. Compare heated runtime with the floor area you clean in one go.

## Battery

A detachable battery, as on the T16 Pro Heat, makes long-term replacement easier and lets you swap in a spare.

## Which should you buy?

**T16 Pro Heat** if you want strong hot-water cleaning and a replaceable battery. **S9 Master** if you need to reach under low furniture and want quieter, longer runs. Note that the T16 Pro Heat and T16 Pro Steam are different models.
