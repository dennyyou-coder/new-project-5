**Choose Shark StainStriker if you want its two-solution activation system, compact under-nine-pound body and current three-year listed warranty. Choose the classic BISSELL Little Green 1400B if you prefer a simpler single-formula workflow, 48-ounce clean tank, longer cord and lower typical entry price.** The better stain result still depends on chemistry, dwell time, brushing, extraction passes and how thoroughly the fabric is rinsed and dried.

“Little Green” now covers several machines, including Mini, Max Pet, HydroSteam and cordless models. This article compares the classic corded 1400B/closely related standard Little Green with the corded Shark PX200/PX201 StainStriker. Check the exact suffix and tools before buying.

## Short Answer

| Decision | Shark StainStriker PX200/PX201 | BISSELL Little Green 1400B |
|---|---|---|
| Formula route | Two solutions mix/activate in the system; no premix under stated use | Water plus one compatible portable-cleaner formula in clean tank |
| Listed weight | PX200 about 8.51 lb; bundles/listings vary | 9.65 lb |
| Hose | About 4.3 ft | About 4–4.5 ft |
| Cord | 12 ft | 15 ft |
| Clean tank | Dual-reservoir system; check model manual | 48 oz |
| Standard tool | 3.5-inch reversible bristle tool; integrated crevice tool | 3-inch Tough Stain Tool, spraying crevice tool, HydroRinse tool on current bundle |
| Warranty headline | Three years on current product page | One year for classic 1400 series |
| Best fit | Chemistry-led pet, upholstery and car spot treatment | Simple value, capacity and broad accessory familiarity |

The companies’ suction and stain-power claims use different methods. This is not a common performance test.

## Product Families Matter

### Shark StainStriker

- PX200/PX200C: core portable platform with dual formulas and reversible bristle tool.
- PX201: bundle can add a pet tool and integrated crevice tool.
- PX251 HairPro: adds hair-focused tools and an onboard self-clean function; it is not identical to the basic unit.

### BISSELL Little Green

- 1400B and related classic models: established corded platform with a 48-ounce clean tank.
- Little Green Mini: smaller tanks and a lighter role.
- Little Green Max Pet: newer tools and body.
- Little Green HydroSteam: adds steam functions and requires a separate comparison.
- Little Green Cordless: battery platform with different tank sizes and runtime.

Do not compare a sale-priced classic Little Green with StainStriker HairPro without pricing the tools and formulas in both boxes.

## Formula Mixing and Stain Chemistry

StainStriker’s main difference is formula architecture. Shark supplies CarpetXpert Deep Clean Pro plus OXY Multiplier. The system combines them near the cleaning path rather than requiring the user to premix both in a tank.

The classic Little Green uses hot tap water—not boiling water—and a compatible BISSELL portable-cleaner formula in the clean tank. Different OXY, pet and stain formulas target different soils.

No chemistry is universal:

- protein stains need different handling from oil;
- tannin, dye and rust can react differently;
- urine requires odor treatment below the visible surface;
- bleach-sensitive fabric can lose color;
- leather, velvet and delicate natural fibers may be unsuitable.

Blot excess material first and follow the fabric care code. Test an inconspicuous area. Never mix household chemicals, bleach, ammonia or an unapproved detergent inside either machine.

## Spray, Brush and Recovery

Both are portable spray-extraction machines:

1. solution is applied;
2. the tool agitates the stain;
3. the suction slot recovers dissolved soil and water.

Technique matters. Over-spraying can wet foam or backing faster than the machine can recover it. Use controlled wet passes followed by several slow dry passes with no spray.

StainStriker’s reversible bristles provide different agitation faces. Little Green’s current classic bundle includes a 3-inch Tough Stain Tool and a narrow spraying crevice tool. Tool width changes both agitation and suction concentration.

Compare:

- spray pattern and trigger control;
- clear-tool visibility;
- suction-slot seal;
- brush stiffness;
- ability to remove hair before wetting;
- access to the nozzle for rinsing;
- replacement-tool availability.

A wider tool is faster on cushions but can lose seal on curved car seats. A narrow tool concentrates extraction but increases passes.

## Tanks and Refilling

The classic Little Green’s 48-ounce clean tank is a clear advantage for multiple seats or stair spots. Its separate dirty tank keeps recovered soil away from the supply.

StainStriker’s compact body uses dedicated solution storage and a dirty-water tank. The two-formula system reduces manual mixing but makes the user dependent on the correct formula combination.

Tank capacity is not productive capacity. Foam, the dirty-tank shutoff, formula ratio and the amount applied per pass can end a job early.

Before purchase:

- remove and reinstall every tank;
- inspect caps and gaskets;
- check whether tanks stand upright at the sink;
- price replacement caps and seals;
- confirm fill markings are readable;
- allow clearance to rinse the dirty tank.

## Hose, Cord and Reach

The classic Little Green’s 15-foot cord is three feet longer than StainStriker’s 12-foot listed cord. Hose reach is close: roughly 4–4.5 feet depending on the exact model.

For stairs and cars, total usable reach matters more than hose length alone. A machine left on a car seat can tip; one placed at the bottom of stairs may pull against the hose.

Check:

- hose bend at the body;
- storage without sharp kinks;
- strain relief;
- tool rotation;
- power-outlet location;
- ability to carry the machine when tanks contain liquid.

The hose is often the most failure-prone ownership component. Do not pull the machine by it.

## Pet Messes, Upholstery and Cars

Both can clean many colorfast carpets, rugs, upholstery, pet beds and vehicle fabrics. Suitability depends on the material, not the brand.

### Pet mess

Remove solids and absorb liquid first. Treat the full depth of contamination without saturating foam. Odor can return if urine remains below the extraction zone.

### Upholstery

Read the care tag. “W” or “WS” fabrics are usually water-cleanable under manufacturer instructions; “S” and “X” codes require other methods.

### Cars

Vacuum dry debris first. Use minimal solution around seat electronics, occupancy sensors, heated seats, airbags and connectors. Leave doors open and dry thoroughly.

Neither machine is for extracting standing water, hazardous fluids, ash or construction dust.

## Residual Water and Drying

Users often judge a spot cleaner by the visible stain immediately after use. The more important measure is what remains after drying:

- moisture in backing or foam;
- sticky detergent residue;
- wick-back from deeper soil;
- odor after 24 hours;
- fabric texture and color.

Finish with dry passes. If the formula permits, use a controlled clean-water rinse to reduce residue, then extract again. Provide airflow with fans and ventilation. Do not cover or sit on damp upholstery.

Recovery ratio depends on tool seal and material. A machine can have strong suction at the motor while recovering poorly from a curved or porous surface.

## Cleaning the Machine

Portable extractors handle dirty organic water. They need immediate cleaning.

After each job:

1. empty dirty water;
2. rinse the dirty tank and lid;
3. flush the hose using the supplied self-clean tool or clean water as instructed;
4. rinse the hand tool and remove hair;
5. wipe seals and tank seats;
6. let the hose, tool and tanks dry open;
7. store without liquid or pressure in the system.

The classic Little Green bundle’s HydroRinse tool can help flush the hose. Shark HairPro includes an onboard self-clean feature, while standard StainStriker care should follow its model manual.

Odor usually comes from wastewater, residue in the hose, trapped hair or components stored wet—not from the brand name.

## Formulas, Tools, Parts and Warranty

StainStriker requires two Shark formula streams for its advertised activation system. Shark sells CarpetXpert and OXY Multiplier separately and lists replacement tool inserts on HairPro.

BISSELL has a large ecosystem of portable formulas and classic Little Green tools. Compatibility still depends on model number.

Current Shark product pages list a three-year limited warranty for PX200/PX201/PX251. The classic BISSELL 1400-series warranty is one year. BISSELL’s general terms say proof of original purchase is required and it can repair or replace under the specified model warranty.

Confirm:

- authorized seller;
- complete model number;
- warranty guide in the box;
- hose and tool coverage;
- tank/gasket stock;
- who pays shipping;
- whether formula damage is excluded;
- commercial-use exclusions.

## Price and Total Cost

Use:

`machine + formula per job + replacement tools/seals + storage + repair shipping + time to clean and dry`

StainStriker can justify a higher price when its formula delivery and included tools reduce pretreatment steps. Little Green can be better value when simple chemistry, capacity and broad support are enough.

For auto detailers, cleaners or rental operators, household warranties may not apply. Price downtime, a spare hose/tool and daily sanitation.

## Which Should You Buy?

### Choose StainStriker if:

- you want the two-solution activation workflow;
- the smaller listed body weight helps portability;
- the current three-year warranty is valid through your seller;
- you will stock both required formulas.

### Choose Little Green if:

- you want a simpler, established platform;
- 48-ounce clean-water capacity and a 15-foot cord matter;
- the lower purchase price funds formula and tools;
- local BISSELL parts/support are stronger.

### Choose neither yet if:

- the fabric care code is unknown;
- the stain involves hazardous material;
- the area cannot dry fully;
- replacement hose or tank parts are unavailable;
- commercial use conflicts with warranty terms.

## FAQ

### Is Shark StainStriker better than BISSELL Little Green?

It has a more specialized two-formula system and a longer current warranty headline. Little Green offers simpler value, larger classic clean tank and longer cord.

### Which leaves upholstery drier?

Dryness depends on spray volume, tool seal, material and dry passes. Neither brand can overcome over-wetting.

### Can I use only water?

Follow the exact manual. Clean-water rinse passes can reduce residue when permitted, but the advertised stain performance depends on approved formulas.

### Can I use other detergents?

Use only formulas approved for the exact machine and material. Unapproved or foaming chemicals can damage pumps, seals or recovery systems.

### Which is better for cars?

Both are suitable for many auto fabrics. Choose by reach, tool fit and ability to dry the seat without wetting electronics.

### Do these heat water?

The classic Little Green uses hot tap water and does not boil or steam it. Standard StainStriker is a chemical spray-extraction system, not a steam cleaner.

## Bottom Line

StainStriker sells a more integrated chemistry system; Little Green sells proven simplicity, capacity and value. The best purchase is the machine whose formulas, tools, hose care and warranty you can support. The best result comes from controlled wetting, repeated extraction and complete drying.
