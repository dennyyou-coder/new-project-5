**Hotpoint appliances have a regional ownership and operating split. In the Americas, the Hotpoint brand is owned and sold by GE Appliances, a Haier company. In the UK and Europe, Hotpoint is operated as part of Beko Europe, following the transfer of Whirlpool Corporation's European major-appliance business.**

The same name therefore leads to different companies, factories, model ranges, support sites and warranties. A correct answer must begin with the country where the appliance was sold.

## Hotpoint Ownership at a Glance

|---|---|---|

| UK and Europe | Hotpoint is operated inside Beko Europe | Operating company, trademark rights and local seller can be different legal layers |

## Who Owns Hotpoint in the United States?

GE Appliances' official support page states that it sells Hotpoint products in the Americas and owns the Hotpoint brand across the USA, Canada, Central America and Latin America. GE Appliances' current privacy and product materials continue to include Hotpoint in its brand portfolio.

GE Appliances itself is a Haier company. Haier acquired the U.S. appliance business from General Electric in 2016, and the operating company continues to use the GE name under licence. Hotpoint in the Americas sits inside that operating platform alongside GE, GE Profile, Café, Monogram and Haier-branded appliances.

This does not make every Hotpoint right worldwide a Haier asset. The official GE Appliances page expressly separates the Americas from Europe.

## Who Owns and Operates Hotpoint in Europe?

The current Hotpoint UK site says Hotpoint is part of Beko Europe in the UK and Europe. Its terms identify Hotpoint UK Appliances Limited as the UK brand operator and note that specific products or services can be supplied by that company, Beko plc, another group company or an authorized provider.

Beko Europe was created in April 2024 by combining Whirlpool Corporation's European major-domestic-appliance business with Arçelik's European appliance operations. Hotpoint was one of the brands carried into the regional platform.

At launch, Beko B.V. owned 75% of Beko Europe and Whirlpool Corporation owned 25%. In June 2026, Beko B.V. completed the acquisition of Whirlpool's remaining interest and moved to 100% ownership. That equity change did not make every Whirlpool-origin trademark a globally Beko-owned brand; the underlying brand and licence scope must still be checked.

## Does Beko Own Hotpoint Worldwide?

No. The Americas Hotpoint business is a separate GE Appliances path. Beko's corporate material describes a portfolio of owned brands and brands used under limited licences. The portfolio presentation itself is a warning against treating every listed name as one kind of legal right.

For Europe, the commercially useful answer is that Beko Europe operates the current Hotpoint appliance business and industrial platform. For trademark diligence, a distributor should still obtain the exact territory, product-class right and contracting entity rather than relying on the group website alone.

The [Beko ownership guide](/blog/who-owns-beko-appliances-beko-europe) explains the current 100% Beko Europe ownership. The [Whirlpool regional-rights guide](/blog/who-owns-whirlpool-appliances-beko-europe) covers the licence structure behind Whirlpool-branded European products.

## How Did the Hotpoint Brand Split Happen?

Hotpoint began as a U.S. electrical-appliance brand, but its international business developed on different corporate tracks. The European Hotpoint path ultimately became part of Whirlpool Corporation's EMEA appliance business. The Americas brand remained associated with the U.S. appliance platform that later became GE Appliances under Haier ownership.

Whirlpool's annual reports explicitly stated that its prior ownership of Hotpoint in EMEA and Asia Pacific was not affiliated with the Hotpoint brand sold in the Americas. The 2024 European transaction then placed the European operating business inside Beko Europe.

This history explains why an old manual, trademark record or corporate history can name Whirlpool while a current UK site names Beko Europe. Both can be accurate for different dates and legal questions.

## Who Makes Hotpoint Appliances?

There is no single worldwide Hotpoint factory answer.

GE Appliances operates a substantial U.S. manufacturing network. Its official materials identify selected GE and Hotpoint top-freezer refrigerator production in Decatur, Alabama, but this is product-specific evidence rather than proof for every Hotpoint range, washer or dishwasher.

Beko Europe operates 11 manufacturing bases across Italy, Poland, Romania and Slovakia according to its current company materials. The network supports multiple brands and product programs. A Beko Europe plant should not automatically be called a dedicated Hotpoint factory, and one plant's output should not be assigned to an unrelated model.

For the exact product, check the rating plate, country-of-origin marking, legal manufacturer, importer, declaration of conformity and warranty certificate.

## Why the Regional Split Matters for Parts and Warranty

A Hotpoint model sold in the United States can have different parts numbering, electrical standards and service systems from a Hotpoint appliance sold in the United Kingdom or Italy. The shared brand name is not evidence that components, apps, firmware or warranties are interchangeable.

The Hotpoint UK terms say the legal entity supplying a product or service is identified at checkout and in the order confirmation. They also allow group companies or authorized providers to perform logistics, installation, repair and warranty activities. That is why the consumer brand alone cannot identify the contract party.

GE Appliances directs Americas customers to its own Hotpoint service system. Buyers should use the model and serial number on the destination-market support site, not whichever Hotpoint page ranks first in search.

## What Buyers and Suppliers Should Verify

1. Start with the destination country and date of sale.
2. Confirm whether the product follows the GE Appliances or Beko Europe path.
3. Record the exact trademark or licence territory and product class.
4. Identify the seller, manufacturer, importer and warranty guarantor separately.
5. Verify the model's origin using the rating plate and shipment documents.
6. Match parts, app region and electrical certification to the exact model.
7. For a distribution agreement, document territory, channels, service and spare-parts responsibility.
8. Keep historical Whirlpool references separate from current Beko Europe operations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Hotpoint owned by GE Appliances?

In the Americas, yes. GE Appliances says it owns the Hotpoint brand across the USA, Canada, Central America and Latin America.

### Is Hotpoint owned by Beko?

In the UK and Europe, Hotpoint is currently operated inside Beko Europe. That does not extend to the separate Americas brand.

### Does Whirlpool still own Beko Europe?

No. Beko B.V. completed the purchase of Whirlpool's remaining 25% stake in June 2026 and now owns 100%.

### Is GE Appliances owned by General Electric?

No. GE Appliances is owned by Haier Smart Home and uses the GE trademark under licence.

### Are Hotpoint appliances made in the United States?

Some U.S.-market models have documented U.S. production, but the answer varies by model. Check the exact origin label.

### Can European and U.S. Hotpoint parts be interchanged?

Do not assume so. Match parts through the exact model and regional service documentation.

## Final Answer

Hotpoint has two major regional corporate paths. GE Appliances owns and sells Hotpoint in the Americas. In the UK and Europe, Hotpoint operates inside Beko Europe, the company created from Arçelik and Whirlpool's European appliance businesses and now wholly owned by Beko B.V.

That regional split controls more than the ownership answer. It affects models, factories, sellers, parts, apps and warranties. Use the country of sale and exact product documents before assigning a Hotpoint appliance to GE Appliances, Beko Europe, a factory or a service provider.
