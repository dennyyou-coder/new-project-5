**RCM is owned by the Raimondi family.** RCM's website and company profile describe RCM S.p.A. as a family-owned business led by the fourth generation of the Raimondi family. No parent company is named.

RCM is Italian. It began operating in 1967 and is based in Casinalbo, in the province of Modena.

## Where are RCM machines made?

RCM says it designs, manufactures and sells its machines from its base in the Modena area, where it has developed products since 1967.

## What does RCM make?

Industrial sweepers, scrubber dryers, combined sweeper-scrubbers, vacuums and single-disc machines for industrial and urban cleaning.

## How does RCM sell?

Worldwide through dealers, supported with product advice, training and assistance.
