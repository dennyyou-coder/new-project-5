**Masterforce is owned as a trademark by Menard, Inc., the company behind Menards.** It is a Menards private-label tool program, not one independent manufacturer with one factory.

Different Masterforce products can involve different contract manufacturers, importers and warranty documents. The correct supplier answer therefore starts with the exact model and Menards SKU—not with a single company name copied across the range.

## Does Menards Own Masterforce?

Yes. U.S. trademark records identify **Menard, Inc.** as owner of Masterforce registrations covering power tools, batteries, hand tools, storage and related equipment. Menards is the customer-facing retail identity.

This structure is similar to other retailer private labels: the retailer's company owns or controls the mark and assortment, while outside factories make specific products under contract.

## Who Actually Makes Masterforce Tools?

There is no verified universal manufacturer.

A 2008 CPSC recall for a Masterforce 3.6V screwdriver named Menards as importer and Great Star Industry Co. as manufacturer. That is valuable evidence for model 241-0717 from that period. It does not prove Great Star manufactures today's FlexPower drills, BOOST impact tools, pressure washers, hand tools or storage products.

![Masterforce model-level supplier verification chain](/images/blog/masterforce-model-verification-map.svg)

For a current product, collect:

1. Masterforce model number and Menards SKU;
2. UPC and production code;
3. manual and warranty PDF;
4. country-of-origin label;
5. responsible importer or service contact; and
6. factory or shipment evidence for commercial sourcing.

The supplier can also change when a product is redesigned. Visual similarity or battery fit does not prove a common factory.

## Masterforce FlexPower and BOOST Explained

Menards currently promotes 100% system-wide battery compatibility across documented Masterforce FlexPower cordless tools, batteries and chargers. BOOST batteries also work with the specified FlexPower or BOOST tools and chargers.

BOOST adds communication between qualifying BOOST tools and batteries to adjust performance. That feature does not create compatibility with Performax, WORX, SKIL or another 20V brand sold at Menards.

Always confirm the exact battery list on the product page. A voltage number alone is not a compatibility standard.

## Masterforce Product Range

Masterforce extends across cordless and corded power tools, hand tools, storage, air equipment, pressure washers, lawn and garden equipment and accessories. This breadth is another reason not to assign the whole brand to one manufacturer.

For cleaning-industry buyers, the relevant products include pressure washers, blowers, vacuums and dust-producing tools with collection ports. Evaluate hoses, filters, nozzles, detergent compatibility, replacement parts and service—not only the headline warranty.

## How the Masterforce Warranty Works

Current Menards pages for many Masterforce 20V cordless tools list a **three-year limited warranty**. The brand's BOOST page also promotes a limited three-year warranty.

That term is not universal. Certain Masterforce mechanics' hand tools and accessories advertise lifetime warranties, while pressure washers and other equipment can carry their own documents. The exact model page and PDF control.

Keep the original receipt. Menards' regional store network is central to returns and service, so buyers outside its core territory should confirm how a claim will be handled before purchasing through resale channels.

## What the 2008 Recall Does—and Does Not—Prove

The CPSC recall covered about 42,000 Masterforce 3.6V cordless screwdrivers, model 241-0717. It named Great Star as manufacturer, Menards as importer and China as country of manufacture.

It does not cover current 20V products and should not be used to claim that every Masterforce tool is made by Great Star. It demonstrates the correct research method: identify the exact model, period, legal roles and remedy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Masterforce tools?

Menard, Inc. owns Masterforce trademark registrations, and Menards operates the retail program.

### Does Great Star make all Masterforce tools?

No evidence supports that catalogue-wide claim. Great Star was named for one recalled historical screwdriver; current products require separate verification.

### Are Masterforce and Performax batteries compatible?

Do not assume compatibility. Masterforce FlexPower/BOOST compatibility applies within the documented Masterforce system.

### How long is the Masterforce warranty?

Many current 20V cordless tools list three years, while some hand tools advertise lifetime coverage. Use the exact product warranty.

### Where are Masterforce tools made?

Origin varies by model and supplier. Check the label, manual and current import records.

## Final Answer

Masterforce is Menard, Inc.'s private-label tool brand, sold through Menards and produced through product-specific supply arrangements. Current FlexPower and BOOST tools form a documented 20V battery system, but shared branding and compatibility do not prove one factory. Verify model, SKU, supplier, origin and warranty separately—especially when buying outside Menards' normal service territory.
