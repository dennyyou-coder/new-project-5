**Hisense appliances are part of the wider Hisense group, whose parent is Hisense Group Holdings Co., Ltd.** A major appliance platform within that structure is the publicly listed Hisense Home Appliances Group Co., Ltd. However, the Hisense name is used by multiple affiliated and regional companies, so one group name cannot identify the legal manufacturer, seller or warranty provider for every product.

Hisense acquired Slovenian appliance manufacturer Gorenje in 2018. That deal also brought Gorenje's premium ASKO brand into Hisense's multi-brand appliance portfolio. Hisense, Gorenje and ASKO therefore share a corporate relationship, but they remain separate brands with different positioning, catalogues and market channels.

## Which Company Owns the Hisense Brand?

Hisense's official legal statement describes references to Hisense as including Hisense Group Holdings Co., Ltd. and affiliated companies. This wording matters: the consumer-facing brand is broader than one universal legal entity.

Hisense Home Appliances Group Co., Ltd. is a listed company within the appliance structure. Its reporting covers refrigeration, air conditioning, laundry, kitchen and environmental-appliance activities through the company and consolidated subsidiaries. Other Hisense businesses cover televisions, displays, technology and additional sectors.

For investors, the listed appliance company is a useful reporting boundary. For buyers, it is not enough. A product label may name a subsidiary, a European responsible operator, a local importer or another group company.

## How Do Gorenje and ASKO Fit In?

Hisense's official history records its acquisition of Gorenje in 2018. Gorenje brought a substantial European appliance operation and an established portfolio of brands. ASKO had already been described by Gorenje as its premium brand for kitchen and laundry appliances.

Hisense now presents itself as a multi-brand company behind Hisense, Gorenje and ASKO, among other names. The relationship can be summarized as:

- Hisense Group is the wider corporate group;
- Hisense is also a consumer brand used across electronics and appliances;
- Gorenje is a European appliance company and consumer brand acquired by Hisense; and
- ASKO is a premium kitchen and laundry brand within the Gorenje/Hisense Europe structure.

Shared ownership does not make the products identical. Model platforms, features, distribution agreements, parts and warranty terms can differ.

## Where Are Hisense, Gorenje and ASKO Appliances Made?

Hisense has manufacturing and operating infrastructure in China and other regions. Hisense Europe publicly presents three European production centers and multiple research-and-development centers. Its media library identifies appliance operations in locations including Slovenia, Serbia and the Czech Republic.

That establishes a real European production footprint, but it does not prove that every Gorenje or ASKO appliance is made in Europe—or that every Hisense appliance comes from China. Product families may be assigned to different plants, and sourcing can change by model generation.

![Hisense appliance model, factory, operator and warranty verification flow](/images/blog/hisense-appliance-verification-map.svg)

One official Hisense UK cordless-vacuum page, for example, names Gorenje gospodinjski aparati, d.o.o. as the EU responsible economic operator. That is evidence for the cited model's regulatory responsibility, not proof that Gorenje manufactured every Hisense vacuum.

For an exact sourcing answer, obtain:

1. the full model code and suffix;
2. the rating label and country of origin;
3. the declaration of conformity and named manufacturer;
4. the factory or supplier audit record for a commercial order; and
5. the importer, responsible operator and warranty terms for the destination market.

## Do the Brands Share Technology and Parts?

Corporate integration can support shared engineering, procurement, software and manufacturing resources. It does not guarantee interchangeability. Refrigerators, washers, ovens and floorcare products carrying different brands may use related platforms, but accessories, firmware, control boards, filters and service documentation can remain model-specific.

Buyers should use official part numbers and written compatibility statements. A visually similar Gorenje component should not be fitted to a Hisense or ASKO appliance without confirmation.

## Who Sells and Services Hisense Appliances?

Hisense uses country-specific consumer sites and regional organizations. The group's customer-center page shows that service contacts differ by country and sometimes use Hisense, Gorenje or other regional infrastructure.

This is normal for a multi-brand international group. The practical warranty route is the one written in the local sales and warranty documents—not simply the parent company's headquarters.

Cross-border purchases require extra caution. A model bought in one European market may have a different suffix, plug, energy label, importer and service arrangement from a similar-looking product sold elsewhere.

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

1. Separate the Hisense group, listed appliance platform and model-level legal manufacturer.
2. Confirm whether the proposed product is sold as Hisense, Gorenje or ASKO in the destination market.
3. Verify the factory and origin from current documents, not from the brand's historical home country.
4. Identify the invoice seller, importer, responsible operator and warranty provider.
5. Check local voltage, energy labels, radio approvals and installation rules.
6. Obtain written parts, software and service commitments for commercial distribution.
7. Do not assume cross-brand compatibility because the brands share a parent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Hisense appliances?

Hisense appliances are part of the Hisense group led by Hisense Group Holdings Co., Ltd. Hisense Home Appliances Group Co., Ltd. is a major listed appliance platform within the group.

### Is Gorenje owned by Hisense?

Yes. Hisense's official history records the acquisition of Gorenje in 2018.

### Is ASKO a Hisense brand?

ASKO is part of the wider Hisense appliance portfolio through Gorenje. It remains a distinct premium kitchen and laundry brand.

### Are Gorenje and Hisense the same company?

They are related through ownership but are not the same consumer brand or necessarily the same legal seller for a product.

### Where are Hisense appliances made?

The group has production in China and other regions, including European appliance production through Hisense Europe. Check the exact model label for country of origin and legal manufacturer.

## Final Answer

Hisense appliances belong to a multi-company group led by Hisense Group Holdings. Hisense Home Appliances is an important listed platform, and the 2018 Gorenje acquisition added both Gorenje and premium brand ASKO to the wider portfolio.

The ownership chart answers who controls the brands; it does not answer every sourcing question. For a specific appliance, verify the model's factory, origin, manufacturer, importer, local seller and warranty provider separately.
