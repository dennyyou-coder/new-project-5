**Kress is owned by Positec Group.** The German-origin tool brand joined Positec in 2017 and now sits beside WORX in the group's portfolio. Positec positions Kress primarily around professional and residential cordless outdoor equipment, commercial battery systems and robotic lawn mowers.

That ownership answer does not mean every Kress product comes from one factory. Positec discloses manufacturing bases in Suzhou, Zhangjiagang, Kunshan and Vietnam, while Kress products, sellers, importers and warranty entities vary by model and country.

## From a German Workshop to Positec

Kress traces its history to Christian Kress's electrical workshop, founded in Germany in 1928. Its official history records a Bisingen factory in 1964, professional portable power tools from 1969 and an R&D operation in Wattwil, Switzerland, from 1979.

The decisive modern change came in 2017, when Kress joined Positec. Positec says it was founded in 1994 and now owns international brands including WORX and Kress, with products and services in nearly 70 countries and regions.

Historical German engineering is therefore part of Kress's identity, but it should not be used as a universal present-day country-of-origin claim.

## Who Manufactures Kress Tools?

Positec is not merely a licensing parent. It describes R&D centers in Italy, Suzhou and Shanghai and manufacturing bases in Suzhou, Zhangjiagang, Kunshan and Vietnam. Its quality page says the Suzhou factory covers 115,000 square meters and includes assembly, motor, die-casting, machining, coating and injection-molding lines.

Those disclosures establish group capability. They do not allocate every Kress mower, trimmer, battery or charger to that site. The exact model label, declaration, manual, importer record and shipping documents remain the better evidence for a purchase.

The Kunshan base is especially relevant to Kress's current direction: Positec says it focuses on commercial garden-product R&D and manufacturing. Even so, buyers should not convert that focus into a claim that every Kress Commercial product is made there.

## Why Robot Mowers Matter to Kress

Kress's strategy changed after the Positec acquisition. The current range emphasizes robotic lawn mowers and cordless outdoor power equipment rather than treating robot mowing as a side category.

Kress RTKn systems use network-based real-time kinematic positioning to support boundary-wire-free operation in qualifying installations. Commercial value depends on more than the mower: site mapping, signal coverage, connectivity, charging layout, safety review, dealer setup, blades, parts and response time all affect uptime.

The brand's dealer model is therefore important. A professional customer is buying an operating system and local support route, not just a machine in a box. Compare dealer capability, territory rights and service commitments before comparing headline acreage.

## What Is the Kress 60V CyberSystem?

Kress markets 60V CyberSystem and 60V Professional equipment for demanding outdoor work. Positec introduced Kress Commercial in 2022 and highlighted an eight-minute charging proposition for specified batteries and chargers.

Treat that figure as product-system specific. Charging time changes with the battery, charger, temperature and permitted operating conditions. The 60V system is also separate from Kress 20V/40V residential products and from WORX batteries. Common group ownership does not establish cross-brand or cross-voltage compatibility.

## Are Kress and WORX the Same?

No. Kress and WORX are separate Positec brands.

WORX is broadly positioned across consumer DIY, home, garden and Landroid robotic products. Kress has a stronger professional outdoor, dealer and commercial-robotics emphasis. A distributor, battery or warranty authorized for one brand should not be assumed valid for the other.

## Warranty, Data and Buyer Checks

Kress warranties differ by product, use, registration, seller and country. Do not apply a residential promotional term to commercial fleet use. Keep the invoice, serial number, battery and charger identities, registration record and dealer work order.

Connected mowers add another responsibility layer. Kress's U.S. privacy notice identifies Positec Tool Corporation as data controller for relevant group sites, while local terms can name regional Positec companies. The data controller, invoice seller, legal manufacturer and repair dealer can be different entities.

Before buying, verify:

1. exact model, production code and country-of-origin label;
2. battery and charger compatibility;
3. dealer authorization and service territory;
4. RTK, cellular or other connectivity requirements;
5. warranty terms for residential or commercial use; and
6. blade, battery, electronics and replacement-machine availability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Kress tools today?

Positec Group owns Kress. The brand joined Positec in 2017.

### Is Kress a German company?

Kress began in Germany in 1928. Today it is a Positec-owned international brand, so German origin should not be confused with the factory origin of every current product.

### Where are Kress tools made?

Positec reports manufacturing bases in China and Vietnam. Check the exact Kress model and shipment documents rather than assigning the whole catalogue one origin.

### Does Positec also own WORX?

Yes. WORX and Kress are sister brands, not interchangeable product or dealer systems.

### Are Kress robot mowers commercial machines?

Kress offers both residential and commercial robotic-mower ranges. Suitability depends on the exact model, site, installation, dealer support and service plan.

## Final Answer

Kress is a Positec Group brand with roots in Germany and a current strategy centered on cordless outdoor equipment and robotic mowing. Positec supplies a substantial R&D and manufacturing system, but a group factory list cannot identify every SKU's origin. For professional procurement, verify the exact mower or tool, battery platform, dealer, connected services, manufacturer, origin and written warranty.
