**Tennant Company owns Tennant, and Tennant Company is owned by its public shareholders.** It is an independent company listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker TNC, and its filings identify no parent company. Tennant is American: it is headquartered in Eden Prairie, Minnesota, and traces its history to George H. Tennant's woodworking business in Minneapolis in 1870.

Tennant makes commercial and industrial floor scrubbers, sweepers, vacuums and extractors, and a growing range of autonomous cleaning machines. It also owns IPC, a professional-cleaning brand it reports within its business.

## Is Tennant owned by another company?

No. Tennant is not a subsidiary of a larger group. Its common stock trades on the NYSE and its annual report on Form 10-K names no parent. That separates Tennant from several of its European rivals: [Nilfisk](/blog/who-owns-nilfisk-freudenberg-manufacturing) has belonged to Freudenberg since April 2026, [Hako](/blog/who-owns-hako-possehl-group) is wholly owned by the Possehl Group, and [Kärcher](/blog/who-owns-karcher-family-professional-cleaning-network) is a private family business.

## Who runs Tennant?

Dave Huml is president and chief executive officer. Richard H. (Rusty) Zay became chief operating officer on July 1, 2026; he was previously chief commercial officer and has been on the leadership team since 2010.

## What does Tennant make?

Tennant's range covers three groups:

- **Floor scrubbers and sweepers**: walk-behind, ride-on and compact machines for commercial and industrial floors.
- **Autonomous cleaning machines**: the ROVR line, including the X6 ROVR autonomous scrubber, and, since October 2026, the P1 VAC, its first autonomous vacuum for soft floors.
- **Vacuums, extractors, parts and service**: commercial vacuuming, carpet care and service programmes.

Robotics is growing far faster than the rest of Tennant. In the third quarter of 2026, robotics sales reached about USD 37.3 million, up 71% from a year earlier, while total net sales rose 6.2% to USD 322.3 million. See [Tennant's Q3 results](/news/tennant-third-quarter-2026-results) and our wider look at [where commercial cleaning robots go next](/blog/commercial-cleaning-robots-next).

## Where are Tennant machines made?

Tennant's 2025 Form 10-K reports 11 manufacturing locations worldwide. The company reports as a single segment that designs, manufactures and markets cleaning products, so the factory for a particular model is not published at company level. The machine's serial plate and shipping documents show its country of origin.

## How does Tennant sell?

Tennant reports serving more than 40,000 customers across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, Africa and Asia Pacific. It sells directly and through distributors, and also provides parts and service. For a fleet contract, the quotation names the Tennant entity or authorised distributor responsible for warranty and service in that country.
