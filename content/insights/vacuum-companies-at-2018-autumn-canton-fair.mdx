## 2018 Fall Canton Fair Begins: A Must-Not-Miss Opportunity for Export-Oriented Companies

For export-oriented enterprises, the 2018 fall Canton Fair was an indispensable opportunity. Typically, companies would showcase their latest products in the most prominent positions at their booths. Among them, there were some interesting applications to be found.

### Kingclean Electrical Appliances

Kingclean Electrical Appliances usually gives off a sense of being somewhat perfunctory when it comes to exhibitions. At this fair, they displayed some machines rather casually. The main machine showcased at the exhibition was a coffee maker!!!

Denny's Industry Commentary:

Segzoo, a company invested in by Kingclean’s founder, is now focusing on coffee machine operations. This move indicates that Kingclean has begun channeling resources into the coffee appliance sector.

> 9. Suzhou Dr. Coffee System Technology Co., Ltd. Registered address: No. 2 Kaiyuan Road, Science and Technology City, Suzhou New District. Main business: complete coffee machines; technical development, research, services and transfer in related hardware and software; production and sale of coffee machines and related appliances with after-sales service; import and export of coffee-machine goods and technology. At 31 December 2016 its audited total assets were RMB 789,000, net assets RMB 701,400, and net loss RMB 798,600. Kingclean (Suzhou) Investment holds 60% of the company and is wholly owned by Ni Zugen, so Ni Zugen is the actual controller. The company and Dr. Coffee share the same actual controller, making Dr. Coffee a related party under Rule 10.1.3 of the Shanghai Stock Exchange Listing Rules. (Kingclean announcement, translated)
