**Sharp is controlled by Hon Hai Precision Industry, better known as Foxconn.** Sharp Corporation, the listed Japanese company behind the SHARP brand, names Taiwan's Hon Hai as its parent. In June 2026 Sharp reported that Hon Hai held 34.1% of its voting rights directly and indirectly, or 54.2% when closely related holders are included.

Sharp is Japanese. Tokuji Hayakawa founded it in 1912, and it is headquartered in Osaka. Hon Hai became its controlling shareholder through a strategic alliance in 2016.

## Is Sharp still Japanese?

Sharp is a Japanese company with a Taiwanese parent. Its headquarters, listing and brand are Japanese, while control has sat with Hon Hai since 2016.

## Who makes Sharp appliances in Europe?

Sharp Home Europe says its European major and small domestic appliance business runs in collaboration with Vestel and uses Vestel's manufacturing and design. Its imprint names Vestel companies or branches as the responsible entities in France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland and the UK. See [who owns Vestel](/blog/who-owns-vestel-zorlu-holding).

## Where are Sharp appliances made elsewhere?

Sharp lists appliance-related manufacturing sites in Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia and Europe. Its range includes washing machines, refrigerators, air conditioners, air purifiers and stick vacuum cleaners.
