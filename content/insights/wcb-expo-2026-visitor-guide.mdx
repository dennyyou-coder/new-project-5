From 18 to 20 November, the [2026 WCB International Cleaning Appliance Expo](/wcb-expo) takes place at the Suzhou Shishan Convention Center in Suzhou, China. 127 companies are exhibiting, and admission is free for visitors.

Many friends have asked me: I am not a brand and I don’t run a factory, so what will I find at WCB?

My answer is simple. If you work in cleaning appliances, whether you are an agent, a brand, an online seller, a product factory or a component supplier, you can find the partners you are looking for here. This guide explains how.

## Who is in the hall

This year, almost the whole cleaning-appliance supply chain is in one place.

**Brands.** [ECOVACS](/brands/ecovacs), [Roborock](/brands/roborock), [Midea](/brands/midea) cleaning, [Navimow](/brands/segway-navimow), [WYBOT](/brands/wybot) and [BISSELL](/brands/bissell) are all bringing products. Until now, seeing the leading brands’ new products together meant a trip to IFA in Berlin, CES in Las Vegas or AWE in Shanghai. This time they are in Suzhou.

**Product factories.** More than ten OEM and ODM factories, covering vacuum cleaners, robot vacuums, floor washers, window-cleaning robots, robotic mowers and pool cleaners.

**Core components.** Dozens of component suppliers: motors, pumps, batteries, sensors, bearings, brushes, seals and moulds.

With brands, finished products, contract manufacturing and components under one roof, you can follow a product from the idea to the factory to each part, and meet the people behind every step.

## Whoever you are, you can find partners

![Who can find what at WCB 2026: agents, brands and sellers, product factories, component suppliers and people still exploring](/images/articles/wcb-expo-2026-visitor-guide/03-who-finds-what.webp)

**If you are an agent or distributor,** you can see this year’s new products from the leading brands, and find newer brands and factories looking for agents. Walk the hall once and you will have a clear idea of which categories are growing and which products suit your market.

**If you are a brand or an online seller,** you can find factories that can build your products. Robot vacuums, floor washers, vacuum cleaners, robotic mowers and pool cleaners: more than ten product factories are in one hall, so you can compare designs, prices and lead times face to face without touring factories one by one.

**If you run a product factory,** you can meet brand and channel customers, and find more competitive component suppliers. Motors, batteries, pumps and sensors are all in the same hall, so you can benchmark your supply chain in a single visit.

**If you supply components,** R&D and purchasing staff from brands and product factories are on site. In three days, in one hall, you can meet most of the customers you would want to see in a year.

**If you are still exploring** where the cleaning industry goes next, you have even more reason to come. Seeing the products, hearing the forums and talking to people in the trade is worth more than ten articles online.

## All three days are worth it

I recommend coming from 18 to 20 November, or for at least two days. The booths are open on all three days, and each day has something different.

**18 November: forum day**

There are two forums, both free for visitors, with eight speakers in total. Talks are in Chinese.

![Speakers and topics at the two WCB forums on 18 November 2026](/images/articles/wcb-expo-2026-visitor-guide/04-forum-speakers.webp "Speakers and topics are from the organiser’s preliminary programme and may change.")

Morning session:

- Denny You: The Road to a Trillion, Together
- Tian Yali, AVC (All View Cloud): China’s cleaning appliance industry in 2026 and its trends
- Dan Xiaoguang, WYBOT: Cordless AI and the new global landscape for pool robots
- Chen Qingbao, Xiaoke Import & Export: New e-commerce opportunities in the Middle East

Afternoon session:

- Shi Jinxing, China Merchants Securities: Robotic mowers, robot vacuums and physical AI
- Xu Xinfeng, Ruijin Technology: Putting AI to work in companies
- Wang Jian, Sige Transmission: Gear transmission applications and innovation
- An industry expert on robotic mowers

From market data and capital markets to overseas e-commerce, AI adoption and core components, one day covers several of the key questions facing the industry. In the first talk of the morning, I will explain where a trillion-yuan cleaning industry comes from, and how far brands, contract manufacturers and component suppliers can each go.

**19 and 20 November: product launch days**

Each day has a launch session, with five or six exhibitors presenting new products and technology. If something catches your eye, you can walk straight to their booth afterwards.

Beyond that, give your time to the booths. 127 exhibitors are hard to cover properly in one day. Walk the whole hall on the first day, then go back to the booths that interest you on the second and third days for deeper conversations.

## How to get there

- Dates: 18–20 November 2026 (Wednesday to Friday)
- Venue: Suzhou Shishan Convention Center, exhibition hall, 78 Jinshan East Road, Suzhou New District, Suzhou, China
- Metro: Suzhou Metro Line 1, Shizishan Station, Exit 6, about a 400-metre walk
- Admission: free for visitors. In China, register through the WeChat mini program “WCB会展通”; from overseas, [plan your visit here](/contact?inquiry=expo_visitor#project-form).

## A few tips

1. **Register in advance.** It takes a few minutes and gets you in faster on the day.
2. **Come with questions.** Write down the products, factories, components and order sizes you are looking for. You will get much more out of the visit.
3. **Bring business cards and add contacts.** Many booth conversations only last a few minutes; swap details so you can keep talking after the show.
4. **Arrive early for the forums.** Both are free and seats are limited.
