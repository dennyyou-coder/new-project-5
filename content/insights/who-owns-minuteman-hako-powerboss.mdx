**Minuteman is owned by Hako Group, which belongs to Germany's Possehl Group.** Hako describes Minuteman International, Inc. as one of its subsidiaries and as the American parent company of the Minuteman, PowerBoss and Multi-Clean brands. Hako itself has been wholly owned by Possehl, a diversified industrial group based in Lübeck, since 2007.

Minuteman is an American business. It is based in Pingree Grove, Illinois, and says it has more than 60 years of experience in commercial cleaning.

## Is Minuteman a German company?

No. Minuteman is an American company with a German owner. Its products, manuals, warranty and dealer network are run from Illinois, while strategic ownership sits with Hako in Bad Oldesloe and, above it, the Possehl Group. Read more in our guide to [who owns Hako](/blog/who-owns-hako-possehl-group).

## Who owns PowerBoss?

PowerBoss belongs to Minuteman. Hako's group description names Minuteman International as the American parent of the Minuteman, PowerBoss and Multi-Clean brands, so PowerBoss also sits inside Hako Group.

## What does Minuteman make?

- **Floor scrubbers and sweepers**: walk-behind, ride-on and autonomous machines, including the RoboScrub line.
- **Carpet care and commercial vacuums**: extractors, upright and backpack vacuums.
- **Specialist vacuums**: critical-filter and hazardous-material vacuums.

Minuteman sells into janitorial and sanitation (JanSan), manufacturing, government, healthcare, aviation, education, hospitality, retail and warehouse markets.

## Where are Minuteman machines made?

Hako describes Minuteman International in Illinois as a producer of commercial and industrial floor-cleaning machines, small commercial-cleaning equipment and special vacuums. Minuteman's website carries a Made in the USA statement at company level. That statement does not say every model is built in the United States, so check the serial plate and documents for a specific machine.

## How does Minuteman sell?

Minuteman sells through dealers. Its commercial warranty, revised in May 2025, requires claims to go through factory-authorised repair stations and directs users to the selling dealer first.
