**Groupe SEB is headed by SEB S.A., a French company listed on Euronext Paris.** It is not a subsidiary of another household-appliance conglomerate. Official corporate disclosures describe a founder-family shareholder concert with long-term influence, while public investors hold other listed shares.

Groupe SEB operates a large portfolio that includes Tefal, Rowenta, Moulinex, Krups, WMF and SUPOR. These brands share a corporate group, but they are not interchangeable legal entities. The factory, seller, importer and warranty provider for a specific product still depend on the model and market.

## Is Groupe SEB a Public or Family-Owned Company?

Both descriptions need qualification. SEB S.A. is publicly traded, so outside investors can own shares. At the same time, Groupe SEB's official governance and shareholder materials describe coordinated founder-family shareholdings intended to preserve long-term influence.

That makes Groupe SEB a listed company with a durable family-control structure, not a privately held company owned by one person. Stanislas de Gramont is identified by Groupe SEB as Chief Executive Officer. Management responsibility should not be confused with share ownership.

The group traces its industrial origins to 1857. SEB S.A. was created as a holding company in 1973 and listed in Paris in 1975. Readers looking for historical operating signals can see [Groupe SEB’s 2018 Report and the Logic of a Global Small Appliance Giant](/blog/groupe-seb-2018-annual-report-signals); this guide concentrates on the current ownership and responsibility map.

## Which Brands Are Part of Groupe SEB?

Groupe SEB uses a multi-brand strategy across consumer, premium and professional markets. Its better-known brands include:

- **Tefal and T-fal** for cookware and small appliances in different markets;
- **Rowenta** for floorcare, linen care and home comfort;
- **Moulinex and Krups** for food preparation, cooking and beverages;
- **WMF, All-Clad and Lagostina** in premium cookware or appliances;
- **SUPOR** in cookware and small appliances, especially in China; and
- professional brands in coffee and commercial equipment.

A portfolio list does not prove that one company owns every trademark in every jurisdiction or serves every customer directly. The relevant regional subsidiary, license, distributor and product documents remain decisive.

## How Does SUPOR Fit Into Groupe SEB?

SUPOR is not merely a label placed on a Groupe SEB product. Zhejiang Supor Co., Ltd. is a listed Chinese company with its own subsidiaries, procurement, production and sales systems. Its 2025 annual reporting identifies SEB INTERNATIONALE S.A.S. as the parent and SEB S.A. as the ultimate parent.

Groupe SEB's history records its investment in SUPOR from 2007. This gives the group a major Chinese operating platform and brand, while SUPOR retains a distinct corporate and market identity. A SUPOR supplier, factory or warranty statement must therefore be checked against Zhejiang Supor and the exact product—not inferred from a French Groupe SEB site.

## Where Does Groupe SEB Manufacture Products?

Groupe SEB's 2025 Universal Registration Document describes a global industrial system with 47 industrial sites in 14 countries. It reports that roughly 60% of production is internal and 40% is externally sourced. Those figures explain the business model, but they do not assign every product to a plant.

Official industrial materials identify category capabilities at named sites, including French and Chinese facilities. SUPOR also discloses its own manufacturing network. Product allocation can change across brands, generations and destination markets.

![Groupe SEB model, factory, channel and warranty verification flow](/images/blog/groupe-seb-product-verification-map.svg)

For a specific order, verify:

1. the exact consumer brand and full model suffix;
2. the trademark or license applicable in the destination country;
3. the legal manufacturer and conformity-document holder;
4. the production site and country of origin;
5. whether the factory is internal or an external partner; and
6. the importer, invoice seller, spare-parts route and warranty provider.

## Is Groupe SEB the Same as Tefal or Rowenta?

No. Groupe SEB is the corporate group. Tefal and Rowenta are consumer brands in its portfolio. A product may also involve a regional sales company, a factory, an importer and an authorized repair network.

This distinction matters for distributors. A Groupe SEB annual report can establish group control, while a declaration of conformity or product label establishes model-level responsibility. Neither document replaces the other.

## How Do Distribution and Repairs Work?

Groupe SEB reports sales in more than 150 countries through retail, branded stores and e-commerce. Individual brands do not necessarily have the same catalogue or channel in every country.

The group also promotes repairability and spare-parts support across many electrical appliances. Buyers should still check the exact market terms, because repair duration, commercial-use exclusions, authorized service and parts availability can differ by product and country.

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

1. Separate SEB S.A., the portfolio brand and the contracting company.
2. Confirm whether a name is owned, licensed or used regionally.
3. Match the full model to its declaration, factory and origin.
4. Validate distributor or retailer authorization for the destination market.
5. Confirm voltage, plug, safety and radio requirements.
6. Obtain written parts, repair and warranty terms.
7. Do not assume compatibility across brands in the same group.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is the owner of Groupe SEB?

SEB S.A. is the listed company at the head of Groupe SEB. Founder-family shareholders retain long-term influence through a concerted structure, alongside public investors.

### Is Groupe SEB owned by Tefal?

No. Tefal is a consumer brand within Groupe SEB, not the parent company.

### Does Groupe SEB own Rowenta?

Yes. Groupe SEB acquired Rowenta and its subsidiaries in 1988. Rowenta remains a separate consumer brand.

### Does Groupe SEB own SUPOR?

Groupe SEB ultimately controls listed Zhejiang Supor through its corporate structure. SUPOR retains its own listed company, subsidiaries and brand identity.

### Where are Groupe SEB products made?

The group uses internal factories across several countries and external sourcing. The exact country of origin must be checked on the product and its documents.

## Final Answer

Groupe SEB is headed by listed French company SEB S.A. and influenced over the long term by coordinated founder-family shareholders. It is not owned by another appliance conglomerate.

Its scale comes from a broad portfolio—including Rowenta, Tefal, WMF and SUPOR—and a mixed internal and external production network. Corporate ownership does not identify the factory or warrantor for a specific appliance, so buyers must verify the model, production site, local operator and service terms separately.
