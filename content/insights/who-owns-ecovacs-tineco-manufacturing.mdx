**ECOVACS is owned through Ecovacs Robotics Co., Ltd., a public company listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange under code 603486.** The listed company has public shareholders, but its 2025 annual report identifies Suzhou Chuangling Smart Investment Management Co., Ltd. as controlling shareholder and founder Qian Dongqi together with David Cheng Qian as actual controllers.

**Who makes ECOVACS products?** Ecovacs Robotics says the group mainly uses self-production and has built capabilities extending from key components to finished robots. That establishes substantial in-house manufacturing, but it does not identify one legal manufacturer, factory or country of origin for every DEEBOT, WINBOT, GOAT or DEEBOT PRO model.

Tineco is not the owner of ECOVACS. It is a separate consumer brand inside the same listed group. ECOVACS focuses on service robots such as DEEBOT, WINBOT and GOAT, while Tineco focuses on smart lifestyle appliances led by wet-and-dry floor cleaners.

## What Company Actually Owns ECOVACS?

The relevant corporate parent is **Ecovacs Robotics Co., Ltd.** The company is listed in Shanghai, so “who owns ECOVACS?” has two correct layers:

- The listed company controls the ECOVACS brand and operating group.
- The listed company's shares are held by multiple investors, with a controlling-shareholder and actual-controller structure disclosed in its annual report.

According to the company's [2025 annual report filed with the Shanghai Stock Exchange](https://www.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-04-25/603486_20260425_WFVJ.pdf), Suzhou Chuangling Smart Investment Management Co., Ltd. was the controlling shareholder. The report named Qian Dongqi and David Cheng Qian as actual controllers.

This is more precise than saying “the founder owns ECOVACS” or “ECOVACS owns itself.” The founder family and control vehicles matter, but the operating parent is a public company with other shareholders and formal governance.

## Is ECOVACS a Chinese Company?

Yes. Ecovacs Robotics is headquartered in Suzhou, Jiangsu, China. The business traces its origin to a factory established in 1998, launched the ECOVACS brand in 2006 and later developed a broader service-robot portfolio.

Its [official 25th-anniversary history](https://www.ecovacs.com/us/newsroom/official-news/ecovacs-25th-anniversary) describes a progression from an OEM vacuum-cleaner factory to a manufacturer and developer of its own products and brands. That history is relevant to manufacturing capability, but it should not be used as proof that every current model comes from one Suzhou assembly line.

The group now contains distinct consumer brands, robotics businesses, component operations, overseas sales companies and other subsidiaries. A global brand can be Chinese-controlled while the seller, importer, warranty provider and product origin differ by market.

## What Is the Relationship Between ECOVACS and Tineco?

ECOVACS and Tineco are sister brands under the same listed group.

The 2025 annual report describes a dual-brand consumer structure: **ECOVACS service robots** and **Tineco smart lifestyle appliances**. It identifies Tineco Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. within the consolidated subsidiary structure. ECOVACS' official group history likewise presents ECOVACS ROBOTICS and Tineco as two brands developed in parallel.

Their product roles are different:

| Brand | Main role | Representative categories |
|---|---|---|
| ECOVACS | Service robotics | DEEBOT robot vacuums, WINBOT window robots, GOAT lawn robots and DEEBOT PRO commercial cleaning robots |
| Tineco | Intelligent household appliances | FLOOR ONE wet-dry cleaners, cordless vacuums and selected kitchen or personal-care products |

Shared group control can create common R&D, component, manufacturing, finance or channel resources. It does not make a Tineco FLOOR ONE an ECOVACS-branded product, and it does not make Tineco the manufacturer named on every DEEBOT document.

## Who Makes ECOVACS Products?

Ecovacs Robotics is both a brand operator and a manufacturer with substantial in-house capability. The 2025 annual report says the company mainly uses self-production and has developed capabilities extending from key components to finished robots.

That is stronger evidence than a generic brand-licensing model. It supports the conclusion that the listed group controls important manufacturing and supply-chain functions rather than simply placing its badge on unrelated imported robots.

It still does not create one universal factory answer. The group includes separate entities for ECOVACS products, Tineco products, commercial robots, overseas sales and components. Production assignments and responsible legal entities can vary by model.

The correct hierarchy is:

1. Ecovacs Robotics Co., Ltd. is the listed group parent.
2. ECOVACS is the service-robot brand.
3. Group manufacturing and component subsidiaries provide industrial capability.
4. A specific legal manufacturer and factory must be matched to the exact product.
5. A regional seller, importer or distributor handles local commercial responsibility.

## Does ECOVACS Manufacture Its Own Motors and Batteries?

The group has invested upstream. Its annual reporting describes motor and drive-component capabilities and a polymer lithium-ion battery project intended to provide customized pouch-cell solutions for ECOVACS and Tineco products.

That does not mean every motor, cell or battery pack in every product is made by the group. “Vertically integrated” and “100% internally sourced” are not equivalent claims. Product bills of materials can combine internal and approved external components.

Commercial buyers should request the battery-cell maker, pack assembler, motor supplier, safety certification and change-control process for the contracted SKU. Group investment is strategic evidence; model records remain the compliance evidence.

## What Products Belong to ECOVACS?

ECOVACS has expanded beyond the DEEBOT robot vacuum:

- **DEEBOT** covers residential robotic vacuuming and mopping systems.
- **WINBOT** covers robotic window cleaning.
- **GOAT** covers residential robotic lawn mowing.
- **DEEBOT PRO** covers commercial cleaning robots for professional facilities.

The commercial range is an important boundary. At Interclean Amsterdam 2026, ECOVACS showed the DEEBOT PRO M1, K1 VAC and M1 3D and announced agreements with European customers. That B2B activity belongs to the ECOVACS robotics system, but it uses a different sales, deployment and service model from a home robot sold through retail.

## Is ECOVACS Owned by Xiaomi, Roborock or iRobot?

No. ECOVACS is not owned by Xiaomi, Roborock or iRobot.

Those companies compete in connected floor care, and industry partnerships can create additional confusion. A technology collaboration, retail relationship or common supplier does not establish equity ownership.

For example, ECOVACS and BSH announced a strategic partnership in 2025 around robot-vacuum technology for a Bosch home-appliance product. The announcement described co-development and ECOVACS technology; it did not say BSH acquired ECOVACS.

Likewise, product comparisons such as [Roborock versus ECOVACS](/blog/roborock-vs-ecovacs-robot-vacuums) address platform choices, not corporate control.

## Who Handles ECOVACS Sales and Warranty?

ECOVACS uses regional websites, sales entities, retailers, distributors and service routes. A global ECOVACS product announcement does not guarantee that the model is officially sold or supported in every country.

Before buying or distributing a product, identify:

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Legal manufacturer on product documents | Establishes model-level manufacturing responsibility |
| Country of origin and factory | Supports customs, procurement and supplier audit |
| Authorized regional seller | Determines legitimate channel and local commercial terms |
| Importer or responsible person | Identifies the entity placing the product on the market |
| App and cloud-service provider | Clarifies account, data and update responsibility |
| Battery and radio certifications | Confirms the exact hardware and destination-market approvals |
| Warranty provider and service network | Establishes who handles parts, labor and returns |
| Commercial deployment agreement | Defines installation, mapping, uptime and onsite support for DEEBOT PRO |
