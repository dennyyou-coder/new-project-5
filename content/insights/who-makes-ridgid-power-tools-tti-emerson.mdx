**RIDGID power tools are not made by the same business that makes every other RIDGID product. Emerson owns the RIDGID brand system through Ridge Tool Company. RIDGID-branded handheld and selected stationary power tools are operated under license through Techtronic Industries, or TTI, with OWT Industries and TTI Consumer Power Tools appearing in official product, service and regulatory records. Home Depot is the main U.S. retail channel, not the brand owner.**

The short version is: **Emerson owns the name; TTI's power-tool organization is responsible for the licensed power-tool line; individual factories and countries of origin vary by model.** This answer should not be copied to RIDGID plumbing tools, wet/dry vacuums, pumps, generators or every product carrying the orange RIDGID mark.

## Who Owns the RIDGID Brand?

RIDGID began with Ridge Tool Company. The brand's [official timeline](https://www.ridgid.com/us/en/ridgidbuilds-timeline) says Ridge Tool was incorporated in Ohio in 1923 and became part of Emerson Electric in 1966.

The current legal record remains consistent with that history. RIDGID's [privacy notice](https://www.ridgid.com/us/en/privacy-notice), revised in January 2026, identifies Ridge Tool Company and related Emerson Professional Tools entities as subsidiaries of Emerson Electric Co. Emerson's fiscal 2025 subsidiary filing also lists Ridge Tool Company, Ridge Tool Manufacturing Company and RIDGID-related entities inside the Emerson group.

That establishes the corporate and trademark side of the answer. It does not mean Emerson's professional plumbing-tool operation physically builds every RIDGID-branded drill, saw or nailer.

## Who Manufactures RIDGID Power Tools?

The clearest public description of the licensed structure comes from a TTI announcement that identifies RIDGID as an Emerson trademark and says RIDGID professional power tools were designed and manufactured by OWT Industries, a wholly owned TTI subsidiary.

That statement is historical, so it should be checked against current product evidence rather than treated as a permanent factory list. Current evidence still shows a direct TTI role:

- RIDGID's current [Lifetime Service Agreement](https://www.ridgid.com/us/en/lsa-warranty) covers eligible RIDGID handheld power tools, selected stationary tools, batteries, pneumatic tools, compressors and outdoor power equipment, and directs technical-service registration to Anderson, South Carolina;
- the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission's [2025 RIDGID framing-nailer recall](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2025/TTI-Consumer-Power-Tools-Recalls-RIDGID-Framing-Nailers-Due-to-Laceration-Hazard) names TTI Consumer Power Tools as the recalling importer and directs owners to the RIDGID power-tools site;
- the same recall identifies two specific RIDGID 18V nailer models and says they were manufactured in China.

Together, these records support TTI as the current operating and product-responsibility system behind the licensed RIDGID power-tool category. They do **not** prove that OWT Industries is the named legal manufacturer on every current tool, or that every RIDGID power tool comes from one TTI factory.

## Emerson, TTI, OWT and Home Depot: What Each One Does

**Emerson and Ridge Tool Company** provide the brand-ownership and trademark context. Their direct RIDGID businesses also cover professional categories that are outside this article's power-tool scope.

**Techtronic Industries** is the industrial group associated with the licensed RIDGID power-tool operation. TTI also operates other tool businesses, but that common parent relationship does not merge brands, battery platforms or warranties.

**OWT Industries** is the TTI subsidiary named in the public RIDGID power-tool manufacturing description. Product labels, manuals, safety filings and service documents should still be used to identify the responsible entity for a current model.

**TTI Consumer Power Tools** appears in current U.S. regulatory evidence as the importer and recall operator for specific RIDGID power tools.

**Home Depot** is the key retail channel. RIDGID's service terms say Lifetime Service Agreement eligibility depends on an eligible product, timely registration and qualifying purchase evidence; the U.S. program specifically emphasizes eligible tools purchased from Home Depot. A retailer receipt can therefore be operationally important without making the retailer the trademark owner or factory.

## RIDGID Power Tools Are Not the Same as RIDGID Wet/Dry Vacuums

This is the most important category boundary. The separate World Clean Biz guide to [RIDGID wet/dry vacuums](/blog/who-owns-ridgid-wet-dry-vacuums-emerson) documents an Emerson Tool Company product, patent and warranty relationship for that category.

The power-tool line has a different operating chain tied to TTI. A drill, impact driver or nailer should not be assigned to Emerson Tool Company merely because an Emerson entity is responsible for a RIDGID vacuum. Conversely, the TTI power-tool answer should not be extended to RIDGID plumbing tools or vacuums.

The same principle applies inside the TTI group. See the World Clean Biz guides to [TTI's brand portfolio](/blog/who-owns-tti-milwaukee-ryobi-hoover-vax-oreck) and [RYOBI's regional ownership structure](/blog/who-owns-ryobi-tti-kyocera) for separate brand answers.

## Where Are RIDGID Power Tools Made?

There is no reliable single-country answer for the full range. The 2025 CPSC recall says two RIDGID 18V framing nailer models were manufactured in China, but that is evidence for those models and serial ranges only.

For any current product, verify:

1. the complete model number and revision;
2. the country-of-origin statement on the tool, packaging or required import record;
3. the named manufacturer or importer in the manual and regulatory file;
4. the market-specific warranty and service operator;
5. the production date or serial range when a recall or product change is involved.

Do not infer origin from color, battery voltage, retailer, model-family appearance or another tool's label.

## Does TTI Ownership Mean RIDGID Batteries Fit Milwaukee or RYOBI?

No. TTI's involvement in multiple tool businesses does not establish electrical, mechanical or software compatibility across brands.

RIDGID's current power-tool page describes a RIDGID 18V system and says its RIDGID 18V batteries are compatible across generations of RIDGID 18V tools. That statement stays inside the RIDGID platform. It does not authorize the use of a RIDGID battery on Milwaukee M18, RYOBI 18V ONE+ or another brand through an adapter.

For purchasing or fleet management, verify the exact tool, battery and charger model numbers, approved combinations, market voltage and warranty terms.

## Who Handles the Warranty and Lifetime Service Agreement?

RIDGID's current terms distinguish several programs. Eligible handheld power tools and selected other categories receive a three-year limited warranty, and qualifying original purchasers can register for the Lifetime Service Agreement under its conditions.

The agreement is not the same as an unconditional lifetime warranty on every RIDGID product. Eligibility, retailer, registration deadline, proof of purchase, serial information, transfer restrictions and covered product category all matter. Reconditioned products and separately sold accessories can follow different rules.

Before buying a used, imported or marketplace tool, confirm whether its serial number, original receipt and market are accepted by the service program. A familiar RIDGID logo is not enough.

## Buyer and Supplier Checklist

For a buyer, distributor, service operator or component supplier, record:

- exact tool, battery and charger model numbers;
- brand owner and product operator as separate fields;
- named manufacturer, importer and warranty entity from current documents;
- factory and origin evidence for the ordered SKU;
- authorized sales channel and proof-of-purchase requirements;
- applicable recalls and serial-number exclusions;
- spare-parts route, service location and repair turnaround;
- approved platform compatibility without cross-brand assumptions.

## FAQ

### Is RIDGID owned by Home Depot?

No. RIDGID sits inside Emerson through Ridge Tool Company. Home Depot is the principal U.S. retail channel for the licensed power-tool line and is important to service-program eligibility.

### Does TTI own RIDGID?

TTI does not own the RIDGID trademark. The public structure is a licensed power-tool operation: Emerson's RIDGID entities own the brand system, while TTI's power-tool organization operates the relevant product category.

### Does OWT Industries make RIDGID tools?

TTI publicly identified OWT Industries as the designer and manufacturer of RIDGID professional power tools. For a current model, confirm the named legal manufacturer and factory from the label, manual and regulatory file because public group-level descriptions do not establish every SKU.

### Are RIDGID power tools made in the USA?

Some RIDGID categories and models have different production histories, but there is no supported all-range U.S. answer. The 2025 framing-nailer recall named China for two specific models. Check the exact product label and documentation.

### Are RIDGID tools and Milwaukee tools made by the same company?

Both power-tool operations are connected to TTI, but they remain separate brands, platforms and warranty systems. Shared group involvement does not make the products identical or batteries interchangeable.

## Final Answer

**Emerson owns the RIDGID brand through Ridge Tool Company. RIDGID-branded handheld and selected stationary power tools are operated through the TTI system under license, with OWT Industries and TTI Consumer Power Tools appearing in official manufacturing, import, recall and service evidence. Home Depot is the major U.S. retailer. The precise manufacturer, factory and country of origin still need to be checked for the individual model.**
