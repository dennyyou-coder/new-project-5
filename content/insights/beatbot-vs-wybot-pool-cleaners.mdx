**Beatbot and WYBOT are two of the fastest-growing cordless robotic pool cleaner brands, and both come from China.** Beatbot, founded in 2022 by Siler Wang, sells premium robots. WYBOT is the brand of WYBOTICS, a Tianjin company with a long history as a pool-robot manufacturer. Both flagships now come with docks that empty debris for you.

## Quick Comparison

| | Beatbot | WYBOT |
|---|---|---|
| Maker | Xingmai Innovation Technology, Suzhou | WYBOTICS, Tianjin |
| Founded | 2022 | Company from 2005; WYBOT brand from 2021 |
| Flagship | [AquaSense X](/products/beatbot-aquasense-x): AstroRinse cleaning station | [WYBOT S3](/products/wybot-s3): Omni Dock, 10 L shore bin, solar + DC |
| Lower-cost range | [Sora 30](/products/beatbot-sora-30) and [Sora 70](/products/beatbot-sora-70) | C- and B-series cordless robots |
| Positioning | Premium | Wide range, also ODM for other brands |

## Which flagship needs less maintenance?

Both aim to stop you rinsing filters by hand. Beatbot's AquaSense X returns to its AstroRinse station to clean itself; WYBOT's S3 docks to recharge and transfers debris to a 10 L shore bin. Compare the station size, where it can stand and how often it needs emptying.

## Which is better value?

Beatbot's Sora models give its floor-wall-waterline cleaning and floating retrieval at lower prices. WYBOT's C-series is aimed at budget buyers. Compare runtime, basket size and warranty for the exact model.

## Who stands behind them?

Beatbot's full shareholder list is not public; WYBOTICS is controlled by Fu Guilan, Yu Qian and their entities, with 71.58% of voting control in April 2026. Both sell mainly online and through retailers; check local warranty and parts.
