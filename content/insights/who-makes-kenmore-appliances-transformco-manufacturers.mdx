**Kenmore appliances are not made by one universal Kenmore factory. Transform SR Brands LLC owns the Kenmore trademark, while different licensed or contracted manufacturing programs produce refrigerators, laundry appliances, cooking products, dishwashers and other categories.** The company behind a specific appliance can therefore change by category, model family, market and production period.

This is the central fact that many simple “who makes Kenmore?” lists miss. A historical supplier relationship may help identify an older machine, but it does not prove who built a current model. The dependable answer starts with the complete model number and the documents for that exact appliance.

## What Company Owns the Kenmore Brand?

The current brand owner is **Transform SR Brands LLC**. Kenmore's official site identifies Kenmore as a registered trademark of that company, while the site's current terms say the website is operated by Transform SR Holding Management LLC for Transformco and its affiliates.

Transformco is therefore the correct place to start for brand ownership and licensing. It is not accurate to describe Whirlpool, LG, Electrolux, Samsung or any other appliance producer as the owner of Kenmore merely because one company may have supplied particular Kenmore models at some point.

Kenmore's own [licensing contact page](https://www.kenmore.com/contact-us) invites businesses to inquire about licensing the Kenmore brand by product category and distribution. That structure helps explain why a consumer can see the same brand across major appliances, floor care, water treatment, cookware and smaller household products without one factory making the entire range.

## Why Is There No Single Kenmore Manufacturer?

Kenmore functions as a broad appliance brand and product program. Its [current product catalogue](https://www.kenmore.com/) spans ranges, dishwashers, laundry, refrigerators, cookware, small kitchen appliances, air purification, floor care, room air, heating and cooling, water softeners and other household categories.

Those products require very different factories, tooling, certifications and service systems. A refrigerator plant is not a laundry plant, and a floor-care license is not evidence for a dishwasher. Kenmore's own history page says the team takes the best that other manufacturers offer—an unusually direct description of the brand's supplier-led model.

The practical chain usually contains several separate layers:

1. Transformco controls the Kenmore trademark and brand program.
2. A licensed or contracted manufacturer develops or produces a category or model family.
3. A named legal entity imports or places the appliance on a market.
4. A retailer sells the unit under its own commercial terms.
5. A warrantor and service network handle eligible claims and parts.

These roles can belong to related companies, but they should never be assumed to be identical.

## Which Companies Have Made Kenmore Appliances?

Kenmore has used different appliance-industry suppliers over its long history. Online model-prefix charts commonly associate older Kenmore families with well-known manufacturers, and official parts records sometimes show components shared across Kenmore and another manufacturer's platforms.

That information is useful for repair research, but it has limits. A shared part can show a common platform or compatible component without proving the current corporate owner, final-assembly site or warranty provider. A prefix may also describe an older source-code system that should not be applied blindly to a new product.

For example, Kenmore's own parts catalogue identifies a Whirlpool OEM refrigerator drawer that fits some Kenmore and Kenmore Elite 106-series units as well as multiple Whirlpool-family series. That is credible service evidence for those named applications. It is not proof that every Kenmore refrigerator was made by Whirlpool.

The safest wording is therefore: **Kenmore appliances have been supplied by multiple manufacturers, and the maker must be established at model level.**

## How Can You Identify Who Made a Specific Kenmore Appliance?

Start with the complete model number, serial number and rating plate. Kenmore's [official manual search](https://www.kenmore.com/manuals) requires the model number and covers washers, dryers, dishwashers, refrigerators, freezers, ice makers, ovens, ranges, stovetops, microwaves and other appliances.

Then compare these records:

| Record | What it can establish |
|---|---|
| Rating plate | Exact model, serial details, electrical data and sometimes a responsible company or origin |
| Official owner's manual | Product family, safety notices, warranty language and named responsible entities |
| EnergyGuide record | Exact regulated model and efficiency disclosure for applicable U.S. appliances |
| Parts catalogue | Platform relationships and manufacturer-specific component references |
| Recall or safety notice | Manufacturer, importer or responsible-company information for affected models |
| Declaration or certification | Legal manufacturer or market operator for the exact product |
| Purchase invoice | Seller, date and product identity needed for warranty eligibility |

![Kenmore appliance model, document, seller and warranty verification chain](/images/blog/kenmore-model-verification-map.webp)

Do not shorten the model number or rely only on a retail nickname. A suffix can distinguish voltage, finish, revision, market or factory assignment. For used appliances, photograph the full label before ordering parts or making an origin claim.

## Who Makes Kenmore Refrigerators, Washers and Dishwashers Today?

There is no responsible way to give one company name for every current Kenmore refrigerator, washer or dishwasher without examining the model records. Kenmore's current site shows that these categories remain active, but its general brand pages do not publish a single universal manufacturer for each entire category.

Current retail supply can also change faster than old repair charts. A supplier may build one platform, leave a category, return with a new program or produce only selected models. The same brand and category can contain appliances designed in different periods and sourced under different contracts.

For purchasing or distribution, request the exact manufacturer name, factory address, country of origin, compliance certificates and warranty entity in writing. “Kenmore authorized” describes the brand relationship; it does not replace factory evidence.

## Are Kenmore Vacuum Cleaners Different?

Yes. Current Kenmore floor care has a clearer category-specific arrangement. Cleva North America describes itself as the sole licensed manufacturer of Kenmore and Kenmore Elite vacuum cleaners and accessories in the United States and Canada.

That is why World Clean Biz maintains a separate guide to [who makes Kenmore vacuum cleaners](/blog/who-makes-kenmore-vacuum-cleaners-cleva-transformco). The vacuum page answers a defined floor-care license question. This page addresses Kenmore's much broader major-appliance and household-product system.

The two conclusions should not be mixed:

- Cleva's current license supports a floor-care answer.
- It does not establish who makes a Kenmore refrigerator, washer, range or dishwasher.

## How Does Kenmore Warranty Responsibility Work?

Kenmore's [current U.S. warranty page](https://www.kenmore.com/warranty-information/) names Transform SR Brands Management LLC as the warrantor for the listed warranties and says proof of original sale by an authorized Kenmore retailer is required for appliance warranty coverage.

The page separates warranty statements by product type. That matters because coverage, duration, labor, parts, commercial-use exclusions and return eligibility can differ between refrigerators, dryers, water heaters, small appliances and floor care.

For a real claim, match four items: the exact model, the purchase date, the authorized seller and the warranty revision applicable to that product. A manufacturer identified through a parts cross-reference is not automatically the company that must pay the consumer's warranty claim.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who makes Kenmore appliances?

Multiple licensed or contracted manufacturers make Kenmore appliances. The company behind one refrigerator, washer, range or dishwasher must be confirmed from that model's label and documents.

### Who owns Kenmore now?

Transform SR Brands LLC owns the registered Kenmore trademark within the Transformco brand structure.

### Does Whirlpool make Kenmore appliances?

Whirlpool-related platforms and parts appear in some Kenmore product histories and service records. That does not mean Whirlpool makes every Kenmore appliance or every current model.

### Does LG make Kenmore appliances?

A [2005 U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recall](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2005/cpsc-lg-electronics-and-sears-roebuck-and-co-announce-recall-to-repair-refrigerators) named LG Electronics as the manufacturer of the affected Kenmore Elite Trio three-door refrigerator models. That is evidence for those recalled historical models, not every Kenmore refrigerator and not the current supply program. Verify the complete model and its official records.

### Can a Kenmore model prefix identify the manufacturer?

It can be a useful research clue, especially for older appliances, but it is not a universal current rule. Confirm the result through the manual, rating plate, parts records and compliance documents.

### Is Kenmore still making appliances?

Kenmore remains an active Transformco-owned brand with current product categories and retail availability. The physical manufacturing is carried out through category- and model-specific supply arrangements.

### Who makes Kenmore vacuum cleaners?

Cleva says it is the sole licensed manufacturer of current Kenmore and Kenmore Elite floor-care products in the United States and Canada. That arrangement does not cover all Kenmore appliances.

## Final Answer

Kenmore appliances are made through a multi-manufacturer licensing and sourcing system. Transform SR Brands LLC owns the trademark, while the factory, importer, seller and service provider can vary by appliance category, model and production period.

The most reliable answer is therefore not an internet list of manufacturer names. Use the complete model number, rating plate, official manual, EnergyGuide or compliance file, parts records, invoice and warranty statement. Those records turn a broad brand question into a verifiable product answer.
