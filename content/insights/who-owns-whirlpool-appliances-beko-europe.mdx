**Whirlpool appliances are not owned by Beko worldwide. Whirlpool Corporation remains the owner of the Whirlpool brand and operates its principal appliance businesses in the Americas and other retained markets. In specified European jurisdictions, Beko Europe sells Whirlpool-branded major appliances under a long-term trademark licence.**

The distinction became more important in June 2026. Beko B.V., a wholly owned subsidiary of Arçelik A.Ş., completed the purchase of Whirlpool EMEA Holdings LLC's remaining 25% interest in Beko Europe B.V. Beko therefore owns 100% of Beko Europe, but the regulatory disclosure says the Whirlpool trademark licence remains in force.

Ownership of the European operating company, ownership of the Whirlpool trademark and responsibility for a particular appliance are three different questions.

**Who makes Whirlpool appliances?** In the Americas, Whirlpool Corporation operates the principal industrial network: its [2025 annual report](https://ar.whirlpoolcorp.com/operations-people-and-community.php) says about 80% of the major appliances it sells in the United States come from its U.S. factories. In the licensed European scope, [Beko Europe](https://www.bekoeurope.com/en/about-us/) operates 11 production sites in Italy, Poland, Romania and Slovakia and manufactures appliances across a portfolio that includes the licensed Whirlpool brand. A specific Whirlpool model can still involve a different Whirlpool plant, Beko Europe plant or licensed-category supplier, so the rating plate, legal manufacturer, origin marking and warranty documents remain decisive.

## What Is the Difference Between Whirlpool and Whirlpool Corporation?

**Whirlpool** is the consumer appliance trademark. **Whirlpool Corporation** is the U.S.-listed home-appliance company whose current portfolio includes Whirlpool, KitchenAid, JennAir, Maytag, Amana, Brastemp, Consul and InSinkErator.

Maytag has its own brand history and manufacturing search intent. The separate guide to [who owns Maytag](/blog/who-owns-maytag-whirlpool-manufacturing) keeps that child-brand question distinct from Whirlpool's parent-company and European licensing structure.

The distinction matters in contracts and sourcing records. A product carton may show the Whirlpool brand while the warranty names Whirlpool Corporation, Whirlpool Canada LP, a Latin American subsidiary, Beko Europe or another local company. The familiar wordmark identifies the brand; it does not by itself identify the seller, factory, importer or warrantor.

Whirlpool Corporation's 2025 annual report describes three principal businesses: North America Major Appliances, Latin America Major Appliances and Global Small Domestic Appliances. The report also says close to 90% of 2025 sales came from the Americas. That is a corporate reporting structure, not a universal map for every regional Whirlpool-branded appliance.

## What Happened to Whirlpool's European Appliance Business?

Whirlpool Corporation and Arçelik announced a transaction to combine their European appliance businesses. At the April 2024 closing, Arçelik owned 75% of the new Beko Europe B.V. and Whirlpool Corporation owned 25%.

The contributed business included Whirlpool Corporation's European major domestic appliance operation and Arçelik's European major domestic appliances, small domestic appliances, consumer electronics and air-conditioning operations. Beko Europe became the regional company behind a portfolio that includes Beko, Whirlpool, Hotpoint, Indesit and Bauknecht, among other brands.

The transaction did not transfer everything Whirlpool Corporation did in Europe. Whirlpool's official closing announcement says the company retained InSinkErator and its EMEA KitchenAid small- and major-domestic-appliance business. That sentence is the key boundary between the Whirlpool brand's licensed European role and KitchenAid's retained Whirlpool ownership.

## Does Whirlpool Corporation Still Own Part of Beko Europe?

No, based on the June 2026 completion record.

On 19 June 2026, Arçelik disclosed that Beko B.V. had signed an agreement to buy Whirlpool EMEA Holdings LLC's 25% interest for EUR 71.45 million. The disclosure said the acquisition would take Beko B.V.'s ownership to 100%, terminate the shareholder relationship and leave the trademark licence agreement in effect.

The Turkish Public Disclosure Platform then listed completion on 23 June 2026. Therefore, pages that still call Beko Europe a 75%/25% joint venture are describing the period between April 2024 and June 2026, not the current ownership position.

This change did not turn the Whirlpool trademark into a Beko-owned global brand. Equity ownership and trademark licensing changed on separate legal tracks.

## Who Controls Whirlpool Appliances in Europe Now?

For the Whirlpool-branded major-appliance business covered by the licence, Beko Europe is the relevant European operator. Its official site describes Whirlpool as one of the brands it uses and notes that the licence is limited to certain jurisdictions.

That wording prevents two common mistakes:

- saying Beko owns Whirlpool everywhere; and
- saying Whirlpool Corporation still owns Beko Europe because it created the company with Arçelik.

The correct commercial answer depends on territory. A Whirlpool refrigerator sold in a Beko Europe jurisdiction can involve the Whirlpool trademark, Beko Europe as regional brand operator, a Beko Europe subsidiary as seller and a named plant as manufacturer. A Whirlpool washer sold in the United States normally sits within Whirlpool Corporation's North American business and a different warranty system.

## Which Whirlpool Businesses Remain With Whirlpool Corporation?

Whirlpool Corporation continues to report the Whirlpool brand in its core portfolio. Its retained businesses include North American and Latin American major appliances and global small domestic appliances. The company also retained KitchenAid in EMEA and continues to operate the KitchenAid small-appliance business globally.

Whirlpool Corporation can also license selected product categories. Its official licensing page lists categories such as air purifiers, compact refrigerators, dehumidifiers, portable and window air conditioners, water heaters, water softeners and water filtration. A licensed Whirlpool product may therefore have a different manufacturer and warranty chain from a Whirlpool Corporation major appliance.

The presence of the same Whirlpool logo does not erase these category and territory differences.

## Where Are Whirlpool Appliances Made?

There is no single country answer.

Whirlpool Corporation's 2025 annual report says 80% of the major appliances it sells in the United States come from its U.S. factories. The company identifies manufacturing operations in several U.S. and Mexican locations, while its Latin American business also has regional manufacturing.

Beko Europe separately reports production sites in Italy, Poland, Romania and Slovakia. Those sites support its European portfolio, including owned and licensed brands, but the site list does not prove which factory made a specific Whirlpool model.

A supplier or buyer should request:

1. the exact model and destination market;
2. the rating plate and carton country-of-origin marking;
3. the legal manufacturer and factory address where required;
4. the declaration of conformity and responsible signatory;
5. the importer or authorized representative; and
6. the warranty provider and service network.

Only those documents connect a corporate network to one appliance.

## Does Beko Manufacture Whirlpool Appliances?

Beko Europe manufactures and markets home appliances in Europe and uses the Whirlpool brand under licence in specified jurisdictions. It is therefore reasonable to say Beko Europe's industrial network can make Whirlpool-branded European products within the disclosed scope.

It is not reasonable to say every Whirlpool appliance is made by Beko. Whirlpool Corporation continues to manufacture and sell appliances in the Americas, and licensed categories can involve additional manufacturers. Even inside Europe, a group plant list does not assign one model to one factory.

The safe formulation is entity- and model-specific: identify the regional operator, then verify the exact manufacturing site from product evidence.

## How Should Distributors Treat the Brand Licence?

A licence determines who may use the trademark for specified products and territories. It does not automatically grant every distributor the right to sell in every country.

Before taking on Whirlpool-branded inventory, a distributor should verify:

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Territory in the supply agreement | Confirms whether the seller may supply the destination market |
| Legal supplier name | Distinguishes Whirlpool Corporation companies, Beko Europe companies and licensees |
| Model-specific origin | Prevents group-level manufacturing claims |
| Trademark notice | Shows whether use is owned or licensed in that market |
| Local conformity documents | Identifies the responsible entity and applicable standards |
| Warranty and spare-parts commitment | Determines who funds service and for how long |
| Digital services and app region | Connected features may be market-restricted |

This is especially important for parallel imports. A genuine appliance purchased in one region can still lack an authorized warranty, correct voltage, language support, radio approval or replacement parts in another.

## How Is KitchenAid Related to the Whirlpool–Beko Europe Structure?

KitchenAid is a Whirlpool Corporation brand, but it was excluded from the transferred EMEA appliance business. Whirlpool Corporation's official 2024 closing announcement expressly says it retained the EMEA KitchenAid small- and major-domestic-appliance business.

That makes KitchenAid an important control example. Beko Europe's licence to use the Whirlpool brand does not establish ownership of KitchenAid. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Whirlpool owned by Beko?

No. Whirlpool Corporation remains the owner of the Whirlpool brand. Beko Europe uses the brand under a licence limited to specified jurisdictions.

### Does Whirlpool Corporation own Beko Europe?

No. Whirlpool Corporation's 25% stake was acquired by Beko B.V. in June 2026. Beko B.V. now owns 100% of Beko Europe.

### Why does Beko Europe sell Whirlpool appliances?

The original transaction included a long-term regional trademark licence. The June 2026 ownership disclosure says that licence remains in force.

### Is KitchenAid part of Beko Europe?

No. Whirlpool Corporation said it retained its EMEA KitchenAid small- and major-domestic-appliance business.

### Are all Whirlpool appliances made in the United States?

No. Whirlpool Corporation has a large U.S. footprint, but manufacturing varies by market and model. European licensed products and other regional or licensed categories use different networks.

### How can I identify who made my Whirlpool appliance?

Check the model and serial label, country-of-origin marking, legal manufacturer, importer, declaration of conformity and warranty terms for the exact product.

## Final Answer

Whirlpool Corporation owns the Whirlpool brand. Beko Europe is the licensed operator for Whirlpool-branded major appliances in specified European jurisdictions, and Beko acquired Whirlpool Corporation's remaining 25% equity interest in Beko Europe in June 2026. The trademark licence survived the equity change.

KitchenAid remained with Whirlpool Corporation, and Whirlpool's operations outside the licensed European scope remain separate. For sourcing, distribution or warranty decisions, identify the destination country, product category, model, legal seller, manufacturer, importer and warrantor instead of turning a regional licence into a global ownership claim.
