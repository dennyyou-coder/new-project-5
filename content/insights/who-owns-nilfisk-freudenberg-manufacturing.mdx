**Nilfisk is owned by Freudenberg following completion of Freudenberg Home and Cleaning Solutions' takeover in April 2026.** Nilfisk entered a new phase as part of the privately held German industrial group after the offer reached approximately 94% acceptance and the buyer initiated the remaining acquisition and delisting steps.

**Who manufactures Nilfisk equipment?** Nilfisk is an operating manufacturer and currently identifies five main production sites in the United States, Mexico, Hungary, Italy and China. That footprint does not assign every scrubber, sweeper, vacuum, pressure washer or autonomous machine to one plant or supplier, so the exact legal manufacturer, factory, components and technology partners must be verified from the model plate, conformity documents and current product file.

Nilfisk remains a distinct professional cleaning-equipment company and brand. It was not renamed Vileda, and Freudenberg ownership does not make every product, dealer, battery or warranty interchangeable across the group's brands.

## When Did Freudenberg Acquire Nilfisk?

Freudenberg Home and Cleaning Solutions launched its takeover offer for Nilfisk in 2025. Nilfisk announced completion in April 2026, stating that Freudenberg had received acceptances corresponding to approximately 94% of the shares and voting rights.

The high acceptance level enabled the buyer to move toward compulsory acquisition of the remaining shares and delisting. As a result, descriptions of Nilfisk as an independently listed public company are now historical rather than current.

Freudenberg is a family-owned German technology group with multiple business areas. Freudenberg Home and Cleaning Solutions is known for consumer cleaning brands, but its ownership of Nilfisk adds a large professional and industrial equipment platform.

For a transaction-focused analysis, see [Freudenberg Acquires Nilfisk: Strategic Implications for the Global Cleaning Industry](/blog/freudenberg-acquires-nilfisk).

## Is Nilfisk Owned by Vileda?

No. Vileda and Nilfisk are separate brands under the broader Freudenberg structure. Vileda is associated mainly with consumer cleaning products, while Nilfisk focuses overwhelmingly on professional customers and powered cleaning equipment.

The distinction matters commercially. A shared parent does not automatically combine dealer appointments, product certifications, spare parts, software, warranties or territory rights. Buyers should contract with the entity named for the specific brand and country.

## What Brands and Products Does Nilfisk Operate?

Nilfisk's official portfolio spans floorcare, vacuum cleaning, high-pressure cleaning and related professional solutions. Major categories include:

- walk-behind and ride-on scrubber dryers;
- sweepers and combination machines;
- autonomous floor-cleaning equipment;
- wet-and-dry, commercial and industrial vacuum cleaners;
- high-pressure washers and stationary cleaning systems; and
- accessories, consumables, digital services and fleet support.

The group also uses the **Advance** brand, especially in North America, and **Viper** for value-oriented professional equipment in many markets. These names share ownership but can have different product ranges and distribution systems.

Nilfisk says more than 90% of its customers are professional users and that it sells in more than 100 countries. Those are company-reported scale indicators, not proof that every market stocks the full range.

## Where Are Nilfisk Products Made?

Nilfisk identifies five manufacturing sites in the United States, Mexico, Hungary, Italy and China. Its history also records the development of its Dongguan operation following the acquisition of Viper.

This footprint supports describing Nilfisk as an operating manufacturer rather than only a brand licensor. It still does not justify assigning every machine to one country. Product families can be allocated to different plants, and components such as batteries, motors, chargers, sensors or electronics may come from external suppliers.

For the exact answer, use the machine's model and serial plate, declaration of conformity, manual, parts catalogue and local warranty documents.

## Does Nilfisk Make Autonomous Cleaning Robots?

Nilfisk offers autonomous floor-cleaning equipment, including the SC25 autonomous scrubber. The product page identifies LionsBot as a technology partner for that model.

This is a useful example of why “who makes it?” can require more than one name. Nilfisk can own the product and customer relationship, a Nilfisk factory or supplier can provide hardware, and a technology partner can contribute autonomy software or systems.

For buyers, the practical questions are:

- Who owns and updates the autonomy software?
- Where is operational data stored and processed?
- Which party provides mapping, commissioning and remote support?
- Who supplies batteries, sensors and safety components?
- What happens if the technology partnership changes?

These responsibilities should be written into fleet and service agreements rather than inferred from the logo.

## How Do Nilfisk Dealers, Parts and Warranties Work?

Nilfisk sells through its own regional organizations and networks of distributors, dealers and service partners. The correct route varies by product, customer type and country.

Professional cleaning equipment often requires commissioning, operator training, preventive maintenance and rapid parts support. A distributor's capability can therefore be as important as the machine specification.

Before purchase, confirm:

1. whether the seller is authorized for the exact product line;
2. the warranty issuer and response-time commitment;
3. technician coverage and parts stocking;
4. battery, charger and consumable availability;
5. software subscriptions and connectivity requirements; and
6. responsibility for autonomous-system support.

A retailer's return policy, an independent dealer's service contract and Nilfisk's manufacturer warranty are separate obligations.

## Are Nilfisk, Advance and Viper Products Interchangeable?

Do not assume so. Shared ownership can enable product-platform cooperation, but model numbers, accessories, batteries, firmware, certification and warranties can remain brand- and market-specific.

Use official parts catalogues and written cross-references before substituting components. A visually similar Advance or Viper machine is not automatically the same Nilfisk model.

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

1. Confirm that current ownership information reflects the completed 2026 Freudenberg takeover.
2. Identify the exact legal seller, distributor and service entity in the destination market.
3. Match the brand—Nilfisk, Advance or Viper—to the correct product and channel.
4. Verify the model's manufacturing origin and certification holder.
5. Record batteries, chargers, motors, sensors and autonomy partners separately.
6. Define uptime, replacement-machine and parts obligations for professional fleets.
7. Check software, data and cybersecurity responsibilities for connected equipment.
8. Do not treat a shared Freudenberg parent as proof of cross-brand compatibility.
