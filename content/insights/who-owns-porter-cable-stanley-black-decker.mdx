**Porter-Cable is owned by Stanley Black & Decker.** The group lists PORTER-CABLE among its current tool brands, and Porter-Cable's own legal and support pages operate within Stanley Black & Decker's brand infrastructure.

The ownership answer does not mean every Porter-Cable tool is made by one factory, made in the United States or technically interchangeable with DEWALT, CRAFTSMAN or another Stanley Black & Decker brand. Manufacturing, retail distribution, battery compatibility and warranty must be checked at model and market level.

## How Did Stanley Black & Decker Acquire Porter-Cable?

Porter-Cable entered today's Stanley Black & Decker portfolio through a chain of corporate transactions.

The brand was part of Pentair's Tools Group when Black & Decker agreed to acquire that business in 2004. The transaction brought Porter-Cable, Delta and other tool operations into Black & Decker. Stanley Works and Black & Decker then combined in 2010 to create Stanley Black & Decker.

The current corporate evidence is simpler than the history: Stanley Black & Decker lists Porter-Cable among its tool brands, and Porter-Cable's current website terms say the site is owned or controlled by Stanley Black & Decker or its affiliates.

Older tools and manuals can name Porter-Cable Corporation, Pentair, Black & Decker or another historical entity. Use the product date when interpreting those documents rather than applying the current parent retroactively.

## Who Founded Porter-Cable?

Porter-Cable began in 1906 as a machine and tool shop founded by R. E. Porter, G. G. Porter and F. E. Cable in Syracuse, New York.

The company moved from jobbing work into manufactured products and later became known for portable professional tools. Its official history highlights the introduction of a portable belt sander in 1926, a compact high-speed circular saw in 1928, a portable band saw in 1953 and influential routers and sanders in later decades.

That heritage explains why Porter-Cable remains associated with woodworking, routing, sanding and jobsite tools. It does not establish the production location of a current cordless kit.

## Is Porter-Cable Still in Business?

Yes. Porter-Cable maintains a current product website, 20V MAX platform page, warranty information, customer support and authorized repair routes.

The brand's range and retail visibility have changed over time, which can create the impression that it was discontinued. Stanley Black & Decker announced in 2021 that Tractor Supply would become the exclusive retail destination for new Porter-Cable cordless power tools and accessories in the United States.

Current availability should still be checked by model. A brand can remain active while particular tools, batteries or retailer programs change. The existence of warranty support for legacy products is not evidence that every old model remains in production.

## Who Manufactures Porter-Cable Tools?

Porter-Cable sits inside Stanley Black & Decker's global Tools & Outdoor supply and manufacturing network. The group reports more than 100 manufacturing facilities across its tool business, including facilities in the United States and other countries.

That corporate footprint does not assign a factory to every Porter-Cable drill, router, nailer, compressor, saw or battery. Product categories and generations can use different group plants, contract suppliers and component sources.

For an exact tool, verify:

- model and type number;
- serial number and manufacturing date code;
- country-of-origin label;
- manufacturer or importer on the manual and declaration;
- retailer and purchase date;
- safety notices and recalls; and
- warranty statement supplied with the product.

The Porter-Cable support form asks for model, type and date-code information because those fields matter to service eligibility.

## Where Are Porter-Cable Tools Made?

There is no reliable one-country answer for the complete Porter-Cable range.

Porter-Cable is an American-origin brand owned by a U.S.-headquartered company, but brand origin and corporate headquarters do not prove product origin. Stanley Black & Decker operates a global production system, and individual models can be made or assembled in different countries.

Marketing claims such as “Made in USA with global materials,” when present, must be tied to the exact model and wording. Do not apply a claim from one tool, plant or brand to the full Porter-Cable catalogue.

For commercial sourcing, request current factory, bill-of-materials, origin and shipment evidence. For retail purchases, use the product nameplate and packaging.

## What Is Porter-Cable's Current Brand Position?

Porter-Cable occupies a practical professional and prosumer position below the most heavily marketed premium contractor platforms in Stanley Black & Decker's portfolio.

The range retains strength in woodworking, compressors, pneumatic fastening, routing, sanding and general cordless tools. The Tractor Supply relationship aligns the brand with rural, property-maintenance and trade customers looking for functional tools and accessible pricing.

This positioning is an editorial market assessment, not a legal product grade. Buyers should compare the exact tool's specifications, duty cycle, service network and accessories rather than assume performance from a group hierarchy.

Porter-Cable's position also should not be described as a private label. It is a Stanley Black & Decker-owned manufacturer brand sold through retailers.

## How the Porter-Cable 20V MAX Platform Works

Porter-Cable's cordless system is marketed as 20V MAX. The platform covers drills, impact tools, saws, sanders, nailers, lights and other products.

“20V MAX” is a nominal marketing convention, not a universal cross-brand interface. Stanley Black & Decker owns several brands that use similar voltage language, but battery rails, electronics, chargers and approvals differ.

Do not assume a Porter-Cable battery fits:

- DEWALT 20V MAX;
- CRAFTSMAN V20;
- BLACK+DECKER 20V MAX;
- BOSTITCH cordless systems; or
- another battery family without explicit manufacturer documentation.

Third-party adapters can bypass intended electrical and mechanical protections and may affect safety or warranty. Use the exact approved battery and charger.

## Is Tractor Supply the Exclusive Porter-Cable Retailer?

Stanley Black & Decker announced in September 2021 that Tractor Supply would become the exclusive retail destination for Porter-Cable cordless power tools and accessories, with new products launching through the retailer.

That statement should be reported with its U.S. market, product-category and date context. It does not necessarily cover corded tools, compressors, replacement parts, service centers, international markets or future channel changes.

Before saying the brand is “only sold at Tractor Supply,” check the current product page and authorized sellers. Online marketplaces can also contain legacy stock and unauthorized sellers.

Retail exclusivity does not give Tractor Supply ownership of the trademark or responsibility for all manufacturing.

## Porter-Cable in Dust Collection and Cleaning

Porter-Cable's cleaning adjacency comes mainly from woodworking, sanding, routing, sawing and jobsite cleanup.

The brand has sold shop vacuums, dust-collection accessories and tools with dust ports in different product generations. Compressors and pneumatic equipment also serve workshop environments where debris management and maintenance matter.

For World Clean Biz buyers, the important controls are:

1. dust-port and hose diameter;
2. extractor airflow and static pressure;
3. bag or filter availability;
4. automatic-start compatibility;
5. dust hazard and local safety rules; and
6. current parts support for legacy tools.

A woodworking dust bag is not a substitute for a suitable extractor when the material or regulation requires stronger control.

## How Does the Porter-Cable Warranty Work?

Porter-Cable warranty duration depends on the statement attached to the product.

The current warranty page describes separate one-year and three-year limited warranties for products that state those periods. It also describes a one-year free-service offer and a 90-day money-back guarantee for eligible products under stated conditions.

Coverage can depend on proof of purchase, authorized-seller status, product category, misuse, wear and repair history. The page explicitly says Latin American products follow country-specific terms.

Keep the receipt, model, type number, serial number and date code. For older tools, confirm whether parts and service remain available before assuming a legacy warranty can be fulfilled through replacement.

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

Before relying on a Porter-Cable ownership or manufacturing claim, record:

1. **Current owner:** Stanley Black & Decker.
2. **Historical period:** Pre-Pentair, Pentair, Black & Decker or Stanley Black & Decker era.
3. **Product identity:** Model, type, serial and date code.
4. **Origin:** Label, declaration, manufacturer and importer.
5. **Battery platform:** Porter-Cable 20V MAX and approved charger.
6. **Retail channel:** Tractor Supply or another currently authorized seller.
7. **Warranty:** Exact one-year, three-year or other statement.
8. **Service:** Authorized center, parts and repair eligibility.
9. **Dust-control interface:** Port, hose, filter and extractor requirements.
10. **Legacy support:** Batteries, consumables and parts for discontinued models.

Stanley Black & Decker owns Porter-Cable and lists it among the group's tool brands.

## Final Answer

Stanley Black & Decker owns Porter-Cable. The brand remains active and retains a century of U.S. tool heritage, especially in woodworking, fastening, compressors and practical cordless products.

The parent company's global scale does not establish one manufacturing country for the brand. Verify the exact model's origin, 20V MAX battery compatibility, retailer and warranty statement. Keep Porter-Cable technically separate from DEWALT, CRAFTSMAN and other sibling brands despite shared ownership.
