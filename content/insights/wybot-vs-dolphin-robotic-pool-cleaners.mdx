**Choose WYBOT when cordless convenience, aggressive feature value and direct online shopping are the priorities. Choose Dolphin when proven corded platforms, authorized-dealer advice, certified repairs and long-term parts access matter more than eliminating the cable.**

That is the useful brand-level answer, but it is not enough to select a product. Both brands sell multiple robotic pool cleaners with different coverage, filtration, runtimes and service terms. The correct comparison pairs models for the same pool and the same cleaning job.

![Cordless and corded robotic pool cleaner comparison covering pool fit, filtration, power, retrieval and service](/images/blog/wybot-vs-dolphin-robotic-pool-cleaners.webp)

## WYBOT vs Dolphin at a Glance

| Decision factor | WYBOT | Dolphin by Maytronics |
|---|---|---|
| Main buying proposition | Cordless choice, current features and online value | Mature product range, dealer knowledge, parts and repair infrastructure |
| Power architecture | Primarily battery-powered cordless products, plus other formats in the range | Large installed base of corded robots, with cordless products also available |
| Typical buying route | Official ecommerce and online retailers; regional distributors vary | Online plus a structured authorized-dealer and service network |
| Maintenance risk | Battery, charging contacts, charger and return freight | Floating cable, power supply, swivel and dealer repair cost |
| Parts proposition | Model-specific modules and warranty support; verify regional stocking | Genuine parts catalog and certified dealer/service route |
| Best fit | Buyers who value easy deployment and no floating cable | Buyers who value repairability, local support and established pool-specialist channels |

The brands are not fixed to one architecture. Confirm the exact SKU instead of assuming that every WYBOT is cordless or every Dolphin is corded.

## Match the Pool Before Matching the Models

Write down:

- pool type, length, area and depth;
- surface material;
- flat or sloped floor;
- wall transition;
- steps, ledges and benches;
- drain and obstacle layout;
- dominant debris;
- required floor, wall and waterline coverage;
- where power or charging will be located;
- who will lift and maintain the cleaner.

Then select models rated for that pool. A low-cost floor cleaner should not be compared with a premium full-pool model just because the logos are different.

## Cordless WYBOT vs Corded Dolphin

### What cordless changes

A cordless cleaner removes the floating power cable during operation. That can reduce tangles, simplify deck handling and make deployment easier where the power outlet is inconvenient.

It also introduces:

- a lithium battery with finite service life;
- a charging interval between cycles;
- charging-contact maintenance;
- safe off-season battery storage;
- battery shipping and warranty logistics;
- the possibility that a cycle ends away from the preferred retrieval point.

WYBOT’s current catalog is built heavily around cordless products. Its B1, for example, is presented as a compact full-pool robot with a 120-minute working cycle and app-selectable modes. Other WYBOT models target different pool sizes and feature levels.

### What corded changes

A corded Dolphin receives power through an external low-voltage supply and floating cable.

Benefits can include:

- no propulsion battery to recharge or replace;
- repeat cycles without waiting for charging;
- long-running product families;
- established repair knowledge for cables, power supplies and motor blocks.

Trade-offs include:

- cable management and storage;
- potential tangling;
- power-supply placement;
- cable, swivel or connector repair;
- more deck equipment during use.

Cordless is not automatically newer and better. Corded is not automatically inconvenient. The right power architecture depends on cycle frequency, pool size, handling and the local repair route.

## Pool Coverage

Product labels such as “full pool” or “wall climbing” need a test protocol.

### Floor

Check:

- coverage consistency;
- fine debris left behind;
- behavior around the main drain;
- pickup of leaves without intake blockage;
- transition across slopes;
- cycle completion before battery depletion.

### Walls

Wall climbing depends on brush contact, water flow, traction and pool surface. Smooth tile, vinyl and sharp transitions can change the result.

Ask for evidence on the same surface family, not a generic marketing video.

### Waterline

Verify whether the robot only touches the waterline or travels laterally while scrubbing. Compare the percentage of the perimeter cleaned and the time allocated to that mode.

### Steps and ledges

Most product specifications do not guarantee every step or tanning ledge. Measure the water depth, shelf width and entry angle. If those areas drive the purchase, require an in-pool demonstration.

## Navigation and App Control

WYBOT increasingly uses app modes, route customization, vision and mapping on selected products. Dolphin’s navigation and connectivity depend heavily on the series and model; some products offer connected scheduling and status functions, while simpler models focus on autonomous cleaning without extensive app controls.

Evaluate:

- coverage rather than the number of sensors;
- recovery from obstacles and drains;
- repeatability across cycles;
- underwater communication limitations;
- whether schedules are stored locally or depend on cloud access;
- firmware-support expectations;
- diagnostic information available to the dealer.

An app is valuable when it changes labor, coverage or troubleshooting. It is not valuable merely because a product page lists Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

## Filtration and Debris

Filter design determines cleaning performance and owner labor.

Compare:

- basket or cartridge volume;
- top-load access;
- leaf intake size;
- standard and ultra-fine media;
- stated micron level;
- clogging with pollen or silt;
- rinse time;
- wet filter weight;
- genuine replacement price;
- regional availability.

WYBOT products use different filter configurations across the range. The B1, for example, lists a 180-micron cartridge. Other models may offer dual-layer filtration.

Dolphin also varies by product. Maytronics sells genuine filter baskets, cartridges and accessories through its parts system. That availability can matter more than a theoretical filtration advantage if a replacement filter cannot be sourced during the season.

Test with the pool’s real debris. A basket that handles oak leaves may not retain fine dust; ultra-fine media that captures silt may load quickly during a leaf event.

## Retrieval and Routine Maintenance

The cleaning cycle is only part of ownership.

Measure:

- time to deploy the cleaner;
- time and effort to retrieve it;
- drained lifting weight;
- filter removal and rinsing;
- hair removal from brushes and axles;
- charging or cable storage;
- time needed to make the cleaner ready for the next cycle.

Cordless products often promote self-parking at a wall. Confirm how consistently it works and whether a retrieval hook is included.

Corded cleaners can be guided toward the edge through the cable, but the cable should never be used to lift the robot unless the manual expressly allows the handling method.

## Warranty and Service

WYBOT’s U.S. warranty policy lists robotic-pool-cleaner coverage of 12 or 24 months depending on model. It states that routine wear parts are generally covered only for defects on delivery and describes repair, replacement or refund as possible remedies. Regional terms can differ.

Maytronics directs Dolphin owners to authorized dealers for sales, diagnosis, repairs and warranty processing. Its dealer locator distinguishes certified service, trained technicians and genuine parts. Warranty length still varies by product and sales channel.

Before purchase, obtain written answers to:

- Is the seller authorized?
- Who pays inbound freight?
- Where is the service center?
- Can the battery, cable, power supply and motor block be replaced?
- Which parts are stocked locally?
- Is a replacement or loan unit available?
- What proof and registration are required?
- Does commercial or rental use change coverage?

The warranty route is especially important for a heavy wet product that is expensive to ship.

## Parts and Repairability

Dolphin’s mature parts system is a strategic advantage for dealers and long-term owners. Maytronics publicly lists genuine parts and uses trained service channels.

WYBOT is improving modularity on current products and supports repairs or replacements under its model-specific warranty. Buyers should still verify whether modules are sold locally after the warranty and whether repair instructions are available to authorized partners.

Ask for a five-year parts list covering:

- filters;
- brushes;
- tracks and wheels;
- charging system or power supply;
- battery;
- cable and swivel;
- seals;
- impeller and motor block;
- control electronics.

Parts availability converts a product from a seasonal appliance into serviceable pool equipment.

## Total Cost of Ownership

Use the same ownership period and cleaning frequency.

**Annualized TCO = (purchase price + accessories + expected repairs − residual value) ÷ ownership years + annual energy + consumables + service**

For WYBOT, include:

- battery reserve;
- charger and contact maintenance;
- lithium return freight;
- filters and brushes;
- possible downtime without local repair.

For Dolphin, include:

- cable, swivel and power-supply reserve;
- dealer service charges;
- filters and brushes;
- caddy or storage;
- cord handling time.

The lowest checkout price can have a higher annual cost if the product is replaced rather than repaired.

## Which Brand Should You Buy?

### Choose WYBOT when:

- a cordless workflow is the main priority;
- the selected model is validated for the pool;
- current features and online value matter;
- the owner accepts battery maintenance;
- regional warranty and parts logistics are clear.

### Choose Dolphin when:

- local dealer advice and repair access matter;
- a corded platform fits the pool and operating routine;
- genuine parts and serviceability are priorities;
- the owner wants a long-established pool-specialist channel;
- a suitable authorized seller is nearby.

### Do not choose either brand until:

- the exact model covers the required zones;
- filtration matches the real debris;
- the owner can lift and maintain it;
- replacement parts are identified;
- the warranty route is written down.

## Dealer and Distributor View

WYBOT can be attractive for ecommerce, promotional bundles and buyers moving quickly toward cordless products. Channel partners need disciplined battery handling, returns and model control because frequent lineup changes can complicate spare-parts stocking.

Dolphin can fit specialist pool stores that earn margin through advice, demonstration, service and parts. The model can require more training and workshop capability, but it also creates a repair and customer-retention route.

## FAQ

### Is WYBOT better than Dolphin?

Not universally. WYBOT can be better for a cordless, feature-led buying brief. Dolphin can be better where dealer service, genuine parts and a mature repair route are more valuable.

### Do all Dolphin cleaners use a cable?

No. Dolphin has cordless products as well as a large corded range. Confirm the exact model.

### How long is the WYBOT warranty?

WYBOT’s U.S. policy lists 12 or 24 months depending on model. Regional terms and sellers can differ.

### Does a corded Dolphin cost more to operate?

Not necessarily. Electricity use is usually only one part of cost. Cable, power supply, filters, service and handling should be compared with a cordless product’s battery, charger and freight.

### Which is better for fine debris?

It depends on the exact filter package and debris load. Request standard and ultra-fine filter results using the pool’s actual silt, pollen or sand.
