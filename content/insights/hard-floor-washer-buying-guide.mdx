**A hard floor washer is the right tool if most of your floors are tile, vinyl, laminate or sealed wood and you want to vacuum and mop in one pass.** Choose by the size of your floor area, how greasy your kitchen gets, how low your furniture is and how much after-use cleaning you will tolerate. Peak suction is not a good way to compare them.

This guide replaces our 2022 edition. Since then the category has moved from a few brands to dozens, and hot water, steam, lay-flat bodies and refill stations have become common.

![Hard floor washer decision chart: floor area, dirt type, furniture height, weight and dock care](/images/articles/hard-floor-washer-buying-guide/02-floor-washer-decision-chart.webp)

## Floor washer, robot or mop?

A floor washer is guided by hand, like a cordless vacuum. It cleans faster and deeper than a robot on a messy kitchen floor, but someone has to push it. A [robot vacuum with a roller mop](/blog/robot-vacuum-buying-guide) does daily maintenance on its own. Many households now use both.

A floor washer is not a carpet cleaner. Some models, such as BISSELL's [CrossWave Edge Cordless](/products/bissell-crosswave-edge-cordless-4328f), have no vacuum-only mode. For rugs and upholstery, see [spot cleaner vs carpet cleaner](/blog/spot-cleaner-vs-carpet-cleaner).

## Step 1: Slim or full-size?

| Type | Typical weight | Tank | Examples | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Slim | About 2 to 2.5 kg | About 300 ml | [Dyson W1 PencilWash](/products/dyson-w1-pencilwash), [Dyson PencilWash](/products/dyson-pencilwash) | Small homes, quick spills, people who find full-size washers heavy |
| Full-size | About 3.5 to 5 kg | About 0.75 to 1 L | [Tineco Floor One S9 Master](/products/tineco-floor-one-s9-master), [Dreame T16 Pro Heat](/products/dreame-t16-pro-heat), [Roborock F25 ACE Pro](/products/roborock-f25-ace-pro), [BISSELL CrossWave Edge](/products/bissell-crosswave-edge-cordless-4328f) | Larger homes, kitchens, pets |
| Wand style | About 5.7 kg | Varies | [Shark AquaReach](/products/shark-aquareach-wd551) | Reaching walls, stairs and cabinets with an extendable wand |

Slim models are lighter and easier to store, but their small tanks mean more refills, and some, like the UK [W1 PencilWash](/products/dyson-w1-pencilwash) package, have no self-clean cycle, so you wash the roller by hand.

## Step 2: Hot water, steam or foam?

Heated cleaning is the main feature race of 2025–2026.

- **Hot water**: the [Dreame T16 Pro Heat](/products/dreame-t16-pro-heat) claims 90°C water and the [Tineco S9 Master](/products/tineco-floor-one-s9-master) uses ThermoBlast for targeted treatment.
- **Steam**: the [Roborock F25 Ultra Steam Gen 2](/products/roborock-f25-ultra-steam-gen-2) lists 5,000 mg/min of steam, and the [Dreame T16 Pro Steam](/products/dreame-t16-pro-steam) has separate steam and hot-water modes.
- **Foam**: the [Roborock F25 ACE Pro](/products/roborock-f25-ace-pro) uses JetFoaming with a dedicated solution.

Two cautions. First, headline temperatures such as 180°C are usually measured inside the heater, not on the floor. Second, **heat drains the battery**. The T16 Pro Heat claims up to 70 minutes in Eco mode but 22 minutes with hot water; the F25 Ultra Steam Gen 2 lists 100 minutes in Auto/Eco but 30 minutes in Steam mode. If you want heat for the whole house, check the heated runtime, not the best case. Check also that steam is approved for your floor, especially wood.

## Step 3: Check reach under furniture

Lay-flat designs that fold to 180° are now common. The [Tineco Floor One S9 Master](/products/tineco-floor-one-s9-master) lies at 9.9 cm, the [Dyson W1](/products/dyson-w1-pencilwash) reaches 7.5 cm, and Roborock's F25 models list 12.5 cm clearance. Some machines lose performance when flat: Eureka's manual for the [FloorShine 830](/products/eureka-floorshine-830) says suction and usable dirty-water capacity drop in lay-flat mode.

Edge cleaning also varies. BISSELL's ZeroGap design on the CrossWave Edge is built to reach walls and cabinets.

## Step 4: Tanks, runtime and weight

- **Clean-water tank**: from 300 ml (Dyson PencilWash) to 1 L (Roborock F25 ACE Pro). Larger tanks mean fewer refills on big floors.
- **Dirty-water tank**: small dirty tanks fill fast. The compact [Tineco Floor One i7 Fold](/products/tineco-floor-one-i7-fold) has 0.4 L.
- **Runtime**: from about 22 minutes (BISSELL CrossWave Edge) to 60 to 100 minutes in eco modes on Roborock and Tineco models.
- **Weight**: from 2.4 kg (Dyson W1) to 4.8 kg (BISSELL CrossWave Edge). Powered drive and steering help with heavy models.
- **Battery**: a removable battery, as on the Dreame T16 Pro Heat, makes long-term replacement easier.

## Step 5: The dock and after-use care

This is where floor washers differ most in daily life. After each use, the roller and dirty-water path need cleaning.

| Dock level | What it does | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Charging only | Charges; you wash the roller | Dyson W1 PencilWash (UK package) |
| Self-clean | Runs water through the roller and path | BISSELL CrossWave Edge, Shark AquaReach WD551 |
| Self-clean and hot-air drying | Cleans and dries the roller to reduce smell | Tineco S9 Master, Eureka FloorShine 830 (75°C), Shark AquaReach WD563 |
| Refill station | Refills the washer with heated water and handles solution | [Tineco Floor One Station S9 Scientist Pro](/products/tineco-floor-one-station-s9-scientist-pro), 5 L reservoir |

Drying matters more than it sounds: a damp roller left in a dock is the main cause of bad smells. For the history of the category and why so many brands joined, see [the story of hard floor washers](/blog/story-of-hard-floor-washers).

## Step 6: Running costs

Plan for replacement rollers, filters and branded cleaning solution. Foam and some heated modes need a specific solution. Check the price of a spare roller and whether it is sold in your country before buying.

## Quick checklist before you buy

1. Mostly hard floors? If not, you still need a vacuum for carpet.
2. Heated runtime long enough for your floor area.
3. Clearance under your lowest furniture, and whether performance drops when lying flat.
4. Weight you can push and carry, including on stairs.
5. Self-clean with drying, or are you happy to wash the roller by hand?
6. Price of rollers, filters and solution, and local warranty service.
