**Teka appliances are owned by Midea Group following the completion of Midea's acquisition of Teka Group on April 30, 2025.** The transaction brought Teka's international appliance business and brand portfolio into the Chinese home-appliance group, but expressly excluded Teka Rus LLC.

The deal answers the parent-ownership question. It does not mean every Teka appliance is made in a Midea factory, sold by the same company or covered by one worldwide warranty.

## When Did Midea Buy Teka?

Teka and its previous owner announced the transaction in June 2024. The acquisition was completed on April 30, 2025 after the required process and approvals. Official completion statements say Midea acquired Teka Group excluding Teka Rus LLC.

This timing matters. An article or supplier document written before completion may still describe the former ownership structure. Current diligence should use post-closing evidence and identify the relevant regional company.

Midea's broader appliance portfolio is covered in [Who Owns Midea Appliances? Toshiba, Eureka, Teka and Global Manufacturing Explained](/blog/who-owns-midea-appliances-brand-portfolio). This page focuses on Teka and the acquisition boundaries.

## What Does the Teka Rus Exclusion Mean?

The exclusion means the transaction did not include Teka Rus LLC. It would therefore be inaccurate to say that Midea acquired every Teka-related company or right worldwide without qualification.

For Russia or transactions connected to that market, buyers should separately verify the legal entity, trademark rights, importer, inventory ownership and warranty provider. The global Teka website or Midea parent relationship is not sufficient evidence for the excluded business.

## Is Teka Still a German Brand?

Teka traces its history to Germany in 1924. It later developed major operations in Spain and expanded internationally across kitchen appliances, sinks and related products. Today it is best described as **a German-origin international kitchen and appliance brand owned by China-based Midea Group**.

Brand origin, corporate headquarters, parent nationality and manufacturing origin are separate facts. None of them alone establishes where a current oven, hood, dishwasher or sink was made.

## What Products Does Teka Sell?

Teka's official international catalogue includes:

- ovens, microwaves, hobs, cookers and ventilation hoods;
- refrigerators, wine coolers, dishwashers and washing machines;
- built-in coffee machines and small kitchen appliances;
- kitchen sinks, taps and accessories; and
- market-specific integrated kitchen solutions.

The product breadth also creates different compliance paths. An electrical appliance, gas hob, food-contact sink and plumbing fitting require different documents, standards and service arrangements.

## Where Are Teka Appliances Made?

At the 2024 transaction stage, Teka said the group operated ten factories and produced more than three million products annually. This demonstrates a substantial industrial footprint, but it is not a current model-to-plant map after the Midea acquisition.

Teka's historical materials describe manufacturing and product expansion across multiple countries. Midea also has a large global manufacturing network. Shared ownership creates opportunities for industrial integration, but it does not prove that a Teka model has moved to a particular Midea plant.

![Teka model, factory, market and warranty verification flow](/images/blog/teka-product-verification-map.svg)

For an exact product, verify:

1. the full model code and country-specific suffix;
2. the legal manufacturer and conformity-document holder;
3. the current factory and country of origin;
4. the importer or responsible economic operator;
5. the authorized seller and invoice issuer; and
6. the local warranty, installation, parts and service route.

## Does Midea Manufacture Teka Appliances?

Midea owns Teka Group and has extensive appliance manufacturing capability. That fact alone does not establish that Midea directly manufactures every Teka product.

The acquired Teka business had its own factories and supply relationships. Post-acquisition production can involve existing Teka sites, Midea facilities or external suppliers depending on the category and model. Only current product and supplier evidence can establish the answer for a particular SKU.

## How Do Teka Sales and Warranty Work?

Teka operates country-specific websites, distributors, retailers and support routes across more than 120 countries. Product availability, model codes, installers and warranty terms vary by market.

The warranty provider may be a regional Teka company, importer, distributor or seller rather than Midea Group itself. Buyers should also confirm whether installation must be performed by an authorized professional and whether imported models qualify for local parts and service.

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

1. Use the April 2025 completed transaction as the current ownership reference.
2. Treat Teka Rus LLC and Russian-market rights as a separate diligence question.
3. Confirm the exact model on the destination-country site.
4. Obtain post-acquisition factory and origin evidence.
5. Validate category-specific electrical, gas, plumbing and food-contact compliance.
6. Identify the local importer, seller and warranty provider.
7. Confirm installation, spare-parts and service capacity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is the parent company of Teka?

Midea Group is Teka Group's parent following completion of the acquisition on April 30, 2025.

### Did Midea buy all of Teka?

The official transaction statements say Midea acquired Teka Group excluding Teka Rus LLC. Russian-market entities and rights therefore require separate verification.

### Is Teka a Chinese brand now?

Teka remains a German-origin international brand. Its current corporate owner is China-based Midea Group.

### Are Teka appliances made by Midea?

Some future or current products may use Midea capabilities, but ownership alone does not prove the factory. Verify the exact model's manufacturer, plant and origin.

### Where are Teka appliances made?

Teka has used a multi-country manufacturing network. The group disclosed ten factories at the 2024 transaction stage, but current origin must be checked model by model.

## Final Answer

Teka is an international kitchen and appliance brand owned by Midea Group since the acquisition closed in April 2025. The transaction excluded Teka Rus LLC, so the ownership answer must carry that regional qualification.

Midea ownership does not provide a universal manufacturing answer. Buyers and distributors should verify every model's current factory, origin, responsible entity, seller, installation requirements and local warranty.
