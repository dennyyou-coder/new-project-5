**Wetrok is owned by the Diethelm Keller Group**, a family-owned Swiss group. Wetrok has been part of it since 1955, and Diethelm Keller describes Wetrok as its Swiss business-to-business professional-cleaning company. The operating company is Wetrok AG, based in Kloten near Zurich.

Wetrok is a Swiss company. It began in 1948 as a technology business that built a single-disc cleaning machine, and it still develops and produces in Switzerland.

## What is the Diethelm Keller Group?

Diethelm Keller is a family-owned Swiss group organised into several operating units; Wetrok makes up its Industrial unit and runs its own products, sales and service.

## What does Wetrok make?

Wetrok sells a complete professional-cleaning system rather than machines alone:

- **Machines**: scrubber-driers, sweepers, single-disc machines, wet and dry vacuums and cleaning robots.
- **Chemicals**: cleaning, care, protection and disinfection products developed in its own laboratory.
- **Tools and consumables**: manual tools, carts and hygiene supplies.
- **Methods and training**: cleaning concepts, training and digital support.

In 2008 Wetrok acquired and integrated Minatol, a traditional Swiss cleaning brand.

## Where are Wetrok machines made?

Diethelm Keller states that Wetrok's research, development and production are based in Switzerland. Wetrok says its own engineering department, chemistry laboratory and teams develop and test machines and cleaning agents in company laboratories and production facilities.

## How does Wetrok sell?

Wetrok has subsidiaries in Germany, Poland, Sweden and Austria, and authorised dealers in about 40 other countries. In Switzerland, Wetrok AG is the contracting party for professional customers and service agreements, and it combines product sales with advice, training, machine service and spare parts.
