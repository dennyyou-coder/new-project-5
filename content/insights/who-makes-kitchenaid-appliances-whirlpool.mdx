**KitchenAid is a Whirlpool Corporation brand, but “Whirlpool makes KitchenAid” is only the beginning of the answer. Whirlpool Corporation operates KitchenAid major appliances and a global small-domestic-appliance business, Whirlpool Properties, Inc. is named as the KitchenAid trademark owner in official terms, and selected KitchenAid categories can be licensed. Manufacturing and warranty responsibility depend on the product and market.**

The iconic stand mixer has unusually clear official evidence: Whirlpool Corporation identifies Greenville, Ohio as its main KitchenAid small-appliance manufacturing site and says the plant makes KitchenAid small appliances including stand mixers. That does not prove that every blender, espresso machine, refrigerator, dishwasher, grill or accessory is made in Greenville.

## Who Owns the KitchenAid Brand?

Whirlpool Corporation owns and operates KitchenAid as one of its principal appliance brands. Its 2025 annual report names KitchenAid alongside Whirlpool, JennAir, Maytag, Amana, Brastemp, Consul and InSinkErator.

KitchenAid began in 1919 with the stand mixer and now spans countertop appliances, kitchenware and major kitchen appliances. The brand's official U.S. site separates those categories in navigation, returns, service and warranty contacts. That separation is operationally meaningful: a dishwasher and a stand mixer can share the KitchenAid name while following different service teams, parts systems and manufacturing routes.

Official KitchenAid ecommerce terms also name **Whirlpool Properties, Inc.** as the owner of the registered KitchenAid trademark. A buyer should therefore avoid recording “KitchenAid LLC” or another assumed entity without seeing it in the relevant contract.

## Is KitchenAid the Same Company as Whirlpool Corporation?

No. KitchenAid is a brand within Whirlpool Corporation's portfolio, not the corporate issuer itself.

Whirlpool Corporation reports the Global Small Domestic Appliances segment and includes KitchenAid activity within its brand and product strategy. Regional warranty documents can name Whirlpool Corporation, Whirlpool Canada LP or local Whirlpool subsidiaries as the responsible party for KitchenAid products.

The exact name matters. A procurement contract with Whirlpool Corporation is not automatically a contract with every regional subsidiary, and a KitchenAid retailer is not automatically authorized for every country or product line.

## Where Are KitchenAid Stand Mixers Made?

Whirlpool Corporation's Greenville, Ohio operation is the clearest documented KitchenAid manufacturing site.

The company says the Greenville plant makes KitchenAid small appliances, including the iconic stand mixer. Earlier official investment releases identify stand mixers, attachments, hand mixers and blenders among the products produced there. Whirlpool's current global-locations page describes Greenville as the main manufacturing facility for the KitchenAid Small Domestic Appliance business.

This supports a specific conclusion: KitchenAid stand mixers and named small-appliance categories have official Greenville manufacturing evidence.

It does not support the broader claim that every KitchenAid product worldwide is made in Ohio. Product programs change, regional versions can differ, and the brand includes major appliances far outside the Greenville site's disclosed scope.

## Who Makes KitchenAid Major Appliances?

KitchenAid's U.S. major-appliance portfolio includes refrigerators, ranges, dishwashers, wall ovens, microwaves, cooktops, undercounter refrigeration, disposals, compactors, hoods and vents. These products sit within Whirlpool Corporation's major-appliance business.

Whirlpool Corporation reports multiple manufacturing operations in North America and says 80% of the major appliances it sells in the United States come from U.S. factories. That statistic covers the company's U.S. major-appliance portfolio, not a KitchenAid-only factory assignment.

For a KitchenAid refrigerator or dishwasher, the defensible manufacturing answer comes from the model label, country-of-origin marking, manufacturer declaration and warranty. A plant can make products for several Whirlpool Corporation brands, and a brand can use several plants.

## Are All KitchenAid Countertop Appliances Made by Whirlpool?

Whirlpool Corporation operates the KitchenAid small-domestic-appliance business and directly documents manufacturing at Greenville. However, the brand's countertop range is wider than the categories named in any single plant disclosure.

Current KitchenAid offerings include stand mixers, attachments, espresso machines, blenders, hand blenders, hand mixers, food processors, grain and rice cookers and cordless appliances. The supplier and assembly route for one family should not be inferred from another.

Before making a “made by Whirlpool” claim for a specific countertop appliance, check:

- the legal manufacturer on the rating label or manual;
- the country of origin on the product and carton;
- the model prefix and regional voltage;
- the importer or responsible person;
- the warranty entity; and
- whether the product is sold directly by KitchenAid or by a licensed partner.

## Which KitchenAid Products Are Licensed?

Whirlpool Corporation's official brand-licensing page lists KitchenAid outdoor grills and barbecue accessories among current licensed product categories. Licensed kitchen textiles, gadgets or other categories can also appear under agreements and regional arrangements.

A licensed KitchenAid product legitimately uses the trademark but can have a manufacturer, seller and warranty provider different from Whirlpool Corporation's major-appliance or Greenville operations. This is common in consumer brands and should be recorded as a licence relationship, not as counterfeit risk by default.

The buyer's task is to verify the licensee and scope. Ask for authorization covering the product category, territory, sales channel and contract period.

## How Do KitchenAid Warranties Show the Operating Boundary?

KitchenAid's official service pages maintain separate contacts for major appliances and countertop appliances. A current U.S./Canada major-appliance warranty refers to the KitchenAid brand of Whirlpool Corporation or Whirlpool Canada, LP and says authorized KitchenAid service providers perform warranty service.

Other official warranty certificates name different Whirlpool subsidiaries as manufacturer, importer, marketer or guarantor by country. This demonstrates why a global brand page cannot substitute for the document packed with the product.

Even within one country, service plans can be provided by an independent company using Whirlpool Corporation trademarks with permission. The service-contract provider, manufacturer warranty and retailer return policy are separate obligations.

## Did KitchenAid Transfer to Beko Europe?

No. Whirlpool Corporation's April 2024 closing announcement for the European appliance transaction says it retained the EMEA KitchenAid small- and major-domestic-appliance business.

The transferred Whirlpool-origin European major-appliance operation became part of Beko Europe, and Beko Europe uses the Whirlpool brand under licence in specified jurisdictions. KitchenAid was expressly carved out.

In June 2026, Beko acquired Whirlpool Corporation's remaining 25% stake in Beko Europe, but the transaction did not change the retained KitchenAid ownership boundary. The Whirlpool trademark licence continued; KitchenAid remained in Whirlpool Corporation's portfolio.

Our [Whirlpool and Beko Europe ownership guide](/blog/who-owns-whirlpool-appliances-beko-europe) explains the regional licence, while the [Beko ownership guide](/blog/who-owns-beko-appliances-beko-europe) covers the current 100% Beko Europe equity structure.

## What Should Retailers and Suppliers Verify?

| Product evidence | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| Exact model and serial number | Product family, generation and service lookup |
| Rating plate and carton | Voltage, manufacturer disclosures and country of origin |
| Official manual | Responsible entities, safety standards and regional version |
| Trademark or licence notice | Whether the category is directly operated or licensed |
| Purchase invoice | Legal seller and warranty start date |
| Warranty certificate | Guarantor, territory, exclusions and repair channel |
| Supplier declaration | Factory and quality-system scope for the contracted SKU |
| Parts catalogue | Repairability and replacement-part availability |

For sourcing, ask whether the project belongs to Whirlpool Corporation's major-appliance business, KitchenAid Small Domestic Appliances, a regional subsidiary or a licensed category. Similar logos do not make the purchasing authority interchangeable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is KitchenAid owned by Whirlpool?

Yes. KitchenAid is part of Whirlpool Corporation's current brand portfolio.

### Who owns the KitchenAid trademark?

Official KitchenAid terms for the cited market identify Whirlpool Properties, Inc. as the registered trademark owner.

### Are KitchenAid stand mixers made in the USA?

Whirlpool Corporation identifies Greenville, Ohio as its main KitchenAid small-appliance manufacturing site and says it makes KitchenAid stand mixers. Verify the label for the exact model.

### Are KitchenAid refrigerators made in Greenville, Ohio?

The reviewed official evidence identifies Greenville with small appliances, not KitchenAid refrigerators. Check the refrigerator's model label and origin documentation.

### Does Beko own KitchenAid in Europe?

No. Whirlpool Corporation expressly retained its EMEA KitchenAid small- and major-appliance business when Beko Europe was formed.

### Are KitchenAid grills made by Whirlpool?

Whirlpool Corporation lists outdoor grills and barbecue accessories as licensed KitchenAid categories. Verify the named licensee and warranty for the product.

## Final Answer

KitchenAid is owned and operated as a brand within Whirlpool Corporation. Whirlpool Properties, Inc. is identified as the trademark owner in official KitchenAid terms. Whirlpool Corporation directly documents KitchenAid small-appliance manufacturing in Greenville, Ohio, including stand mixers, but that evidence should not be generalized to every countertop or major appliance.

KitchenAid major appliances, small appliances and licensed categories can have different factories, sellers and warranties. KitchenAid also remained with Whirlpool Corporation in EMEA rather than transferring to Beko Europe. The exact model, country, legal manufacturer, licence and warranty certificate provide the final product-level answer.
