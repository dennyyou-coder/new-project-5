**Truvox is owned by Tacony Corporation.** Truvox International Limited's 2025 group accounts describe it as a wholly owned subsidiary of Tacony, a US company, and say there is no single ultimate controlling party. Tacony acquired Truvox in 2007.

Truvox is British. Its floor-care history dates to 1960, and Truvox International Limited was incorporated in 1962. It is based in Hedge End, Southampton.

## Is Truvox a British company?

Yes. Truvox has a US owner, but the company is registered and based in England, and it designs and produces its machines in Southampton.

## What does Truvox make?

Scrubber dryers, single-disc and three-brush machines such as the Multiwash, carpet and spray-extraction cleaners, commercial vacuums, sweepers and air movers.

## How does Truvox sell?

Through distributors and dealers. Its warranty terms make the reseller responsible for warranty to its customers, and a claim needs the original invoice with the machine's serial number.
