**Tennant and Nilfisk are the two largest specialist makers of commercial floor scrubbers, and both are a safe choice for most sites. The decision usually comes down to the specific machine, the local dealer or direct service team, and whether you also need robots, industrial vacuums or pressure washers.** Tennant is stronger in robotic floor care under its own name; Nilfisk has the broader professional range.

## Which is better for robots?

Tennant has made autonomous cleaning a growth priority. Its robotics sales rose 71% to about USD 37.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, and it launched its first autonomous vacuum, the P1 VAC, in October 2026. Nilfisk sells robotic scrubbers too, including the SC25, which LionsBot makes for it in Singapore. If robots are central to your plan, compare the robot model, its navigation and the support behind it, not just the brand.

## Which is better for heavy industry?

Nilfisk's range is wider for industrial sites: besides floor care it has a long history in industrial vacuums and pressure washers, so one supplier can cover more of a plant. Tennant focuses on floor care and sells IPC machines alongside its own.

## Service and dealers

Both sell directly and through distributors, and service quality depends on your region. Ask each for the local technician coverage, response time, parts availability and the price of a planned maintenance contract. See the [floor scrubber maintenance checklist and service contracts](/blog/floor-scrubber-maintenance-checklist).

## Ownership and stability

Tennant is an independent listed company. Nilfisk was taken private by Freudenberg in 2026, which adds a large industrial owner. Neither change affects how a machine is serviced day to day, but it is worth asking about product roadmaps and parts commitments for long contracts.
