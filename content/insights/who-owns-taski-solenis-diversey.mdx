**TASKI is owned by Solenis.** TASKI's own website calls it a Solenis brand. Solenis, a specialty chemicals company, completed its acquisition of Diversey, TASKI's previous owner, on July 5, 2023, which brought the TASKI machine business into the combined group. TASKI is still marketed through the Diversey and Solenis commercial organisation.

TASKI is a Swiss brand. Its history goes back to Alfred Sutter's business, founded in Switzerland in 1955, and its machines are tested at its factory in Münchwilen.

## Is TASKI part of Diversey?

TASKI came to Solenis as part of Diversey. Official TASKI materials still describe it as one of Diversey's business areas and assign many TASKI trademarks to Diversey, Inc. or its licensors, while the TASKI website names it a Solenis brand. In practice, Diversey sits inside Solenis, and TASKI sits inside that combined business. In June 2024 Solenis introduced a single global brand identity while keeping product brands such as TASKI.

## What does TASKI make?

- **Scrubber dryers**: the ULTIMAXX and swingo ranges, from compact to large machines.
- **Vacuum, steam and carpet-care equipment**.
- **Robotics and connected services**: autonomous machines and IntelliTrail fleet tools, plus maintenance and parts programmes.

As part of a cleaning-chemicals group, TASKI presents its machines alongside chemical dosing, cleaning processes and service, and publishes sector material for markets such as hospitality and healthcare.

## Where are TASKI machines made?

TASKI's hospitality brochure says every TASKI machine is tested before leaving its factory in Münchwilen, Switzerland, and the company states that the ULTIMAXX 360 was designed and manufactured in Switzerland. TASKI does not say where its robotic machines and their autonomy systems are built.
