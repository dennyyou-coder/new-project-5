**Fimap is part of a group controlled by Comac S.p.A.** Fimap's own privacy policy says so. Comac's privacy policy also refers to a group controlled by Comac S.p.A. Neither company names a parent above Comac or publishes the exact shareholding chain.

Both are Italian. Comac S.p.A. and Fimap S.p.A. are based in Santa Maria di Zevio, in the province of Verona, and both design and build professional floor-cleaning machines.

## Are Fimap and Comac the same company?

No. They are separate legal companies with separate brands, product ranges, websites and dealer networks. What connects them is control: Fimap sits in the group that Comac S.p.A. controls. A Fimap machine is sold and supported under the Fimap name, and a Comac machine under the Comac name.

## Comac history

Comac's history goes back to 1974, when Costruzione Macchine was created to build floor scrubbers in the Verona area. Giancarlo Ruffo and Domenico Accadia are named as co-founders. The company marked 50 years in 2024.

## What do Fimap and Comac make?

- **Fimap**: scrubber-driers, sweepers and sweeper-scrubbers, vacuums, extractors, single-disc machines and the CB-1 autonomous scrubber. Fimap says it exports to 80 countries.
- **Comac**: scrubber-driers, sweepers and street sweepers, vacuums, single-disc machines and the SELF.Y autonomous scrubber, positioned for airports, supermarkets, offices and healthcare.

## Where are Fimap and Comac machines made?

Fimap says it designs and produces its machines in Italy. Comac's history documents production in the Verona area since the 1970s, first in San Giovanni Lupatoto and since 2010 in Santa Maria di Zevio. For a particular model, the serial plate and declaration of conformity confirm the producer.
