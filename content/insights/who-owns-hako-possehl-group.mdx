**Hako is owned by the Possehl Group.** Possehl, a diversified industrial group based in Lübeck, Germany, has been Hako GmbH's sole shareholder since 2007. Hako is a German company: Hans Koch founded it in Pinneberg in 1948, and it is now headquartered in Bad Oldesloe, Schleswig-Holstein.

Hako makes professional floor-cleaning machines, sold as Scrubmaster scrubber-driers and Sweepmaster sweepers, plus municipal vehicles under the Citymaster and Multicar names. Its group also includes the Minuteman, PowerBoss, Multi-Clean and Oertzen brands.

## Who is the Possehl Group?

Possehl is a diversified industrial group based in Lübeck. Hako is one of its businesses and is run by its own management board.

## Is Minuteman owned by Hako?

Yes. Hako's group structure lists Minuteman, based in Pingree Grove, Illinois, and PowerBoss, a Minuteman subsidiary, along with Multi-Clean and the high-pressure specialist Oertzen.

## Who runs Hako?

Hako has a three-person management board: Joachim Blache leads operations, Axel Jensen technology and products, and Frank Ulbricht sales and marketing.

## Where are Hako machines made?

Hako's sustainability report lists three production plants and nine sales offices in Germany. The same report says 69% of its suppliers are based in Germany and about 90% in Europe. Machines from the North American group brands are not necessarily made in the German plants; the serial plate and documents show where a given machine was built.

## How does Hako sell?

Hako sells through subsidiaries and sales and service partners. Its European service network has about 650 technicians. Its products are marketed for logistics, retail, healthcare, industry and municipal cleaning.
