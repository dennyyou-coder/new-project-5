**Parkside is Lidl's private-label tool and garden brand. There is no single company that can accurately be called the manufacturer of every Parkside tool.** Lidl controls the retail brand proposition, while different products can name different suppliers, responsible economic operators, importers or service companies.

Official Lidl records currently connect specified Parkside products with companies including Grizzly Tools GmbH & Co. KG, Kompernaß Handelsgesellschaft mbH and OWIM GmbH & Co. KG. Those roles are model-specific and do not automatically prove which physical factory made the product.

## Is Parkside Owned by Lidl?

Yes. Lidl's official communications describe Parkside as one of its own brands and position it as a central workshop and garden range. Lidl is the retailer and brand organizer; it is not evidence that Lidl employees manufacture the tools in Lidl-owned factories.

Private-label programs separate several roles: the retailer defines the assortment and channel, a commercial supplier develops or sources the product, a legal entity carries regulatory responsibilities, a factory performs production, and another company may handle parts or warranty work.

## Which Companies Make Parkside Tools?

There is no honest catalogue-wide one-name answer.

- A current Parkside 12V drill page names **Grizzly Tools GmbH & Co. KG** as the responsible EU economic operator.
- A current 20V mower page names **Kompernaß Handelsgesellschaft mbH**.
- Lidl's official recall for a specified Parkside laser distance meter names **OWIM GmbH & Co. KG** and applies only to the identified model and IAN.

These records prove that multiple entities participate in the range. “Responsible economic operator” can cover compliance, importing or market responsibility; it should not automatically be rewritten as “owns Parkside” or “operates the physical factory.”

For manufacturing due diligence, use the product label, manual, EU declaration of conformity, responsible-person entry, country-of-origin marking and shipment documentation together.

## Why the IAN Number Matters

Parkside tools commonly carry an IAN, or article identification number. It helps distinguish products that share a familiar marketing name but come from different generations, suppliers or technical designs.

Record the complete model and IAN before ordering parts, batteries or service. A manual for a similarly named drill may contain a different charger, parts list, warranty address or declaration. The IAN is also important in recalls: a notice covering one IAN does not cover every tool with the Parkside name.

## How the X20V Team Works

Lidl says the Parkside X20V Team now covers more than 70 devices and that X20V batteries are compatible across the documented X20V Team range. The system spans workshop, household and garden products, including drills, saws, vacuums, mowers and blowers.

Lidl also explains that specified 40V products use two X20V batteries. This is a platform design—two matched packs operating in an approved product—not permission to improvise combinations. The separate X12V Team remains a different system.

Compatibility checks should include battery family, pack capacity, charger model, paired-battery requirements, firmware or smart features and the exact manual. “Parkside battery” is too broad a description for procurement.

## Parkside in Cleaning and Outdoor Equipment

Parkside's adjacency to professional cleaning is stronger than a drill-only brand. The X20V range includes wet-dry vacuums and other cleanup products, while blowers, pressure-cleaning equipment and garden tools support property maintenance.

For a business buyer, the low purchase price is only one factor. Confirm replacement filters, hoses, batteries, chargers and switches; the service route; and whether the warranty permits the planned commercial workload. Retail availability can be episodic, making spare-unit and consumables planning important.

## Warranty and Recall Boundaries

Parkside warranty terms vary by country, product, purchase date and use. The product page and manual for the exact IAN are more reliable than a general claim such as “all Parkside tools have a three-year warranty.” Keep the receipt and photograph the rating label before it wears.

Recalls are equally precise. The reviewed 2025 laser-distance-meter notice named one product and IAN. It did not establish a defect in all Parkside measuring tools or in products supplied by other entities.

## What Buyers and Importers Should Verify

1. Exact model, IAN and production date.
2. Contract seller and destination-market responsible entity.
3. Country of origin and physical factory evidence for commercial orders.
4. Declaration, certification and recall status.
5. X12V, X20V or approved two-pack configuration.
6. Warranty coverage for consumer or professional use.
7. Parts, filters, batteries and charger availability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Parkside tools?

Lidl presents Parkside as a Lidl private-label brand.

### Does one German company make all Parkside tools?

No. Official records name different responsible entities for different products. Each model and IAN requires its own check.

### Does Kompernass make Parkside tools?

Kompernass is named as the responsible operator for specified products and runs parts channels for Lidl/Kaufland products. That does not make it the owner or universal physical manufacturer of Parkside.

### Are all Parkside 20V batteries interchangeable?

Lidl states compatibility within the documented X20V Team. Confirm the exact tool, pack and charger; X12V and other systems are separate.

### Where are Parkside tools made?

Origin can vary. Read the exact product label and declaration instead of assigning the entire private-label range one country.

## Final Answer

Parkside is Lidl's private-label tool brand, supplied through a multi-company product system rather than one universal manufacturer. Grizzly Tools, Kompernass and OWIM appear in official records for specified products, but their exact legal role and the physical factory must be verified at model and IAN level. X20V Team creates useful platform compatibility, yet it does not erase product, supplier, warranty or origin differences.
