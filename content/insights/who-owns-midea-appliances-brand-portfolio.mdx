**Midea appliances are owned and operated by Midea Group Co., Ltd., a publicly listed Chinese technology and manufacturing company.** Midea Group trades in Shenzhen and Hong Kong and runs consumer appliances through its Smart Home business.

There is no separate universal manufacturer called “Midea Appliances.” The Midea name can refer to the listed group, the master consumer brand, a business unit or a regional company. Acquisitions also added specific rights and businesses connected to **Toshiba Lifestyle, Eureka and Teka**. Those relationships are not identical: one involves majority ownership plus a long-term brand license, another was a vacuum-brand asset acquisition, and the newest was a group acquisition with an explicit territorial exclusion.

## Is Midea a Public or Private Company?

Midea Group is a public company. Its 2025 annual report identifies **Midea Group Co., Ltd.** as the issuer, with A shares listed in Shenzhen under code 000333 and H shares listed in Hong Kong under code 0300.

This makes “who owns Midea?” different from asking who founded it. He Xiangjian founded the business that became Midea in 1968 and remains important to its history and shareholder context. Current company governance, however, belongs to the listed issuer and its board, not to a stand-alone private brand company.

Midea's investor materials identify Fang Hongbo as chairman and president. Buyers and suppliers should therefore distinguish the founder, significant shareholders, current management, the listed company and the legal entity named on a local contract.

The exact company name matters because regional businesses can use names such as Midea America Corp. or other country subsidiaries. Those entities do not replace Midea Group as the parent-company answer, and the parent company does not automatically become the seller or warranty provider for every transaction.

## What Is Midea's Smart Home Business?

Midea Group spans more than consumer appliances. Its businesses include Smart Home, Industrial Technologies, Building Technologies, Robotics & Automation, medical activities and logistics.

The **Smart Home business** is the core operator for home appliances, connected-home systems and related consumer ecosystems. It covers air treatment, refrigeration, laundry, kitchen appliances, water appliances, small appliances and floor care.

That distinction prevents two common mistakes:

1. treating every company with Midea in its name as the manufacturer of a refrigerator or vacuum;
2. treating an industrial component subsidiary as the consumer warranty company.

For product research, start with the Smart Home business and then move to the exact brand, model, regional seller and factory record.

## Which Appliance Brands Does Midea Own?

Midea has built a multi-brand portfolio through internal development and acquisitions. Its current official materials identify brands and identities including:

- **Midea**, the global master home-appliance brand;
- **Little Swan**, a major laundry and appliance brand in China;
- **Toshiba home appliances**, operated through the business acquired by Midea in 2016, subject to the applicable brand arrangements;
- **COLMO**, positioned for premium smart-home products;
- **WAHIN**, aimed at younger connected consumers;
- **Comfee**, used internationally across selected appliance categories;
- **Eureka**, the historic U.S.-origin floor-care brand acquired from Electrolux;
- **Teka**, along with related kitchen brands following Midea's acquisition of Teka Group.

The wider group also includes industrial and technology brands that should not be mixed into a consumer-appliance ownership answer.

Ownership does not make these labels interchangeable. A Midea distributor is not automatically authorized for Toshiba or Eureka products. A warranty issued by Midea America does not automatically cover a product bought through another brand or market.

## What Part of Toshiba Appliances Does Midea Own?

In 2016, Midea acquired **80.1% of Toshiba Lifestyle Products & Services Corporation**, the home-appliance business commonly called Toshiba Lifestyle. Midea's 2016 annual report also described a **40-year global license** to use the Toshiba brand for the covered home-appliance business.

This is a majority-owned operating company plus a trademark license—not ownership of the entire Toshiba corporate group. Toshiba-branded infrastructure, elevators, electronic components and other businesses can follow different corporate paths. Even within appliances, local product documents and trademark notices determine which company and license apply.

For buyers, “Toshiba is owned by Midea” is therefore too broad. A more precise statement is that Midea holds the controlling interest in Toshiba Lifestyle and long-term brand rights for the covered appliance business. The exact seller, importer and warranty provider can still differ by country.

## How Is Eureka Connected to Midea?

Midea acquired the **North American Eureka vacuum-cleaner brand and most related assets** from Electrolux in December 2016. Electrolux's annual reporting disclosed gross consideration of about US$40 million; a widely repeated US$60 million number referred to prior sales, not the purchase price.

Eureka now gives Midea a separate cleaning-appliance identity across upright, stick, canister, wet-dry and robotic products. Midea America operates the U.S. Eureka website, while model documents and regulatory records identify specialized companies such as Midea Robozone for selected robot-vacuum platforms.

The boundary matters. **Sanitaire** was sold separately by Electrolux to BISSELL in 2018, and **Eureka Forbes** is a different Indian company. Neither belongs inside Midea's Eureka organization merely because of historical or naming overlap. Our detailed [Eureka ownership guide](/blog/who-owns-eureka-midea-electrolux-manufacturing) explains the transaction, legal entities and excluded brands.

## What Did Midea Acquire From Teka?

Midea completed the acquisition of **Teka Group** on April 29, 2025 after announcing the proposed transaction in 2024. The closing release explicitly excluded **Teka Rus LLC**.

The transaction added a European- and Latin American-centered kitchen and home-solutions business with the **Teka, Küppersbusch and Intra** brands. Teka's product base includes ovens, cooktops, hoods, sinks and faucets, and the group brought its own factories, commercial network and brand heritage.

The exclusion should remain visible in ownership research. A group announcement cannot be copied into a Russian-market claim without checking the local entity and trademark position. The same principle applies when a distributor seeks authorization for only one Teka Group brand, product class or territory.

## Who Makes Midea Appliances?

Midea Group is one of the world's largest appliance manufacturers. It combines company-owned production, specialized subsidiaries, vertically integrated components and external supply-chain partners.

The group scale is clear. Midea's current investor page, using 2025 annual-report data, says it had **43 major overseas manufacturing bases**, **29 overseas R&D centers** and localized self-operated business in **50 major countries**. It reported total 2025 revenue of RMB 458.5 billion and overseas revenue of RMB 195.9 billion.

![Midea Group 2025 global operating network and model-level verification chain](/images/blog/midea-global-operating-network-map.svg)

These figures describe the group. They do not answer where one air conditioner, dishwasher, robot vacuum or microwave was made. Other Midea announcements use different totals because they were published on different dates or count global, overseas, acquired and specialized facilities differently. For example, the April 2025 Teka completion release described 44 manufacturing bases and separately noted Teka's ten factories. Those numbers should be dated and defined rather than combined into one artificial total.

A product can involve several layers:

1. Midea Group as the parent;
2. Smart Home as the operating business;
3. Midea or another portfolio brand;
4. a regional sales and warranty company;
5. a specialized product company;
6. a company-owned or contracted factory.

The rating plate, model manual, declaration of conformity and import record determine which layers apply to the actual SKU.

## Where Are Midea Appliances Made?

Midea manufactures in China and across a broad international network. Its overseas base count reflects the group's localization strategy, with production and R&D positioned near major markets.

That does not mean every country site makes every category. Appliance plants specialize. A facility making air conditioners does not prove the origin of a refrigerator, washer or floor-care product. Production assignments can also change as tariffs, capacity, local demand and model generations change.

For a defensible origin statement, buyers should collect:

- the complete model number and regional suffix;
- rating-plate and carton photographs;
- manufacturer and factory declarations;
- country-of-origin marking;
- importer and customs documents;
- current certification records.

Group presentations are useful for supplier strategy. They are not enough for customs classification, compliance or consumer labeling.

## Does Midea Make Vacuum Cleaners and Robot Vacuums?

Yes. Midea's current global floor-care range includes robot vacuums, stick vacuums, canister vacuums and wet-dry floor cleaners. The group also owns **Eureka**, which is marketed as a separate cleaning-appliance brand.

Midea's floor-care structure shows why entity boundaries matter. Selected manuals and regulatory records can name specialized companies such as Midea Robozone Technology for particular robot-vacuum models. A Midea-branded wet-dry cleaner may follow a different manufacturing and sales route. Eureka products retain their own brand, websites and regional commercial system.

For distributors, the key questions include software region, app provider, radio certification, battery and consumables supply, map-data handling, spare parts and authorized service. Parent-company manufacturing scale does not resolve those operational details.

## Does Midea Manufacture Products for Other Brands?

Midea has extensive manufacturing and component capabilities and participates in supply chains beyond its own consumer brand. However, it is unsafe to name Midea as the manufacturer of another brand without model-level evidence.

OEM and ODM relationships are often confidential, product-specific and time-limited. A factory that made one model in one year may not make the next generation. A shared compressor or motor supplier also does not prove that Midea assembled the finished product.

Buyers should rely on current manufacturer declarations, regulatory filings, factory audits and commercial documents rather than marketplace claims or supplier lists copied across product categories.

## How Does Midea Warranty Work?

Warranty is issued by the responsible regional company under local product terms. In the United States, Midea's current one-year limited-warranty page names **Midea America Corp.** as the provider for covered products and purchases. Other products, components, countries and commercial uses can have different terms.

That U.S. warranty should not be presented as a global rule. Before purchase, confirm:

- the destination-country warranty page;
- exact model coverage;
- parts and labor treatment;
- registration and proof-of-purchase requirements;
- commercial-use exclusions;
- authorized service and parts availability.

Purchasing from an unauthorized channel can also affect support. The seller, product region and warranty entity should match.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Midea appliances?

Midea appliances are operated by Midea Group Co., Ltd., a public company listed in Shenzhen and Hong Kong.

### Is Midea owned by another appliance company?

No. Midea Group is the parent company. It has public shareholders and owns a portfolio of appliance and technology businesses.

### Is Midea a Chinese company?

Yes. Midea Group is headquartered in Foshan, Guangdong, China, while operating a large global manufacturing, R&D and sales network.

### Are all Midea appliances made in China?

No. Midea has a substantial Chinese base and reports 43 major overseas manufacturing bases. The exact origin must be checked by model.

### Does Midea own Toshiba appliances?

Midea acquired 80.1% of Toshiba Lifestyle Products & Services and a 40-year Toshiba brand license for the covered home-appliance business in 2016. It does not own every Toshiba business.

### Does Midea own Eureka?

Yes. Midea acquired the North American Eureka vacuum business and most related assets from Electrolux in 2016. Sanitaire and Eureka Forbes are separate.

### Does Midea own Teka?

Yes, with an important boundary. Midea completed the Teka Group acquisition in April 2025, excluding Teka Rus LLC.

### Who provides Midea warranty service?

The responsible regional Midea company or authorized service network provides coverage under the model-specific local warranty.

## Final Answer

Midea appliances belong to Midea Group, a public company listed in Shenzhen and Hong Kong. The group's Smart Home business operates the Midea consumer brand and a wider appliance portfolio spanning major appliances, small appliances and floor care.

The Toshiba, Eureka and Teka relationships have different legal boundaries. Midea acquired 80.1% of Toshiba Lifestyle and long-term Toshiba appliance brand rights in 2016; acquired Eureka's North American vacuum business from Electrolux in 2016; and completed the Teka Group acquisition in 2025 while excluding Teka Rus. Midea's manufacturing scale is global, but the group chart does not identify the factory or legal responsibility for a specific product. Buyers and suppliers should verify the exact model, brand rights, manufacturer, factory, origin, local seller, software provider and warranty company.
