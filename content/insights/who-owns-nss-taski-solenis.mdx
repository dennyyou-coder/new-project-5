**NSS is owned by Solenis, through its TASKI floor-care business.** TASKI's timeline says it acquired NSS Enterprises, Inc. in 2023, and the NSS brand has joined the TASKI family. TASKI itself is a Solenis brand, which came with Solenis's 2023 acquisition of Diversey.

NSS is American, with more than 100 years of history in commercial floor care. It is based in Toledo, Ohio.

## Is NSS part of TASKI?

Yes. NSS joined the TASKI family in 2023, and its products now sit in TASKI's North American range. Read more in [who owns TASKI](/blog/who-owns-taski-solenis-diversey).

## Where are NSS machines made?

In Toledo, Ohio. The current NSS page says TASKI and NSS machines are built side by side there. In 2024 TASKI announced it would also produce Swiss-engineered TASKI floor-care machines at the 200,000-square-foot Toledo site.

## What does NSS make?

Walk-behind and ride-on scrubbers, floor machines and burnishers, commercial vacuums and extractors. Repairs go through authorised service centres.
