**Choose the Aiper EcoSurfer S2 when app scheduling, a published 4 L basket and 150-micron filtration are the priorities. Choose the Betta SE Plus when you prefer a simple handheld remote, a top-access basket, stated saltwater compatibility and a visible parts-and-service route.**

The better machine still depends on shade, pool currents, debris load and local warranty support. Neither product cleans the floor, walls or waterline.

Aiper’s current official name is **EcoSurfer S2**, although many shoppers still search for “Aiper Surfer S2.” This comparison uses the current EcoSurfer S2 specification.

## What a Robotic Pool Skimmer Actually Does

A surface skimmer intercepts material before it sinks. Its normal targets are leaves, pollen, insects, grass clippings, pet hair and other floating debris.

That job is different from the work of a [robotic pool cleaner](/blog/robotic-pool-cleaner-buying-guide). A floor-and-wall robot scrubs submerged surfaces and carries heavier debris through a separate filter system. A surface robot patrols the air-water boundary.

A surface skimmer also does not replace:

- the pool’s circulation pump and fixed skimmer;
- the main filter;
- sanitizer control and water testing;
- brushing, vacuuming or periodic deep cleaning.

The value is workload reduction. Removing material while it still floats can reduce the amount that later reaches the floor or main filtration system.

## Solar Charging and Cloudy-Weather Operation

Both products combine solar charging with an adapter. That is more practical than a solar-only design in a shaded pool or during several cloudy days.

### Aiper EcoSurfer S2

[Aiper’s official page](https://aiper.com/us/aiper-surfer-s2) lists 35 hours of battery life after adapter charging and a 4–6 hour charging time. Its SolarSeeker feature is described as moving the robot toward sunlight when the battery is low, taking a short solar charge and then resuming work.

That does not make the energy budget unlimited. Solar recovery depends on direct light, panel condition, water temperature, motor load and the time the robot spends in shade.

### Betta SE Plus

[Betta’s official page](https://bettabot.com/products/betta-se-plus) lists more than 30 hours of operation, five to six hours of direct-sun charging and about 3.5 hours with the adapter. The product therefore offers a shorter published adapter-charge time than Aiper, although the runtime figures were not produced under one common test.

Betta’s manual says solar charging occurs only when the power switch is on. That is a small operational detail, but it matters when an owner believes the unit has been charging while stored.

### What to test

Do not choose from the runtime headline alone. Run the candidate in the intended pool and observe:

1. morning and afternoon shade;
2. two days of normal debris;
3. the circulation pump at typical speed;
4. recovery after a cloudy period;
5. battery level before the next planned cleaning window.

For a dealer, this is a better demonstration than leaving both products in a clear, sunlit display pool.

## Debris Pickup, Basket and Filtration

Aiper publishes a 4 L pull-out basket and 150-micron filtration. Its DebrisGuard baffle is intended to reduce debris escape when the machine reverses or approaches the side.

Betta publishes a 200-micron fine-mesh basket with a top handle. The owner can bring the machine to the edge, open the top and lift the basket without removing the complete robot.

The numbers suggest different priorities, but the lower micron figure is not an automatic victory. Real collection depends on:

- the inlet width and approach speed;
- the seal around the filter;
- how quickly wet pollen loads the mesh;
- whether leaves bridge across the intake;
- debris loss during turns or retrieval.

A fine filter can capture smaller particles but may need more frequent rinsing. A larger opening can accept leaves but still perform poorly if material escapes during a turn.

The best comparison uses the pool’s actual debris. Test broad leaves, small insects and the fine material that usually accumulates near the fixed skimmer.

## Navigation, Pool Currents and Getting Stuck

Aiper lists two optical sensors, anti-stranding design and automatic self-rescue. Betta uses ultrasonic radar and a shallow-water safeguard.

Neither sensor description predicts behavior around every pool feature. A difficult surface environment can include:

- a strong return jet;
- suction near the fixed skimmer;
- a tanning ledge;
- an attached spa;
- a ladder or floating hose.

The circulation system can dominate the path. A return jet may repeatedly push a lightweight robot into one corner. The fixed skimmer can hold it against an opening. Shallow steps can restrict the draft available for turning.

Observe the entire cleaning cycle before deciding that a robot “covers” the pool. Coverage means reaching the debris zones repeatedly, not drawing an attractive path in a short demonstration.

Betta’s [SE Plus manual](https://bettabot.com/pages/betta-se-plus-smart-pool-skimmer-manual) warns against running the unit while another pool-cleaning device is in the water. It also says not to swim while the machine is operating. Follow the manual for the exact regional unit rather than assuming two robots or swimmers can share the pool safely.

## App Control or a Dedicated Remote

EcoSurfer S2 has the stronger software proposition on paper. Aiper lists app start and stop, scheduling, remote steering, battery monitoring and cleaning history.

That is useful when the owner wants a defined schedule or wants to check whether the robot completed a planned session. It also introduces account, connectivity and software-support dependencies.

Betta SE Plus uses a handheld remote. Betta’s current comparison table does not list app control for this model.

The simpler control can be an advantage for buyers who do not want another account or Wi-Fi setup. It is less useful when remote monitoring and automatic schedules are central to the purchase.

Dealers should demonstrate the exact control flow before sale. A customer expecting an app on SE Plus—or assuming the Aiper app works reliably beyond local connection conditions—may create an avoidable return.

## Chlorine, Saltwater and Water-Care Boundaries

Betta states that the SE Plus works in saltwater and chlorine pools. Its twin motors are marketed as salt-chlorine tolerant.

Aiper includes a holder for a three-inch chlorine tablet with adjustable flow. Treat that as a dispensing convenience, not a water-care system.

Neither feature replaces:

- measured free chlorine or sanitizer level;
- pH and alkalinity testing;
- circulation and filtration;
- chemical compatibility checks;
- a professional plan for unusual water conditions.

Verify the local manual before adding any chemical tablet. Concentrated sanitizer, material compatibility and storage practices can affect both safety and warranty.

## Cleaning and Maintenance

Surface robots work in UV, warm water and chemically treated environments. Their baskets also collect organic matter that can create odor and restrict flow.

After a heavy cycle:

1. park or retrieve the robot using the approved method;
2. empty the basket away from the pool;
3. rinse the basket with normal low-pressure water;
4. inspect the inlet, propellers and sensor windows;
5. allow the required parts to dry before adapter charging.

Betta specifically warns against pressure washing. The same conservative rule is sensible for seals, sensors and motor openings unless the manufacturer states otherwise.

Long-term ownership also depends on battery service, motor modules, filters, chargers and remote replacement. Ask how each part is supplied before the warranty expires.

## Warranty, Parts and Dealer Support

Betta’s US page states a one-year warranty and links to registration, claims, out-of-warranty service, accessories and dealer contact. That visibility is useful, but response time and stock still need local verification.

Aiper provides warranty-processing and repair-application portals. The applicable term should be confirmed for the seller, country and purchase date rather than inferred from another Aiper model.

For a retailer or distributor, request:

- written territory and channel terms;
- DOA and return procedures;
- spare baskets, chargers and control devices;
- battery and motor repair policy;
- response-time and freight responsibility;
- training material for pool-flow troubleshooting.

The first-year margin can disappear quickly if a dealer absorbs wet-product freight or replaces complete machines for basket, charger or motor faults.

## Which One Should You Buy?

### Choose Aiper EcoSurfer S2 when:

- app scheduling and status history matter;
- the published 4 L basket and 150-micron filter match the debris task;
- the pool has usable sunlight and SolarSeeker is valuable;
- a local Aiper service route is confirmed.

### Choose Betta SE Plus when:

- you prefer a dedicated remote and a simpler control path;
- top-access basket removal matters;
- stated saltwater compatibility is required;
- the published one-year warranty and parts-service structure are acceptable.

### Do not buy either one before testing when:

- the pool is heavily shaded;
- strong return jets dominate the surface;
- the fixed skimmer opening traps floating equipment;
- several robots or floating devices normally operate together;
- local battery, motor or charger support is unclear.

## FAQ

### Is Aiper Surfer S2 the same as Aiper EcoSurfer S2?

EcoSurfer S2 is the current official product name. “Aiper Surfer S2” remains a common search term and may appear in older listings or informal references.

### Which model has app control?

Aiper lists app start/stop, scheduling, remote steering and cleaning history for EcoSurfer S2. Betta’s current product comparison does not list app control for SE Plus; it uses a handheld remote.

### Which one has finer filtration?

Aiper publishes 150-micron filtration, while Betta publishes 200 microns. That difference does not establish total pickup performance because inlet design, sealing, loading and debris escape also matter.

### Can either robot clean the pool floor?

No. These are surface skimmers. Use a separate floor-and-wall cleaner for submerged surfaces.

### Can I swim while a robotic skimmer is running?

Follow the exact manual. Betta’s SE Plus manual says not to swim while the unit is operating and to remove it while the pool is in use.

### Can I run a skimmer with another pool robot?

Do not assume so. Betta’s manual says not to operate it while another pool-cleaning device is in the pool. Cords and floating equipment can also interfere with surface navigation.

### Which is better for a saltwater pool?

Betta explicitly states saltwater and chlorine-pool compatibility. For Aiper, verify the current regional manual and warranty before purchase.
