**Columbus is a Staehle family business.** The brand belongs to G. Staehle GmbH u. Co. KG, a Stuttgart family business that is still run by its owners. Its general partner, Staehle Verwaltungs GmbH, is managed by Jörg Peter Staehle and Marc Oliver Staehle. No parent company is named.

Columbus is German. The Staehle business dates to 1899, and Gustav Staehle created the first Columbus cleaning machine in 1926.

## Where are Columbus machines made?

In Stuttgart. Columbus says it develops and produces its cleaning machines in the heart of the city and has made single-disc machines there for more than 70 years, labelled Made in Germany.

## What does Columbus make?

Scrubber dryers, including ride-on machines since 2002; sweepers; industrial and commercial vacuums; spray extraction; single-disc, excenter and specialist machines. It launched the RA 40 automatic scrubber in 1976 and has offered a full range since 2010.

## How does Columbus sell?

Columbus sells internationally and offers consultation, quotations, demonstrations and service through its own contact system.
