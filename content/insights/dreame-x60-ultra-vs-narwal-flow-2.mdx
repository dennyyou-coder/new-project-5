**The Dreame X60 Ultra and Narwal Flow 2 take different routes to clean floors.** Dreame focuses on a very low body and strong suction, with mops washed in a hot-water dock. Narwal's Flow 2 uses a FlowWash track mop that circulates fresh water while it cleans. The choice depends on your furniture height and how much hard floor you have.

## Low furniture

At 7.95 cm the X60 Ultra reaches under much lower furniture than the 95 mm Flow 2. Measure your lowest sofa or bed before choosing.

## Mopping

The Flow 2's track is rinsed continuously, which suits large hard-floor homes. Narwal's official pages disagree on some temperature figures, so check the manual for your region.

## Which should you buy?

**X60 Ultra** for low clearance, mixed floors and strong suction. **Flow 2** for hard-floor homes that want continuously clean mopping and a possible plumbed station.
