Cleaning products are arriving in large numbers at this year's IFA. Robot vacuums keep adding rollers, steam and mechanical systems, while cordless vacuums and floor washers are putting more functions into their docks. Pool cleaners and robotic mowers are also attracting a growing number of indoor-cleaning brands.

Picking only the most eye-catching machines can obscure a company's broader product line. This roundup brings together the cleaning, pool and garden products we have collected, company by company, before examining the changes that make the new launches worth watching.

Information is current to September 6, 2026. Show debuts, returning products, technology prototypes and 2027 previews are identified separately. Appearance at the exhibition does not automatically make a product new.

## Roborock

![Roborock cleaning products with event photography; collage 01](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/02-roborock-floor-en.webp)

![Roborock cleaning products with event photography; collage 02](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/03-roborock-robots-outdoor-en.webp)

The robot vacuum lineup includes Saros 20 Flow Complete, Saros 20 Neo, and the previously unveiled Saros Rover and Saros Z70. Floor washers include F25 Ultra Steam Gen2 and F25 Pro Turbo Combo. Outdoors, Roborock is showing the RockAqua P1 pool cleaner and the RockNeo Q2 LiDAR and RockMow Z1 LiDAR robotic mowers.

Saros 20 Flow Complete and Saros 20 Neo are the main indoor-cleaning launches. The former uses a roller mop, while the latter retains dual rotating mop pads. Keeping both structures in the same premium generation gives households a choice: rollers offer continuous washing, while rotating pads retain an established design and user base.

F25 Ultra Steam Gen2 develops the steam-cleaning approach further, while F25 Pro Turbo Combo expands combination use. Beyond these regular upgrades, I am more interested in RockAqua P1, Roborock's first move into pool cleaning. Shallow platforms, the waterline and underwater navigation impose conditions quite different from indoor cleaning. Moving into this category involves much more than using robot-vacuum technology in a different location.

Saros Rover remains a prototype to watch; this IFA is not its first appearance. RockMow Z1 LiDAR was also unveiled at CES earlier this year.

## Dreame

![Dreame cleaning products with event photography; collage 03](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/04-dreame-robots-en.webp)

![Dreame cleaning products with event photography; collage 04](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/05-dreame-wet-dry-en.webp)

![Dreame cleaning products with event photography; collage 05](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/06-dreame-vacuums-en.webp)

![Dreame cleaning products with event photography; collage 06](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/07-dreame-prototypes-en.webp)

![Dreame cleaning products with event photography; collage 07](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/08-dreame-window-garden-en.webp)

Dreame's lineup is large enough to warrant separating the categories.

Robot vacuums and robotic floor washers include Aqua20 Pro Ultra Roller X Complete, X60 Pro Ultra Complete, X60 Pro Ultra Matrix, and the TriForm three-form floor-washing robot.

Handheld floor washers include T12 Pro, T14 Pro, T15 Pro Heat, T16 Pro Heat and T16 Pro Steam. This T-series range was announced in May and is being shown again for the European market.

Cordless vacuums include Z40 Pro, V40, Air Station and R20 Gen2, with bundle names varying by market. V40 Aqua Station is a concept product. Pano10 covers window cleaning, while the A4 AWD Pro mower comes in versions for different lawn sizes.

Other displays include the Cyber X stair-mobility platform, ECHO S1, ECHO P1, SmartSwap dry/wet roller switching and RollExtend roller-extension technology. These should not all be treated as standalone products that consumers can already buy.

Aqua20 Pro Ultra Roller X Complete combines steam cleaning with a roller mop to improve the removal of stubborn stains. As steam moves from handheld floor washers into robot vacuums, the questions concern actual stain removal, suitable flooring and total energy consumption. The advertised steam temperature should not be treated as the temperature at the floor surface.

The vacuums have different priorities. Z40 Pro emphasizes cleaning capability and handling; V40 focuses on dust-cup and hair management. Air Station combines a lightweight cleaner with a dust-collection dock, while R20 Gen2 continues to address conventional cordless-vacuum needs. V40 Aqua Station explores dock-based maintenance after dry and wet cleaning, but it should still be viewed as a concept.

TriForm's integration of the robot, manual cleaning and dock is more complete than the early idea of attaching a handle to a robot. I recognize the progress Dreame has made in product design, but I still do not expect this function to become a major reason for mass-market purchases. How often users really need to take over for a local stain, and whether accommodating manual operation compromises everyday automatic cleaning, remain open questions.

A4 AWD Pro belongs to the next stage of the product rollout. It is planned for Germany in the first quarter of 2027 and should not be described as already widely available this year.

## ECOVACS

![ECOVACS cleaning products with event photography; collage 08](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/09-ecovacs-floor-en.webp)

![ECOVACS cleaning products with event photography; collage 09](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/10-ecovacs-window-outdoor-en.webp)

The robot vacuum lineup includes X12S OmniCyclone, X12S Pro OMNI, X11S OmniCyclone and X11S Pro OMNI, alongside T90S PRO OMNI, T90 MAX PRO OMNI, T90S OMNI, T90 MAX OMNI and DEEBOT mini2.

Window-cleaning products include WINBOT W2S PRO OMNI, WINBOT W2S PRO and the previously released WINBOT W3 OMNI. Outdoor previews for 2027 include the GOAT T mower series and ULTRAMARINE L1 PRO pool robot.

The number of model names should not be read as nine entirely new platforms. The X, T and mini series address different positions, with dock and equipment variations creating further versions within each family.

The X12S roller-cleaning upgrade and OmniCyclone bagless dust collection deserve particular attention. One affects mopping; the other affects running costs and maintenance. For a company with a broad product line, these improvements matter more than simply adding another model number.

The WINBOT W2S PRO versions allow customers to choose whether they need an integrated workstation. W3 OMNI was released earlier this year, so its appearance here is not another debut. Further launch information is still needed for GOAT T and ULTRAMARINE L1 PRO.

## Tineco

![Tineco cleaning products with event photography; collage 10](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/11-tineco-new-en.webp)

![Tineco cleaning products with event photography; collage 11](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/12-tineco-existing-en.webp)

Key launches include FLOOR ONE S11 Artist Fusion Steam, FLOOR ONE S9 Artist Boost Steam, PURE ONE STATION 7 ZeroTouch, and the CARPET ONE Steam version displayed at this show.

The wider lineup includes FLOOR ONE STATION S9 Scientist Pro, FLOOR ONE STATION S9 Artist, FLOOR ONE i7 Fold, PURE ONE S70, PURE ONE STATION 5 Pro and PURE ONE A90S. Several were released previously and are not making their first appearance here.

S11 Artist Fusion Steam and S9 Artist Boost Steam extend steam floor washing. PURE ONE STATION 7 ZeroTouch concentrates on what happens after dust collection, adding automation to bag sealing, replacement and debris compaction.

Vacuum cleaners with self-emptying docks are familiar products, but airborne dust and contact with dirt during disposal still affect the experience. Investment in this part of ownership is easier for consumers to understand than another suction figure.

CARPET ONE Steam combines steam with carpet extraction. Removing the visible stain is only part of carpet cleaning: dirty-water recovery and drying time also matter. That is why this product needs to be assessed separately from a hard-floor washer.

## MOVA

![MOVA cleaning products with event photography; collage 12](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/13-mova-floor-en.webp)

![MOVA cleaning products with event photography; collage 13](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/14-mova-outdoor-tech-en.webp)

![MOVA cleaning products with event photography; collage 14](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/15-mova-research-en.webp)

Robot vacuums include V70 Ultra Complete and Z70 Ultra Roller Complete. Floor washers include the previously released M50 Ultra and X4 Pro, window cleaning is covered by T1 Station, and LiDAX Ultra Gen2 is the new mower.

Technology displays include the Knot80 dexterous hand and robotic arm, UltraTrim 3.0/4.0 edge-cutting systems, the AstraX garden-arm concept, TactiPercept tactile technology and the AquaSonar pool-communication system. Rover X10 is already part of the pool lineup and is not counted as a new launch at this show.

V70 Ultra Complete continues to use extending structures to reach beneath furniture and along edges, while Z70 Ultra Roller Complete develops roller mopping. The underlying household questions remain specific: can the machine reach the area, and is the mop clean enough when it touches the floor?

I am more interested in LiDAX Ultra Gen2's edge trimming and AquaSonar's communication improvements than in increasingly elaborate robotic-arm demonstrations. These functions may draw less attention on a stand, yet they could reduce the work users still have to do themselves. Arms, tactile surfaces and finished-product launches must also be distinguished. A technology demonstration does not mean the feature has entered mass production.

## Narwal

![Narwal cleaning products with event photography; collage 15](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/16-narwal-en.webp)

The lineup includes Freo 20, Freo 20 Edge, the white Flow 2, V50 Cube, and Essential and Flagship floor-washer previews for 2027.

Freo 20 brings track-style mopping to more product positions. The white Flow 2 mainly adds an appearance choice and should not be treated as a new platform.

What interests me is the extension of a cleaning structure previously concentrated at the premium end into a wider range. For consumers, getting the same mopping technology at a more accessible price is often more attractive than an extra flagship feature.

V50 Cube uses a compact dust-collection dock as Narwal continues into cordless vacuums. Its competition is closer to everyday handling, storage and the effort of use. The 2027 floor washers remain advance previews.

## iRobot

![iRobot cleaning products with event photography; collage 16](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/17-irobot-en.webp)

Products include Roomba Max 875 Combo, Roomba Plus 675 Combo, Roomba Plus 575 Combo and the Roomba Duo prototype.

Max 875 Combo is a recently announced product, with roller mopping and an AutoWash dock as its main changes. Together with the Plus series, it addresses different market positions.

Roomba Duo is the more striking proposal. A large robot handles broader floor areas while a smaller robot enters low-clearance spaces. The larger machine also serves as a mobile service station for the smaller one.

This attempts to combine stronger floor washing with access beneath low furniture, but it adds the coordination of two robots. Reliable cooperation and convenient maintenance must be demonstrated by a production product. It cannot yet be judged as a mature retail offering.

## DJI

![DJI cleaning products; collage 17](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/18-dji-en.webp)

The cleaning focus is the ROMO 2 series, making its European-market appearance.

For DJI, I would concentrate on perception and movement in complicated homes. Whether a robot can finish cleaning in narrow spaces, and whether temporary obstacles force the user to intervene, will determine how its technical background translates into a cleaning-product advantage.

A European appearance must also be separated from a global debut. A product's first display on a European stand does not reset its launch date.

## eufy

![eufy cleaning products; collage 18](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/19-eufy-en.webp)

Indoor products include Omni E35, Omni C30 and Omni C30 Edge. Garden products include E15 Gen 2 and the S2 Max preview for 2027.

Omni E35 follows the HydroJet roller-cleaning approach. The C30 series uses dual rotating mop pads, with C30 Edge placing further emphasis on low-clearance access and edges. These are differences in cleaning structure; the model numbers alone do not explain the hierarchy.

E35's roller and dust handling deserve attention, while the C30 series retains a relatively mature mopping approach. Keeping both routes is a practical choice for a brand that needs sales across different price bands.

The mower range is also expanding across product tiers, although S2 Max remains a future-product preview.

## Kärcher

![Kärcher cleaning products with event photography; collage 19](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/20-karcher-en.webp)

Products include the RVF 5 Comfort robot vacuum, FCV 5 Agile and FCV 6 Agile floor washers, SE 2 Spot Active Heat spot cleaner, and SC 2 Deluxe Compact steam cleaner.

RVF 5 Comfort uses roller mopping and adds edge cleaning and stain-directed treatment. Kärcher has a long history in professional and conventional cleaning equipment. Consumers will naturally expect that experience to translate into cleaning results as it enters another robotic category.

The FCV series focuses on cleaning beneath low furniture, SE 2 Spot Active Heat adds heated cleaning, and SC 2 Deluxe Compact emphasizes compact storage. The range remains organized around different surfaces and types of dirt, with fairly clear divisions between the products' tasks.

## Miele

![Miele cleaning products with event photography; collage 20](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/21-miele-en.webp)

Duoflex HX2 is the main product. It was announced at the IFA preview event in June, with European availability planned for spring 2027.

Duoflex HX2 offers attachments and arrangements around vacuuming, wet cleaning and dock maintenance, including AquaTurbo, AutoCut and AutoClean. These functions depend on the package; optional capabilities should not all be presented as standard equipment.

The existing Triflex HX3 Plus Aqua provides context for the product line. Its inclusion in a research dossier does not establish it as a new launch at this show.

With premium cordless products, I am more interested in whether adding wet cleaning preserves easy handling, and how much maintenance the dock actually removes. A comprehensive feature list does not necessarily make everyday use easier.

## BISSELL

![BISSELL cleaning products; collage 21](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/22-bissell-en.webp)

New products include PowerClean DualBrush, PowerClean DualBrush Pro and CrossWave Reach Plus, alongside the previously introduced DryVac Tool accessory.

PowerClean DualBrush strengthens dual-brush cleaning, making hair and carpet performance worth examining. CrossWave Reach Plus continues to improve edge coverage.

DryVac Tool adds dry vacuuming to selected SpotClean machines, rather than every model. Using an accessory to extend equipment already owned may be easier to justify than buying another appliance, provided it does not make operation significantly less convenient.

## UWANT

![UWANT cleaning products with event photography; collage 22](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/23-uwant-en.webp)

Products include DX800 Steam, the existing V800, and the forthcoming SwiftWash N600 and SwiftVac V700 Pro.

DX800 Steam continues to combine steam and hot-water cleaning. SwiftWash N600 changes the arrangement of the water tank and cleaning head, while SwiftVac V700 Pro explores a cleaning structure different from a conventional brush roll.

UWANT is changing product structures as it enters more European channels. Whether the tank layout feels lighter in use and the new head handles hair easily needs practical testing. For European consumers encountering the brand for the first time, intuitive operation and convenient maintenance will matter more than technology names.

## Bosch

![Bosch cleaning products with event photography; collage 23](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/24-bosch-en.webp)

The cleaning focus is BCRI3BX1, the built-in robot vacuum developed with ECOVACS.

Its dock fits into a kitchen-cabinet system with fixed water supply and drainage, reducing the presence of a freestanding dock in the living room. The product appeared previously and entered the sales stage in 2026. It is not making its debut at this IFA.

The purchase occasion differs from that of a conventional robot vacuum: it fits more naturally into kitchen planning or renovation. Space integration is its advantage, while installation and later replacement are its constraints. A freestanding product is usually easier to buy and install in a finished home.

## AEG

![AEG cleaning products; collage 24](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/25-aeg-en.webp)

Cleaning products include ErgoMini and Trilobite 8000 GEN 2.

ErgoMini is a lightweight handheld vacuum that also offers functions such as blowing. It appeared at the June IFA preview event. Trilobite 8000 GEN 2 continues to address automatic floor cleaning.

As more brands emphasize large, multifunctional docks, a small, easy-to-pick-up machine such as ErgoMini deserves attention. A tabletop, sofa or freshly dropped crumbs do not always call for a full-size floor-cleaning appliance.

## Midea

![Midea cleaning products with event photography; collage 25](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/26-midea-en.webp)

AT8 Ultra is the principal cleaning launch verified for this roundup.

It uses EasyFoam foam cleaning, hot water and edge treatment to improve floor cleaning. The value of foam must be judged against actual dirt, alongside detergent consumption and residue left after washing.

Existing products from other brands in the Midea group should not automatically be added to the AT8 Ultra launch lineup simply because they share a parent company.

## xLean

![xLean cleaning products; collage 39](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/27-xlean-en.webp)

The focus is TR1 and the accompanying EvoMate OS upgrade.

TR1's dual-form design appeared at IFA 2025. This year's changes are more about software and interaction, including arranging cleaning tasks through natural language. The same hardware concept should not be described as appearing for the first time again.

My view of this type of product has not changed. Combining automatic and manual cleaning has some value, but frequency of use determines whether consumers will pay for it. Software can make operation easier; it cannot supply the underlying demand.

## Aiper

![Aiper cleaning products; collage 26](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/28-aiper-pool-en.webp)

![Aiper cleaning products; collage 27](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/29-aiper-irrigation-en.webp)

Pool products covered by the collected material include Scuba V3 Ultra, Scuba V3, Scuba S3 and Scuba X1 Pro Max, together with the EcoSurfer Senti and EcoSurfer S2 surface cleaners.

The garden range includes IrriSense 2, IrriSense 2 SE, Smart Gateway, a soil sensor, a weather station and solar-power components. Together they form the Smart Yard display. They are not all first-time launches at this show.

IrriSense 2 SE is an addition worth watching. Aiper is extending its business from pool cleaning into irrigation, bringing weather and soil information into routine garden management.

The value of this combination depends on whether the devices actually reduce manual adjustment. A pool and a lawn may share a backyard, but putting their controls in the same app does not by itself create useful cooperation.

## Beatbot

![Beatbot cleaning products; collage 28](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/30-beatbot-en.webp)

The focus is Sora 70 and AquaSense X, both of which appeared around CES earlier this year.

Sora 70 covers surface and underwater cleaning. AquaSense X extends post-cleaning maintenance through its AstroRinse dock. Their IFA appearance continues the promotion of this year's products.

Users often still need to clean a pool robot's filter basket after a cycle. AquaSense X invests in a task with a clear practical need. Whether that justifies a more complicated dock depends on maintenance frequency, footprint and purchase cost.

## WYBOT

![WYBOT cleaning products; collage 29](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/31-wybot-en.webp)

The show products that can be clearly identified include S3 and the new M3. Other model names appearing in reports should not be merged with M3 until their identities are verified.

M3 strengthens underwater debris pickup, filtration and sensing, while S3 places emphasis on automatic waste discharge.

These address different parts of ownership. M3 focuses on cleaning inside the pool; S3 focuses on the work left to the user afterward. Competition is extending into post-cleaning maintenance, which is more interesting than another increase in single-cycle runtime.

## Navimow

![Navimow cleaning products with event photography; collage 30](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/32-navimow-en.webp)

The focus is H5 Pro and the previously released X4 series.

H5 Pro adds an extending cutting structure, all-wheel drive and multiple positioning methods. It is planned for early 2027. X4 appeared at CES this year and continues to be displayed.

Robotic mowers often handle most of a lawn well while leaving the fence line to the user. H5 Pro's attention to the edge has direct value, but turning damage to the grass also needs observation. Reaching the edge and protecting the lawn must be achieved together to reduce work meaningfully.

## MAMMOTION

![MAMMOTION cleaning products; collage 31](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/33-mammotion-en.webp)

Residential previews include LUBA 4 AWD 3000F, 6000F and 12000F for 2027. The commercial direction includes Maston Rotary and Maston Flail.

LUBA 4 AWD continues to develop mobility and positioning on difficult terrain. Edge-trimming attachments must be matched to specific versions; support should not be assumed across the entire series.

Maston takes the business further into professional grounds maintenance. Continuous operation, equipment servicing and fleet management change the purchase criteria from those of a home garden. A stable service system will directly affect subsequent buying decisions.

## Sunseeker

![Sunseeker cleaning products with event photography; collage 32](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/34-sunseeker-en.webp)

The 2027 products include S7 LiDAR Ultra AWD 3000, S5 LiDAR Ultra Pro AWD 1800 and the S3 LiDAR series. Published family names must be distinguished from regional model names. Unconfirmed variants should not be filled in by guesswork.

Software and sensing displays include Smart Garden, MapOS and AllSense 3.0. Related new products are planned to start arriving in February 2027.

The series address different gardens through their LiDAR and mobility configurations. The more interesting development is a three-dimensional semantic map intended to distinguish areas of the garden and assign more suitable work.

Reliable operation could reduce the burden of setting up a map. Moving from recognizing areas to coordinating irrigation and other equipment still requires implementation in subsequent products.

## ANTHBOT

![ANTHBOT cleaning products; collage 33](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/35-anthbot-en.webp)

The focus is the R7 series, with the EdgeEase extending trimming structure, LiDAR, vision and all-wheel drive.

It also concentrates on lawn edges to reduce the user's follow-up trimming. Irregular flower beds, spaces beneath fences and changes in height will test the mechanism more effectively than a regular paved border.

A launch demonstration can establish that the mechanism moves. Sustained performance in domestic gardens needs actual use. Regional R7 versions and availability should also follow the relevant local announcements.

## Yarbo

![Yarbo cleaning products; collage 34](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/36-yarbo-en.webp)

Products include M20 and M20i in the M series, alongside the existing Y series.

Modules cover mowing, snow removal and collection, with trimming and Smart Assist among the further extensions. The M-series hardware on display advances the prototypes seen at CES.

Yarbo wants one base to handle garden work across seasons. Sharing a base may improve utilization for a household that needs both mowing and snow removal. The rationale for a modular system weakens when only one module does most of the work throughout the year.

The more compact M series also expands the range of homes that might accommodate this approach.

## Lymow

![Lymow cleaning products; collage 35](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/37-lymow-en.webp)

Products include the previously released One Plus and a newly shown two-in-one blower and trimmer.

One Plus continues the robotic-mowing approach, while the new handheld tool covers supplementary manual work. The tool is planned for 2027.

This combination is close to how gardens are actually maintained. A robot can handle routine mowing while leaves and local edges still require attention. Making the supplementary tool easier to carry has value too; every task does not have to be performed by the same robot.

## NexLawn

![NexLawn cleaning products with event photography; collage 36](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/38-nexlawn-en.webp)

Products include AURION, NAVIA PRO AWD, Vidar 1200 AWD, Vidar 2000 AWD and Vayor 600. Related new products are planned to arrive progressively in the first half of 2027.

AURION addresses larger areas and professional applications. NAVIA PRO AWD focuses on edge trimming, while Vidar and Vayor cover different sizes of domestic lawn.

The lineup extends from small gardens to larger operations. Runtime, mobility and maintenance requirements vary with area. Each series will need a clear use case so that buyers do not have to rely only on model names and area ratings.

## TerraMow

![TerraMow cleaning products; collage 37](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/39-terramow-en.webp)

X AWD is the main product here. Mini 500 was released previously and serves as a reference for the small-garden range; it is not counted as a new launch at this show.

X AWD combines multiple cameras, network positioning and all-wheel drive. Bidirectional travel reduces some turning maneuvers.

Fewer turns may reduce repeated tire traffic on the grass, particularly in confined areas. That still depends on route planning in real gardens. The drive mechanism alone cannot establish the final result.

## Dyson

![Dyson cleaning products with event photography; collage 38](/images/articles/ifa-2026-cleaning-products-company-roundup/40-dyson-en.webp)

Dyson held a separate launch event in Berlin alongside IFA. This needs to be distinguished from a launch on an exhibition stand inside the show.

Products include R3 Nurovi Spot+Scrub UV, R2 Nurovi Wash+Dry, R1 Nurovi Dry and W1 PencilWash+Dok.

R3 uses wet roller cleaning, with ultraviolet and green illumination to assist stain detection. UV here should not be interpreted directly as ultraviolet disinfection. R2 uses a different mopping structure, R1 concentrates on dry cleaning, and W1 combines a lightweight floor washer with a self-cleaning dock.

Dyson has retained dry cleaning and different wet-cleaning approaches across its robots. I am more interested in whether each structure produces a clear advantage in use: a dry-only machine needs to be sufficiently simple, while wet-cleaning products need to show that stain removal and maintenance justify their added complexity.
