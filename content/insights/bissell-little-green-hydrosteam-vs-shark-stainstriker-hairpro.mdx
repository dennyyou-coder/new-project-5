**Choose the BISSELL Little Green HydroSteam Pet 3618 when oily or sticky stains and a real Steam, SteamWash or Wash process justify the larger machine. Choose the Shark StainStriker HairPro PX251 when embedded pet hair, lighter carrying weight, dual-solution stain treatment and hair-separated recovery matter more than heat.**

The two portable cleaners solve different difficult tasks. BISSELL adds a heater and steam modes. Shark keeps the machine lighter and builds the tool and recovery path around wet and dry pet hair.

## Confirm the Exact Models

BISSELL sells several HydroSteam variants. Model 3618 is the Little Green HydroSteam Pet. Model 3532 shares the main machine architecture but has different listing details and tools. A retailer should not merge the 3-inch and 5-inch tool bundles.

Shark StainStriker also has several versions. PX251 is HairPro. The lower-priced PX201 standard StainStriker does not include the same hair-focused recovery system and bundle.

Before purchase, check:

- model number on machine and carton;
- included stain and hair tools;
- included solution sizes;
- voltage and plug;
- warranty country;
- replacement hose, tank, inserts and formulas;
- whether a retailer-exclusive package changes accessories.

The earlier [standard Shark StainStriker vs BISSELL Little Green comparison](/blog/shark-stainstriker-vs-bissell-little-green) addresses the base families. This article focuses on the higher-differentiation HydroSteam and HairPro models.

## Heat vs Dual-Activated Chemistry

BISSELL 3618 uses a flow-through heater with a temperature sensor. It heats the water and formula to create steam. The machine then gives the operator three routes:

- **SteamWash** combines steam, formula and extraction for oily or sticky soils;
- **Steam** refreshes suitable upholstery and can address approved grout or tile tasks;
- **Wash** uses spray and extraction for ordinary spills, pet accidents and vehicle interiors.

BISSELL claims 50% better oily-stain cleaning in SteamWash under its test. That does not mean every stain improves by 50%, and steam can be wrong for heat-sensitive materials.

Shark does not heat the solution. StainStriker uses two formulas: Deep Clean Pet and OXY Multiplier. The system combines them in the handheld cleaning process rather than requiring the user to pre-mix one tank. Shark says the activated chemistry attacks stains and odor without heat or pre-treatment under its conditions.

Heat and chemistry are not interchangeable:

- oily, waxy or sticky soil can respond to controlled heat;
- oxidizing chemistry can help with compatible organic color and odor;
- protein, dye, ink, adhesive and natural-fiber stains may need different limits;
- unknown stains can set or spread if treated incorrectly.

Identify the stain before choosing the strongest mode.

## Fresh Spills vs Old Stains

For a fresh spill:

1. remove solids without grinding them in;
2. blot excess liquid;
3. test material and colorfastness;
4. use the least aggressive approved method;
5. extract slowly;
6. repeat dry passes.

For an old stain, take a photo and record what prior chemistry was used. Household cleaners can leave surfactant, bleach, solvent or soap residue that reacts with a new solution.

HydroSteam has a clearer advantage when an old oily or sticky stain is on a steam-safe synthetic surface. HairPro has a clearer advantage when the stain zone also contains embedded fur that would clog an ordinary tool.

Neither machine guarantees removal of permanent dye transfer, bleach damage, fiber discoloration or material degradation.

## Pet Hair: Shark’s Main Differentiator

HairPro is not simply a stain tool with a different name. Shark designs the recovery path to manage hair:

- hair-gripping fins loosen embedded fur;
- a Hair Extractor collects fur from the surface;
- separation keeps large hair masses out of the ordinary dirty-water path;
- a collection cup makes disposal easier.

This matters on pet beds, sofa cushions, carpeted stairs and vehicle upholstery. Wet hair can form a felt-like plug in a narrow nozzle or hose.

BISSELL HydroSteam can extract hair that enters the tool, but its principal differentiation is heat, not a dedicated hair-separation architecture. Vacuum loose dry hair before wet cleaning with either machine. This reduces clogging and stops fur from becoming pasted into fabric by moisture.

Test HairPro with the actual coat type. Short stiff fur behaves differently from long soft undercoat. After extraction, inspect the fins, cup, tool throat, hose inlet and dirty tank.

## Water Recovery and Drying

Portable spot cleaners apply liquid and then recover part of it. A visually cleaner surface can still be too wet.

BISSELL says upholstery can dry in about 25 minutes in Steam Mode on new polyester under its test. That result is limited to a specific mode and material. Wash or SteamWash, thicker foam, humidity and repeated spraying can take much longer.

Shark does not publish an equivalent dry-time number on the PX251 page. Its lower weight and strong suction claim do not by themselves establish lower residual moisture.

Use a repeatable method:

- weigh a removable test cushion before cleaning if practical;
- apply the same number of wet passes;
- make slow extraction-only passes until little water is visible;
- weigh or assess moisture at fixed intervals;
- provide ventilation;
- do not replace cushions or use vehicle seats while damp.

Over-wetting can move soil deeper, leave rings, weaken adhesive and create odor. More solution is not automatically more cleaning.

## Tanks and Job Size

BISSELL publishes a 64 oz clean tank and 51 oz dirty tank. Those capacities allow a meaningful spot-cleaning session but also add weight when full.

Shark’s current PX251 product page does not publish tank capacities. Do not import a tank figure from PX201, PX255 or a retailer listing without matching the exact model.

For either product, usable job size depends on:

- surface area;
- spray rate;
- number of rinse passes;
- how much soil and foam fill the dirty tank;
- whether the machine must cool or be cleaned;
- how far the hose reaches without moving the base.

For retail demonstration, fill both with the amount used in a normal job and carry them up one flight of stairs. Empty the dirty tanks into a sink. Handling full tanks is more revealing than dry shipping weight.

## Portability, Cord and Hose

Shark weighs 8.51 lb, substantially less than BISSELL’s listed 13 lb. That favors vehicle work, stairs and frequent spot response.

BISSELL provides a 15 ft cord and 4.8 ft hose. Shark provides a 12 ft cord and 4.5 ft hose. BISSELL offers slightly more reach, but the base is heavier.

Check:

- carry-handle comfort;
- stability while pulling the hose;
- cord and hose storage;
- stair placement;
- ability to reach the middle of a vehicle;
- tank removal without dripping;
- whether the machine tips when the hose is stretched.

Never balance a portable extractor on a stair tread if the manual does not show safe placement. Keep the base level and below the work area where possible.

## Tools and Surface Access

BISSELL 3618 includes a 5-inch Tough Stain Tool and Multi-Surface Tool. The wider tool can cover upholstery efficiently; the multi-surface accessory extends the machine beyond carpet-only use where approved.

Shark PX251 includes a mini stain tool, 3.5-inch reversible-bristle tool, Hair Extractor and additional insert. The narrower tools suit seat bolsters, stair edges and localized hair.

Tool width affects both speed and extraction pressure. A wide nozzle covers more area, while a narrow nozzle can concentrate airflow and fit contours.

Test:

- flat sofa cushion;
- vertical back;
- piping and seams;
- stair nosing;
- car-seat bolster;
- pet-bed corner;
- a small hard-surface grout area only if the manual approves it.

Hair and debris accumulate at transparent nozzle lips. Choose a tool that opens or rinses easily.

## Material and Fabric Limits

Do not interpret “multi-surface” as “all materials.”

Before cleaning:

- read the furniture or carpet care label;
- confirm water-based extraction is allowed;
- check the machine manual and formula label;
- test a hidden area for color transfer, texture and ring formation;
- protect nearby wood, electronics, metal and adhesive seams.

Exercise extra caution with:

- silk, velvet and delicate natural fibers;
- leather and suede;
- vintage or unstable dyes;
- foam-backed fabrics and laminated headliners;
- mattresses and thick cushions that cannot dry quickly;
- unsealed wood or stone;
- heat-sensitive adhesive and coated fabrics.

Steam adds another compatibility gate. Use it only where the surface manufacturer and BISSELL instructions permit.

## Formula and Odor

Shark’s dual-solution system is central to PX251. Use the intended Deep Clean Pet and OXY Multiplier at the specified positions and amounts. Adding ordinary detergent can create foam, reduce recovery and damage components.

BISSELL also requires approved compact-machine formulas and its mode instructions. Steam does not make chemical compatibility irrelevant.

For pet odor:

- remove solids and bulk liquid first;
- treat the full contamination area, not only the visible mark;
- avoid saturating foam or subfloor;
- rinse/extract according to instructions;
- dry fully;
- recognize that deep contamination may require professional treatment or replacement.

No portable machine can guarantee odor removal when urine has penetrated cushion foam, carpet pad, wood or vehicle insulation.

Keep animals and children away until the cleaned surface is fully dry and the formula label’s directions are satisfied.

## Cleaning the Machine After Use

The maintenance routine determines whether the next job smells clean.

For both machines:

1. empty dirty water promptly;
2. rinse the dirty tank and lid;
3. flush or rinse the hose and tool as the manual directs;
4. remove hair from nozzle, fins, seals and float;
5. inspect clean-water and solution valves;
6. let parts air-dry fully before closed storage;
7. wipe the base and cord;
8. store without dirty solution in the recovery system.

BISSELL’s support portal has separate guides for leaking, no steam, low spray and suction. A retailer should demonstrate heater warm-up, steam-safe mode selection and post-use cleaning.

Shark promotes onboard self-cleaning in its HairPro category material. Confirm the exact PX251 procedure and understand that a flush cycle does not clean every hair cup, tank seal or tool crevice.

Never run unapproved vinegar, bleach, solvent, essential oil or high-foam detergent through either machine.

## Consumables and Long-Term Cost

HydroSteam ownership includes approved formula, brushes/tools and heater/water-system care. Hard water may affect the heated path; follow BISSELL’s water instructions rather than improvising a descaling chemical.

HairPro ownership includes two solution types plus removable inserts and hair tools. Shark sells the OXY formula, chemical bundles and replacement tools separately. Availability can change.

Calculate:

- formula cost per job;
- shipping and local stock;
- replacement tool/inserts;
- hose and tank parts;
- warranty length and exclusions;
- cleaning time;
- revenue lost when a specific formula is out of stock.

For a retailer, recurring formulas can be valuable only if customers can replenish them reliably.

## Home, Auto and Retail Decision

Choose BISSELL 3618 when:

- oily and sticky stains are common;
- steam-safe upholstery or hard surfaces are part of the task;
- 64/51 oz tanks and longer reach suit the job;
- 13 lb carrying weight is acceptable;
- the user will follow material and heater maintenance instructions.

Choose Shark PX251 when:

- pet hair is embedded in fabric or carpet;
- frequent carrying to stairs or vehicles matters;
- dual-activated formula fits the stain program;
- dedicated hair collection reduces cleanup;
- steam is unnecessary or unsafe for the target materials.

Retailers can stock both without unnecessary overlap: position HydroSteam by heat-compatible stain task and HairPro by pet-hair recovery. Train staff not to claim that steam is always stronger or that chemical activation works on every material.

## Final Decision

The HydroSteam 3618 is the more capable stain-treatment platform when controlled heat is appropriate. Its cost is more weight, greater surface risk if misused and a more complex heated water path.

The StainStriker HairPro PX251 is the more specialized pet-hair platform. It is lighter and separates hair before it becomes a wet clog, but it cannot provide a true steam process.

For either model, good technique matters:

- dry vacuum first;
- identify the stain;
- test a hidden area;
- use approved formula;
- avoid over-wetting;
- make extra dry passes;
- clean and dry the machine immediately.

## FAQ

### Is Little Green HydroSteam better than StainStriker HairPro?

HydroSteam is better for compatible oily or sticky stains that benefit from heat. HairPro is better for embedded pet hair and lighter portability. Neither is universally better.

### Does Shark StainStriker HairPro use steam?

No. PX251 uses a dual-activated solution system and suction, not a heater or steam.

### Does BISSELL HydroSteam pick up pet hair?

It can recover loose material through its tools, but it does not have Shark’s dedicated HairPro separator and collection cup. Dry-vacuum hair before wet cleaning.

### Which machine is lighter?

Shark PX251 is listed at 8.51 lb; BISSELL 3618 at 13 lb. Filled tanks increase carried weight.

### Which has larger tanks?

BISSELL publishes 64 oz clean and 51 oz dirty capacity. Shark’s current PX251 product page does not list tank capacities, so verify the exact unit rather than borrowing another StainStriker figure.

### Can I use steam on every sofa?

No. Check the upholstery manufacturer, care label and BISSELL manual. Heat and moisture can damage delicate fibers, dyes, coatings, foam and adhesive.

### How do I make upholstery dry faster?

Use controlled spray, make several slow extraction-only passes, provide airflow and avoid saturating foam. Do not use the item until fully dry.

### Can either machine remove pet urine odor permanently?

They can treat compatible surface contamination using approved formulas. Odor may remain when urine has reached foam, padding, wood or insulation; deep contamination may require professional work or replacement.
