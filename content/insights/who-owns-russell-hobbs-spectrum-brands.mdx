**Russell Hobbs is owned by Empower Brands, the home and personal care business of Spectrum Brands.** Spectrum Brands Holdings, Inc., a US-listed consumer products company headquartered in Middleton, Wisconsin, has owned the Russell Hobbs business since 2010. In May 2026 funds managed by Oaktree Capital Management invested in the home and personal care business, taking about 27%, with Spectrum keeping the rest. That business now trades as Empower Brands, alongside Remington, George Foreman, PowerXL and BLACK+DECKER household appliances, which it sells under licence.

Russell Hobbs is a British brand. Its product history goes back to 1952, and its European business is run from Manchester.

## Is Russell Hobbs still British?

The brand is British in origin, but its owner is American. In Europe, Spectrum Brands (UK) Limited is named as the seller and legal manufacturer for covered products, and Russell Hobbs Deutschland GmbH is the EU authorised representative.

## Where are Russell Hobbs products made?

Spectrum Brands' fiscal 2025 annual report says substantially all products in its Home and Personal Care segment, which includes Russell Hobbs, are made by third-party suppliers in the Asia-Pacific region. Spectrum owns most of the tooling and moulds those suppliers use.

## What does Russell Hobbs make?

Kettles, toasters and other beverage appliances, food preparation and cooking appliances, and irons and garment care. They are sold through large retailers, online, wholesalers and specialty shops.

## Is Empower Brands buying Helen of Troy?

Empower Brands has proposed it. On November 4, 2026, it made public a non-binding offer of USD 37 per share in cash for Helen of Troy, the owner of OXO, Hydro Flask and Osprey, valuing that company at about USD 1.6 billion including debt. Helen of Troy's management had called the offer inadequate. No agreement has been signed, so Russell Hobbs' ownership does not change for now.
