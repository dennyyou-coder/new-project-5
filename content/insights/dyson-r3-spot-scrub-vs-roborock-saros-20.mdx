**Dyson's R3 Nurovi Spot+Scrub UV and Roborock's Saros 20 approach the same job differently.** Dyson builds around finding stains: a UV and AI scanner checks the floor and a heated roller treats what it finds. Roborock's Saros 20 focuses on reaching everywhere, with a low body and a lifting chassis.

## Stains

The R3's UV stain scanner and heated roller are built for kitchens and spills. Dyson's UV is for detecting stains, not disinfecting the floor.

## Reach

The Saros 20 is the better climber and fits under lower furniture.

## Which should you buy?

**Dyson R3** for hard-floor homes where stains are the main problem and you want a bagless dock. **Saros 20** for homes with low furniture, rugs and tall thresholds. Check Dyson availability and price in your market; they vary.
