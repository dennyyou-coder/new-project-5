**Haier-branded home appliances are operated by Haier Smart Home Co., Ltd., a publicly listed appliance company controlled within the wider Haier Group.** Haier Smart Home is the central listed platform behind the Haier appliance brand and a multinational portfolio that includes Casarte, Leader, GE Appliances, Fisher & Paykel, AQUA and Candy.

This does not mean every portfolio brand has been collapsed into one factory or one sales company. GE Appliances, Fisher & Paykel and Candy retain distinct operating histories, regional organizations and product responsibilities. Trademark ownership, company ownership, manufacturing and warranty must be checked separately.

## What Is the Difference Between Haier Group and Haier Smart Home?

**Haier Group** is the wider corporate group. **Haier Smart Home Co., Ltd.** is the listed company that operates the principal global home-appliance business.

Haier Smart Home's corporate filings state that Haier Group controls the listed company directly and indirectly. The listed company trades in Shanghai, Hong Kong and Frankfurt under separate market codes. This structure makes “Haier is owned by Haier” an incomplete answer: the appliance operator, listed issuer and controlling group need to be named at the correct level.

For product and procurement questions, Haier Smart Home is usually the relevant starting point. For ultimate control or group-wide strategy, Haier Group is the broader reference. A local contract may then name a regional subsidiary or individual brand company instead of either parent.

## Which Appliance Brands Does Haier Smart Home Own?

Haier Smart Home describes a multi-brand global portfolio serving different markets and price positions:

- **Haier** is the global mass-market master brand.
- **Casarte** is positioned as a premium home-appliance brand, especially in China.
- **Leader** targets younger and connected-lifestyle segments in China.
- **GE Appliances** is a major U.S. appliance company acquired in 2016.
- **Fisher & Paykel** is a premium New Zealand-origin appliance brand acquired in 2012.
- **AQUA** operates in Japan and selected Asian markets.
- **Candy** is the European appliance group acquired in 2019 and includes regional brand assets such as Candy and Hoover.

The portfolio gives Haier Smart Home broad coverage across refrigeration, laundry, cooking, dishwashing, air treatment and cleaning-related categories. It also makes brand-level due diligence more important. A Haier refrigerator, GE Appliances washer and Fisher & Paykel dishwasher may ultimately sit within the same listed group while using different design centers, factories, sales entities and service systems.

## Does Haier Own GE Appliances?

Yes. Haier completed the acquisition of **GE Appliances** in June 2016. The transaction moved the U.S. appliance business into Haier's global appliance platform while GE Appliances kept its headquarters in Louisville, Kentucky.

The familiar GE brand creates an important legal distinction. The 2016 transaction announcement said GE Appliances would continue using the GE Appliances brand under a long-term trademark license, with an initial 40-year term including extensions. Therefore, company ownership and trademark ownership are not identical.

GE Appliances continues to operate as a U.S.-centered business with its own leadership, brands, plants, distribution and service relationships. Haier ownership should not be used to relabel every GE Appliances product as “made in China.” The country of origin remains a model-level fact.

## Does Haier Own Fisher & Paykel?

Yes. Haier completed its acquisition of **Fisher & Paykel Appliances** at the end of 2012 after building an earlier strategic relationship.

Fisher & Paykel remains a distinct premium appliance identity with its own product design language and international organization. Haier's current corporate page for the business describes operations across dozens of countries and a manufacturing network spanning multiple regions.

This is a useful example of portfolio ownership without complete brand erasure. A supplier should identify whether an opportunity belongs to Haier Smart Home centrally, Fisher & Paykel's operating company or a named factory program. The owner at the top of the chart may not be the contracting party.

## Does Haier Own Candy and Hoover?

Haier completed the acquisition of **Candy S.p.A.** in January 2019. The deal expanded Haier's European position and added Candy's appliance operations and brands to Haier Smart Home's portfolio.

Hoover requires a regional answer. Candy's business includes Hoover-branded appliances in Europe, while **Techtronic Industries, or TTI, owns Hoover in the Americas**. The same wordmark can therefore lead to different parent companies, websites, factories and warranties depending on territory.

Our detailed [Hoover ownership guide](/blog/who-owns-hoover-tti-haier-candy) explains that split. Buyers should never use a European Hoover page to infer ownership or service responsibility for a U.S. Hoover product, or vice versa.

## Where Are Haier Appliances Made?

Haier Smart Home uses a global, localized production and sales model. Its corporate reporting describes a strategy often summarized as serving local markets with local research, manufacturing and marketing capabilities.

That network includes facilities associated with Haier-branded products and with acquired businesses such as GE Appliances, Fisher & Paykel and Candy. Production in China is important, but the portfolio also manufactures appliances in North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific and other regions.

As a result, “Haier appliances are made in China” is too broad. Some Haier-branded models are made in China, while others can be produced closer to their destination market. The broader portfolio is even more varied: a GE Appliances product assembled in the United States and a Fisher & Paykel product made at another regional site still sit within Haier Smart Home.

The reliable evidence is the individual product's rating plate, carton marking, declaration of conformity and importer documentation. A group annual report can establish the network but not the origin of a specific SKU.

## Does Haier Manufacture Every Appliance in Its Portfolio?

Haier Smart Home is a major manufacturer, yet ownership of a brand does not prove that every product is assembled in a Haier-branded plant.

Different portfolio companies control their own product programs. Final assembly may take place in a company-owned plant, while components or selected products come from approved external suppliers. Manufacturing assignments can also change as factories specialize, capacity moves or product generations are updated.

For B2B qualification, ask which operating company owns the program, which factory makes the exact model, whether the factory is owned or contracted, and which entity signs the quality agreement. These questions are more useful than asking only who owns the brand.

## How Is Haier Connected to Cleaning Appliances?

Haier Smart Home's appliance ecosystem overlaps the cleaning sector in several ways.

Its portfolio covers laundry and dishwashing, both central cleaning categories for retailers and component suppliers. Haier and regional brands also offer vacuum or floor-care products in selected markets. Candy and Hoover add a particularly visible connection to European cleaning appliances, while GE Appliances and Fisher & Paykel broaden the group's access to large-appliance buyers.

For a vacuum supplier, the commercial opportunity may therefore sit at several levels: a local Haier product team, a Candy or Hoover Europe program, a portfolio retailer, or a shared component platform. The correct brand map helps identify the door, but the model and factory documents determine the actual project.

For supplier outreach, verify whether the request comes from Haier Smart Home, Haier Group, GE Appliances, Fisher & Paykel, Candy or a regional subsidiary. Similar branding does not make bank accounts, contracts or purchasing authority interchangeable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Haier appliances?

Haier Smart Home Co., Ltd. operates the Haier appliance business and is controlled within the wider Haier Group.

### Is Haier Smart Home the same company as Haier Group?

No. Haier Smart Home is the publicly listed appliance company; Haier Group is the broader controlling group.

### Is GE Appliances owned by Haier?

Yes. Haier acquired GE Appliances in 2016. The U.S. business continues using the GE brand under a long-term trademark license.

### Is Fisher & Paykel owned by Haier?

Yes. Haier completed the acquisition of Fisher & Paykel Appliances in 2012, while the premium brand retained its own identity and operating network.

### Is Candy owned by Haier?

Yes. Haier completed its acquisition of Candy in January 2019, adding the European appliance group to Haier Smart Home's portfolio.

### Does Haier own Hoover?

Only in the relevant European appliance context through Candy. TTI owns Hoover in the Americas, so the country must be specified.

### Are all Haier appliances made in China?

No. Haier Smart Home uses a global manufacturing network. Check the country-of-origin label and documents for the exact model.

## Final Answer

Haier appliances are operated by listed Haier Smart Home, which is controlled within Haier Group. Haier Smart Home also owns a global appliance portfolio including GE Appliances, Fisher & Paykel, Candy, AQUA, Casarte and Leader.

The portfolio is connected by ownership, but it is not one undifferentiated manufacturer. GE Appliances uses the GE trademark under license, Fisher & Paykel retains a distinct premium operation, and Hoover ownership is divided by region. Buyers and suppliers should identify the exact brand, country, operating company, factory, importer, trademark position and warranty provider before turning the Haier group chart into a product-level conclusion.
