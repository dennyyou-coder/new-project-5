**Bosch Power Tools is part of the Bosch Group, whose parent company is Robert Bosch GmbH.** The operating organization includes Robert Bosch Power Tools GmbH, but the ultimate corporate answer is the privately held Bosch Group rather than a separate public tool company.

Bosch's ownership structure is unusual. The charitable Robert Bosch Stiftung holds roughly 94% of Robert Bosch GmbH's share capital, but Bosch says the foundation does not influence the group's strategic or business direction. Around 93% of the voting rights are held by Robert Bosch Industrietreuhand KG, an industrial trust that performs the entrepreneurial ownership function.

Bosch Power Tools should also be separated from Bosch home appliances. Power Tools and BSH Hausgeräte are distinct divisions within the Consumer Goods business sector. The shared Bosch group does not make their products, batteries, factories or warranties interchangeable.

## Who Owns the Bosch Group?

Robert Bosch GmbH sits at the top of the operating group.

Bosch's official [ownership disclosure](https://www.bosch.com/company/) says Robert Bosch Stiftung holds about 94% of the share capital. A Bosch-family corporation holds roughly 5%, and Robert Bosch GmbH holds roughly 1% itself.

The voting structure is different. Robert Bosch Industrietreuhand holds about 93% of the voting rights, while descendants of founder Robert Bosch hold the remaining roughly 7%. Bosch designed this separation to protect entrepreneurial independence and long-term decision-making.

It is therefore incomplete to say "the Bosch foundation runs Bosch." The foundation is the dominant capital shareholder and receives dividends for charitable work, but the industrial trust exercises most voting rights and performs the entrepreneurial ownership role.

## Is Bosch Power Tools the Same Company as Bosch Appliances?

No. They belong to the same broader group but operate in different divisions.

Bosch's [facts and figures](https://www.bosch.com/company/facts-and-figures/) place both businesses inside Consumer Goods. Power Tools covers professional and DIY power tools, garden tools, measuring technology and accessories. BSH Hausgeräte covers major and small home appliances, including some vacuum-cleaning products.

This distinction matters because a Bosch drill and a Bosch dishwasher can carry the same consumer-facing name while following different operating companies, product systems and service organizations. The BSH relationship with Siemens and other appliance brands belongs to the appliance story, not the power-tool ownership answer.

## How Large Is Bosch Power Tools?

Bosch Power Tools reported around 17,300 associates and approximately €5 billion in 2025 sales as of April 2026. Its current [company profile](https://www.bosch-pt.com/ww/en/company/about-us/) describes six brands, including Bosch and Dremel, and a product scope spanning professional tools, DIY, garden tools, accessories and measuring technology.

The Bosch Group's 2025 annual report says the Power Tools division added 120 new products and is expanding the Expert performance line from accessories into power tools and measuring technology from 2026. It describes Home, Pro and Expert as distinct performance and user positions.

Those figures describe the division's scale and strategy. They do not mean every Bosch-branded product is produced by the same factory or legal entity.

## Who Manufactures Bosch Power Tools?

Bosch operates a global development, production and supply network. No single plant or country manufactures the entire Bosch Power Tools range.

Some products come through Bosch-owned factories. Others may involve approved suppliers or specialized production arrangements. The correct evidence is attached to the model: product label, declaration, manual, certification, importer record and warranty terms.

The distinction is especially important because Bosch uses the same corporate brand across tools, garden equipment, measuring technology and appliances. A Bosch name on two products does not prove they share a factory or even the same operating division.

For factory due diligence, match the exact model to:

1. The legal manufacturer in the relevant compliance file.
2. The production site and quality audit.
3. The regional importer or responsible person.
4. The local seller and warranty provider.
5. The battery and charger platform stated in the manual.

## What Is AMPShare?

AMPShare is Bosch's professional 18V multi-brand battery alliance.

The official [AMPShare guide](https://www.bosch-professional.com/us/en/ampshare/) says compatible Bosch 18V professional tools and participating alliance products use the AMPShare battery system. It also makes an important service distinction: warranty and service responsibility remains with the company identified by the product's type plate and brand label.

That means battery compatibility does not transfer ownership. An alliance partner does not become a Bosch subsidiary, and Bosch does not automatically become the warranty company for every partner tool.

AMPShare is relevant to professional procurement because it can reduce battery and charger duplication across participating brands. Buyers still need an approved compatibility list for the exact tool and market.

## What Is POWER FOR ALL?

POWER FOR ALL is the separate 18V ecosystem for DIY, home and garden products.

Bosch's official [POWER FOR ALL page](https://www.bosch-diy.com/gb/en/landing/power-for-all/power-for-all) describes more than 30 million Bosch 18V POWER FOR ALL batteries in the market, over 10 participating brands and more than 150 products. It says around 3 million batteries are manufactured annually in Bosch-owned factories.

The alliance connects products such as home-and-garden tools, lawn equipment and selected household devices. That breadth helps explain why Bosch appears in tools, garden and cleaning searches.

POWER FOR ALL is not the same as AMPShare. A product being 18V does not make it compatible across the two systems. Users should follow the exact system name and current compatibility documentation.

## Bosch in Garden and Cleaning Equipment

Bosch Power Tools officially covers garden tools as well as power tools. The professional side also offers dust-extraction solutions, while the home-and-garden ecosystem reaches devices such as lawn equipment and selected vacuums through POWER FOR ALL participants.

The ownership boundary remains important. A Bosch-branded vacuum may sit in BSH's appliance portfolio, while a dust extractor may belong to the Power Tools division. A Gardena or Husqvarna device in POWER FOR ALL remains a product of its own brand and responsible company.

For a distributor, one battery alliance can support cross-category merchandising. It does not eliminate the need to verify the manufacturer, importer, service company and warranty route for each product.

## Does Robert Bosch Stiftung Own Bosch Power Tools?

At the capital level, the foundation's roughly 94% holding in Robert Bosch GmbH sits above the Bosch Group and therefore above the Power Tools division.

At the governance level, the answer needs qualification. Bosch explicitly says the foundation does not influence strategic or business direction. Most voting rights sit with Robert Bosch Industrietreuhand. The accurate explanation separates economic ownership from voting control.

This is not the same structure as a conventional listed parent whose institutional shareholders vote their shares directly, nor is it the same as a family-owned tool company with one operating shareholder.

## What Distributors and Suppliers Should Verify

- **Division:** Determine whether the product belongs to Power Tools, BSH or another Bosch business.
- **Legal entity:** Use the company named on the contract, invoice and compliance declaration.
- **Market:** Confirm the regional importer, seller and service organization.
- **Model:** Record the exact SKU, type number and battery system.
- **Factory:** Match the quality audit to the actual production site.
- **Alliance:** Confirm AMPShare or POWER FOR ALL compatibility from current official documentation.
- **Warranty:** Use the brand and company on the product documentation; alliance membership does not transfer service responsibility.

## Final Answer

Bosch Power Tools belongs to the Bosch Group under Robert Bosch GmbH. The charitable Robert Bosch Stiftung holds most of the capital, while an industrial trust holds most voting rights. That separation is central to understanding who economically owns Bosch and who performs the entrepreneurial governance role.

For products, keep the divisions and battery systems separate. Bosch Power Tools is not BSH home appliances, AMPShare is not POWER FOR ALL, and group ownership does not identify the factory or warranty company behind every model.
