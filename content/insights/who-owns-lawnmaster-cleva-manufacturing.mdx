**LawnMaster is a Cleva garden-equipment brand.** Cleva North America and Cleva Europe both present LawnMaster within the Cleva business, while reviewed United States trademark evidence identifies Suzhou Cleva Electric Appliance Co., Ltd. for the LAWNMASTER mark.

That is the clearest ownership answer, but it is not a reason to treat every Cleva company as interchangeable. The trademark owner, regional operator, online seller, importer, product manufacturer and warranty provider can be different legal entities.

## Is LawnMaster Owned by Cleva?

Yes. Cleva North America describes LawnMaster as part of its brand portfolio, and Cleva's European operations present and sell LawnMaster garden equipment. The reviewed US trademark proceeding record names **Suzhou Cleva Electric Appliance Co., Ltd.** for the LAWNMASTER mark.

The careful wording matters. LawnMaster is the consumer-facing brand. Suzhou Cleva is the named trademark entity in the reviewed US evidence and appears in selected product documentation. Cleva North America, Inc. runs the reviewed North American site and support route. Cleva Europe Ltd and other regional entities can handle sales, importing, service or warranty in European markets.

These companies are connected within the Cleva structure, but a buyer should use the entity printed on the contract, invoice, declaration and warranty for the specific product.

## What Is Cleva?

Cleva describes itself as a manufacturer of home and garden appliances founded in 2004. Its published capabilities span vacuums, lawn mowers and other garden power equipment. LawnMaster therefore sits within a broader manufacturing group rather than being an unrelated label licensed to an unknown operator.

Group capability still has limits as evidence. A statement that Cleva designs and manufactures appliances in-house does not prove that every LawnMaster model is produced at one factory, by one legal manufacturer or in one country. Contract production, component sourcing and product allocation can change across categories and generations.

## What Products Does LawnMaster Sell?

Current regional ranges include:

- OcuMow vision and optical-navigation robotic lawn mowers;
- cordless lawn mowers, trimmers, blowers, chainsaws and hedge tools;
- corded lawn and garden equipment; and
- selected engine-powered products in North America.

The portfolio is not identical worldwide. A model sold in the United States may not have the same battery platform, safety certification, accessories or support terms as a similarly named model sold in the United Kingdom or European Union.

## Who Makes LawnMaster Lawn Mowers and Garden Tools?

Official Cleva materials support real manufacturing involvement. Cleva Europe says the group designs and manufactures home and garden appliances, and a reviewed European manual for the LawnMaster CLMF3637E mower identifies Suzhou Cleva Electric Appliance Co., Ltd. in the documentation.

This is stronger evidence than assuming the retailer makes the product. It is still model-specific evidence. LawnMaster does not publish a complete current table connecting every mower, trimmer, blower and robotic mower to a factory.

For a purchasing or distribution decision, verify:

1. the complete model number and destination-market suffix;
2. the legal manufacturer on the declaration and product label;
3. the physical factory and stated country of origin;
4. the importer or responsible economic operator;
5. the authorized seller and invoice issuer; and
6. the model-specific warranty, parts and service route.

## Are LawnMaster Products Made in China?

Cleva has a major manufacturing base in Suzhou, China, and the reviewed mower documentation names Suzhou Cleva. That supports Chinese manufacturing for at least part of the LawnMaster supply chain.

It does not justify saying that every LawnMaster product is made in China. Country of origin is an exact-product claim. Buyers should request the current product label, factory declaration, certificate and shipping documents for the model being purchased.

## Is LawnMaster the Same in the United States and Europe?

No. The brand identity is shared, but the operating context differs.

Cleva North America manages the reviewed US LawnMaster site, orders, registration, returns, parts and service channels. Cleva Europe states that it established its UK operation in 2016 and supports sales and after-sales activity in Europe. The UK and EU catalogues also present model mixes that differ from the North American range.

Regional differences can include voltage, battery system, charger, navigation features, included accessories, warranty length, commercial-use exclusions and the company responsible for service. Cross-border purchasing can therefore create avoidable compatibility and warranty problems.

## What Is LawnMaster OcuMow?

OcuMow is LawnMaster's robotic-mower family. Reviewed UK and European ranges include camera- or sensor-navigation models, with boundary-wire-free, auto-charging and drop-and-mow configurations varying by product.

OcuMow is not one universal platform. Before comparing models, check lawn-area rating, navigation method, charging arrangement, slope capability, obstacle handling, rain behavior, theft protection, software requirements and local repair support.

## Are LawnMaster Batteries Interchangeable?

Only when the exact tool, battery and charger are approved for the same platform. LawnMaster markets regional voltage families, including MX 24V and other systems. A shared brand name or similar physical connector is not proof of compatibility.

Use the model manual and approved compatibility list. Do not combine batteries or chargers across regions, voltages or generations without written confirmation.

## How Does the LawnMaster Warranty Work?

Warranty terms are regional and product-specific. The reviewed US warranty presentation varies by product family and limits coverage for commercial, industrial or rental use. The reviewed UK store presents a separate guarantee framework.

The practical questions are who issued the invoice, whether the seller is authorized, which serial numbers are eligible, who pays freight and whether parts and repair capability exist locally. A general brand promise is not a substitute for the written terms attached to the exact model.

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

1. Confirm the applicable trademark or distribution rights for the territory.
2. Identify the Cleva entity or authorized distributor signing the agreement.
3. Lock the exact model, battery platform and included accessories.
4. Obtain current factory, origin and compliance evidence.
5. Test robotic-mower navigation against the intended lawn conditions.
6. Confirm spare parts, batteries, software support and repair capability.
7. Record the importer, seller and written warranty provider.

## Final Answer

LawnMaster is a Cleva garden-equipment brand. Reviewed evidence connects the LAWNMASTER mark to Suzhou Cleva in the United States, while Cleva North America and Cleva Europe operate sales and support in their respective markets.

The ownership answer does not identify one universal factory, seller or warranty company. For a reliable decision, verify the exact model, market, legal manufacturer, factory, battery platform, importer and service terms.
