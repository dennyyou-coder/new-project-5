**Clarke is owned by Nilfisk, which belongs to Freudenberg.** Nilfisk presents Clarke as one of its family of brands for commercial floor care. In April 2026, after Freudenberg's takeover offer completed, Nilfisk became part of Freudenberg Home and Cleaning Solutions, the German Freudenberg Group's cleaning business.

Clarke is an American brand. Alex A. Clarke created the Clarke Company in 1916. Nilfisk's US operation, in Brooklyn Park, Minnesota, markets Clarke today.

## Is Clarke part of Nilfisk?

Yes. Clarke is a Nilfisk brand. Nilfisk markets Clarke machines through its US professional-cleaning website and handles quotes, warranty registration and support. See [who owns Nilfisk](/blog/who-owns-nilfisk-freudenberg-manufacturing).

## What does Clarke make?

Rugged floor-maintenance equipment for commercial use: walk-behind and ride-on scrubbers, floor machines and burnishers, sweepers, extractors and vacuums.

## Where are Clarke machines made?

Nilfisk's product pages give model numbers, specifications and documents, but not a single factory for every Clarke machine. Check the serial plate and documents of the specific model.
