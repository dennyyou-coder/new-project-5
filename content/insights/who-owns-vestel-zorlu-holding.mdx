**Vestel is controlled by Zorlu Holding.** Zorlu Holding AŞ, the holding company of Turkey's Zorlu Group, owns 52.77% of Vestel Elektronik Sanayi ve Ticaret A.Ş., the listed core of the Vestel group. The rest of the shares are free float.

Vestel is Turkish. It was founded in 1984 and joined Zorlu Group in 1994.

## Where are Vestel appliances made?

At Vestel City in Manisa, western Türkiye. Vestel Beyaz Eşya's 2024 report lists six Manisa plants for refrigerators, washing machines, dryers, dishwashers, air conditioning, water heaters and cooking appliances.

## Does Vestel make products for other brands?

Yes. Vestel sells under its own brands and under licensed global brands, and also makes products for other companies as an ODM. In Europe it runs Sharp's home appliance business in partnership with Sharp, providing manufacturing and design; see [who owns Sharp](/blog/who-owns-sharp-foxconn-hon-hai).

## How does Vestel sell?

Vestel Ticaret A.Ş., wholly owned by Vestel Elektronik, handles domestic and international sales and marketing for its appliances.
