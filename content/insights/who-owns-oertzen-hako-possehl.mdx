**Oertzen is owned by Hako, part of the Possehl Group.** Hako's sustainability report says its merger with von Oertzen GmbH in 2023 added waterjet products to Hako GmbH's range. Hako now sells Oertzen high-pressure cleaners, and Hako has been wholly owned by Germany's Possehl Group since 2007.

Oertzen is German, with more than 70 years of waterjet experience. The historic von Oertzen business was based in Ammersbek, near Hamburg.

## Is Oertzen part of Hako?

Yes. Hako expanded into waterjet technology through von Oertzen from 2018 and merged with the company in 2023. Oertzen products now appear on Hako's website. See [who owns Hako](/blog/who-owns-hako-possehl-group).

## What does Oertzen make?

Cold-water and hot-water high-pressure cleaners, heating modules, ultra-high-pressure and custom systems, and Weco weed-control and extinguishing systems. Its brochure carries a Made in Germany claim.

## Who uses Oertzen machines?

Industry, agriculture, transport, workshops, construction, municipalities, aviation, shipping and food businesses. Service technicians cover Germany, and export partners cover other markets.
