**Makita is owned by the shareholders of Makita Corporation, an independent Japanese public company.** It is listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Prime Market and the Nagoya Stock Exchange Premier Market under stock code 6586. Makita does not report another power-tool group as its corporate parent.

That makes Makita different from brands owned by larger portfolios, such as DeWalt under Stanley Black & Decker or Milwaukee under Techtronic Industries. Institutional custodians and other investors appear in Makita's shareholder table, but a large shareholding is not the same as a parent-subsidiary relationship.

Makita's Japanese ownership and headquarters also do not mean every product is made in Japan. The company operates a global manufacturing network, and origin varies by model, factory and production period.

## Is Makita a Public or Private Company?

Makita is public.

The company's official [corporate profile](https://www.makita.biz/company/about/data/) identifies Makita Corporation as a listed company headquartered in Anjo, Aichi, Japan. As of March 31, 2026, it reported 280,017,520 shares outstanding, including treasury stock, and listings in Tokyo and Nagoya.

Makita's [shareholder information](https://www.makita.biz/ir/stock_bond/stock_bond_04.html) lists trust banks, financial institutions, companies and overseas investors among the major and broader shareholder groups. These holdings can change. They should not be turned into a claim that a bank, fund manager or customer owns Makita as its operating parent.

The practical answer is that Makita Corporation is the parent company and its public shareholders own the corporation collectively.

## Is Makita Japanese-Owned?

Makita is a Japanese-incorporated public company with its headquarters in Aichi Prefecture. It was founded in 1915 and incorporated in 1938.

"Japanese-owned" is a useful shorthand for its corporate identity, but it needs context. The shareholder base includes domestic and foreign investors, and Makita operates internationally. National identity, stock ownership and country of manufacture are related but different facts.

A distributor should therefore use the actual Makita legal entity on the contract. A consumer should use the origin marking on the product. Neither task can be completed from the brand's nationality alone.

## Who Manufactures Makita Tools?

Makita Corporation and its production network manufacture Makita products, but no single plant supplies the entire global range.

The company has developed production capacity in Japan and overseas. Its integrated reporting describes manufacturing operations across several regions and a strategy of diversifying production locations and sourcing channels. That strategy reduces dependence on one country, but it also makes model-level verification necessary.

The correct question is not simply "Where is Makita made?" It is "Where was this model, for this market and production date, made?"

Useful evidence includes:

1. The country-of-origin label on the tool, battery or accessory.
2. The manufacturing company and address in the manual or declaration.
3. The importer or responsible person for the destination market.
4. The production code and factory documentation.
5. The warranty company and authorized service route.

An official list of factories proves that Makita has capacity in those locations. It does not prove which factory made an unverified SKU.

## How Large Is Makita?

Makita's profile for the year ended March 31, 2026 reports consolidated revenue of ¥777.6 billion and 17,586 employees. The company describes its business as the production and sale of electric power tools, gardening equipment, pneumatic tools and household equipment.

Makita also operates directly managed business bases in around 50 countries and sales networks reaching around 180 countries, according to its current [business strategy](https://www.makita.biz/company/strategy/management/). That service footprint is strategically important because professional tool competition depends on parts, repair and channel support as well as product specifications.

For buyers, the network does not mean every regional subsidiary performs every role. The company on the invoice, the importer, the warranty provider and the factory can still be different Makita entities.

## What Are LXT, XGT and CXT?

They are Makita cordless platforms with different voltage and application positions.

LXT is the broad 18V platform. XGT is the 40Vmax platform that Makita is expanding into higher-output tools and outdoor equipment. CXT serves compact applications. Makita's 2025 integrated report describes the lineup as ranging from compact CXT products to mid-range 18V LXT and 40Vmax XGT.

The platforms help Makita cover different user needs, but they should not be described as one universally interchangeable battery system. A Makita brand name on two tools does not prove direct battery compatibility. Users should check the exact battery, charger and adapter documentation.

Makita's current strategy places particular emphasis on expanding the 40Vmax series while continuing to serve its established cordless base. That is a product-platform decision, not a change in corporate ownership.

## Makita in Outdoor and Cleaning Equipment

Makita extends beyond drills and saws.

Its official business scope includes gardening and household equipment. Depending on the market, Makita offers cordless lawn mowers, trimmers, blowers, pressure-related equipment, dust extractors and vacuums. These categories make the company relevant to landscaping, facilities work and professional cleaning.

The connection should remain evidence-based. Makita's broad outdoor range does not automatically establish a current robot-lawn-mower business in every region. Its vacuum and dust-extraction products also do not make Makita a full-line consumer floor-care company equivalent to a dedicated appliance brand.

For a distributor, the useful question is how Makita's battery platforms and service network support adjacent categories. For an OEM or supplier, the question is which Makita entity controls the specification, purchase order and quality process for the exact product.

## Is Makita Owned by Milwaukee, DeWalt or Bosch?

No.

Milwaukee belongs to TTI. DeWalt belongs to Stanley Black & Decker. Bosch Power Tools is part of the Bosch Group. Makita Corporation is separately listed and has no disclosed corporate parent among those groups.

The companies compete in many of the same tool and outdoor-equipment categories. Similar voltages, retailers or suppliers do not create an ownership relationship.

See [Who Owns DeWalt?](https://worldcleanbiz.com/blog/who-owns-dewalt-stanley-black-decker) and [Who Owns TTI?](https://worldcleanbiz.com/blog/who-owns-tti-milwaukee-ryobi-hoover-vax-oreck) for the different portfolio structures.

## What Distributors and Suppliers Should Verify

- **Corporate entity:** Confirm which Makita company signs the agreement.
- **Market:** Identify the local seller, importer and service organization.
- **Model:** Record the complete SKU, voltage platform and production code.
- **Factory:** Match factory audits to the actual production site and model.
- **Origin:** Use labels and customs documents, not the headquarters address.
- **Compliance:** Verify the declaration, certification holder and responsible person.
- **Warranty:** Confirm who supplies parts and handles recalls or repairs.
- **Compatibility:** Use current Makita documentation for batteries, chargers and accessories.
