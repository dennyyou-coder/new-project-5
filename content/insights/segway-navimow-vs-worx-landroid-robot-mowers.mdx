**Choose Segway Navimow when you want a current wire-free system built around mapped virtual boundaries and systematic navigation. Choose WORX Landroid when you want a broader platform choice—including inexpensive boundary-wire models, vision-only products and new RTK-assisted Vision Cloud machines—or when the PowerShare battery ecosystem matters.**

The comparison changed in 2026. “Navimow is wire-free, Landroid uses a wire” is no longer accurate. Both brands now offer wire-free options, but they reach that result through different product families and navigation strategies.

## First Decide Which Landroid You Mean

WORX uses the Landroid name across several architectures.

### Boundary-wire Landroid

The traditional WR-series products use a perimeter wire. They can be cost-effective and do not depend on satellite positioning to define the lawn.

They require:

- wire planning and installation;
- repairs after aeration, edging or animal damage;
- careful island and passage design;
- an installation record for future service.

### Landroid Vision AI

Vision AI uses a camera and neural network to distinguish grass, obstacles and areas it should not cross. WORX promotes it as an unbox-and-mow system without boundary wire, RTK antenna or initial mapping.

This can work well where visual lawn edges are clear. Magnetic strips and RFID tags may still be useful for ambiguous exclusions and multi-zone movement.

### Landroid Vision Cloud

WORX’s 2026 European lineup adds Vision Cloud models, including 2WD and 4WD products. WORX describes centimeter-level RTK precision, automatic mapping and connectivity through Wi-Fi or included 4G on relevant products.

This makes the current Landroid comparison much closer to Navimow’s mapped wire-free proposition.

## Which Navimow Platform?

Navimow also has multiple generations and series. Current products may combine:

- RTK or network RTK;

Segway’s 2026 range includes updated i and X products for different lawn sizes and terrain. Buyers must confirm the exact regional model because product names, connectivity and included hardware can vary.

The correct comparison may be:

- entry Navimow vs boundary-wire Landroid;

## Navigation: RTK, LiDAR, Vision or Wire?

### RTK

RTK can provide precise virtual boundaries and orderly routes when satellite and correction signals are stable.

Validate:

- sky visibility at boundaries;
- tree canopy;
- walls and narrow courtyards;
- base-station or network service;
- recovery after a signal interruption;
- ongoing data-plan terms.

### LiDAR

LiDAR can improve localization around buildings and under trees. It still needs a clear physical environment and reliable software. Dense hedges, reflective surfaces, moving objects and seasonal changes can affect mapping.

### Vision

Vision identifies grass and obstacles through images. It can avoid the need for a satellite correction link, but it depends on visually readable boundaries.

Test:

- grass meeting a flower bed at the same height;
- gravel and stepping stones;
- fallen leaves;
- shadows;
- wet grass;
- nighttime operation;
- toys, animals and thin objects.

### Boundary wire

A wire is physical infrastructure. It can be reliable in a stable lawn, including under tree cover. It is less attractive where the garden changes often or where wire damage is likely.

There is no universally best navigation method. Choose the failure mode the property can tolerate.

## Installation

### Navimow installation

Installation generally includes:

- charging-station location;
- network and satellite or LiDAR assessment;
- mapping the perimeter;
- setting no-go zones;
- defining passages;
- testing return-to-charge;
- validating edge offset.

### Landroid installation

The process depends on the product:

- boundary-wire models require physical layout and pegging or burial;
- Vision AI can need little initial mapping but may require strips or tags in ambiguous areas;
- Vision Cloud uses mapped boundaries and connectivity.

Measure the actual commissioning time. A “wire-free” mower can still require hours of mapping, station optimization and exception handling.

Dealers should quote installation separately from the machine when a site survey and commissioning are required.

## Lawn Size and Cutting Capacity

Do not buy only from the maximum lawn-area label.

Adjust for:

- slope and rough ground;
- grass growth rate;
- narrow passages;
- excluded islands;
- travel between zones;
- irrigation schedule;
- usable mowing hours;
- charging cycles;
- local noise restrictions.

A mower rated for the nominal area may struggle if it can only work during a short dry window or spends significant time moving between separated lawns.

## Slopes and Traction

Slope claims need three definitions:

1. maximum continuous mowing slope;
2. maximum boundary slope;
3. short transition or obstacle capability.

Convert percent grade carefully. A 50% grade is not 50 degrees.

AWD can improve traction and slope handling, but adds cost, weight and drivetrain components. It does not fix unsafe boundaries, loose soil or a charging station placed on a slope.

Test across:

- dry and wet grass;
- side slopes;
- dips;
- roots;
- retaining-wall edges;
- soft ground;
- the route back to the charger.

## Edge Cutting

WORX has long marketed Cut to Edge on supported Landroid products. The offset blade deck can reduce the strip left beside a hard, level edge.

Navimow can route close to virtual boundaries, with performance depending on model, deck geometry and mapping offset.

Neither mower can eliminate trimming everywhere. Raised walls, fences, flower beds and drop-offs require a safety margin. Compare remaining manual trimming in minutes per week.

## Obstacles, Pets and Safety

Vision systems can improve obstacle recognition, but no consumer robot mower should be treated as perfectly safe.

Follow the manual and:

- remove toys, hoses and thin objects;
- inspect for wildlife;
- keep children and pets away during operation;
- avoid unattended mowing when visibility is poor;
- use lift, tilt, PIN and disabling functions;
- inspect blades and guards.

Night mowing can increase risk to small animals even if the mower promotes low-noise operation. A conservative daytime schedule is easier to supervise.

## FAQ

### Does WORX Landroid still need a boundary wire?

Some models do. Landroid Vision AI and Vision Cloud products are wire-free, while traditional Landroid models use a perimeter wire.

### Is Navimow always RTK-based?

No. Current models combine different positioning technologies, including RTK, network RTK, vision and LiDAR depending on series and market.

### Which is better under trees?

LiDAR or vision can reduce dependence on satellite visibility, while a physical boundary wire can also work under canopy. The result depends on the exact model and site.

### Is AWD necessary?

Not for a flat, simple lawn. AWD becomes more valuable on slopes and difficult terrain but adds cost and mechanical complexity.

### Which mower leaves less edge trimming?

WORX’s Cut to Edge can reduce trimming along suitable hard edges. Navimow can follow mapped boundaries closely. Raised borders and safety margins still require manual trimming.
