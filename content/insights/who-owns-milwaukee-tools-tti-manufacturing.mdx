**Milwaukee Tool is owned by Techtronic Industries Company Limited, usually called TTI.** TTI acquired the Milwaukee brand and business in 2005. TTI is a public company listed in Hong Kong under stock code 669, while Milwaukee Electric Tool Corporation operates as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary within the group.

Milwaukee is therefore not an independent public company, and it is not owned by Home Depot. It is also not owned by Ryobi. TTI operates Milwaukee as an owned professional brand, while its Ryobi power-tool and outdoor-equipment business uses the RYOBI trademark under license in specified markets.

The ownership answer does not identify the factory behind every Milwaukee product. TTI has a global manufacturing and supplier network, and product origin can change by model and production period. Procurement teams should treat the parent company, brand operator, manufacturer, importer and warranty provider as separate questions.

## When Did TTI Buy Milwaukee?

TTI acquired Milwaukee in 2005. The group's official history places that transaction after its expansion into floor care and before its 2007 acquisition of the Hoover floor-care business.

That history matters because older Milwaukee references describe a standalone U.S. tool company. Milwaukee began in 1924, but the present corporate chain runs through TTI. The current Milwaukee business should not be described as a privately held American parent company merely because its brand history and major operations are rooted in the United States.

TTI's [2025 annual report](https://www.ttigroup.com/documents/annual-report-2025/index.php) presents Milwaukee as the group's flagship professional business. It also lists Milwaukee Electric Tool Corporation as an indirect wholly owned principal subsidiary. These disclosures provide stronger ownership evidence than retailer pages, social posts or product labels.

For the group-wide relationship among Milwaukee, Ryobi, Hoover, Vax and Oreck, see [Who Owns TTI?](https://worldcleanbiz.com/blog/who-owns-tti-milwaukee-ryobi-hoover-vax-oreck).

## Is Milwaukee Still an American Company?

Milwaukee can be described as an American-founded brand and operating business within a Hong Kong-listed global group. That is more accurate than forcing it into a single national label.

Milwaukee Tool maintains its headquarters in Brookfield, Wisconsin, and TTI has continued to invest in U.S. engineering, manufacturing and service operations. At the same time, the parent company operates a diversified global manufacturing footprint. TTI's 2025 report says Milwaukee migrated affected products to lower-tariff jurisdictions by the end of that year, illustrating that sourcing decisions can move even when brand ownership does not.

Country of origin must therefore be checked on the exact tool, battery, accessory or vacuum. "Milwaukee is an American brand" does not mean every Milwaukee SKU is made in the United States. "TTI is based in Hong Kong" does not mean every SKU is made in China.

## Who Manufactures Milwaukee Tools?

There is no reliable one-factory answer for the full Milwaukee range.

Milwaukee's official company history says it made a strategic decision to design, develop and manufacture its own battery packs and motors while building internal electronics capability. That claim is important because it describes vertical integration around core cordless technology. It should not be expanded into the unsupported claim that Milwaukee internally manufactures every component or finished product.

TTI's group reporting describes a global manufacturing footprint and supply chain. A finished Milwaukee product may involve a TTI subsidiary, an internal plant, external component suppliers or another approved production arrangement. The correct evidence depends on the exact model.

A buyer should check:

1. The country-of-origin marking on the product and packaging.
2. The manufacturer or responsible company on the rating label and compliance declaration.
3. The importer shown for the destination market.
4. The legal seller named on the invoice or distribution agreement.
5. The warranty provider and authorized service route.

This model-level check is especially important for batteries, chargers, radios, storage products and licensed accessories, where the responsible entity may not be identical across the range.

## What Are M12, M18 and MX FUEL?

They are Milwaukee cordless platforms designed for different application and power requirements.

M12 is the compact 12-volt system. M18 is the main 18-volt professional platform. MX FUEL extends cordless power into larger equipment. FUEL is a performance designation used on products combining Milwaukee's brushless-motor, battery and electronic-control technologies; it is not a separate owner.

Milwaukee's official history says the company introduced M12 and M18 with a commitment to continue developing the systems without forcing users to abandon the platforms. TTI's 2025 annual report describes more than 300 solutions in M18 and shows the platform extending beyond drills and saws into lighting, storage integration, outdoor equipment and jobsite cleanup.

Compatibility still needs exact product documentation. A shared M18 label establishes the battery-system relationship defined by Milwaukee, but it does not make an M18 battery compatible with M12 or MX FUEL. It also does not create compatibility with another TTI brand.

## Milwaukee in Outdoor and Cleaning Equipment

Milwaukee is relevant to World Clean Biz because its cordless platform reaches both outdoor maintenance and jobsite cleaning.

TTI's 2025 report describes Milwaukee outdoor equipment for landscape maintenance, tree care and snow removal across the M12, M18 and MX FUEL systems. It also features the M18 FUEL NEXUS 6-gallon wet/dry vacuum with PACKOUT compatibility and wireless dust-control functions.

That product evidence supports a Milwaukee wet/dry-vacuum and dust-management business. It does not support the claim that Milwaukee operates TTI's consumer floor-care brands or currently sells a robot lawn mower. Hoover, Vax, Oreck and Dirt Devil sit elsewhere in TTI's portfolio, with their own brand and regional responsibilities.

For cleaning-equipment buyers, the useful connection is the battery and jobsite ecosystem. A contractor may value one platform across tools, lighting, dust extraction and wet/dry cleanup. A distributor still needs to verify which company imports, warrants and services each category.

## How Milwaukee Differs from Ryobi Inside TTI

Milwaukee is an owned brand. Ryobi is a licensed trademark in the relevant TTI power-tool and outdoor-equipment markets.

The commercial positions also differ. Milwaukee is centered on professional trades and specialized applications. Ryobi is built around a broad DIY and home-and-garden ecosystem. Common group operation can create shared capabilities in sourcing and product development, but it does not prove shared batteries, factories, parts or warranties.

See [Who Owns Ryobi?](https://worldcleanbiz.com/blog/who-owns-ryobi-tti-kyocera) for the regional licensing and Kyocera distinction.

## What Distributors and Suppliers Should Verify

Before making a sourcing or ownership claim, record the following:

- **Group and operating company:** TTI is the parent; identify the Milwaukee entity signing the contract.
- **Market:** Confirm the country and regional seller or importer.
- **Product:** Record model, voltage system, type number and production date.
- **Origin:** Use the label and customs documents, not the brand's headquarters.
- **Factory:** Match the audit and quality file to the actual site and model.
- **Compliance:** Verify declarations, certifications and responsible-person details.
- **Warranty:** Identify who repairs, replaces and recalls the product locally.
- **Platform:** Confirm battery and accessory compatibility in current official documentation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Milwaukee Tools?

Techtronic Industries Company Limited owns Milwaukee Tool. TTI acquired the Milwaukee brand and business in 2005.

### Is Milwaukee owned by China?

Milwaukee belongs to TTI, a Hong Kong-listed global company. That corporate fact does not establish one country of origin for every Milwaukee product.

### Does Home Depot own Milwaukee?

No. Home Depot is an important retail channel, not Milwaukee's parent company.

### Are Milwaukee and Ryobi owned by the same company?

TTI owns Milwaukee and operates licensed Ryobi tool and outdoor-equipment businesses in specified markets. TTI does not own Ryobi Limited or the RYOBI trademark globally.

### Are all Milwaukee tools made in the USA?

No single origin applies to the whole range. Check the exact product label, packaging and compliance documents.

### Does Milwaukee make wet/dry vacuums?

Yes. Milwaukee sells jobsite wet/dry vacuums and dust-management products within its cordless ecosystem, including M18 products documented by TTI.

### Does Milwaukee make robot lawn mowers?

The official material reviewed for this article supports outdoor power equipment but does not establish a current Milwaukee robot-mower line. Do not infer one from TTI's broader outdoor portfolio.

## Final Answer

Milwaukee Tool is owned by Techtronic Industries, which acquired the business in 2005. Milwaukee operates as TTI's professional flagship, with M12, M18 and MX FUEL extending the brand from power tools into outdoor equipment and jobsite cleaning.

The parent-company answer is only the first layer of due diligence. For a purchase, distribution agreement or factory claim, verify the exact model's origin, manufacturer, importer and warranty company. TTI ownership does not make every Milwaukee product a one-country or one-factory product.
