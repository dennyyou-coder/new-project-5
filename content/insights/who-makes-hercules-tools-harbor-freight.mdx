**Hercules is owned by Harbor Freight Tools and positioned as the retailer's professional power-tool line.** Official manuals and current product pages state that HERCULES is a trademark of Harbor Freight Tools, while Harbor Freight calls it an exclusive brand.

The company does not publicly name one universal manufacturer for all Hercules products. Brand ownership, engineering, importing, factory production and warranty responsibility are related but separate roles.

## Is Hercules a Harbor Freight Brand?

Yes. Harbor Freight's corporate newsroom describes Hercules as an exclusive professional-quality brand. Manuals state that the HERCULES trademark belongs to Harbor Freight Tools.

Retail comparison copy may place Hercules beside DeWalt, Milwaukee, Makita or other professional brands. A performance comparison does not create an ownership or manufacturing relationship with those companies.

## Who Manufactures Hercules Tools?

Harbor Freight manages the product system and says its engineers develop tools for professional standards. The public record does not establish one supplier for every drill, grinder, saw, table saw, battery or accessory.

The exact evidence chain should include item number, model, UPC, rating label, country of origin, manual, certification record and import documentation. A supplier identified for one item should not be expanded to the entire brand.

This is especially important because Hercules spans cordless, corded, benchtop and accessory categories. Those products do not require the same factory capabilities or supply chain.

## Hercules 12V and 20V Platforms

Hercules currently offers compact 12V tools and a wider 20V platform. Certain chargers can accept documented Hercules 12V and 20V battery packs, but that does not make the batteries themselves interchangeable between tools.

![Hercules platform and warranty decision map](/images/blog/hercules-platform-warranty-map.svg)

Harbor Freight promoted 8Ah and 12Ah 20V Extreme Performance batteries using 21700 cells for high-demand tools. Those packs belong to the Hercules 20V system. They should not be used as evidence of compatibility with Bauer or another Harbor Freight brand.

For fleet purchasing, separate:

- 12V compact-tool needs;
- 20V general and high-output tools;
- corded and benchtop equipment;
- batteries and chargers; and
- accessories that are mechanically universal versus batteries that are not.

## Why Harbor Freight Positions Hercules Above Bauer

Harbor Freight markets Bauer as a broad value and DIY platform and Hercules as a professional line. Hercules receives more brushless products, higher-output battery options and longer warranty coverage on qualifying tools.

That positioning is a retailer strategy, not proof that every Hercules tool has a different factory from every Bauer tool. Buyers should evaluate each tool's duty cycle, thermal protection, service route and replacement availability rather than relying only on the tier label.

## Hercules Warranty Explained

Harbor Freight's five-year headline has a specific boundary. Current qualifying **Hercules 20V brushless tools purchased on or after August 19, 2022** carry a five-year limited warranty. Harbor Freight's 2022 launch material separately described three-year coverage for the new Extreme Performance batteries.

Other Hercules products can show a 90-day warranty. For example, current 12V or non-qualifying product pages may point to the standard limited terms. A buyer should not apply the five-year rule to every corded tool, 12V tool, accessory, battery or charger.

Keep proof of purchase. Warranty registration is not required under the reviewed five-year manual, but the product and receipt remain necessary for a claim.

## Hercules and Cleaning Work

Hercules includes dust extractors or wet-dry vacuums, grinders, sanders and other tools used in construction cleanup and surface preparation. The important procurement questions are filter availability, dust class or collection capability, hose fit, tool activation, battery runtime and local replacement.

Professional positioning does not make every vacuum suitable for regulated hazardous dust. Check the exact machine, filter and applicable exposure-control requirements.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Hercules tools?

Harbor Freight Tools owns and operates the Hercules brand.

### Is Hercules made by DeWalt or Milwaukee?

There is no evidence that those brands own Hercules. Harbor Freight owns the trademark; exact manufacturing must be checked by item.

### Are Hercules and Bauer batteries compatible?

No compatibility should be assumed. They are separate Harbor Freight brand systems.

### Are all Hercules tools covered for five years?

No. The five-year term applies to qualifying Hercules 20V brushless tools purchased on or after August 19, 2022. Other categories can have different terms.

### Where are Hercules tools made?

Origin can vary. Use the exact tool label, manual and shipment records rather than a brand-wide country claim.

## Final Answer

Hercules is Harbor Freight's exclusive professional-positioned tool brand. Harbor Freight owns the trademark and controls product strategy, channel and support, but does not disclose one universal manufacturer. Buyers should verify factory and origin at item level, keep 12V and 20V compatibility separate, and apply the five-year warranty only to eligible 20V brushless tools.
