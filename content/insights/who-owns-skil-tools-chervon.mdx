**SKIL tools are owned by Chervon.** Chervon (HK) Ltd. completed its acquisition of the SKIL business from Bosch Power Tools in 2017. The North American transaction closed on December 31, 2016, and the European transaction was completed on March 31, 2017.

**Who manufactures SKIL tools?** Chervon is both SKIL's owner and an integrated power-tool manufacturer whose current business unit covers research, sourcing, manufacturing and testing, with production facilities in Nanjing, China, and Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Those disclosures do not assign every SKIL SKU to one plant, so the product label, declaration, importer and factory records remain necessary for a model-specific answer.

Bosch is therefore part of SKIL's ownership history, not its current parent. Chervon now presents SKIL as one of its own brands and distributes it primarily in North America, Europe and Australia. Chervon also owns or operates other tool brands, including FLEX, EGO and SKILSAW, but shared group ownership does not make their batteries, warranties or product positions interchangeable.

## When Did Chervon Buy SKIL From Bosch?

Chervon and Bosch signed their agreement in August 2016. Chervon's completion announcement says the North American SKIL transaction closed on December 31, 2016, while the European sale closed on March 31, 2017. Chervon now uses 2017 as the acquisition year in its brand and business-unit history.

The transaction transferred the operating SKIL businesses that Bosch Power Tools had run in North America and Europe. Chervon said approximately 100 employees in Breda in the Netherlands, Mount Prospect in Illinois and Hangzhou in China transferred with the business.

The acquisition was not Chervon's first connection to Bosch. Chervon says it became a major OEM supplier to Bosch Power Tools in 2003, and the two companies created a Nanjing benchtop-tool joint venture in 2007. Those earlier supplier and joint-venture relationships help explain the corporate history, but they do not mean Bosch kept ownership after the sale.

## Did Bosch Own SKIL Tools?

Yes. Bosch acquired SKIL in 1996 and made it part of the Bosch Power Tools division. That ownership lasted until the Chervon transaction.

This history still causes search confusion because older manuals, products, service documents and regulatory notices can name Bosch or a Bosch company. A legacy Bosch-era SKIL tool may have a different battery, warranty or service route from a current Chervon-era product.

Use the production date and model number when researching an older tool. The current ownership answer should not be retroactively applied to documents created when Bosch owned the business, and an old Bosch relationship should not be presented as current ownership.

## Who Founded SKIL?

SKIL traces its origin to the portable electric handsaw developed by Edmond Michel. Chervon's centennial history says Michel and Joseph W. Sullivan founded the Michel Electric Handsaw Company in 1924 after Michel's work on an electric cutting solution inspired by sugar-cane harvesting.

The portable circular saw became central to the SKIL identity. Chervon says more than 2,000 saws had been produced by the end of 1926, and the later Model 77 became an industry reference. The brand expanded internationally over subsequent decades before entering the Bosch group in 1996.

Historical invention claims describe the brand's origin. They do not identify the manufacturer, origin or technology of today's cordless tools.

## Who Manufactures SKIL Tools?

Chervon is both the brand owner and a large power-tool manufacturer, but a precise manufacturing answer still belongs at model level.

Chervon's Power Tools Business Unit says its value chain includes research and development, sourcing, manufacturing, testing, marketing, distribution and after-sales service. It identifies production facilities in Nanjing, China, and Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Chervon's current corporate site also describes the group as designing and manufacturing handheld tools, benchtop tools and outdoor power equipment.

Those disclosures support the conclusion that SKIL sits inside an integrated manufacturing group. They do not allocate every SKIL model to a named plant. A group can use multiple factories, component suppliers and legal entities, and production can move between product generations.

For an exact SKIL tool, verify:

- model and serial or date code;
- country-of-origin label;
- manufacturer or importer named on the product and manual;
- declaration, certification or test report;
- factory and shipment evidence for commercial orders; and
- current warranty and recall documents.

## Where Are SKIL Tools Made?

SKIL tools should not be assigned one universal country of origin.

Chervon's current power-tool disclosures identify manufacturing capacity in both China and Vietnam. The December 2024 U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recall for specified SKIL 40V batteries named Chervon North America as importer and China as the country of manufacture for those batteries.

That recall is useful model-specific evidence, but it is not a catalogue-wide origin statement. The origin of a circular saw, mower, drill, vacuum or battery may differ. Chervon's North American headquarters in Illinois supports product development, sales and service activities; it does not by itself prove that a tool was made in the United States.

Always distinguish the parent's headquarters, the operating office, the factory and the country printed on the exact product.

## What Are PWRCORE 12, PWRCORE 20 and PWRCORE 40?

PWRCORE is SKIL's current cordless-system family.

Chervon relaunched SKIL's cordless proposition in 2018 around PWRCORE 12 and PWRCORE 20 lithium-ion systems. The current brand range also includes PWRCORE 40 outdoor equipment. SKIL's 2025 U.S. catalogue shows PWRCORE 20 kits containing drills, saws, sanders, lights, batteries and chargers, while the current SKIL site covers power tools and outdoor equipment.

The number identifies the intended SKIL platform, not open compatibility:

1. PWRCORE 12 tools use the SKIL 12V system.
2. PWRCORE 20 tools use the SKIL 20V system.
3. PWRCORE 40 is aimed at higher-demand outdoor equipment.
4. A common Chervon parent does not make SKIL batteries compatible with EGO, FLEX or another group brand.
5. Legacy Bosch-era SKIL batteries should not be assumed compatible with current Chervon-era tools.

Match the exact tool, battery and charger documentation before purchase or resale.

## Is SKIL the Same as SKILSAW?

They share a common origin and Chervon ownership, but buyers should treat SKIL and SKILSAW as distinct current brand propositions.

SKIL covers a broad consumer and prosumer range of power tools, benchtop tools and outdoor equipment. SKILSAW has a stronger professional cutting identity built around worm-drive and other saw products. Chervon lists both names in its brand portfolio and has marketed them separately in retail channels.

The historical use of “Skilsaw” as a familiar name for a circular saw adds another layer of confusion. For warranty, accessories, parts and product registration, use the exact brand and model printed on the product rather than treating the names as interchangeable.

## SKIL in Outdoor and Cleaning Equipment

SKIL extends beyond drilling and cutting into lawn and garden products. Chervon's SKIL page lists lawn and garden alongside saws, drills, impact tools, batteries and chargers. PWRCORE 40 supports outdoor products, while PWRCORE 20 appears in property-maintenance and workshop applications.

The cleaning adjacency is narrower than the core tool range but still relevant. SKIL catalogues include dust-extraction accessories and portable vacuum or blower applications in certain markets and generations. For World Clean Biz buyers, the important link is the shared need for battery, filter, collection, dust-control and after-sales verification across tool and cleanup workflows.

Do not assume that a dust extractor, blower, mower and drill share the same supplier or warranty merely because they carry the SKIL name.

## How Does the SKIL Warranty Work?

In the United States and Canada, the current warranty is issued by Chervon North America.

SKIL's warranty page says consumer outdoor equipment and 12V/20V cordless handheld tools generally receive a three-year limited warranty, with an optional five-year term when the eligible product is registered within 30 days. The same table gives only 90 days for professional, industrial or commercial use in those categories. Other products can have different periods.

This distinction matters because a headline “five-year warranty” is not universal. Registration, authorized-seller status, product category, market and use case can change the result. The current page also says Chervon may reject claims for products bought from unauthorized sellers where permitted by law.

Keep the dated receipt, registration confirmation, model, serial number, battery and charger identity. For other countries, use the local SKIL dealer or importer rather than applying North American terms.

## What the SKIL 40V Battery Recall Covers

The December 19, 2024 CPSC recall covers specified SKIL PWRCORE 40 model BY8708-00 5.0Ah batteries manufactured before May 1, 2021 and carrying listed date codes. The batteries were sold separately and in specified mower kits.

CPSC says only the battery is recalled, not every SKIL 40V tool. Chervon North America is the recall contact and importer, and the listed batteries were manufactured in China. Owners should stop using a matched battery and follow the current official replacement or refund instructions.

The notice demonstrates why ownership and product responsibility should be separated. Chervon owns SKIL, but the safety action still depends on an exact battery model and production code.

## What Buyers, Dealers and Suppliers Should Verify

Before relying on a SKIL ownership or manufacturing claim, record:

1. **Ownership period:** Bosch-era or Chervon-era product.
2. **Brand identity:** SKIL or the separately positioned SKILSAW brand.
3. **Product identity:** Exact model, serial number, date code and kit.
4. **Battery system:** PWRCORE 12, 20 or 40, including charger compatibility.
5. **Manufacturer and origin:** Product label, declaration, factory and production period.
6. **Importer or seller:** Legal entities for the destination market.
7. **Warranty:** Consumer or commercial use, registration requirement and authorized seller.
8. **Recall status:** Exact product, battery and date-code match.
9. **Service and parts:** Authorized repair route and availability of batteries, chargers, filters and consumables.
10. **Channel:** Current authorized retailer or distributor in the relevant country.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns SKIL tools today?

Chervon owns SKIL. It completed the acquisition of the SKIL business from Bosch Power Tools in 2017.

### Is SKIL still owned by Bosch?

No. Bosch owned SKIL from 1996 until the sale to Chervon. Bosch is part of the brand's history, not its current parent.

### Is SKIL owned by Chervon or Lowe's?

Chervon owns SKIL. Lowe's has been an important U.S. retail channel, but retail distribution is not brand ownership. In 2026, Chervon also expanded SKIL into Tractor Supply stores nationwide.

### Where are SKIL tools made?

Chervon discloses power-tool production facilities in Nanjing, China, and Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. The exact SKIL model's label and documents determine its origin.

### Are SKIL and SKILSAW the same?

They share history and Chervon ownership but operate as distinct brand propositions. Use the exact product brand for parts, warranty and service.

### Are SKIL batteries compatible with Bosch tools?

Do not assume compatibility. Bosch no longer owns SKIL, and current PWRCORE platforms are separate from Bosch battery systems.

### Are SKIL batteries compatible with EGO or FLEX?

Common Chervon ownership does not establish compatibility. Use only the battery and charger combinations documented for the exact SKIL platform.

### Does SKIL make outdoor power equipment?

Yes. SKIL's current portfolio includes lawn and garden products, including PWRCORE 40 equipment, as well as core power and benchtop tools.

## Final Answer

Chervon owns SKIL after completing its acquisition of the North American and European SKIL businesses from Bosch in 2017. Bosch owned the brand from 1996 through the sale, but it is no longer the current parent.

SKIL now operates inside Chervon's integrated power-tool group, which discloses manufacturing facilities in China and Vietnam. That group-level fact does not identify the origin of every SKU. Verify the exact model's factory, importer, battery platform, warranty and recall status—and keep SKIL distinct from both Bosch and the separately positioned SKILSAW brand.
