**DJI ROMO is owned by DJI.** ROMO is DJI's robot vacuum brand, not a separate company. Its manuals, apps and firmware notes carry DJI branding and copyright. DJI, the drone and camera maker, is privately held and was founded by Frank Wang, who is still its chief executive.

ROMO is Chinese. DJI was founded in 2006, is headquartered in Shenzhen and launched ROMO in August 2025.

## Is DJI a public company?

No. DJI is privately held, and it does not publish a current shareholder table.

## Why did DJI make a robot vacuum?

DJI says ROMO's perception and path planning draw on its wider robotics and drone technology. For the strategy behind the move, see our [DJI ROMO launch analysis](/blog/dji-romo-launch-analysis) and [can DJI become top three in robot vacuums](/blog/can-dji-become-top-three-in-robot-vacuums).

## Where are DJI ROMO robots made?

DJI's public materials do not name one assembly factory for every ROMO model and market. The label and packaging of a specific unit show where it was made.

## What does ROMO sell?

The ROMO P, A and S series, plus an app, accessories and consumables. Availability, prices and plumbing-kit options vary by market.
