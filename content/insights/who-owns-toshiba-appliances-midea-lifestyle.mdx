**Toshiba-branded home appliances are operated primarily through Toshiba Lifestyle Products & Services, a company controlled by Midea Group since 2016.** Midea acquired 80.1% of Toshiba Lifestyle and received a 40-year worldwide license to use the Toshiba trademark for home appliances.

That does not mean Midea bought Toshiba Corporation. Toshiba Corporation and the licensed home-appliance business remain different legal and commercial subjects. It also does not mean every Toshiba appliance worldwide comes from one factory or one sales company.

## What Did Midea Acquire in 2016?

Toshiba Corporation and Midea Group announced their definitive agreement in March 2016. Under the transaction, Midea acquired an 80.1% stake in Toshiba Lifestyle Products & Services Corporation, the company responsible for Toshiba's home-appliance business.

The parties completed the share transfer in June 2016. Toshiba Lifestyle became a subsidiary within Midea Group, while the transaction documents described Toshiba Corporation retaining the remaining 19.9% interest at completion.

The operating company matters because it is closer to the products than the parent-company headline. Toshiba Lifestyle's current company overview says its business includes the development, manufacture and sale of household electrical appliances. Its corporate history records the Midea transaction and its subsequent product and organizational development.

## Does Midea Own the Toshiba Brand?

Not in the broad sense. The 2016 agreement gave Midea a **40-year worldwide license** to use the Toshiba brand for home appliances. A trademark license authorizes use within agreed categories, territories and terms; it does not automatically transfer ownership of Toshiba Corporation or every Toshiba-branded business.

This distinction explains why the correct short answer depends on the question:

- **Corporate owner of Toshiba Corporation:** not Midea merely because of the appliance agreement;
- **Controlling shareholder of Toshiba Lifestyle:** Midea Group;
- **Owner of the Toshiba trademark:** Toshiba Corporation and its relevant rights-holding entities;
- **Licensed operator for Toshiba home appliances:** the Midea-controlled appliance business under the long-term agreement.

Televisions, elevators, industrial systems and other Toshiba businesses can follow different structures. The home-appliance license should not be extended to unrelated categories without evidence.

## What Products Does Toshiba Lifestyle Cover?

Toshiba Lifestyle's official materials cover refrigerators, washing machines, cooking appliances, microwave ovens, rice cookers, vacuum cleaners, air-conditioning-related products and other household categories, with the exact portfolio varying by market.

The company retains Toshiba's appliance heritage while operating inside Midea Group. That combination supports product development, procurement and manufacturing at group scale, but it should not be simplified into “all products are rebadged Midea appliances.” A shared parent can coordinate technology and supply chains while maintaining different product specifications, design teams, quality processes and market positioning.

## Where Are Toshiba Appliances Made?

There is no single country-of-origin answer for every Toshiba appliance. Toshiba Lifestyle's corporate materials describe a network involving Japan and overseas group companies, while Midea operates a much broader global manufacturing system.

The 2016 ownership transaction does not identify the factory for a current refrigerator, washer, vacuum or microwave. Depending on the model and destination, the responsible party may involve Toshiba Lifestyle, another Midea group entity, a regional company or a contracted manufacturer.

For a product-level answer, check:

1. the complete model and serial number;
2. the rating label and country-of-origin marking;
3. the manual, declaration and certification holder;
4. the named manufacturer, importer or responsible supplier;
5. the local warranty certificate and service contact; and
6. replacement-parts and software support for that exact market.

Retail listings can omit or shorten these details. The physical label and official model documents should take priority.

## Are Toshiba and Midea Appliances the Same?

No. They are related through group control, but the brands remain distinct. Products can use different industrial design, controls, components, software, accessories, distribution and warranty policies.

Even when two products share an underlying platform or factory, that does not prove full interchangeability. Parts, firmware, safety certification and service documentation can be tied to a specific model. Buyers should not substitute a Midea component into a Toshiba appliance—or the reverse—without an official cross-reference.

## Who Handles Toshiba Appliance Warranty and Service?

Warranty responsibility is market- and model-specific. The licensed brand structure can involve several separate roles:

- Toshiba Lifestyle or a regional group company as brand operator;
- a local importer or distributor as the legally named supplier;
- an authorized service company performing diagnosis and repair;
- a retailer applying its own return policy; and
- a factory or component supplier behind the product.

The logo alone does not identify which party must accept a claim. Use the warranty document supplied in the purchase country and confirm that imported products are eligible for local service before buying across borders.

## What Buyers and Distributors Should Verify

For consumers, the most practical checks are local warranty coverage, parts availability and the exact model documents. For distributors and sourcing teams, the diligence should go further:

1. Identify the legal contracting entity and its authorization to sell Toshiba-branded appliances.
2. Confirm the product category and territory covered by the brand arrangement.
3. Record the actual manufacturer and factory for each SKU.
4. Verify certification ownership, labeling and importer obligations.
5. Separate retail returns, statutory consumer rights and manufacturer warranty.
6. Check spare-parts, technician, software and recall procedures.
7. Avoid presenting a trademark license as a sale of Toshiba Corporation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Toshiba Appliances owned by Midea?

Midea controls Toshiba Lifestyle Products & Services, the operating company for Toshiba home appliances, after acquiring 80.1% in 2016. The Toshiba trademark itself remains licensed for this category rather than broadly sold to Midea.

### Did Midea buy Toshiba Corporation?

No. The transaction covered control of Toshiba Lifestyle and a long-term home-appliance trademark license, not Toshiba Corporation as a whole.

### How long can Midea use the Toshiba name for appliances?

The announced agreement granted a 40-year worldwide license for Toshiba-branded home appliances.

### Are Toshiba appliances made in Japan?

Some operations and products have Japanese connections, but origin varies. Check the exact model's rating label and official documents.

### Are Toshiba and Midea parts interchangeable?

Do not assume so. Use official parts lists and model-specific cross-references.

### Who should repair a Toshiba appliance?

Use the authorized service route stated in the local warranty. Imported models may not receive the same coverage.

## Final Answer

Midea Group controls Toshiba's home-appliance operating company through its 80.1% acquisition of Toshiba Lifestyle Products & Services. Midea also received a 40-year worldwide license to use the Toshiba brand for home appliances.

The relationship is therefore a combination of subsidiary control and trademark licensing—not a purchase of Toshiba Corporation. For any individual appliance, confirm the model label, responsible manufacturer, country of origin, importer and local warranty instead of relying only on the brand name.
