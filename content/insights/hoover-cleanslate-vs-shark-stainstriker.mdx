**Choose Hoover CleanSlate when you want a conventional one-formula workflow, a wide cleaning tool plus tight-spot access and an explicitly listed set of replaceable tanks, hose and rinse-tool parts. Choose Shark StainStriker when its dual-activated formula system, compact body and current three-year warranty fit your stain workload.**

Model suffixes and bundles matter. Compare CleanSlate Pet with the standard corded StainStriker PX200/PX201—not with a steam machine, HairPro bundle or full-size upright extractor.

## How Portable Extraction Works

Both machines perform the same core sequence:

1. spray diluted cleaning solution;
2. agitate the textile with a brush;
3. recover dissolved soil and water through a suction slot;
4. separate recovered liquid in a dirty tank.

The result depends on chemistry, dwell time, brush contact, tool seal, dry passes and ventilation.

Before wetting:

- vacuum loose dry debris;
- remove solids;
- blot excess liquid;
- identify the stain;
- read the textile care code;
- test an inconspicuous area.

Never mix bleach, ammonia, solvents or an unapproved high-foam detergent in either machine.

## Stain Removal and Formula Strategy

StainStriker’s differentiator is a two-solution system. CarpetXpert Deep Clean Pro and OXY Multiplier combine near use, avoiding manual premixing under the intended procedure.

This can simplify treatment of common pet, food and household stains. It also creates a recurring dependency on two compatible formula streams.

CleanSlate uses a more conventional tank-mix workflow with compatible Hoover solution. That makes dilution visible and can simplify inventory, but the user must measure correctly.

Evaluate each formula against:

- protein;
- tannin;
- oil;
- dye;
- urine and odor;
- old, oxidized stains.

No portable machine guarantees removal of every stain. Heat, bleach-sensitive dyes and prior chemical treatment can permanently set or alter fabric.

## Tools: Wide Areas vs Tight Access

CleanSlate commonly includes:

- a wider tool for cushions and carpeted stairs;
- a TightSpot tool for seams and corners;
- a hose-rinse tool.

StainStriker PX200/PX201 centers on a 3.5-inch reversible bristle tool and integrated crevice access, with bundle-specific pet tools.

Tool width changes the extraction seal. A wide nozzle is fast on flat carpet but can bridge curved bolsters. A narrow nozzle concentrates suction and reaches car-seat seams but requires more passes.

Test:

- spray uniformity;
- trigger control;
- visibility through the nozzle;
- bristle stiffness;
- hair collection before wetting;
- access for rinsing;
- replacement availability.

## Tanks, Hose and Reach

Separate clean and dirty tanks are easier to reason about than one mixed chamber. Check:

- usable, not just molded, capacity;
- float shutoff;
- gasket fit;
- one-handed removal;
- whether tanks stand at a sink;
- fill-line readability;
- corners that trap wastewater.

Hose routing is a major ownership issue. Repeated tight storage bends can crack the corrugated hose or strain its body connection.

For stairs and cars, add cord and hose reach, then subtract the distance needed to keep the machine level. Never place a full unit where it can fall or tip.

Hoover’s current support page lists replacement CleanSlate dirty and solution tanks, a hose assembly, a TightSpot tool and a hose-rinse tool. Availability at the time of failure still needs checking.

## Upholstery, Stairs and Car Interiors

### Upholstery

Use only on water-cleanable materials under the furniture maker’s instructions. “W” and some “WS” fabrics may be suitable; “S” and “X” codes require different care.

### Stairs

Work from a stable position. Keep the machine below the active step where possible and prevent the cord or hose from becoming a trip line.

### Cars

Vacuum first. Use minimal liquid around heated seats, occupancy sensors, side airbags, switches and electrical connectors. Extract repeatedly and leave doors open with strong airflow.

Neither product is a wet/dry utility vacuum for floods, hazardous liquid, ash or construction slurry.

## Residue, Drying and Odor

A stain that looks gone while wet can wick back from padding. A fabric that feels clean can retain sticky detergent.

Use:

- controlled wet passes;
- slow extraction passes without spray;
- a clean-water rinse only if the manual and fabric permit;
- repeated dry passes;
- fans and ventilation;
- a 24-hour reinspection.

Portable extractors can over-wet upholstery faster than they recover water. The operator—not the motor claim—controls this risk.

## Cleaning the Machine

Dirty organic water creates odor quickly. After every use:

1. empty and rinse the recovery tank;
2. rinse the lid, float and gasket;
3. flush the hose with the supplied method;
4. remove hair from the tool;
5. wipe tank seats and suction openings;
6. dry the tanks, hose and tools open;
7. store without formula.

CleanSlate’s dedicated rinse tool is a practical feature. StainStriker care varies by exact model; do not assume the basic PX200 has every HairPro self-clean feature.

## Parts, Warranty and Total Cost

Shark currently lists a three-year limited warranty on standard StainStriker product pages. Hoover’s CleanSlate Pet support page lists a two-year warranty when bought from Hoover.com. Seller, country and commercial use can change coverage.

Calculate:

`machine + formula per job + replacement tools/tanks/hose + shipping + cleaning labor + downtime`

For a detailer or cleaner, consumer warranty terms may exclude business use. A slightly higher-cost machine with locally stocked hose and tank parts can have lower business risk.

Before purchase, confirm:

- full model code;
- authorized seller;
- warranty guide;
- formula availability;
- hose coverage;
- tank and gasket stock;
- turnaround time;
- return shipping responsibility.

## FAQ

### Is Hoover CleanSlate better than Shark StainStriker?

CleanSlate has an attractive tool and service-parts proposition. StainStriker has a distinctive two-solution workflow and longer current warranty headline. Choose by the exact stain, bundle and local support.

### Which is better for car detailing?

Compare tool fit in seat seams, reach, hose storage, replacement parts and drying. Consumer warranties may not cover commercial use.

### Can I put boiling water in either machine?

No. Follow the fill-temperature limit in the exact manual. Excess heat can damage tanks, seals, pumps and fabric.

### Can I use another brand’s formula?

Use only solutions approved for the exact machine and textile. Wrong chemistry can foam, leave residue or damage components.

### Why does a cleaned spot smell later?

Common causes are contamination below the extraction depth, too much solution, poor recovery, a dirty hose or incomplete drying.
