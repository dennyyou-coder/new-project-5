**GARDENA is owned by Husqvarna Group.** The German garden brand joined Husqvarna in 2007. Husqvarna AB, the Swedish group's listed parent company, reports GARDENA as a separate division.

GARDENA is German. Werner Kress and Eberhard Kastner founded it in Ulm in 1961, and it is still based there.

## Is GARDENA the same as Husqvarna?

No. GARDENA and Husqvarna are separate brands in the same group. Husqvarna Group reports Gardena Division separately from its Husqvarna Forest & Garden and Construction divisions, and GARDENA keeps its own products, website and brand.

## What does GARDENA make?

GARDENA's main areas are watering, lawn care, tree and shrub care, and soil cultivation. Its smart SILENO robotic mowers connect to the GARDENA smart app, which uses Husqvarna Group account services. GARDENA joined the POWER FOR ALL battery alliance in 2020.

## Where are GARDENA products made?

Husqvarna Group runs GARDENA as a distinct division. In Germany, GARDENA Manufacturing GmbH is named as the contracting entity for its webshop and service. The country of manufacture for a given product is shown on its packaging.
