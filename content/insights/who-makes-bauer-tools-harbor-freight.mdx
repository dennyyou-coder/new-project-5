**Bauer is owned and sold by Harbor Freight Tools.** Harbor Freight's current product pages state that BAUER is a trademark of Harbor Freight Tools, and its 2026 corporate material lists Bauer among the retailer's exclusive tool brands.

Harbor Freight does not publicly identify one company as the manufacturer of every Bauer tool. The retailer controls the brand and product program, while individual drills, saws, vacuums, sprayers, batteries and accessories can come from different factories and production contracts.

## Is Bauer a Harbor Freight Brand?

Yes. Harbor Freight describes Bauer as one of its exclusive power-tool brands. Current product disclosures explicitly say **“BAUER is a trademark of Harbor Freight Tools.”**

That makes Harbor Freight the correct ownership answer—not TTI, Stanley Black & Decker, Bosch, Chervon or a retailer licensing an outside consumer brand. Harbor Freight sets the assortment, pricing, retail channel and support route.

## Who Actually Manufactures Bauer Tools?

The precise answer is model-specific.

Harbor Freight says its engineers and industry experts design tools for its exclusive portfolio, but its public catalogue does not provide a complete factory or OEM map. A supplier can change between generations even when the retail brand and item family stay familiar.

For a procurement or compliance check, collect:

- item number, UPC and model;
- rating label and country of origin;
- owner’s manual and certification file;
- importer or responsible entity;
- production date or lot; and
- factory evidence when buying directly or at commercial volume.

A marketplace list that names an alleged OEM is not sufficient evidence for the full Bauer range.

## Where Are Bauer Tools Made?

There is no responsible catalogue-wide country answer. The U.S. CPSC's 2024 recall for Bauer item 58671, a 20V four-gallon backpack sprayer, named Harbor Freight Tools as importer and China as the country of manufacture. That proves the origin of the recalled sprayer—not every Bauer drill, battery or vacuum.

The recall also shows why the item number matters. It covered one item/UPC combination and did not recall the entire Bauer 20V platform.

## How the Bauer 20V Platform Works

Harbor Freight currently promotes more than 65 compatible products in the Bauer 20V system. The assortment reaches beyond drills and saws into lighting, outdoor equipment, blowers, vacuums, paint equipment and property-maintenance tools.

![Bauer 20V platform and item-level verification map](/images/blog/bauer-20v-platform-map.svg)

Compatibility still has limits:

1. “20V” is a Bauer platform label, not an open industry standard.
2. Bauer packs should not be assumed compatible with Hercules, Warrior or another Harbor Freight brand.
3. A shared battery does not mean every tool has the same supplier, origin or warranty.
4. Confirm the exact battery, charger and tool manual before use.

## Bauer in Cleaning and Property Maintenance

Bauer is relevant to cleaning buyers because the platform includes wet-dry vacuums, portable vacuums, blowers, pressure-related equipment and sprayers. These products create recurring needs for filters, hoses, nozzles, batteries and switches.

For commercial use, compare availability of consumables and replacement parts with the expected duty cycle. A low tool price can become expensive if a fleet lacks standardized filters, spare batteries or rapid replacement.

The recalled backpack sprayer is especially important for cleaning and landscaping operators. Owners should match item 58671 and UPC 193175443663 against the current CPSC remedy rather than relying on appearance alone.

## Bauer Warranty and Service

Many current Bauer product pages state a **90-day limited warranty** against defects in materials and workmanship. This is not the same as the longer limited warranty Harbor Freight advertises for qualifying Hercules 20V brushless tools.

Harbor Freight also sells optional protection plans, but a paid plan is separate from the manufacturer warranty. Keep the receipt, item number, serial or date code and plan documentation. Check the exact product page and manual because terms can change.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who owns Bauer tools?

Harbor Freight Tools owns and operates the Bauer brand program.

### Does Harbor Freight manufacture Bauer tools itself?

Harbor Freight controls the brand and product system, but public evidence does not identify one universal factory. Manufacturing must be verified by item and production run.

### Are Bauer and Hercules batteries interchangeable?

Do not assume so. They are separate Harbor Freight brand platforms with different documented batteries and product positions.

### How many tools use Bauer 20V batteries?

Harbor Freight currently describes the Bauer 20V system as covering more than 65 compatible products.

### Do Bauer tools have a lifetime warranty?

No universal lifetime warranty applies. Many current Bauer power-tool pages show a 90-day limited warranty; verify the exact item and any separately purchased plan.

## Final Answer

Bauer is Harbor Freight's exclusive value-focused tool brand. Harbor Freight owns the trademark, controls the retail program and serves as importer or recall contact for specified products. It does not publish one universal Bauer manufacturer, so factory and origin claims must remain item-specific. The 20V platform is broad, but buyers should separately verify compatibility, warranty, recall status, parts and intended duty cycle.
