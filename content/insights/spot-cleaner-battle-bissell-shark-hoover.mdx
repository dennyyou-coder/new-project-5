For years, the portable spot cleaner was BISSELL's corner of floorcare. The Little Green, a small green box with a hose, sold for little more than USD 100, and few large brands thought the category worth fighting over.

That has changed. Between 2022 and 2024 TTI's Hoover and SharkNinja's Shark both launched dedicated spot cleaners, priced almost exactly against Little Green. Today the three American floorcare leaders are fighting over a machine that costs about USD 130.

## How the three brands got here

| Brand | Owner | Spot-cleaner line | Entry point | Price at launch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BISSELL | Bissell family | Little Green, Little Green HydroSteam | Long-running; HydroSteam Pet launched September 2023 | Category reference |
| Hoover | TTI (Hong Kong) | CleanSlate, CleanSlate XL, ONEPWR CleanSlate cordless | CleanSlate Pet, May 2022 | USD 129.99 |
| Shark | SharkNinja (NYSE) | StainStriker, plus CarpetXpert with StainStriker | Around 2024 | USD 139.99 (US PX201) |

![Timeline of the spot cleaner market: BISSELL Little Green, Hoover CleanSlate from 2022, BISSELL HydroSteam Pet in 2023 and Shark StainStriker around 2024](/images/articles/spot-cleaner-battle-bissell-shark-hoover/02-spot-cleaner-timeline.webp)

**BISSELL** built the category. Its Little Green line became one of the best-known products in American home cleaning, helped a great deal by social media during the pandemic years. In September 2023 BISSELL launched the Little Green HydroSteam Pet with TikTok creator Barbara Costello, known as Brunch with Babs, and called it the only portable deep cleaner with HydroSteam. A year later it upgraded the Little Green family again before the holiday season.

**Hoover**, owned by Techtronic Industries, came in with the CleanSlate Pet in May 2022 at USD 129.99. TTI then added a larger CleanSlate XL and, in its 2024 annual report, a cordless ONEPWR CleanSlate that runs on its power-tool-style battery platform. TTI's 2023 report called Hoover the world's fastest-growing spot cleaning brand; that is the company's own claim.

**Shark** arrived last. The StainStriker sells for USD 139.99 in the US and GBP 149.99 in the UK, and Shark sells it alone or together with its CarpetXpert full-size carpet cleaner. Shark's US product claims cite tests dated May 2024, which places the rollout around 2024.

## Why a USD 130 machine is worth fighting over

The money is not large by floorcare standards. Third-party research firms put the global carpet spot cleaner market at roughly USD 1.8 to 2 billion a year, but their estimates do not agree with each other and none is audited. For comparison, SharkNinja's Cleaning Appliances segment alone sold USD 617.2 million in the third quarter of 2026.

So why do Hoover and Shark bother? I see three reasons.

**First, it is the pet-owner's machine.** Every one of these launches was marketed to pet owners. A household that buys a spot cleaner for a dog or cat is the same household that buys a pet vacuum, a carpet cleaner and replacement formula. The spot cleaner is a low-cost way into that home.

**Second, it repeats.** Spot cleaners use branded cleaning formula, and the machines are cheap enough to replace or add to. That gives the brand a recurring relationship that a USD 600 cordless vacuum does not.

**Third, it is made for social media.** A before-and-after stain video is easy to film and easy to share. BISSELL used this better than anyone, and its HydroSteam Pet launch with a TikTok creator was built around it. For Shark, whose marketing machine is its biggest strength, it was a natural category to enter.

## What I expect next

This is my judgment, not a forecast from data.

**BISSELL will keep the lead in the near term**, but it now has to defend on two fronts at once: Hoover on price and battery platform, and Shark on marketing and retail reach. BISSELL is family-owned and has just changed CEO; Adam Madigan took over on October 1, 2026, with Mark Bissell staying as executive chairman. How hard it fights for this category will be one of the first signs of his priorities.

**The next challengers are likely to come from China.** Chinese brands such as Tineco and Dreame already make wet-dry floor washers, which use the same basic parts: a clean-water tank, a pump, a spray nozzle and a recovery tank. Moving into portable spot cleaners would be a short step for them, and the Chinese supply chain already makes many of these machines for other brands. I would not be surprised to see more Chinese-branded spot cleaners on Amazon over the next year or two.

**Cordless will become the battleground within the category.** Hoover has already moved its CleanSlate onto a shared battery platform. For brands with a battery system across many products, a cordless spot cleaner is an easy add-on, and it is harder for single-category players to match.

For buyers and retailers, the result is more choice at around USD 130 to 150, more pet-specific versions, and more pressure on formula pricing.
