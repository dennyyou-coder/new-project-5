**EGO and Greenworks are two of the biggest battery outdoor-tool brands, and both are Chinese-owned.** EGO, from Chervon, puts everything on one 56V battery system and positions itself at the premium end. Greenworks, from founder Yin Chen's Changzhou group, covers a wider spread of prices and voltages. The best choice depends on whether you want one battery for every tool or the lowest price for each job.

## Quick Comparison

| | EGO | Greenworks |
|---|---|---|

## Which battery system is better?

EGO's single 56V platform means every EGO battery fits every EGO tool, which matters if you plan to buy several. Greenworks splits its range across voltages, so check which platform a tool uses before assuming a battery fits.

## Which has robotic mowers?

Both. Greenworks sells AiMowbot robot mowers. EGO sells robotic mowers in Europe and the UK; check availability in your country. For more robot mower brands, see the [robot mower buying guides](/categories/robotic-mowers).

## Ownership

EGO is a brand of Chervon, whose CEO since September 2026 is Joseph Galli Jr., formerly head of TTI. Greenworks is controlled by founder Yin Chen, with STIHL holding a minority stake. Neither is owned by the other.
