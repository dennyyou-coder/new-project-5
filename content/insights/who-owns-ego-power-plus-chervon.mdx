**EGO Power+ is owned by Chervon.** Chervon presents EGO as one of its global brands, alongside FLEX, SKIL and DEVON. The group's holding company, Chervon Holdings Limited, is listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (2285). Chervon launched the EGO battery platform in 2014.

EGO is a Chinese-owned brand. Chervon traces its manufacturing business to 1993 and produces outdoor power equipment in Nanjing, China, and Binh Duong, Vietnam. In North America, EGO is sold by Chervon North America, based in Naperville, Illinois.

## Is EGO a Chinese company?

EGO is a brand rather than a company, and its owner, Chervon, is Chinese. The group started as a manufacturer in 1993 and built its own brands later. EGO's US and Canadian warranty is issued by Chervon North America, Inc.

## Who runs Chervon?

Joseph Galli Jr., who previously led Techtronic Industries, the owner of Milwaukee and Ryobi, became Chervon's chief executive on September 10, 2026. Founder Pan Longquan stepped down as CEO but remains chairman.

## Where are EGO tools made?

Chervon's outdoor power equipment unit lists production operations in Nanjing, China, and Binh Duong, Vietnam. Chervon describes a value chain spanning research, sourcing, manufacturing, testing and distribution.

## What does EGO make?

EGO sells mowers, string trimmers, blowers, chainsaws, snow blowers and commercial outdoor equipment on its 56V ARC Lithium battery system, and sells robotic mowers in Europe and the UK. EGO is sold in North America, Europe and Australia.
