**Gausium is the brand of Shanghai Gaussian Automation Technology Development Co., Ltd.; its shareholders are not disclosed.** Gausium's privacy policy names this Shanghai company as the one that independently develops and produces its cleaning robots. Gausium's official materials do not name a parent company.

Gausium is Chinese. The company was founded in 2013 and is headquartered in Shanghai. The brand was previously marketed in English as Gaussian Robotics, which matches the name of its legal company.

## Is Gausium a Chinese company?

Yes. The development and production company is in Shanghai. Gausium also publishes global office contacts and a dealer programme, but its public materials do not set out one contracting or warranty company for every country. In a given market, the quotation names the Gausium entity or dealer responsible.

## What does Gausium make?

- **Autonomous scrubbers and multi-purpose robots**: Scrubber 50 and 75, Omnie and Phantas.
- **Robotic vacuums and sweepers**: Vacuum 40 and the Beetle industrial sweeper.
- **Docking stations, cloud platform and apps** for fleet management.

Its robots run in airports, metros, offices and schools. Recent deployments include 27 Omnie robots on the Shenzhen Metro, a fleet of more than 90 robots in Latvia and a two-campus project at Tian An Cyber Park in Shenzhen.

## Where are Gausium robots made?

According to its privacy policy, Gausium robots are developed and produced by the Shanghai company. Gausium does not publish factory addresses in the sources we reviewed.
