## What the CrossWave Edge Cordless is

The CrossWave Edge Cordless is one of three CrossWave models BISSELL Australia lists for 2026, alongside the corded CrossWave EdgeFind and the cordless OmniForce EdgeFind. It vacuums and washes in one pass and adds ZeroGap edge cleaning for skirting boards and corners.

It is the entry cordless model in that group, at A$449 on the official Australian store.

## Read the specifications correctly

The 22-minute runtime is a maximum that varies by mode. The tanks hold 750 ml of clean water and 580 ml of dirty water, and the machine weighs 4.8 kg.

BISSELL lists 69 dBA. It does not have a vacuum-only mode, so dry debris on its own is picked up only while washing.

## Versions and market differences

Model 4328F is the code on BISSELL Australia's store; match it when comparing offers and reviews. The corded EdgeFind and the OmniForce EdgeFind add features such as FurFinder headlights (EdgeFind) and a PowerVac dry mode (OmniForce EdgeFind).

Warranty in Australia is two years with a 60-day money-back guarantee.

## Maintenance and ownership

Run the Self-Cleaning mode in the tray after each use and empty the dirty tank promptly. BISSELL recommends replacing the filter every three to four months.

Use lukewarm water and compatible formula only; BISSELL warns that hot water can warp the tank.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the 4328F for smaller homes with sealed hard floors that want a low-cost cordless washer with edge reach. Larger homes, or buyers who want hot-water washing and dock drying, should compare premium models.
