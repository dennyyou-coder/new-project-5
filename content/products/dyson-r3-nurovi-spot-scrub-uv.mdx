## What the R3 Nurovi Spot+Scrub UV is

The R3 is the top model in Dyson's 2026 Nurovi robot range. Its distinguishing feature is an AI stain scanner that uses UV light to check the floor between cleaning passes, so the robot can return to a stain until it is gone.

## Read the specifications correctly

Dyson lists 30,000 Pa of motor suction, a roller washed with 158°F (70°C) water from a 12-point hydration system, and recognition of almost 200 spills, stains and objects. The 65% suction increase is measured against Dyson's earlier Spot+Scrub AI, not against other brands. The up-to-15-passes figure applies to the toughest stains.

## Versions and market differences

Dyson presented the R3 on 3 September 2026, and the US product page was selling it with verified-owner reviews by October. The checked page did not display a price in text, and other markets follow their own launch schedules. The R1 and R2 Nurovi models launched at the same time with different cleaning systems.

## Maintenance and ownership

The dock is bagless: ten cyclones separate debris, and Dyson rates it for 100 days hands-free. It washes the roller with 158°F water, dries it with 131°F air over three hours and auto-doses Dyson 02 Probiotic solution, which is a consumable. Empty the bin, clean the roller and keep the UV and camera windows clean.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the R3 for hard-floor homes with frequent spills and buyers who prefer a bagless dock. Buyers outside the US should check local pricing and availability.
