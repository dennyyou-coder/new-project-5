## What to check before buying

Homes where low furniture and room thresholds interrupt daily robot cleaning. Start with the job the machine must do in your own space, then compare the features that can change that outcome. A specification sheet is useful for narrowing a shortlist; it does not replace a demonstration of the difficult part of your cleaning routine.

Measure every transition: the maximum double-step claim is not a single vertical step rating.

## Versions and market differences

This profile covers Saros 20, not Saros 20 Sonic or Saros 20 Flow. Manual-water and refill/drain dock configurations have different installation needs.

## Maintenance and ownership

Check the local price and availability of bags, filters, brushes and mop pads. Plan access to the dock tanks and wash tray before choosing its location.

Ask the seller to identify the local warranty provider, exclusions, repair location and spare-parts lead time in writing. Warranty terms and bundled accessories belong to the specific market and seller; this profile does not promise universal coverage.
