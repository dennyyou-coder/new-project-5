## Where Sora 70 sits in the Beatbot range

Beatbot introduced Sora 70 at [CES in January 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/beatbot-launches-sora-70-full-coverage-innovative-skimming-zero-hassle-302653086.html) as the first model in a separate Sora series. Its appeal is the combination of water-surface collection and underwater cleaning in a package that still uses manual basket care. That is a different ownership proposition from [AquaSense X](/products/beatbot-aquasense-x), whose service station is a major part of the purchase.

The first question is whether your pool needs active collection across the surface as well as cleaning below it. Leaves that remain floating can keep returning after a floor-only robot has finished. If your existing skimmer already handles them effectively, a simpler underwater cleaner may cover the remaining task. Decide from the debris pattern in your own pool before choosing between feature lists.

**WCB assessment:** shortlist Sora 70 for that combined cleaning role, while treating filter rinsing and lifting as continuing owner responsibilities. WCB has not independently tested its cleaning results or reliability.

## Core features in everyday use

### Floating debris and underwater dirt need different passes

JetPulse uses a water-jet approach to collect floating debris. The practical test is what happens near corners, ladders and changing wind, not only on a calm open surface. Observe whether leaves are collected or merely moved, and whether a second pass fits the time available before swimming.

### Shallow-platform coverage has two conditions

The [feature-page footnotes](https://beatbot.com/pages/sora-70) require both sufficient water depth and a platform at least one metre long and one metre wide. A narrow step does not meet that footprint just because it is submerged deeply enough. Measure the shelf, its entry route and the normal water level before comparing this feature with another cleaner’s shallow-water claim.

### Surface parking makes retrieval more approachable

Automatic surface parking can reduce the need to reach for a robot at the bottom. It does not remove the final lift from the water or the need to drain and carry the unit. Test the handle position against your coping height. The app’s one-touch return is specifically a surface function; it should not be described as continuous underwater remote control.

### A large basket with optional finer filtration

The [US store](https://beatbot.com/products/sora-70) distinguishes the supplied basket from the optional fine-filter accessory. Start with the type of dirt left behind after a normal pass. Buying finer media is most useful when it addresses that observed problem and you are willing to maintain it. It does not remove the need for the pool’s normal filtration and water care.

## Where it fits — and where to look elsewhere

**Pools with a mixture of leaves and settled dirt:** compare a full cleaning cycle with the routine you use now. Include the time spent retrieving the robot, rinsing the basket and dealing with missed edges. A machine that combines jobs should reduce the total routine, not merely offer more selectable modes.

**Pools with a broad tanning platform:** Sora 70 is worth investigating if the dimensions meet the published requirements. Request a demonstration that includes the approach to the platform as well as cleaning while on it. An isolated clip of the robot sitting on a shelf cannot establish that it will enter and leave yours reliably.

**Owners prioritising less filter handling:** compare AquaSense X and its station requirements. If station space or plumbing access is inconvenient, manual care may be the more practical choice. If floating-leaf collection is unnecessary, [ULTRAMARINE P1](/products/ecovacs-ultramarine-p1) offers a different, simpler task profile with its own depth restrictions.

## Versions and market differences

This guide follows the US Sora 70 listing, which offered Midnight Blue and Lavender Purple at the same observed base price. Sora 10, Sora 30, Sora P1 and retailer combinations should not inherit these specifications simply because their names overlap. Compare the precise model code, included filter and warranty territory.

The official pages contain some unevenly updated copy: one section describes five modes while another lists six, and older text says the fine-filter accessory is coming later even though the store offers it separately. This guide avoids a definitive mode-count claim and identifies the filter as an optional purchase. Confirm current firmware options and accessory delivery when those details determine the decision.

## Maintenance and ownership

Surface parking leaves the basket-cleaning task with the owner. Remove debris promptly, inspect the filter and brushes, and follow the supplied manual’s washing, drying and charging instructions. Avoid judging maintenance from the empty-basket weight alone; a full basket and an awkward deck position can change how comfortable the routine feels.

The optional fine-filter basket was listed at USD 59.99 on September 6, 2026. Check the selected offer before adding other accessories or assuming a promotional gift is included. The US store advertises a three-year warranty, but purchase territory, seller and the written terms still matter. Use [official support](https://beatbot.com/pages/beatbot-care) to confirm repairs and the correct local-language manual. The supplemental manufacturer PDF linked below is in Spanish and is labelled accordingly.

## WCB buying view

Sora 70 is a sensible candidate when surface cleaning and supported shallow-platform access solve real gaps in your routine. It becomes less relevant when an existing skimmer already collects the floating debris or when narrow steps are the main problem. Compare the completed pool and the maintenance afterward with a less expensive underwater-only option and a station-equipped alternative before deciding where the extra spending is useful.
