## The PE position in the L60 family

L60 Ultra PE is a specific configuration within Dreame’s 2026 L60 range. The US specification combines a 30,000 Pa maximum suction claim with a 103.8 mm body and a HyperStream DuoBrush arrangement. It should not inherit the slimmer body or higher suction figure of the regular L60 Ultra simply because the names are similar.

The useful question is whether the PE’s brush system, obstacle handling and station functions fit the home. A maximum pressure figure alone does not establish cleaning quality across carpets, joints and larger debris. This guide reports manufacturer specifications and their conditions; it does not present an independent hands-on pickup test.

## Measure furniture and floor transitions

The PE lists a 350 by 350 by 103.8 mm robot body and LDS-based SmartPathfinder navigation. Measure the lowest point under the actual furniture, allowing practical clearance rather than treating body height as the guaranteed access threshold. A lower competing model can make a material difference where dust accumulates beneath low cabinets or sofas.

Obstacle claims also need their geometry. Dreame gives 2.2 cm for a single threshold and 4 cm for a double-level transition made up of 2.2 and 1.8 cm sections, with spacing greater than 3 cm. That does not mean it can climb a single vertical 4 cm step. Identify whether the home’s transitions resemble the stated test arrangement.

## Brushes, carpets and station heat

HyperStream DuoBrush uses a dual-brush arrangement, distinct from the FE’s TriCut Brush 3.0. The side-brush and mop extension features address edges, while the stated mop lift is 10.5 mm. Check rug thickness and the relevant carpet settings; pad lift is not a guarantee that every thick or fringed rug will stay clear of damp material.

The station advertises up to 100°C washing, but the detailed footnote defines the washboard PTC heating area. It is not a measured floor temperature or a universal water-outlet temperature. Hot-air drying is listed separately. These functions support pad care at the dock and should not be turned into unsupported claims of whole-home sterilisation.

## Versions and market differences

Dreame’s L60 series announcement is dated 28 April 2026. The US PE listing showed $999.99 on 12 September 2026, with white and black options. Check the exact PE name and package: an accessory bundle or another L60 suffix can change both the price and the hardware being compared.

The regular L60 Ultra lists a lower 82 mm body and a higher 35,000 Pa maximum, while FE lists 97 mm and 28,000 Pa in its detailed specification. PE therefore should not be treated as an automatic upgrade over every similarly named model. Compare the features needed for the home and the current regional price of each configuration.

## Maintenance and ownership

The PE specification lists a 250 ml robot bin, a 3.2 L station bag and 4.5 L clean-water and 4 L used-water tanks. Those capacities do not prescribe fixed emptying intervals. Pet hair, large floor areas and frequent mopping can increase the frequency of bin, bag and water-system care.

Automatic solution dispensing and dirt-responsive rewash or remop features reduce some manual decisions, but owners still need to inspect brushes, remove trapped material, clean the wash area and maintain tanks and sensors. Use approved cleaning solution and follow the manual. An optional plumbing arrangement requires the correct regional kit and suitable installation; it should not be assumed to be included in the standard tank-based package.

## When PE is the sensible shortlist

PE deserves consideration when its dual-brush setup and specified transition capability address the household’s priorities, and its 103.8 mm body clears the furniture. FE is worth comparing for a lower listed body and lower dated purchase price, with different brush and threshold specifications. The regular L60 Ultra is relevant where low furniture access is central.

Before ordering, measure the hardest transition and the lowest furniture opening, identify the dominant debris and review replacement bags, pads and brushes. Compare a complete ownership package rather than a headline pressure number. The specification table and official references make those distinctions visible without assuming identical performance from similar model names.
