## What to check before buying

Hard-floor homes where food stains and edge mopping matter in the buying decision. Start with the job the machine must do in your own space, then compare the features that can change that outcome. A specification sheet is useful for narrowing a shortlist; it does not replace a demonstration of the difficult part of your cleaning routine.

Check roller access, dirty-water handling and carpet protection; automated washing still needs upkeep.

## Versions and market differences

This page covers X12 OmniCyclone. Other X12 dock packages and country variants may differ. The manufacturer compares the roller with the previous generation; WCB has not reproduced that comparison in a controlled test.

## Maintenance and ownership

Check roller, filter and brush part numbers locally. Allow room to remove and clean the dust container and water tanks rather than placing the dock in a tight cabinet.

Ask the seller to identify the local warranty provider, exclusions, repair location and spare-parts lead time in writing. Warranty terms and bundled accessories belong to the specific market and seller; this profile does not promise universal coverage.
