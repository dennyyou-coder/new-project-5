## What the Vision Cloud 4WD is

Landroid Vision Cloud is WORX's 2026 wire-free platform. It combines stereo-camera Vision AI for obstacle and edge recognition, V-SLAM mapping, and RTK positioning whose corrections arrive over the cloud instead of from an antenna in the garden.

The 4WD version adds four motors, independent front and rear axles and front-wheel steering, which WORX says protects turf on turns. WR344 is the 1-acre version.

## Read the specifications correctly

The 84% (40°) slope figure is a four-wheel-drive rating; the two-wheel-drive Vision Cloud is a different machine. One panel on the product page says 83%, so read the slope as about 40°.

Cutting width is 8.7 in with electric height adjustment from 1.57 to 3.54 in. The weight with battery is 39.9 lb.

## Versions and market differences

The US product page offers ¼-acre (WR341), ½-acre (WR342), 1-acre (WR344) and 1½-acre (WR346) versions. The FiatLux night-vision light module is standard on selected models and optional across the range.

RTK Cloud availability depends on location; the page asks buyers to check their address. Prices were not shown on the checked page.

## Maintenance and ownership

Keep the stereo cameras clean and replace blades regularly; the box includes spare blade kits. The deck is listed as hose-washable.

Software updates arrive over the air or by USB. Plan for cellular and cloud dependence: positioning quality relies on the RTK Cloud service and on 4G or Wi-Fi coverage.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the WR344 for sloped, complex lawns up to 1 acre where installing a local RTK antenna is unattractive. Confirm RTK Cloud coverage at the address and the local price first.
