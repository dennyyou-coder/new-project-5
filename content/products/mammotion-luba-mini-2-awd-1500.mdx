## What to check before buying

Lawns where slope traction and the amount of remaining edge trimming deserve close attention. Start with the job the machine must do in your own space, then compare the features that can change that outcome. A specification sheet is useful for narrowing a shortlist; it does not replace a demonstration of the difficult part of your cleaning routine.

Verify the navigation version and boundary slope rules; a maximum slope claim is not a safe operating plan.

## Versions and market differences

The currently referenced EU 1500 listing describes LiDAR and dual-camera navigation. Other LUBA mini 2 versions, older listings and bundle text can refer to different positioning systems. Match the SKU and manual before ordering.

## Maintenance and ownership

Check replacement blades for both cutting discs, service support and connectivity renewal terms. Walk the lawn for drop-offs, wildlife and movable objects before scheduling unattended operation.

Ask the seller to identify the local warranty provider, exclusions, repair location and spare-parts lead time in writing. Warranty terms and bundled accessories belong to the specific market and seller; this profile does not promise universal coverage.
