## What the Roomba Max 875 Combo is

The Max 875 Combo is iRobot's most powerful robot to date and the flagship of its 2026 range, launched after the company's acquisition by Picea. It combines a sealed carpet-cleaning airflow system with a large roller mop and an AutoWash dock that washes and dries the roller.

Its design addresses both of iRobot's traditional strengths on carpet and the roller mopping that competitors have pushed into the premium segment.

## Read the specifications correctly

iRobot lists 35,000 Pa of suction and 20 L/s of airflow. The SealForce skirt keeps airflow close to the carpet, and iRobot claims three times deeper carpet cleaning than vacuuming without it. Several other claims compare the 875 with earlier Roombas, such as 3.75x deeper scrubbing than the Roomba Max 775.

Those comparisons are useful inside iRobot's range but are not cross-brand measurements.

## Versions and market differences

The launch release gives a USD 1,199 MSRP with the AutoWash dock and availability at irobot.com from 2 September 2026, with North American retail later in September. Other regions may receive the model on different dates or with different docks; check local listings.

## Maintenance and ownership

The dock washes the roller with 176°F water, dries it with 131°F air, doses StayClean concentrate and empties debris into a carbon AllergenLock bag rated for up to 90 days. Bags, concentrate and the roller are consumables.

Clean the dock's wash tray and check the dual anti-tangle brushes regularly; heated washing reduces but does not remove routine care.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the Max 875 Combo for homes with substantial carpet as well as hard floors. Compare dock size, consumable costs and local service with Roborock and Dreame flagships before deciding.
