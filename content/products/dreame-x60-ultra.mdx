## What to check before buying

Mixed-floor homes looking for low-clearance access and less daily mop handling. Start with the job the machine must do in your own space, then compare the features that can change that outcome. A specification sheet is useful for narrowing a shortlist; it does not replace a demonstration of the difficult part of your cleaning routine.

Check carpet behavior, dock access and local consumable costs before using peak suction to choose.

## Versions and market differences

The reference is the US X60 Ultra. X60 Max, Ultra Complete and retailer bundles should be checked separately for hardware, accessories and service coverage.

## Maintenance and ownership

Budget for dust bags, filters, mop pads and brush replacement. Confirm that the dock wash area is accessible and that cleaning solution is approved for the machine.

Ask the seller to identify the local warranty provider, exclusions, repair location and spare-parts lead time in writing. Warranty terms and bundled accessories belong to the specific market and seller; this profile does not promise universal coverage.
