## What the station changes

FLOOR ONE STATION S9 Scientist Pro combines a manually guided washer with a larger clean-water reservoir at its station. The [US listing](https://store.tineco.com/products/tineco-floor-one-station-s9-scientist-pro-wet-dry-vacuum-cleaner) describes automatic heated-water refilling when the machine returns. This architecture matters when frequent small-tank refills are an annoyance. It does not turn the washer into a robot or provide an uninterrupted water connection while the user moves through the home.

Separate three capacities when planning a session: the station’s clean-water supply, the washer’s onboard clean water and its collected used water. A large reservoir can reduce one refill task while the dirty-water tank still needs attention. WCB has not measured the number of room-cleaning cycles between those tasks; buyers should evaluate the entire routine with their own floor area and dirt load.

## Spot treatment and floor finish

HydroBurst targets residue with a water jet. It is a different treatment from steam, even though the wider S9 range includes products with other heating systems. Test it on a recurring kitchen or dining-area mark that the flooring instructions permit you to wet-clean. Observe the dried finish and the number of passes rather than selecting from a heater headline alone.

The StreakFree Scraper changes position with travel direction. That makes both forward and backward passes relevant to a demonstration, especially when turning away from a wall or withdrawing from under furniture. Automatic iLoop adjustment can vary water and suction, but the operator should still inspect moisture and residue. A machine’s response to visible dirt is not a substitute for checking the result.

## Runtime, handling and station space

The advertised maximum runtime should remain attached to its operating conditions. It does not establish a 100-minute heated-water session in every mode. Include returns to the station, used-water disposal and treatment of difficult patches when judging how long a whole cleaning job takes. Battery endurance is only one part of that elapsed time.

Try SmoothDrive assistance along a tight route and separately assess carrying over a step. Measure the lowest obstruction under furniture, leaving a margin beyond the published lay-flat profile. At the station, allow room to remove and refill a large reservoir comfortably. The best location balances access to a suitable water-disposal point with a clear route onto the floor.

## Maintenance and ownership

The owner still handles collected dirty water and debris. The listing describes emptying dirty water before station care, a step worth testing during a demonstration. Check how easily the collection parts come apart and whether they can dry in the available storage space. More automation at the station should be evaluated against the manual steps that remain.

FlashDry’s heated-air figure and SilentDry’s noise claim refer to station processes. Neither describes the temperature on the floor or the noise of active washing. Inspect the roller after the selected cycle and follow cleaning instructions for the station itself. Obtain local prices for rollers, filters and approved solution; those repeated costs belong alongside the initial station purchase.

## WCB buying view

Scientist Pro is relevant when its refill architecture removes a frequent inconvenience. Compare [T16 Pro Heat](/products/dreame-t16-pro-heat) for a hot-water washer with a different battery arrangement and [F25 ACE Pro](/products/roborock-f25-ace-pro) for foam-assisted cleaning. Decide using the full water-handling routine, floor finish and available station space. The large reservoir is valuable only if the resulting workflow is easier to sustain.
