## What the S10 MaxV Ultra Steam is

The S10 MaxV Ultra Steam is a Roborock flagship launched first in South Korea in October 2026. It combines VibraRise 5.0 vibrating mopping, a lifting chassis for raised thresholds and a dock that adds steam to hot-water mop washing.

## Read the specifications correctly

Roborock Korea lists 36,000 Pa maximum motor output, 14 N mop pressure and 4,000 vibrations per minute, all from internal tests. The 8.8 cm climbing figure applies to a two-step threshold of 4.5 and 4.3 cm. The bacteria-removal claim describes the dock's treatment of the mop in Roborock's tests, not disinfection of floors.

## Versions and market differences

The Korean page offers two options: the standard dock and a version with automatic water supply and drainage. Aju Press reported preorders from 1 to 11 October and a retail launch on 12 October 2026. Specifications, names and dock options can change in later markets.

## Maintenance and ownership

The dock self-cleans its base with water up to 100°C, dries the dust path with air up to 63°C and empties dust for up to 65 days. It also doses detergent and refills the robot's tank with warm water. The mop, the DuoDivide brush and FlexiArm side brush still need routine inspection.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist it for homes with raised room transitions and buyers who value dock hygiene. Compare it with Roborock's Saros 20 for climbing and with roller-mop flagships for mopping style.
