## What the Roomba Plus 678 Combo is

The Plus 678 Combo shares the SealForce carpet airflow and AutoWash dock concept of the Max 875 Combo at a lower price. Instead of the 875's heated mist, it uses SprayForce AI, a high-pressure spray that pretreats stains before the roller reaches them.

## Read the specifications correctly

iRobot lists 30,000 Pa suction, 18 L/s airflow and 15 N roller pressure. Its 40% mopping-efficiency claim compares the 678 with and without SprayForce, not with other brands. The release does not give a roller speed for this model; do not assume the 875's 300 rpm.

## Versions and market differences

The launch release gives a USD 899 MSRP and availability at irobot.com from 2 September 2026, with North American retail later in September. International availability was not stated in the release.

## Maintenance and ownership

The dock washes the roller with 176°F water, dries it with 131°F air and empties debris for up to 90 days. Automatic concentrate dosing is not listed for the 678, so plan to add cleaning solution manually if needed.

The hair-cutting main brush and anti-tangle edge brush reduce wrapping but should still be checked, and the spray nozzle should be kept clear.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the Plus 678 Combo for carpet-heavy homes that want a washing dock below USD 1,000 in the US. If stubborn kitchen stains are the priority, compare it with the 875's heated mist.
