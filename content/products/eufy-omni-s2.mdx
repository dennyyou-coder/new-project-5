## S2 launch history and positioning

Omni S2 is a roller-mop robot with a tall automated station and a broad set of cleaning features. Its [US retail listing](https://www.eufy.com/products/t2081111) gives a dated purchase-price reference, while the [EU feature page](https://www.eufy.com/eu-en/robot-vacuum-s2) details the HydroJet system used in this guide. Confirm the exact local version before assuming every regional feature or consumable package is identical.

The launch timeline deserves precision. S2 was previewed at IFA 2025, and Anker’s [CES 2026 announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/anker-innovations-showcases-iconic-lineup-of-charging-smart-home-and-entertainment-devices-during-ces-2026-302653788.html) scheduled full US retail for January 20, 2026. That regional launch is why it appears in this year’s selection. It is not being presented as a model first revealed in 2026, and this guide does not claim WCB hands-on testing.

## Wide roller cleaning and wet-floor results

A broad roller can cover a useful strip of floor, but room geometry determines how much it reaches on each pass. Dining chairs, narrow gaps and curved furniture break up the open-floor path. When comparing S2, examine the area beside baseboards and around table legs after the floor dries. More water-delivery holes do not independently prove fewer missed spots.

The refresh and recovery system should be evaluated with ordinary household residue. A kitchen film, a footprint trail and dried drink marks can require different settings. Watch the complete cleaning routine, including any return to the station. The relevant result is the work removed from your schedule, rather than a brief close-up of a roller spinning in a prepared demonstration.

## Carpet transitions and movement limits

The advertised mop lift is a useful feature to investigate in a mixed-floor home. It still needs to clear your actual rug, including its edges and pile. A high-pile capability claim is not a promise about every soft rug or fringe. Start with supervised runs and confirm the app’s carpet treatment matches how you want the rooms cleaned.

Threshold claims also depend on geometry. A single rise and a two-stage transition are different obstacles. More importantly, do not confuse these abilities with climbing a staircase. MarsWalker was shown as separate hardware in earlier announcements; its presence in a demonstration does not establish that it is available or included in an ordinary S2 retail purchase.

### Cleaning a floor without the station

[eufy’s S2 multi-floor guidance](https://service.eufy.com/eu/article-description/Does-the-S2-Robot-Support-Multiple-Floors-Can-the-S2-Robot-Save-Multiple-Maps) allows up to five saved maps. Carry the robot to the new floor, open the required doors and clear loose objects before creating a map. Leave it undisturbed while mapping; it returns to the starting point when no station is available there.

For a wet-cleaning task, wash the mop at the station before carrying S2 upstairs. eufy suggests low water and suction settings to reduce return trips. If onboard water runs out, it can switch to vacuum-only operation. Refill through the station as needed and carry the robot back for self-cleaning after the job. A saved upstairs map therefore does not remove the need for carrying and water management.

## Maintenance and ownership

The station automates recurring tasks, but the owner still manages water, collected debris and wear parts. Assess whether the tank arrangement is comfortable to service at the planned location. Leave enough space to reach the controls and remove components. If the unit is placed in a cupboard, confirm the manual’s clearance and ventilation requirements before planning around a tight fit.

Compare the roller removal process, scraper access and replacement supply with [Narwal Flow 2](/products/narwal-flow-2) and [Saros 20 Flow](/products/roborock-saros-20-flow). These systems use different cleaning surfaces and care routines. Budget for the parts you will actually replace, plus approved solution and any fragrance you choose to use, rather than treating an automated dock as cost-free maintenance.

### A care schedule tied to the S2 manual

The [English T2081 user guide](https://salesforce-knowledge-download.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/000032309/en_US/000032309.pdf) recommends cleaning the dirty-water reservoir and filter tray every 30 operating hours, and the dustbin and rolling brush every 60 hours. Its replacement reference is 180 hours for the rolling mop and 360 hours for the high-performance filter. These are manufacturer service recommendations, not guaranteed component lifetimes; inspect sooner after unusually dirty work and follow app reminders.

The dustbin and filter must air-dry completely after rinsing before reuse. The manual excludes brushes, hot water and detergent from filter cleaning. Its care schedule appears on PDF page 19 and filter instructions on page 20. Use S2-specific consumables and instructions rather than importing C28 service intervals.

### What error 6119 helps you check

The [official S2 blockage guide](https://service.eufy.com/eu/article-description/What-Should-I-Do-If-My-S2-Shows-Error-6119-Base-Station-Dust-Collection-Duct-Blocked) starts with the dust bag, then checks the robot’s dustbin and rear collection openings, followed by the station’s collection port. A full bag and an obstructed passage need different attention. Follow the illustrated procedure and the appliance’s safety instructions, then run a collection cycle to check the result. eufy also suggests Standard dust collection and more frequent emptying for pet-hair-heavy use.

## WCB buying view

S2 is relevant for buyers who want a wide self-rinsing roller and a substantial carpet-lift feature in one system. Its value depends on physical fit, actual rug transitions and an acceptable station-care routine. Compare it using those tasks and the full local purchase cost. Keep stair-climbing expectations separate, and treat pressure, air-watt and object-recognition headlines as manufacturer claims that need practical context.
