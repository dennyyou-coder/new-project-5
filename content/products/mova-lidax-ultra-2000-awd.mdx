## What the AWD chassis changes

LiDAX Ultra 2000 AWD combines four driven wheels, independent suspension and two cutting discs in a larger mower than the non-AWD LiDAX Ultra 1000. The main reasons to consider it are traction, a wider 40 cm cutting path and the ability to manage more demanding lawn geometry. A higher capacity label alone is not the whole change.

This guide follows the exact global 2000 AWD variant and the dated March 2026 launch campaign. It should not be confused with the non-AWD series or a later AWD Flex family. The official documentation supports the specification, while real mowing output and traction still require evaluation in the intended garden.

## Interpret slopes and obstacle claims

MOVA advertises four 116 W hub motors, independent suspension and obstacle crossing up to 6 cm. The slope limit is 80%, approximately 38.6 degrees. Percent gradient expresses rise relative to horizontal distance; treating it as 80 degrees would dramatically misstate the capability.

Actual slope use also depends on wet grass, turns, soil and the location of boundaries. Check the route to the charging station and areas where the mower would need to turn near a drop. Mechanical capability does not remove the need for mapped exclusions and appropriate separation from pools, roads or other unsuitable boundaries.

## Separate capacity, runtime and charging

The 2000 version supports a 2,000 m² map, while dual-map management allows two independent areas. That does not establish 4,000 m² of daily mowing. The separate 800–1,000 m² per-charge and 150–170 minute figures are laboratory operating claims that must be considered alongside charging and transfer between isolated lawns.

The page lists a 7.5 Ah battery and 243 Wh energy, with a 36 V marketing class. Use the stated energy rather than deriving a conflicting value from the class label. Charging also uses different intervals: 65 minutes refers to 15–95%, while about 90 minutes describes a full charge. They should remain separately labelled.

## Versions and market differences

Select the complete LiDAX Ultra 2000 AWD name and regional model code. The 1000 and 1600 AWD listings, the non-AWD LiDAX range and ViAX use different capacity or hardware combinations. This guide does not treat a global placeholder price as a usable offer; the exact regional retail price remains unverified.

The global page advertises three years of 4G service. Confirm the included module, local network coverage, service activation and renewal terms before assigning value to that claim. Two saved maps may also require an additional charging station or manual relocation when the mower cannot travel between the lawns.

## Maintenance and ownership

The cutting system uses two discs with six blades each and electronically adjustable 3–10 cm height. Wider cutting can reduce the number of passes on open ground, but narrow areas and frequent turns reduce that benefit. Inspect blades and the underside according to the manual, with power safely disabled before working near the cutting assembly.

UltraTrim 2.0 extends the discs and advertises a remaining gap under 3 cm along raised boundaries. Straddling is suitable only for safe, level, traversable edges; planting, drops and awkward borders can still need separate trimming. The 23.6 kg weight and 740 × 532 mm footprint also matter for handling and storage. IPX6 does not permit immersion or disregard of cleaning instructions.

## When a smaller mower is enough

Choose the 2000 AWD shortlist when its traction, cutting width and supported area solve measured requirements. ViAX 500 offers a smaller front-drive alternative for a more modest lawn, while the non-AWD LiDAX Ultra 1000 provides another distinct rear-drive configuration. Neither should be described as the same mower with a different battery label.

Before ordering, compare the actual lawn map, slopes, clear passages, charging position and available operating hours. Ask for a local demonstration when a difficult transition or hillside is the central reason for choosing AWD, and obtain the delivered regional price with accessories and service terms itemised.
