## Why the Premium package exists

WD563 combines the AquaReach wand concept with a more complete set of tools and a heated care dock. The extra equipment is most relevant when a household regularly moves between open flooring and narrow or raised hard surfaces. It is less compelling when the appliance will only make short passes across an uncomplicated kitchen floor.

The buying decision should focus on which included functions remove recurring work. An accessory that stays in a cupboard contributes little practical value. Conversely, a tool that reliably replaces an awkward manual step may matter more than a headline specification. This guide evaluates the published configuration without assigning a cleaning score or claiming a hands-on test.

## Two tools need two practical checks

The Stain Scrubber and Detailer Mop create different contact areas for targeted work. Before choosing the package, think about the spaces that need access and the type of residue usually found there. A larger contact pad and a focused pickup opening do not necessarily solve the same problem, even when they connect to the same wand.

Ask to see how the tool is attached, moved around a corner and cleaned afterwards. The appliance must also remain positioned safely while the wand is used. For work near cabinets and bathroom fixtures, verify the surface finish and avoid directing liquid into joints, fittings or areas whose moisture tolerance is unknown.

## Understand what the heated dock does

The launch announcement describes warm-air drying as part of roller care. That function can be relevant where storing a damp roller is inconvenient. It does not change the cleaner into a steam appliance, and its air-temperature claim cannot establish the temperature reached by flooring during use.

Likewise, a laboratory reduction of bacteria on a brushroll is a narrowly defined test. It is not evidence that a room or every surface cleaned by the appliance has been disinfected. Buyers should assess the dock for its care routine, drying requirements and placement rather than treating a hygiene percentage as a universal result.

## Versions and market differences

Shark’s naming makes the model number particularly important here. The launch release used AquaReach Pro, while the current store identifies WD563 as Premium and gives WD561 the Pro label. Keep the code on the order, carton and support record aligned. Similar names are insufficient to confirm identical contents.

The WD563 store snapshot was $479.99 on 12 September 2026. Its published endurance is less straightforward: the package headline says 50 minutes, while a specification field says 60. We leave that conflict visible and unverified. A buyer whose cleaning plan depends on a particular runtime should request a model-specific explanation of the mode and test conditions.

## Maintenance and ownership

The dock can rinse and dry the roller, but it cannot take the dirty-water container to a sink or dispose of trapped solids. Budget a short cleanup period after each session. Inspect the wand tools as well as the floor head, because changing attachments creates additional places where residue can remain.

Included spare pads and filters may help rotate parts during care, but their presence does not make ownership cost fixed. Cleaning frequency, hair load and detergent consumption vary. Check replacement availability before importing a package, and confirm that the local warranty applies to the exact model and seller rather than assuming a US term applies everywhere.

## Compare the whole session

The most useful comparison with WD551 is the work performed after and around the main floor pass. If heated care and the second wand tool are used regularly, the higher package has a concrete purpose. If those features are seldom needed, their extra purchase cost and storage requirements deserve more weight.

For comparisons with other floor washers, also consider access beneath furniture, turning effort, tank handling and the walk to the dock. Try carrying the machine as well as moving it on a showroom floor. These checks can reveal a poor fit that neither a larger accessory bundle nor a longer advertised battery figure would resolve.
