## What Steam Gen 2 is designed to do

F25 Ultra Steam Gen 2 adds a steam-focused option to Roborock’s manually guided floor-care range. The [official product page](https://global.roborock.com/pages/roborock-f25-ultra-steam-gen-2) describes several operating modes rather than one fixed cleaning treatment. That matters because daily dust and a dried kitchen spill may call for different settings. The owner still chooses the route and steers the machine, even with powered-wheel assistance.

This source-based guide covers the model introduced in the [IFA 2026 lineup](https://au.roborock.com/pages/ifa-2026). WCB has not tested cleaning speed, noise or stain removal. A regional selling price is not verified here. Treat the launch as a shortlist opportunity and confirm the exact local package before comparing its value with a washer already available for delivery.

## Heat claims need their measurement points

The steam headline refers to an internal heater reading. The hot-water and dock-temperature claims describe other parts of the system. Those figures should not be collapsed into a single temperature that supposedly reaches the floor. Ask a seller to identify the operating mode and measurement location whenever heat is presented as the main reason to purchase.

Compatibility comes before performance claims. Check whether the flooring supplier permits steam, hot water and the proposed detergent. A successful laboratory test on a particular wood sample cannot establish approval for another finish, adhesive or construction. For a mixed-floor home, plan where modes must change and where the cleaner should not be used at all.

## Plan around the mode you will use

Steam operation has a much shorter published runtime than the Auto/Eco maximum. Estimate the area you expect to steam, the time needed for difficult patches and the remaining ordinary cleaning. A maximum number achieved in another mode is not a useful promise for that session. Repeated passes, heavy residue and turning around furniture also consume time.

The warm-start claim deserves similar care: it follows prior steam operation and a short stop. It does not establish performance when first removing a cold machine from storage. Test the whole routine during a demonstration, including preparation, steering, cleaning beneath furniture and returning to the dock. Convenience is the combined experience, not one isolated fast step.

## Versions and market differences

Keep Ultra Steam Gen 2 on the quotation. The F25 range includes different cleaning approaches, including the [ACE Pro](/products/roborock-f25-ace-pro) with JetFoaming. A shared body style or steering system does not prove identical steam hardware, detergent requirements or replacement rollers. Ask for the regional manual and part numbers that match the purchased model.

An announcement page and a local retail offer answer different questions. Before ordering, verify price, delivery, warranty and the included roller or solution bundle. Compare like-for-like packages and account for approved consumables. If a model is offered as an import, clarify repair arrangements before assuming the domestic brand support channel will service it.

## Maintenance and ownership

Self-washing and drying handle parts of roller care, but collected liquid and solids still require attention. Place the dock where it is easy to reach a sink and remove the collection components. Consider splash-safe surfaces and space for parts to dry according to the manual. A machine stored in a difficult corner can make an otherwise short cleaning task inconvenient.

Price the roller, filters, collection components and approved solution locally. Inspect for trapped hair and debris as directed, even when the scraper system is designed to reduce tangling. Choose a drying cycle that suits the time available, then check the actual roller condition. A quoted air-duct temperature does not prove every recess in the collection system has dried.

## WCB buying view

Steam Gen 2 is relevant when heated cleaning addresses a recurring problem and the floor permits it. Compare its practical steam session with the foam-focused ACE Pro and the simpler [PencilWash](/products/dyson-pencilwash) care routine. Resolve local price and support, then choose on demonstrated floor finish, comfortable steering and realistic after-use care. Keep the qualified temperature and runtime numbers attached to the conditions that produced them.
