## What the Qrevo 2 Pro is

The Qrevo 2 Pro is a 2026 Qrevo-series robot with twin spinning mops, a multifunctional washing dock and a newly developed reversible side brush aimed at room corners. It sits below Roborock's Saros and Qrevo Edge models in price and features.

## Read the specifications correctly

The UK page headlines 25,000 Pa HyperForce suction but also lists an 18,500 Pa digital motor figure; both come from Roborock's internal tests. The 200 rpm mop speed applies in a specific custom mode. The 30% corner-coverage gain is measured against a standard side brush, not against other brands.

## Versions and market differences

Roborock Japan sold the model through Yamada Denki from 2 October 2026 after reservations from 24 September. The UK site lists it for sale through Roborock and Amazon UK. The Japanese notice did not repeat the full specification, so use the UK page for figures and check local listings for price.

## Maintenance and ownership

The dock washes the mops at up to 75°C, dries them at up to 45°C and empties dust for up to 65 days. The cleaning tray can go up to 170 days between maintenance in Roborock's conditions. Mop pads, the dust bag and the DuoDivide main brush still need routine checks.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the Qrevo 2 Pro for buyers who want hot-water mop washing and good corner coverage without paying for a flagship lifting chassis or roller mop.
