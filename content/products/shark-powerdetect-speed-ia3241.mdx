## What the auto-empty system changes

Shark PowerDetect Speed Clean & Empty IA3241 is a dry cordless vacuum built around a practical routine: collect everyday debris, return the cleaner to its station and let the dock take over dust transfer and charging. The US system launched on April 14, 2026. Its main buying argument is reducing how often you handle the small onboard dust cup, while retaining a detachable handheld for stairs, upholstery and corners.

![Shark PowerDetect Speed IA3241 cordless vacuum standing on its auto-empty charging station](/images/articles/shark-powerdetect-speed-ia3241/02-auto-empty-station.webp)

[Official SharkNinja launch photograph](https://s202.q4cdn.com/977000893/files/doc_multimedia/2026/4/1010952764/4d01acf0-7001-462a-b190-140c4c3c8de7.jpg).

The station’s 2 L capacity and claimed storage of up to 45 days can suit frequent small cleanups. Households collecting substantial pet hair should assess their actual emptying interval. The station also needs accessible floor space near power, which matters in a narrow cupboard or small apartment.

## Pickup, sensing and furniture access

TurboPro Detect is Shark’s self-cleaning brushroll design for pickup on forward and backward strokes. The current US IA3241 page lists Dirt Detect, Floor Detect and Direction Detect. These are manufacturer-described functions, not evidence that WCB has measured pickup superiority. The launch release also mentions Edge Detect; this guide follows the narrower, exact US product listing rather than transferring every family-level claim.

MultiFLEX bends the wand to reach below furniture and folds it for storage. Check the actual head clearance under sofas before treating bendability as full access. Blue Reveal illumination helps expose dust on the floor; it is distinct from the sensing system and does not certify that a surface is hygienically clean.

### Independent test check

[RTINGS published its IA3241 laboratory review on August 5, 2026](https://www.rtings.com/vacuum/reviews/shark/powerdetect-speed), using an Ice Blue Clean & Empty unit. It reported effective carpet pet-hair pickup, with weaker results on high-pile carpet, floor cracks and fine-particle containment. Those findings add a practical limit to the manufacturer’s filtration and pickup claims. They are RTINGS results under its own test method; WCB has not independently tested this vacuum.

## Runtime, weight and cleaning sessions

The advertised 60 minutes uses ECO mode with a non-motorized tool on the hand vacuum. That configuration differs from routine powered-floorhead cleaning. Buyers who want to vacuum several carpeted rooms continuously should seek a test matching that workload. The listed six-hour recharge makes returning the cleaner to its station after use especially relevant.

Shark specifies a 3.5 lb hand vacuum and markets the cleaner at about 7 lb. Its IA3241 specification table separately shows 14.2 lb without explaining the measurement scope. We therefore do not label that figure as handheld or cleaner-only weight. For stairs and prolonged overhead use, assess the complete configuration you will carry. Likewise, 350 W is electrical power; it cannot be substituted for a suction rating in air watts.

## Versions and market differences

This guide covers the US Ice Blue IA3241. On September 14, 2026, Shark’s US store showed $499.99 for this package and $429.99 for the Copper Dusk IA1241, a $70 difference before any checkout changes. IA1241 supplies a charging cord and lists a crevice tool plus upholstery tool; IA3241 includes the station, crevice tool and Pet Multi Tool. Blue Reveal is explicitly promoted for IA3241.

The regular version is relevant if you prefer manual emptying and a simpler storage arrangement. Choose the station package at purchase if automatic dust transfer is central to your decision: later dock compatibility has not been verified. Regional IA1000 or IA3000 family names do not establish identical batteries, accessories, chargers or warranty coverage.

## Maintenance and ownership

Automatic emptying still depends on clear air paths and clean filters. Inspect the brushroll, nozzle, onboard cup and dock inlet when pickup or dust transfer weakens. A self-cleaning brushroll can reduce hair accumulation but does not remove the need to check trapped material. The claimed 45-day interval concerns stored debris, not permission to postpone every maintenance task.

The official Australia/New Zealand IA3000-series manual recommends cleaning filters at least monthly, rinsing with cold water only and allowing them to dry fully before refitting. This is a regional family reference; follow the instructions supplied with a US IA3241 for its exact procedure and parts. Confirm local replacement-filter availability and warranty terms before importing. Do not infer US odor-cartridge requirements from an overseas accessory package.
