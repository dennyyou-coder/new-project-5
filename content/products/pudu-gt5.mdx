## What the PUDU GT5 is

The GT5 is the large-lawn model in Pudu Robotics' GT Series, the company's first range of commercial robotic mowers. Pudu, known for indoor service and cleaning robots, positions it for extensive commercial lawns where mowing and clipping collection need to happen together.

## Read the specifications correctly

The 60-inch rotary deck and the 2-acres-per-hour target describe productivity in Pudu's stated conditions. The 10-hour runtime and 16-acre-per-charge figures also depend on grass, terrain and collection load. The release does not state cutting height, slope rating or bin capacity for the GT5.

## Versions and market differences

The GT Series comprises the GT3, GT5 and GT7. Pudu announced the range on 1 September 2026 and showed the GT5 working in GaLaBau's outdoor demonstration area. Pricing and regional sales channels were not stated; commercial buyers should contact Pudu or its distributors.

## Maintenance and ownership

Commercial mowers need scheduled blade sharpening, deck cleaning, collection-bin emptying and sensor cleaning. Autonomous dumping reduces operator visits but depends on site configuration. Plan charging, storage and a fallback for manual operation.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the GT5 for grounds teams managing large open lawns who want to automate mowing and collection. For golf greens and sports turf, compare the GT7.
