## What to check before buying

Owners wanting a wire-free installation on a modest lawn with manageable slopes. Start with the job the machine must do in your own space, then compare the features that can change that outcome. A specification sheet is useful for narrowing a shortlist; it does not replace a demonstration of the difficult part of your cleaning routine.

Keep this non-AWD model separate from the LiDAX Ultra AWD range when comparing traction.

## Versions and market differences

This is the US LiDAX Ultra 1000 non-AWD listing. Larger-area models and AWD versions are separate products, not interchangeable capacity settings.

## Maintenance and ownership

Confirm blade and disc availability, safe blade-change instructions, winter storage and local repairs. Check whether optional connectivity or anti-theft services add recurring costs.

Ask the seller to identify the local warranty provider, exclusions, repair location and spare-parts lead time in writing. Warranty terms and bundled accessories belong to the specific market and seller; this profile does not promise universal coverage.
