## What to check before buying

Pool owners seeking fewer manual retrievals and willing to accommodate a fixed dock installation. Start with the job the machine must do in your own space, then compare the features that can change that outcome. A specification sheet is useful for narrowing a shortlist; it does not replace a demonstration of the difficult part of your cleaning routine.

Check dock geometry, power and maintenance access first; solar assistance does not guarantee continuous operation.

## Versions and market differences

This is S3 with its docking system, not S2 Solar or S2 Solar Vision. Confirm the exact filter pack and dock kit; the official page describes multiple filter configurations.

## Maintenance and ownership

The official FAQ recommends weekly debris-box and bag cleaning and cleaning the internal basket every two weeks. Adjust attention to debris load and follow the supplied manual; automatic emptying is not maintenance-free.

Ask the seller to identify the local warranty provider, exclusions, repair location and spare-parts lead time in writing. Warranty terms and bundled accessories belong to the specific market and seller; this profile does not promise universal coverage.
