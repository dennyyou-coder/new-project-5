## What the P70 offers

P70 Pro Ultra brings a rotary-mop cleaning system, automatic station care and edge extension into MOVA's 2026 range without the mechanical-leg arrangement of V70 and Z70. It is most relevant to buyers with ordinary room transitions who want the station to handle routine pad washing, drying and dust collection.

The US black-model listing supplies the dated price in this guide, while the global page supplies the main specification. This separation matters because promotional packs and accessories are not always identical. A low purchase price is useful only when the supplied brush, station and regional service arrangement meet the intended needs.

## Start with clearance and thresholds

The P70 body measures 350 × 350 × 103.8 mm, and its threshold rating is 22 mm. These are practical selection limits, particularly for older furniture and raised bathroom transitions. It should not inherit a 4 cm or 9 cm claim simply because another MOVA robot uses a similar-looking station.

Measure furniture at the lowest obstruction, including supporting rails. For a door sill close to the rated limit, the angle, surface and available approach space can determine the result. If a room cannot be reached reliably, consider whether a ramp or separate cleaning schedule is acceptable before buying the robot.

## Rotary mops and heated station care

The two pads are advertised at up to 260 RPM and 12 N contact force. RoboSwing extends the edge mop by up to 4 cm. These are descriptions of the mechanism; actual results depend on soil, water setting, number of passes and whether the pad can reach the intended edge.

The 100°C claim needs particular care. MOVA defines it as a PTC washboard surface temperature, rather than a measured 100°C wash-water outlet. The twenty station spray nozzles and hot-air drying serve pad maintenance after cleaning. They do not establish that the robot applies that temperature to floors or removes every stain in a single pass.

## Versions and market differences

The CleanChop brush is optional on the global listing, so it should not automatically appear in a description of standard box contents. The global and US pages also differ on a side-brush lift detail. Buyers who depend on a particular accessory or clearance should obtain the regional model specification and included-parts list.

The guide image uses an official white rendering, while the price snapshot refers to the black US listing. Confirm the selected colour, cleaning-solution supply, spare pads and warranty territory. App recognition features should be checked on current firmware when a specific function is part of the purchasing decision.

## Maintenance and ownership

Automatic pad removal and 10.5 mm lift offer different ways to manage carpet areas. Test the chosen settings with actual rug pile and fringes instead of relying on ultrasonic detection alone. Loose objects and tangled brush ends can still interrupt a scheduled run.

At the station, the 4 L clean-water and 3.5 L used-water tanks need access from above. Regularly empty dirty water, inspect the washboard, remove trapped hair and service bags and filters. A one-hour drying mode is a manufacturer operating claim; check that the pads are actually dry under the home's humidity and ventilation conditions.

## Comparing the next step up

V70 adds a lower retractable-sensor body, mechanical legs and bagless collection. Those changes matter when furniture or thresholds are a genuine limitation. Dreame L60 Ultra provides a different alternative with an 82 mm body and higher documented crossing limits, while keeping a pad-based cleaning format.

Use the comparison to identify the required access and maintenance features, then compare local delivered prices and consumables. The three suction figures do not establish a common cleaning-performance ranking, and regional promotions should not substitute for checking the exact supplied model.
