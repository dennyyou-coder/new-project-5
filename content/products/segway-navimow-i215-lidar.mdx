## A distinct i2 navigation option

Navimow i215 LiDAR is the US retail model covered by this guide, following the [CES 2026 i2 LiDAR introduction](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/segway-navimow-expands-robotic-mower-lineup-with-smarter-more-effortless-lawn-care-solutions-debuting-at-ces-2026-302653790.html). The [official listing](https://navimow.com/products/navimow-i2-lidar-robot-lawn-mower) emphasizes solid-state LiDAR, vision and a real-scene map. Those features are intended to make a lawn understandable to the mower and owner without installing an external RTK antenna.

WCB has not tested mapping accuracy or mowing performance on this model. The useful buying question is how reliably it handles your actual lawn layout. Trees, narrow connections, boundary changes and the station approach matter more than a sensor’s point-count headline. Include those areas in a demonstration and review the map before relying on a schedule.

## Keep coverage and zone count separate

The recommended lawn size and the maximum mapping allowance are different figures. The latter is shared across the entire mapped property, including all zones. Creating more zones does not multiply capacity. Start with the total maintained grass area and then examine how much time is lost moving between sections, turning around obstacles and returning to charge.

A site near the upper limit deserves a practical schedule assessment. Leave room for growth conditions and periods when mowing is unsuitable or the garden is occupied. Isolated Zone Mapping helps describe disconnected areas, but it does not create a physical path between them. If the machine must be carried, include that effort and the charging arrangement in the ownership plan.

## Terrain and edges are separate decisions

The i215 should not be treated as the [i210 AWD](/products/segway-navimow-i210-awd) with a larger capacity label. Its navigation and traction approach differ. The cited slope test uses dry grass and an unobstructed incline. Inspect the transition onto slopes and the boundary near the bottom, not just the steepest open patch. Changing conditions can alter the difficulty of the same route.

EdgeSense ride-on mowing also differs from a separate trimming tool. Check whether the lawn borders a level surface that permits the intended approach. Raised walls, drops and flower beds create other requirements. Compare the remaining edge work after a complete pass, rather than assuming an edge-feature name means every boundary will be finished without owner attention.

## Versions and market differences

This guide uses the selected US i215 LiDAR offer and its dated USD 1,599 price. A garage bundle carries a different total. Confirm the mower, station, installation parts, accessories and local warranty as one package. Keep the LiDAR suffix and model code on the invoice so support and replacement parts match the purchased machine.

Ask for the current regional cutting-height specification and the instructions for any included low-cutting kit. A retailer’s general Navimow comparison may combine different models. Connectivity terms also deserve an exact-model check; do not transfer another series’ included service period or renewal price. If remote monitoring is essential, verify both the hardware and service conditions before purchase.

## Maintenance and ownership

Plan routine blade inspection, grass removal and sensor care according to the manual. A reliable mowing schedule depends on the machine remaining mechanically free and able to observe its surroundings. Check local blade availability and the repair process. For a seasonal outdoor appliance, battery storage and service turnaround can matter as much as an initial accessory discount.

Consider the noise context when scheduling near neighbors. The published laboratory figure was measured without cutting, so it cannot predict every real mowing session. Observe the machine on your grass if noise is a deciding factor. Follow operating-area restrictions around people and pets, and schedule with local wildlife activity in mind rather than treating recognition software as a complete safety solution.

## WCB buying view

i215 LiDAR is worth shortlisting for a suitable lawn when antenna-free mapping and a larger recommended area are useful. Compare it with the AWD i210 and compact [YUKA mini 2 1000](/products/mammotion-yuka-mini-2-1000) on the same access and terrain questions. Validate the combined mapped area, resolve the regional cutting range and confirm service terms. A successful representative route is better purchase evidence than multiplying zone counts or comparing unqualified sensor numbers.
