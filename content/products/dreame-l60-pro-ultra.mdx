## What the Pro designation adds

L60 Pro Ultra and L60 Ultra share a 35,000 Pa maximum suction claim, but the useful differences concern navigation, room access and mopping. The Pro uses retractable VersaLift DToF navigation, ProLeap mechanical legs and thermal OmniScrub pads. Those changes address particular home layouts rather than making the regular model obsolete in every situation.

Dreame introduced the L60 series in its 28 April 2026 announcement. This guide follows the current US Pro listing for specifications and price. The assessment explains the documented hardware and ownership implications; it does not represent a hands-on cleaning, noise or obstacle-avoidance test.

## Compare the body before the model tier

The Pro measures 89 mm with the sensor retracted and 111 mm raised. Regular L60 Ultra is listed at 82 mm, so the higher-tier product is not the thinner option. Measure the lowest point beneath the furniture that matters, including rails and uneven floor surfaces, and leave space beyond the nominal body dimension.

ProLeap also needs a careful reading. Dreame advertises up to 8.8 cm across a two-step arrangement, with the intermediate platform wider than 23 cm. The single-step rating is 4.5 cm. A narrow raised sill, slippery edge or confined approach may behave differently from that documented test geometry.

## Separate floor heating from dock heating

Thermal OmniScrub uses phase-change pads to retain heat from station washing. Dreame's laboratory footnote specifies 30–40°C after one minute of cleaning, with a peak of 40°C; this is not a promise of continuously active floor heating. The separate 100°C footnote describes the washboard PTC heating area, not a measured floor temperature or a universal water-outlet temperature.

The pad system offers 10.5 mm lift and automatic removal, while the edge mop extends up to 40 mm. Match those features to rug pile, moisture-sensitive surfaces and cabinet edges. Follow the floor supplier's care instructions and configure water use accordingly; heating and scrubbing specifications are not universal proof of suitability for every finish.

## Versions and market differences

L60 Pro Ultra, L60 Ultra and other L60 suffixes are distinct model listings. The Pro has a 6,400 mAh battery and 300 mL onboard dust bin; the regular US L60 Ultra lists 5,200 mAh and 275 mL. Shared product-family photographs or similar stations should not be used to transfer those figures between versions.

The dated US price belongs to L60 Pro Ultra. Before comparing another region, check the complete model code, colour, accessory bundle, compatible solution and warranty coverage. If app integrations matter, verify the current regional firmware and supported settings rather than assuming every announced feature is active immediately.

## Maintenance and ownership

The station has a 3.2 L dust bag and separate 4.5 L clean-water and 4 L used-water tanks. At 590 mm tall, it also needs additional space above for tank removal. Placement under a shelf should be checked against the maintenance motion, not just the closed station dimensions.

Regular care still includes emptying used water, cleaning the washboard and inspecting pads, brush ends, filters and sensors. A larger battery can extend the available energy budget, but it does not eliminate station visits or establish a whole-home runtime in maximum suction. Compare the full cleaning-and-maintenance routine rather than one isolated capacity figure.

## Choosing the Pro over the regular L60

Choose the Pro when a measured threshold genuinely needs its additional crossing ability or when thermal pad mopping is a priority. Choose the regular L60 shortlist when lower furniture access matters more and its documented transition limits are sufficient. Both use the same advertised maximum suction, so that number does not explain the price difference.

MOVA V70 provides a further comparison for buyers interested in mechanical legs and rotary pads, with bagless collection as a different ownership choice. Compare local prices only after matching access requirements, station placement and consumables, because these practical differences shape daily use.
