## What the PUDU GT7 is

The GT7 is the precision model in Pudu Robotics' GT Series: a triplex reel mower for golf courses and sports turf. Its three floating reel units follow ground contours, and its positioning combines RTK with LiDAR and camera vision.

## Read the specifications correctly

Pudu gives a 62-inch (160 cm) cutting width, RTK accuracy of up to ±2.5 cm under supported conditions and slopes up to 30 degrees. Height of cut is adjustable without tools, but the release gives no numeric range, runtime or productivity figure for the GT7.

## Versions and market differences

The GT7 shares its launch with the GT3 and GT5, announced on 1 September 2026 and shown at GaLaBau. Pricing and regional availability were not published.

## Maintenance and ownership

Reel mowers need regular backlapping or grinding and careful bedknife adjustment to keep a clean cut. Keep RTK, LiDAR and camera sensors clean, and plan charging and secure storage on site. Turf managers should define mowing patterns and exclusion zones before unattended operation.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the GT7 for golf and sports-turf operators evaluating autonomous reel mowing. For general commercial lawns with clipping collection, compare the GT5.
