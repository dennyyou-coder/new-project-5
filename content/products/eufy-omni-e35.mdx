## What the Omni E35 is

The Omni E35 is eufy's roller-mop robot vacuum for the 2026 season. It pairs a self-rinsing HydroJet roller with an AeroTurbo multi-cyclone dust path and a ThermoClean station that washes and dries the mop. It sits below the brand's S-series flagship and above the entry C-series models announced later in September.

The design choices are aimed at hard floors with daily spills: a roller that keeps rinsing itself as it moves, firm downward pressure, and hot-water washing at the dock to limit smells between runs.

## Read the specifications correctly

The 35,000 Pa suction figure is a motor-level laboratory number. It is useful for comparing eufy models but does not translate directly into carpet pickup. The 16 N roller pressure and the 280+ object recognition count are also manufacturer figures.

The station figures describe different jobs: 60°C water for washing the roller, 55°C air for drying it, and an up-to-60-day interval for emptying dust. Treat each as its own claim rather than adding them into a single hygiene score.

## Versions and market differences

eufy's European and UK rollout started on 4 September 2026, while the US opened preorders the same day for a later release. Prices differ by market: the official Irish store listed €1,099 on 7 November 2026, and US launch coverage reported a regular price of about USD 1,300 with an early offer near USD 1,000.

Model naming also varies; some listings call it the eufy E35 Omni. Match the model code T210MV11 when comparing offers.

## Maintenance and ownership

The station handles dust emptying, clean-water refilling, detergent dosing and dirty-water collection, but the roller, its scraper, the water tanks and the wash tray still need regular rinsing. The cyclone stage reduces how quickly the filter clogs; it does not remove the need to check and clean it.

Plan space for the station and access to its tanks. A station that is hard to reach usually gets serviced less often.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the E35 for mostly hard-floor homes that want roller mopping with hot-water washing at a mid-premium price. Buyers who need stated threshold heights or body height should confirm them on the local product page, as the checked page did not list them in text.
