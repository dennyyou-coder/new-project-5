## What the Automower 312V is

The Automower 312V is one of three Husqvarna robotic mowers announced in January 2026 with AI vision. It drops the boundary wire in favour of virtual boundaries, combining satellite positioning with a built-in camera that recognises and avoids obstacles.

It sits above the 308V, which covers up to 800 m², and is aimed at mid-sized suburban gardens sold through Husqvarna's dealer network.

## Read the specifications correctly

The 1,200 m² figure is a capacity rating with a ±20% tolerance. Lawns with many zones, narrow passages or tree cover will take longer to cover.

The slope figures describe two different places: up to 40% inside the installation, but only 15% at the boundary. Cutting width is 22 cm, and cutting height is set by hand between 20 mm and 50 mm.

## Versions and market differences

The UK RRP is £1,499, and the UK product page listed the same price including VAT on 7 November 2026. Other markets publish their own prices and dealer terms.

The 308V is the smaller sibling for lawns up to 800 m². Match the article number 970 83 11-03 when comparing UK offers.

## Maintenance and ownership

The camera and blades need regular cleaning and checking, and virtual boundaries should be reviewed after landscaping changes. Wi-Fi must reach the whole lawn for wire-free installation, so check coverage before buying.

Because Husqvarna sells through authorised dealers, ask about installation help, winter service and blade supplies at purchase.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the 312V for gardens up to about 1,200 m² with good Wi-Fi coverage and moderate slopes, where an established dealer network matters. For steeper or larger properties, compare all-wheel-drive models.
