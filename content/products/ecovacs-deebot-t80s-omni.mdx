## Judge the roller by the finished floor

OZMO Roller 2.0 turns at a published 220 rpm, and TruEdge extension is intended to move treatment closer to boundaries. Test a straight wall, an inside corner and the spaces around chair legs. A demonstration should show the floor after drying and the areas the robot missed. Edge reach is only useful when the navigation route brings the roller into a workable position.

The 24,800 Pa suction claim and 3,800 Pa roller-pressure claim describe different functions. They should not be combined into an overall cleaning score. Carpet pickup, wet residue removal and moisture control each need their own observation. If the home mixes rugs and hard floors, check the exact lifting and carpet settings instead of assuming one mode suits the complete route.

## Navigation and low furniture

The cited configuration combines Mini-ToF navigation with AIVI sensing that includes RGB and structured light. Test common obstacles in the normal lighting of the home, while removing cords and objects that can create trouble. A recognition label does not guarantee every small or transparent item will be avoided. Look for repeatable coverage and understandable recovery when a path is blocked.

The 3.9-inch height is close to many nominal furniture gaps. Measure farther under the furniture, where crossbars and sagging fabric may reduce space. The crossing claim also needs a practical check against the shape of a doorway track. Plan manual carrying between levels and a station location the robot can reach reliably after washing a dirty area.

## Maintenance and ownership

The station’s heated washing and drying claims describe care at the dock, not treatment temperatures on the floor. After a heavy job, inspect the roller, used-water collection and accessible station surfaces. Long advertised bag or tray intervals depend on a particular workload and do not remove regular attention to collected moisture and debris.

Check the local supply and prices of dust bags, filters, roller parts and approved solution. Position the station so the owner can remove tanks and reach the tray without moving furniture. If the route regularly collects pet hair or kitchen residue, assess how easily those materials leave the system. Successful automation includes a care routine that is straightforward after the robot has finished.
