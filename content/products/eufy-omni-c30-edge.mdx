## What the C30 Edge is

The Omni C30 Edge is the edge-cleaning version of eufy's 2026 C30 series. It uses rotating pentagonal mop pads rather than the roller found on the Omni E35, and adds an extending mop that reaches toward walls and furniture, guided by a side-facing laser.

The C30 family sits below the E35 in eufy's range and brings a self-emptying station to a lower price point.

## Read the specifications correctly

eufy lists 35,000 Pa of suction and 10 N of mop pressure, both from its own laboratory testing. The MopMaster 3.0 extension improves reach along straight edges; room shape, furniture legs and skirting profiles still limit how much of the edge it can wipe.

The checked US page did not state the body height, station wash and dry temperatures or the dust-bag interval in text. Do not borrow those figures from the E35 or other eufy models.

## Versions and market differences

The Nordic release scheduled C30 Edge sales for 8 October and standard C30 sales for 31 October 2026. The US store listed the C30 Edge at USD 899.99 on 7 November 2026. Early-bird promotions appeared on the series page but were not tied to the listed price.

The standard C30 does not have the extending edge mop. Check which version a retailer is offering.

## Maintenance and ownership

Rotating pads need regular washing and periodic replacement, and the cyclone dust path still feeds a filter that should be checked. The station empties dust but does not clean the mop mounts, side brush or wheels.

If the station's water tanks are used, plan a routine for emptying the dirty-water tank and rinsing both tanks to avoid odours.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the C30 Edge for homes where edge wiping matters and the budget sits below eufy's roller-mop models. Buyers who need a guaranteed low body height or specific station temperatures should confirm them locally first.
