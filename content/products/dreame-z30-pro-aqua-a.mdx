## One vacuum platform with two jobs

Z30 Pro Aqua-A targets a household that needs both dry vacuuming and wet floor care. The [global specification](https://global.dreametech.com/products/z30-pro-aqua-a) separates the main vacuum, dry tools and AquaCycle wet head. That distinction is useful for sofas, corners and hard floors, but it also means changing attachments and storing the parts. Consider how often you will actually switch between those jobs during a normal cleaning session.

This guide uses official documentation and the exact German Aqua-A offer. It is not a hands-on assessment of pickup, streaking or ergonomics. The main selection question is whether a shared vacuum platform is more convenient for your home than keeping a dry vacuum and a dedicated washer ready separately.

## Read runtime and weight by configuration

The maximum runtime is measured with a crevice nozzle in Eco mode. It cannot predict how long a powered floor brush or wet-cleaning setup will run. Estimate your intended session using the relevant attachment and power setting. If a seller demonstrates only the lightest configuration, ask to repeat the handling test with the wet head and its normal water load.

The same distinction applies to weight. A body-only figure excludes attachments that affect wrist load, steering and lifting. Test a turn beside a kitchen cabinet, a pass under a table and the movement from floor to charging base. These ordinary actions reveal more about daily convenience than a bare-unit specification or a short straight-line demonstration.

## Wet-head capacity and dry-tool flexibility

Clean water, dirty water and dry dust have separate collection capacities. Plan where the wet head will be emptied after use. Do not treat the dry dust cup as a liquid container or assume any powered brush can collect spills. Follow the approved task and cleaning method for each attachment, including the floor manufacturer’s restrictions on moisture.

The folding tube may help with access under furniture, while the interchangeable dry tools expand the appliance’s role beyond the floor. Compare that flexibility with a dedicated [F25 ACE Pro](/products/roborock-f25-ace-pro) or [PencilWash](/products/dyson-pencilwash). Their floor-cleaning routines and tank sizes answer a different need. A larger washer tank can matter more than attachment flexibility in a home dominated by hard floors.

## Versions and market differences

The [German store](https://de.dreametech.com/products/stielstaubsauger-mit-nassreinigungsfunktion-z30-pro-aqua-a) identifies this model as Z30 Pro Aqua-A and supplied the dated EUR 429 reference. Confirm the contents of the selected package before comparing it with a promotion elsewhere. A spare roller, additional powered tool or different base can change the value of an apparently similar offer. A crossed-out price is not evidence that all sellers supply the same package.

Keep Aqua-A on the invoice and check the model code, plug, warranty provider and consumable compatibility. Shorter Aqua naming in another region does not establish an identical bundle. If replacing an earlier Dreame vacuum, verify attachment compatibility directly rather than assuming that the shared Z30 family name makes every connector or charging arrangement interchangeable.

## Maintenance and ownership

The global FAQ makes the charging setup unusually important: the wet-cleaning brush must be attached at the base. Plan storage around the actual charging configuration, including the position of other tools. A convenient wall space for a dry stick vacuum may not provide suitable access for the complete wet-cleaning arrangement. Confirm the regional manual before installing anything permanently.

After wet work, allow time to handle dirty water and care for the roller and collection passages. Heated drying supports that process but does not remove all manual cleaning. Price the wet roller, dry filters, brush components and approved solution. For a combined appliance, good local parts support matters because one unavailable attachment can interrupt an entire part of the cleaning routine.

## WCB buying view

Shortlist Aqua-A when its interchangeable architecture matches your actual mix of floor, upholstery and detail work. Choose on assembled handling, the wet-head maintenance routine and a verified local package. The strongest reason to buy is useful task coverage in one system. A runtime achieved with a crevice tool or a body-only weight should remain a qualified specification, rather than the deciding promise for wet floor cleaning.
