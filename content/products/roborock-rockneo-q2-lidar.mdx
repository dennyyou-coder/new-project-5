## What the RockNeo Q2 LiDAR is

The RockNeo Q2 LiDAR is Roborock's second-generation entry robot mower, shown at IFA 2026. It navigates by LiDAR and cameras instead of a boundary wire or RTK base station, which can help in gardens where trees or buildings block satellite signals.

## Read the specifications correctly

Roborock's page gives three tested figures: a 3 cm edge distance for the side blade, slopes up to 45% and passages as narrow as 0.6 m. The 3 cm figure is the hardware distance to the boundary, not a guarantee of a finished edge on every border. Lawn area and cutting width were not stated on the checked page.

## Versions and market differences

Notebookcheck reported preorders at a €899 recommended retail price after IFA. Swedish and Australian coverage said local pricing and dates would follow, and US availability was not announced. Check the local Roborock garden site for the confirmed price.

## Maintenance and ownership

Keep the LiDAR window and cameras clean, check and replace blades regularly and clear grass from the deck and wheels. Without RTK, mapping depends on the LiDAR seeing stable features, so large changes to the garden may require re-mapping.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the RockNeo Q2 LiDAR for small and medium gardens with obstacles to satellite signals, especially where wire installation is impractical. Larger or open lawns may suit RTK-based or AWD models better.
