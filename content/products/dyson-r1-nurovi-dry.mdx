## What R1 adds to the Dyson robot range

The R1 Nurovi Dry is the dedicated vacuuming choice in Dyson’s September 2026 robot lineup. Its appeal depends on the work you want to delegate: collecting loose dirt across accessible floors while you retain another method for washing. For a carpet-led household, a water tank and mop dock may add little everyday value. For a kitchen-led household, their absence could be the deciding limitation.

This is a guide to the published specification and ownership requirements. WCB has not measured this robot’s cleaning performance. A useful buying demonstration should include the debris and floor surfaces you actually encounter, rather than a prepared patch on a showroom floor.

## Pickup, hair and room edges

Twin-capture separates the mechanical movement of larger debris from the airflow used for finer dust. That gives buyers a more useful question than whether the suction number is impressive: does the head collect crumbs and dust together without leaving a second job? Ask for a mixed-debris demonstration along a skirting board and across a rug transition.

The side sweeper is intended to improve reach at boundaries. Check the result behind chair legs and in corners, where a circular body cannot always approach from the ideal direction. Hair-management features may reduce interruptions, but long strands, threads and rug tassels remain reasons to inspect the cleaning path regularly.

## Mapping and a practical cleaning schedule

Place the dock where the robot can return without threading through movable furniture. Before the first mapping run, clear cables and small objects and leave the doors to intended rooms open, following Dyson’s owner guidance. Test the normal route with the furniture back in its usual position before relying on a scheduled clean.

The quoted runtime is a maximum, not a promise that an entire furnished property will finish in one session. Obstacles, carpet work and return travel consume time. Start with the busiest rooms, inspect the completed map and missed areas, and then decide whether to schedule additional rooms or a separate pass on another day.

## Versions and market differences

This profile covers the UK R1 in Light grey/Vinca blue. R2 and R3 use different cleaning equipment; their washing and stain-detection features should not be read as software upgrades available to R1. The product was announced on 3 September 2026, and the UK store price recorded here is a dated reference rather than a worldwide launch offer.

Dyson’s UK pages contain both purchase and notification messages, so confirm the delivery estimate for the selected unit. For an import, ask the seller to identify the regional product code, compatible app setup, supplied electrical equipment and service arrangements before comparing prices. ## Maintenance and ownership

The bagless dock removes one recurring supply to buy, but it leaves an owner responsible for the collected dust. Plan an accessible emptying location and a routine for inspecting the container, filters and transfer path. The advertised storage interval concerns accumulated dry debris; a heavily shedding pet or frequent cleaning can change how quickly it fills.

Dyson’s Australian support instructions recommend filter washing and brush and sweeper checks at least monthly. Use the care procedure for the supplied unit, allow washed parts to dry as directed, and keep cleaning products away from components that are not approved for them. Replacement-part prices and UK service turnaround have not been verified for this guide.

## How to judge value before buying

The strongest purchase case is a household with a clear dry-cleaning workload and no need to automate mopping. Compare the cost of the robot with the time spent managing missed rooms, moving obstacles and maintaining the dock. A lower maintenance burden only matters if the robot can consistently reach the areas you want cleaned.

During the return period, test charging, one scheduled run, your hardest threshold and cleanup after ordinary pet hair or food crumbs. Record intervention frequency as well as visible pickup. If washing remains the larger chore, inspect R2’s additional equipment before deciding that R1’s lower listed price is the better overall purchase.
