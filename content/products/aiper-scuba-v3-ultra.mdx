## What to check before buying

Pools where benches, shallow shelves and a mixture of floating and settled debris need attention. Start with the job the machine must do in your own space, then compare the features that can change that outcome. A specification sheet is useful for narrowing a shortlist; it does not replace a demonstration of the difficult part of your cleaning routine.

A minimum depth does not guarantee access to every shelf; check approach angles and surface compatibility.

## Versions and market differences

This is Scuba V3 Ultra, not Scuba V3 or another Scuba series model. Regional bundles, filter accessories and stock availability should be confirmed at purchase.

## Maintenance and ownership

Check filter replacement costs and the correct rinsing routine. Plan a comfortable lifting, draining, charging and storage route; automated retrieval assistance does not mean the robot stores itself.

Ask the seller to identify the local warranty provider, exclusions, repair location and spare-parts lead time in writing. Warranty terms and bundled accessories belong to the specific market and seller; this profile does not promise universal coverage.
