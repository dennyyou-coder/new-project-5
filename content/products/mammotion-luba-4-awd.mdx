## What the LUBA 4 AWD is

LUBA 4 AWD is Mammotion's 2026 all-wheel-drive robot mower series, unveiled at IFA. It combines four cameras, 360° LiDAR and NetRTK positioning with independent front-wheel steering for large and sloped lawns.

## Read the specifications correctly

The three versions differ in rated area and cutting system: the 3000F and 6000F use two discs and a 440 mm width, while the 12000F uses three discs and a 530 mm width. The 80% (38.6°) slope and 60 mm obstacle figures are manufacturer ratings; wet grass and soft ground reduce real traction.

## Versions and market differences

Mammotion said preorders were expected in Q4 2026 without a single global retail date or official pricing. Regional availability and bundles, including the edge-trimming module, may differ by market.

## Maintenance and ownership

Keep cameras and the LiDAR window clean, check blades and the cutting discs, and clear grass from the AWD wheels. The 210 W fast charger shortens charging, but battery capacity was not stated in the model guide.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist LUBA 4 AWD for large or steep lawns and owners who want camera, LiDAR and RTK positioning together. Smaller flat lawns may not need AWD.
