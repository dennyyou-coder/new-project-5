## Why the regular L60 needs its own guide

L60 Ultra has the same advertised 35,000 Pa maximum suction as L60 Pro Ultra, but it is a different proposition for furniture access. Its 82 mm body is lower than the Pro with its sensor retracted. That can matter more than the product tier when a bed or sofa is the main area a buyer wants to automate.

This page covers the regular US L60 Ultra introduced with the L60 series in April 2026. The Pro, PE and FE suffixes should remain separate when checking navigation, batteries, cleaning systems and price. The assessment uses current manufacturer documentation and does not claim independent cleaning or obstacle-avoidance test results.

## Check low openings and raised transitions

The fixed 3D ToF design avoids the raised-and-retracted sensor states of the Pro. The robot still needs a clear route beneath furniture: crossbars, floor unevenness and cables can be more restrictive than the visible opening. Measure the lowest point and follow the official minimum 84 mm entry-clearance requirement, despite the listed 82 mm body height.

Dreame rates a single step at 3 cm and a combined two-step arrangement at 5 cm, with an intermediate platform wider than 12 cm. One inch conversion in the specification table is inconsistent, so this guide uses the metric limit supported by the detailed footnote. Neither figure establishes a single vertical 5 cm climb.

## What the mopping system includes

Regular L60 Ultra uses dual OmniScrub rotary pads, 10.5 mm pad lift and automatic pad removal. These provide options for mixed hard floors and carpets, but carpet pile, fringes and loose rugs still need suitable settings. A sensor detecting carpet is not the same as confirming that every wet component clears it.

The station advertises up to 100°C mop washing and 45°C drying. The detailed 100°C footnote defines the washboard PTC heating area, not a measured water outlet or floor temperature. The Pro's separate thermal floor-mopping claim should not appear in a regular L60 specification simply because both products share a family name. Match water settings and cleaning solution to the floor's care requirements.

## Versions and market differences

The US regular model lists a 5,200 mAh battery, 275 mL onboard dust bin and 80 mL onboard water tank. These should be checked independently from station capacities and from the Pro's larger battery and bin. Similar exterior styling does not establish that parts or operating limits are interchangeable.

The dated $1,299.99 snapshot belongs to the regular US L60 Ultra listing. Confirm the full model code, colour, included accessories, regional service coverage and current promotion before ordering. If a seller abbreviates the title to L60, request enough information to identify which version is actually supplied.

## Maintenance and ownership

The station uses a 3.2 L dust bag, 4.5 L clean-water tank and 4 L used-water tank. Its 600 mL solution compartment supports automatic dosing, but only compatible solution should be used according to the product instructions. Dirty-water handling and periodic washboard cleaning remain part of the routine.

At 590 mm tall, the station needs room above for lifting the tanks. Check that cupboard doors and nearby furniture do not obstruct bag replacement or cleaning. Brush ends, filters, sensors and mop mounts also need attention; automatic emptying should not be interpreted as proof that the onboard bin never needs inspection.

## Deciding whether to pay for the Pro

The regular model makes sense when lower furniture access is the priority and its 3 cm single-step limit covers the home. The Pro becomes more relevant for demanding transitions, retractable navigation and thermal pad mopping. Because both advertise the same maximum suction, their meaningful differences are not explained by that number.

MOVA P70 provides another rotary-pad comparison with a taller body and a 22 mm threshold rating. Compare the actual floor plan, local delivered price and consumable availability before choosing. A lower price elsewhere is not useful if the selected model cannot reach the rooms or furniture that motivated the purchase.
