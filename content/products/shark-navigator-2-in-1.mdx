## What the Navigator 2-in-1 is

The Navigator 2-in-1 is SharkNinja's lower-priced combination robot for the US market. It maps rooms with 360° LiDAR, cleans in rows, mops hard floors while steering around rugs, and empties debris into a bagless base.

It sits below Shark's PowerDetect robots and gives buyers mapped vacuum-and-mop cleaning without a mop-washing station.

## Read the specifications correctly

Shark describes performance through comparisons, such as 4X suction versus the Dreame D10 Plus Gen2 and 2X stain cleaning versus the Roomba 105 Combo. These are manufacturer comparisons, not absolute ratings. The release does not state suction in pascals, runtime, battery capacity or tank volume.

The 30- and 60-day base ratings describe how long debris can collect before emptying in Shark's assumptions; homes with pets or heavy traffic will fill the base sooner.

## Versions and market differences

Two versions share the robot but differ in the base. The 60-day version was USD 599 at SharkNinja.com from 29 September 2026, with Amazon to follow. The 30-day version at USD 499 was announced for Amazon, Costco and Sam's Club without a date.

The launch was US-focused; check local listings before assuming the same versions elsewhere.

## Maintenance and ownership

The base is bagless, so it needs emptying and occasional rinsing of the bin. The mop pad and its water tank need manual cleaning after mopping runs, because the base does not wash the mop. The self-cleaning brushroll reduces hair wrap but should still be checked.

Keep the LiDAR turret clear of dust and confirm the robot fits under low furniture, as the release does not give a height.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the Navigator 2-in-1 for budget-conscious US buyers who want LiDAR mapping and occasional mopping. Buyers who mop daily may prefer a model whose station washes the mop.
