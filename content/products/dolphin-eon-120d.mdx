## What the EON 120d is

The EON 120d is the upper model in Maytronics' first cordless Dolphin line, introduced at CES Unveiled in January 2026. Maytronics positions it on coverage: floor, walls, waterline, steps, corners and tanning ledges as shallow as 8 inches.

It adds DebrisLock ultra-fine filtration, which the EON 100 does not have, and connects to the Maytronics One app over Wi-Fi.

## Read the specifications correctly

Maytronics publishes several runtime figures. The product page lists cleaning cycles of 1.5, 2 or 2.5 hours and an UltraRun claim of up to 2 weeks of pool cleaning on one charge; the September blog says up to 4 hours per charge. These describe different things, so read cycle length and charging interval separately.

The 50 ft rating is a maximum pool length, not an area figure.

## Versions and market differences

The US store listed USD 1,449 on 7 November 2026, while Maytronics' September blog quoted USD 1,199; check the current price. The EON 100 starts at USD 899 without DebrisLock.

Maytronics also sells a Dolphin Nautilus EON 120d under a different product number. Match 99994410-US for this model.

## Maintenance and ownership

Empty and rinse the top-access filter after each cycle; ultra-fine filters clog faster in pools with heavy debris. App functions work only when the robot is above the waterline.

The robot weighs about 24 lb, so lifting it out at the waterline is part of the routine. The warranty is 3 years.

## Who should shortlist it

Shortlist the EON 120d for pools with steps, benches or shallow ledges where coverage matters more than surface skimming. Compare it with station-equipped rivals if hands-free charging and emptying are priorities.
