## What to check before buying

Owners who value easier filter care and have suitable deck space for the service station. Start with the job the machine must do in your own space, then compare the features that can change that outcome. A specification sheet is useful for narrowing a shortlist; it does not replace a demonstration of the difficult part of your cleaning routine.

The robot must be lifted from the pool and placed on the station; allow for both robot and station maintenance.

## Versions and market differences

This profile covers AquaSense X with the AstroRinse station. AquaSense 2 Ultra and other Beatbot models are different platforms. Regional launch and purchase conditions may differ.

## Maintenance and ownership

Check station drainage and water-supply requirements before choosing a location. Budget for robot filters, station cleaning and any clarification consumables you plan to use.

Ask the seller to identify the local warranty provider, exclusions, repair location and spare-parts lead time in writing. Warranty terms and bundled accessories belong to the specific market and seller; this profile does not promise universal coverage.
